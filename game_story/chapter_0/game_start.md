# Initial Game Background

You have just ascended to the throne as the new king, at the age of forty, with grand ambitions and lofty aspirations. Yet you face enormous challenges.

Your father died in the northern war. The realm he left you is intact, solvent and quietly rotten.

Within the kingdom, hidden currents are running through the royal court, and rival factions are engaged in fierce political struggles.

Your half-brother, **Duke Tomas**, openly covets your throne. It is an open secret. He is well liked by the kingdom's great houses, he has the patience you lack, and since the northern war he has held the granary keys of the river country that feeds your capital.

Your queen, **Victoria**, comes of a banking family, and her kin hold the counting-houses of half your realm's towns. Together with them she is constantly seeking to expand her clan's influence and power.

Your daughter, **Princess Nora O-kala**, is nineteen years old. A fierce and formidable soldier, she is serving in the army to hone her skills and undergo rigorous training. Your relationship with her, however, appears to be strained.

Your **Chancellor** is greedy, cunning and devious. He appears to have something incriminating hanging over him, which is apparently known to your **Steward**. The two have a notoriously poor relationship and are constantly at odds with each other.

To the west, the riders of the **Khanate of {raider_realm}** are becoming increasingly restless and appear to be preparing to raid eastward.

To the north, the **Queen of {neighbor_realm}** has seized control of {empire_realm}'s Emperor, reducing him to little more than a puppet. She has also been steadily annexing surrounding minor city-states and harbors openly hostile intentions toward your kingdom.

Among your advisers, the person you trust most is **Lord Malvern**. He was instrumental in securing your position on the throne and wields considerable influence among the court. However, he is advanced in age and in poor health.

The kingdom also appears to be facing a **food crisis**. Unrest is beginning to emerge in some regions. There are rumors that a barefoot preacher has been rallying the people to organize demonstrations and protests, demanding that the kingdom increase food relief and punish corrupt officials.

Your reign has only just begun.

The throne is yours—but the kingdom may already be on the brink of chaos.
