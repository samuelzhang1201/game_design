# Initial Game Background

You have just ascended to the throne as the new king, at the age of 40, with grand ambitions and lofty aspirations. Yet you face enormous challenges.

Within the kingdom, hidden currents are running through the royal court, and rival factions are engaged in fierce political struggles.

Your half-brother, **Duke Tomas**, openly covets your throne. It is an open secret. However, he maintains close relationships with many of the kingdom’s nobles and controls the development of the kingdom’s technology.

Your queen, **Victoria**, oversees the kingdom’s financial affairs. Together with her own extended family, she is constantly seeking to expand her clan’s influence and power.

Your daughter, **Princess Nora O-kala**, is 19 years old. A fierce and formidable female general, she is currently serving in the military to hone her skills and undergo rigorous training. Your relationship with her, however, appears to be strained.

The **Chief Minister of Internal Affairs** is greedy, cunning, and devious. He appears to have something incriminating hanging over him, which is apparently known to the **Minister of Finance**. The two have a notoriously poor relationship and are constantly at odds with each other.

To the north, the barbarians of **Doncaster** are becoming increasingly restless and appear to be preparing to raid southward.

To the east, the **Queen of the Duchy of Bentleigh** has seized control of the Empire’s Emperor, reducing him to little more than a puppet. She has also been steadily annexing surrounding minor city-states and harbors openly hostile intentions toward your kingdom.

Among your advisers, the person you trust most is **Lord Malvern**. He was instrumental in securing your position on the throne and wields considerable influence among the court. However, he is advanced in age and in poor health.

The kingdom also appears to be facing a **food crisis**. Unrest is beginning to emerge in some regions. There are rumors that someone has been rallying the people to organize demonstrations and protests, demanding that the kingdom increase food relief and punish corrupt officials.

Your reign has only just begun.

The throne is yours—but the kingdom may already be on the brink of chaos.
