# Tools and art pipelines (`tools/`)

Headless bakes, importers, installers, snapshots and benches (`Godot -s`, or the `.tscn` runners). Every tool takes `--map` and defaults to `maps.csv` `is_default`; none names a pack (`tools/packs.py`). Guides: `tools/NEW_MAP.md` (a new pack, in order), `tools/map3d/README.md` (the Terrain3D build), `assets/flux/README.md` (the 2D art pipeline), `tools/units3d/README.md` and each model folder's README. Root rules apply: assets land in the asset layout, ComfyUI stops at images, no Hunyuan3D, Tripo on the paid plan only.

## Map pack import and bakes

- `tools/import_azgaar.gd` reads only the pack's `*Full*.json` (never a PNG) and writes the pack tables and the `world_*_index.png` hit-test bitmaps; the other `world_*.png` come from the bakes in `NEW_MAP.md`. All pack coordinates are the JSON's own; nothing is calibrated or scaled. Hand edits are overwritten on re-import.
- Bakes that STAY (the 3D build reads them): `bake_relief.gd`, `bake_biome_weights.gd`, `bake_sea_distance.gd`, `bake_settlements.gd`, `bake_manors.gd`. `bake_relief.gd` bakes relief AND weights, each with its own resolution and blur (`map_relief_scale` / `_blur_px`, `map_biome_weights_scale` / `map_biome_blur_px`); `bake_biome_weights.gd` alone bakes weights at its default — **run `bake_relief.gd` last**, and after changing any band, scale, blur or detail value.
- **The Terrain3D build** is ONE command, `tools/map3d/build_pack.sh [--map ID]`: `export_manor_land.gd` → `bake_world.py` → `bake_trees.py` → `build_world.gd` → `bake_layers.gd`, all headless, its data into `maps.csv` `map3d_dir` (empty: `assets/3D/terrain/<id>`) and its world SCENE into `scenes/map3d/worlds/<id>.tscn` (`DataRepository.map3d_scene_of`; `Map3DWorldLoader.data_dir` joins them). Only the bake's raw `height.r32` / `control.r32` / `color.rgba8` stay local (too big for git). `build_world.gd --material-only` re-applies the terrain material to a committed build without the raw inputs. Changing a geometry script means bumping `Map3DLayerBake.FORMAT` (`scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md`).
- `bake_world.py` reads `world_relief.png` for the open sea and `world_biome_weights_0..3.png` for the ground; it sows the open fields (`sow_patchwork`) and calls `bake_houses.py` (lanes, packing, the landmark's levelled terrace, `fortify` walls and gates, `harbour` piers, cliff harbours and moored ships). `bake_trees.py` plants woods (`test_bake_trees.py` over the built pack). `pack_aerial.py` packs aerial terrain layers seamless and writes their VRAM `.import` (`--match-mean` keeps a new layer at the old mean colour so the bake's colour map needs no rebuild); import them before building. `build_tree_atlas.py` builds the crown atlas from `data/map3d_tree_crowns.csv`.

## Snapshots and benches

- **`tools/snapshot_screen.gd`** (run as `res://scenes/tools/snapshot_screen.tscn`) renders one screen to a PNG with the game's theme applied: `--screen court|conversation|main_menu|root`, `--layer`, `--manor`, `--speaker`, `--resolution WxH` (the REAL window — the only faithful size check) or `--scale`, `--quiet` (no council), `--dev`, `--bal key=value`, `--set TARGET:PROP=VALUE` (a look value — a `res://` `.tres` or a 3D-world node's export — for one shot, to bracket it before it is set in the editor), `--still` (the window takes no input: the owner's resting pointer cannot hover or zoom the shot), `--day-share F`, `--weather KIND[,KIND…]`, `--zoom3d`, `--click3d fx,fy`, `--hover3d fx,fy`, `--march3d fx,fy [--march-confirm] [--march-commit] [--speed N]`, `--trace-couriers`, `--supply-front` / `--supply-front-reg CODE` / `--supply-front-before` (a line from a town ahead of a unit, broken by the day step, or left flowing), `--spy3d x,y;…`, `--play-days N`, `--missives COL [--show-on-map]`, `--shot-on …`, `--shot-frame N`, `--also F:path`, `--council-click N@F`, `--council-hover N@F`, `--click-reply N@F`, `--title --title-click …`, the `--ledger*` and `--war*` flags (`scripts/ledger3d/CLAUDE.md`, `scripts/war_room/CLAUDE.md`). It drives everything by REAL input. A shot ignores the player's DEV MODE unless `--dev`. Never headless for a 3D shot (it hangs).
- **`tools/sim_invasion.gd`** (headless, engine calls only, no 3D) plays the prologue's invasion day by day and prints the hosts (mode, target, lead column in km), battles and rounds, the siege, the raids' take, the crown's treasury and contentment moves, and a summary with the state hash: `-- --map ID --seed N --defence none|weak|strong --days N [--walls] [--bal key=value]`; the defence sends units to the main host's chosen town on its crossing day, settling as arrivals (the seat, then the camp round it: the town's garrison) — weak: the units of the smallest pack regiment; strong: of the three largest. An unknown flag is a usage error (exit 2). Its `cost:` line times `EnemyAi.think_day` alone on a copy of each day (the clock is read in the tool, never the engine). The rules: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`.
- **Benches**: `tools/map3d/bench.gd` and `scenes/tools/war_room/bench.tscn` — see `scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md`'s performance gates.

## 2D art (ComfyUI)

- **Two models only**: Z-Image Turbo and Qwen-Image-2512 (`generate.py` `MODEL_PRESETS`, default `qwen2512fast`); no Flux model. `assets/flux/` keeps its name because every installer and table names it.
- Prompts live in `assets/flux/*_prompts.csv` and the library `docs/references/art_prompt_library.md`; `generate.py` renders, `post.py` cuts out, `install_map_art.py <target>` installs. **An installed file is sized by its USE** (design px × the largest window scale, ~3×), never one size for all; 1024 is only the generation size.
- Targets: `ui` (dimension icons I-40, I-47..I-61 and the manor stat icons I-62..I-72 → `assets/2D/icons/resources/`, colour kept), `buildings` (H- → `assets/2D/icons/buildings/bld_<code>.png`), `goods_icons` (G- → `assets/2D/icons/goods/`), `compass` (I-73), `paintings` (estate paintings B-01..B-36, one per industry × size, in the CHARACTERS' v2 style on the `[C-ESTATE]` canvas, cropped to the scene and boxed → `assets/2D/paintings/estates/`), `army_paintings` (B-37..B-42), `cast`, `arms`, `app_icon`. A key gets its texture in its `.tres` table once installed (a test holds every dimension has its art).
- **Portraits** come from the library's `K-` chapter, not `C-` (generic archetypes): one `K-` entry per character id in `data/founding_council.csv` / `data/roles.csv` with a `**外貌**` line (looks and clothing only; ages and traits taken from the CSVs). `assets/flux/cast_art.csv` maps id → `K-`; `install_map_art.py cast` fills `assets/2D/portraits/cast/<id>.png` (chip) and `full/<id>.png` (figure). Portraits keep their colour.
- **Arms are GENERATED from the pack's Azgaar `coa`**, never Azgaar's charge SVGs (WappenWiki art under CC BY-NC-SA): `assets/flux/arms_prompts.py [--pack <id>]` writes the prompts in plain heraldic words, `generate.py --prompts-file arms_prompts.csv --style-file STYLE_CORE_arms_matte.txt` renders the matte flat style, `install_map_art.py arms` (`ARMS_PACK=<id>`) cuts and fits them into `assets/2D/arms/<map_id>/<key>.png`. Generate only when `tools/arms_report.gd --map <id>` says the borrow pool ran short. Spec `2026-09-15-house-arms-design.md`.
- **The application icon** `icon.png` (X-01, its own matte emblem style, `app_icon_prompts.csv`, `APP_ICON_SEED` chosen to read at 16 px), installed full-bleed by `install_map_art.py app_icon`.
- **Table props** (the council seal U-60 and friends): `assets/flux/table_prop_prompts.csv`, `STYLE_CORE_table_prop.txt`, cut out by colour. The Missive board's styles are derived from installed art by `tools/missive_board_art.py` (nothing generated).
- **Tree crowns**: aerial photographs of trees on white (`assets/flux/tree_crown_prompts.csv`, `STYLE_CORE_aerial_tree.txt`), cut and packed by `build_tree_atlas.py`.
- **Event illustration prompt rules** (prologue pictures; five rounds, 2026-09-21). Read before writing or re-rolling an event picture's prompt:
  1. **画布拼在 prompt 末尾，抢得过场景词。** `STYLE_CORE` 在最前面说的"12–14 世纪"压不住场景自己的名词——写 `livery` 就出拿破仑制服，写 `ministers` 就出 19 世纪会议室，写 `travelling gown` 就出维多利亚裙。凡是要钉死的约束，写进 `[C-...]` 画布，不要指望风格核心。
  2. **画面里有纸，就一定会长出假文字。** 负面词里的 `printed typography, lettering` 挡不住账簿、请愿书、条约。唯一有效的办法是**让纸本身看不见字**：合上、折起、背过去、只露封蜡，或者以极斜的角度只看得见尺线；并且在 subject 里明写"看不见任何字"。
  3. **一个场景里有国王和别人打架，模型会把王冠戴错人。** 与其反复重摇，不如**把主角移出画面**——"从国王自己的位置看出去，画面里只有他伸出的手和袖子"，歧义就没了。
  附带：Flux 把 `corn` 画成玉米。中世纪欧洲的麦田一律写 `wheat`，并加 `no maize`。
  4. **手和脸糟，多半是构图问题，不是参数问题。** 同 seed 下 32 步对 20 步几乎没差别。一条 12:5 的宽带里七张脸、十四只手，4B 模型一定崩。画布里规定"最多三个清晰人物，其余小而虚"，并且**每只手都要有交代**——平放且手指并拢、交叠、握住一个点名的东西，或者出画。越肩构图（前景人物只有后脑和肩膀）是最省事的解法。
  5. **画布只禁止，不规定。** 为了压时代漂移往画布里写"锁子甲、头盔、鸢形盾"，结果每张图都生效：大臣成了士兵，织工成了卫兵。改成"没被点名为士兵的人不许穿甲"，需要盔甲的场景自己写。
  6. **人会被画两遍。** "站在他们身后的门口"经常变成"旁边一个、门口一个"。要么明写"画面里只有 N 个人"，要么把那个人挪进主群。

## 3D models

- **Asset layout**: every 3D asset the game uses is in `assets/3D/{category}/{object}/`, whatever produced it in that object's `model_wip/` (git-ignored but its `.gdignore`; a category-wide source in `assets/3D/{category}/model_wip/`); every 2D asset in `assets/2D/{category}/{set}/`, a set being what one installer fills. Fonts, audio, `assets/maps/` and `assets/flux/` stay outside. A model's `.glb` stays in `assets/` (the import is made from it), its scene in `scenes/models/`; the Terrain3D build writes its world scene to `scenes/map3d/worlds/<map>.tscn` and its data to `assets/3D/terrain/<map>/` (`test_scene_layout.gd`).
- **Tripo staging**: the owner drops every Tripo export into `assets/3D/model_wip/tripo_inbox/<category>/<object>/`, beside the `REF_*.png` it was generated from; processing moves it to the object's `model_wip/tripo/` (README in the inbox). Make an object's inbox folder, with its reference, when its reference is picked.

- **Houses** (14 types plus church / palace VARIANTS, `assets/3D/buildings/<type>/`): Tripo 3D generations from references rendered by `tools/map3d/gen_house_refs.py` (`assets/flux/house_ref_prompts.csv`), processed in the owner's Blender by `tools/map3d/tripo_houses/tripo_houses.py` (README there): a voxel shell collapsed to a triangle budget, roof and wall split into `hh_roof_` / `hh_slate_` / `hh_wall_<type>` surfaces on one baked atlas so the runtime tints apply, a stone footing, grime; the palaces HIGH DEFINITION (`build_hd`: Tripo's own UVs and normal map, roofs re-slated straight). Variants are rows of the same rank in `data/map3d_houses.csv`, picked per settlement by weight (`bake_houses._pick_landmark`). Every house `.glb.import` has `meshes/force_disable_compression=true` (quantising per surface makes the hole census read window rims as holes). A mesh may be a SHELL but never have HOLES (`census_house_meshes.py`, `test_map3d_houses_meshes.gd`). `scale_houses.py` rescales a built mesh and measures it back into the table. Simple realism, never toy: a generator asked for a "game asset" returns a toy castle; model against a photoreal reference.
- **Walls, piers and harbour ships** (`wall`, `wall_tower`, `wall_gate`, `pier`, `pier_cliff`, `pier_stair` in `assets/3D/buildings/`; `fishing_boat`, `cog`, `longship`, `carrack` in `assets/3D/ships/`): Tripo generations processed by `tools/map3d/tripo_harbour/tripo_harbour.py` (README there) on the houses' pipeline — fitted, a wall module cut to its length with flat ends, untinted stone under slate, a pier's deck and a ship's waterline at z = 0; an open-sheet source (the longship, the carrack, the cliff harbour's rock) is welded and filled like the palaces, never voxel-shelled. `make_scenes.gd` there (`-- --only <type>`, never headless) writes each type's inherited scene and its `house_models.tres` entry (an `-s` run drops the table's UIDs: keep them). Rows of `data/map3d_houses.csv` (roles `wall`, `pier`, `ship`), laid by `bake_houses.fortify` / `harbour`.
- **Regiments** (`assets/3D/units/<arm>/`, `army_models.tres`): the infantry (`tools/units3d/infantry_tripo/`) and the cavalry (`tools/units3d/cavalry_tripo/`) are Tripo generations fitted onto a rig in the owner's Blender (each plate rigid on its bone, one 2048 atlas each); the cavalry's rig and clips are `tools/units3d/cavalry_rig/`'s (the blender-auto-rig / natural-animation phases on that mesh, installed by its `install.py`: clip prefix stripped, head bones renamed, turned to face −Z); the royal piece (the king on horseback) is the owner's own rigged export, installed by `tools/units3d/royal_install/` (turned to face −Z, head bones renamed); the archers are a Tripo generation auto-rigged by Tripo, the v1 model's bow and quiver put on and the clips ported by `tools/units3d/archer_tripo/` (README there; two surfaces), installed by `archer_install/`; the courier (`assets/3D/units/courier/`, `courier_models.tres`) is rigged and animated by `tools/units3d/courier_rig/` (GALLOP / IDLE, README there) on `courier_build.py`'s model; clips MARCH / STANDBY / GARRISON (+ ATTACK, DEFEND), the looping ones set to LOOP in the `.import` (the glTF importer loops nothing by itself). `data/army_art.csv` holds `size`, `stand_deg`, `stride_m`, `rank_depth`. Parts models for the folio's Take apart are separate static exports (`<arm>_parts.glb`).
- **The artist's kit** (`docs/art_team/`, README there): locked ComfyUI workflows built from `generate.py`'s presets and the style files, and the `cc_art_check` Blender add-on, whose export writes the node and image names the model scenes need (`tools/art_kit/blender/cc_art_check/specs.json`, from the shipped GLBs by `extract_specs.py` / `glb_names.py`), so a delivery overwrites the `.glb` as it is. `tools/art_kit/build_kit.py` rebuilds it; rerun it (and `extract_specs.py` after a skeleton, house row or shipped model changes).
- **The WAR ROOM's art**: `tools/war_room/blender/` and `assets/flux/war_room_ref_prompts.csv`; textures kept as Basis Universal in the import. Its wooden pieces are Tripo generations (references `assets/flux/war_piece_prompts.csv`) processed by `wr_pieces.py` there (welded before the collapse: glTF splits seams, and collapsed apart they crack open) and dressed by `tools/war_room/make_piece_scenes.gd` (not headless).
- `tools/migrate_asset_layout.py --apply` moves an old checkout's ignored files into the asset layout.
