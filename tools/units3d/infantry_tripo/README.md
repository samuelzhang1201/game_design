## Steps

| step | what it does |
| --- | --- |
| `load` | both exports into the scene `Tripo_Infantry`, in metres (the helmet's crown at 1.85 m); play-every-frame and muted (Blender 5.2 crashed syncing playback to CoreAudio) |
| `reduce` | each part collapsed by one ratio to `BUDGET` (the spear left whole: collapsed, its shaft pinched into a spike) |
| `rig` | the blueprint's 29 bones fitted to this man (`HIP`..`ARM`, the feet along the sabatons' own length), plus `shield` under the left forearm |
| `skin` | each part weighted by `PART_BONES` — one bone rigid, several by distance — then joined into `infantry`; `inf_spear` kept apart for the actions |
| `animate` | MARCH (`march.py`, `STANCE` 0.4), STANDBY (`garrison.py`, `CLIP`), GARRISON (`guard.py`) |
| `texture` | a coverage bake, then colour / roughness-metallic EMITTED and normals baked from the textured export, AO multiplied in |
| `tone_steel` | the grey, bright pixels (steel) toned to `STEEL_TARGET`, warm, no glossier than `STEEL_ROUGH_MIN` |
| `lengthen_spear` | the shaft above the fist stretched by `SPEAR_LONGER` (by part: 23 the head, 45 the shaft) |
| `export` | `infantry_blueprint/export.py` with `CLIPS`: +Y forward, one glTF animation per clip, the MARCH stride printed |

## GARRISON (`guard.py`)

The owner's rulings of 2026-09-29/30, in order: the shield upright before the chest, the right
hand raised, the spear over the shield pointing ahead, the body leaning (重新设计驻防动作…); after
a reference picture, the middle hoplite (驻防动作如上图中间那个人); the spear level with the eyes
(和士兵的视线方向平齐); and finally the left leg bent forward, the right back, the stance open,
the body low, the shield up to the chin, a reasonable overhand arm (左腿弯曲向前，右腿在后…).
Every number is `P` at the top of the file; `GUARD={...}` passed in the exec globals overrides it.

## Traps it paid for

* **A shield fixed to the forearm wrenches the arm.** Tripo hangs the shield along a lowered
  forearm; standing it before the chest swung the upper arm straight out through it. It rides
  its own bone, and the arm reaches in behind it by IK.
* **A fist aligned to the shaft is wrung ~90°.** The fist may turn freely about the shaft: the
  turn that leaves the wrist straightest is searched, and what twist remains goes to the forearm
  and part to the upper arm.
* **Plates shared between two bones by distance tear** when a joint bends far (pauldrons,
  armpit gussets, couters): they ride ONE bone whole.
* **A variable named `Rh` in the fist search overwrote the hips' `Rh`** and swapped the thighs
  (脚谁让你交叉的): keep the names apart.
* **Each foot must turn by its OWN rest direction** — `march.py` turned both by the left one's;
  with Tripo's feet splayed 19° and 26° that walked him out-toed (外八字).
* **A sentinel fill colour cannot mark the unbaked pixels**: the bake blends into what is
  there at the islands' edges and bled magenta into the steel; a white-emission coverage bake
  onto black marks them instead.
* **Lengthening the spear by HEIGHT is not idempotent** (the stretched shaft rises past the
  head's old foot): it goes by Tripo part.
