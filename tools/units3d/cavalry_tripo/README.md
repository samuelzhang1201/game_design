# The Tripo knight (the game's cavalry piece)

> **Since 2026-10-06 the shipped rig and clips are `tools/units3d/cavalry_rig/`'s** (owner: 请使用 royal_rig_work
> 类似的方法，重新设计 cavalry 骑兵的动作): it takes THIS pipeline's game mesh (`cavalry_tripo_work.blend`'s
> `cavalry`, its lance, shield and atlas) as it is and gives it a measured 53-bone rig and five new clips.
> This folder still builds the mesh; `animate.py`'s clips are superseded.

`assets/3D/units/cavalry/cavalry.glb` since 2026-09-30 (owner: 骑兵改用 tripo 3d 建模，使用之前步兵
同样的方法). Generated by Tripo 3D on the owner's **paid** plan — only paid-plan outputs are ours
(Tripo's terms §5.2.2) — from `assets/3D/units/cavalry/model_wip/tripo_kit/zimage_threequarter_s1.png`
(Z-Image, our own render of the in-game design WITHOUT the shield, `gen_refs.py`), and processed
**in the owner's open Blender** through the Blender MCP, never headless:

```python
P = ".../tools/units3d/cavalry_tripo/tripo.py"; T = {"__file__": P}; exec(open(P).read(), T)
T["scene"](); T["load"](); T["fit"](); T["frame"]()
T["cull"](); T["lance"](); T["reduce"](); T["shield"]()      # in this order, each ONCE
T["skeleton"](); T["texture"](); T["weigh"](); T["join"]()
AP = ".../tools/units3d/cavalry_tripo/animate.py"; A = {"__file__": AP, "T": T}; exec(open(AP).read(), A)
A["animate"](); A["export"](".../assets/3D/units")
```

then Godot's import (`Godot --headless --path . --import`, and `git checkout project.godot`).
`cavalry.glb.import` carries `meshes/force_disable_compression=true` and MARCH / STANDBY /
GARRISON set to loop in `_subresources` (ATTACK is one shot).

## Steps

| step | what it does |
| --- | --- |
| `load` | both exports into the scene `Tripo_Cavalry`, parents baked into the meshes |
| `fit` | the parts are a second generation of the shape (19 mm mean off the textured mesh): ICP, to 6 mm |
| `frame` | nose to +Y, the horse 2.45 m nose to tail (the scanned cavalry's and the courier's), hooves on z = 0 |
| `cull` | faces the fused mesh does not have (inside another part) dropped |
| `lance` | Tripo's three lance parts replaced by ONE straight lance through the off fist, tip at 3.319 m (the old piece's height: the runtime sizes a piece by its box), butt by the off stirrup, with the vamplate Tripo dropped |
| `reduce` | every part collapsed by one ratio to `BUDGET` 18,400 |
| `shield` | the hand-built heater on the rider's BACK (owner: 盾牌要背在身后, 盾牌单独做, 盾牌不要那根带子), 0.50 x 0.66 m, green with a white pale, steel rim and boss |
| `skeleton` | the scanned cavalry's 43 bone NAMES placed on this horse and rider; `shield` on the chest, `lance` on the off hand |
| `texture` | one 2048 atlas: far pass then near pass from the textured mesh; shield and lance from their own `paint` / `pbr` corners; metallic x 0.25; the RIDER's steel toned like the infantry's |
| `weigh` | horse: bone heat on its own welded parts; rider: each plate rigid on its nearest bone; reins bit to near fist; tack, shield, lance rigid |
| `join` | one mesh `cavalry`, 4 weights a vertex, on the rig |
| `animate` | MARCH, ATTACK (model_cavalry's numbers), STANDBY (new), GARRISON (the shield taken to the arm) |
| `export` | `cavalry.glb` (+Y forward, one animation per NLA track), `units3d.json`, the MARCH stride |

## The clips

* **MARCH** (48 frames at 30 fps, a loop) and **ATTACK** (75, one shot): `model_cavalry`'s,
  number for number, without its shield (it rides the back). ATTACK's lance turns are
  `LANCE_REST` 12° less: this lance leans 9.5° forward at rest and the old numbers drove
  its tip into the ground.
* **STANDBY** (120, a loop): the horse at rest shifting its weight, a swish, a nod; the rider
  breathing and looking about; lance upright, shield on the back.
* **GARRISON** (120, a loop): ON GUARD (owner 2026-09-27) with the shield on the ARM (owner
  2026-09-30: 用第一种): the lance couched, the `shield` bone keyed in pose space onto
  `GUARD_CENTRE` / `GUARD_OUT` before his near side (turned with his chest), the near arm
  reaching it by IK, the neck swaying. The move from the back to the arm is the runtime's
  STANDBY/MARCH <-> GARRISON blend.
* Keyed from frame 0 (the courier's lesson: from 1 the clip holds its first pose a frame).

## Traps it paid for

* **Bone heat on the fused textured mesh left 100% bare**: the rider, the old lance and the
  horse run through each other in it. The heat is solved on the horse's own parts welded
  together (8k tris), which the courier did not need.
* **Tripo's lance was three parts that were never coaxial**: the lower shaft ran behind the
  fist, and the seams hid behind the fist and the knee. Sliding it up opened both seams; it is
  rebuilt by hand.
* **The dappled grey coat is as grey and as bright as steel**: the steel toning runs only
  inside the rider's islands (a white-emission mask bake).
* **A float colour attribute is LINEAR**: the shield's sRGB paint written straight in baked
  washed-out; `lin()` converts it.
* **A hidden object is not exported**: the rig hidden in the viewport went out as a bare mesh
  with no skeleton and no clips; `export()` unhides both and checks the selection.
* **The glTF importer does not loop a clip** unless the `.import` says so: the scanned
  cavalry's and the infantry's clips have `loop_mode` 0.
