# P5.2 — Ransom, exchange, release and civilian identity

The approved P5.2 contract is implemented on the battle-system branch. P5.3 presentation remains separate.

## Delivered behavior

- Canonical proposals freeze exact selected custody, wounds, named people, negotiated price, parties and noncombat terms. Proposals do not pay or release. Required realm responses are explicit and rejection is terminal.
- Fulfillment rechecks every batch, received law, party and treasury, then publishes payment, bilateral release, personnel reservation, promises and family effects together. Exact replay returns the saved receipt before mutation; conflicting requests fail.
- Ransom uses the original realm treasury, apportions gross across sorted current holders by selected personnel, then floors each holder's received captor share. The remainder goes to that holder's crown. Actual captor credit and current holder remain distinct in civilian receipts; fiscal splitting does not transfer custody.
- A data-backed price reference uses noble identity, named wealth or the unnamed wealth assumption and current payer capacity. This informs negotiation; the agreed whole-gold price is frozen separately. Prices, gratitude and the initial share remain provisional tuning.
- All released personnel become civilians once. Original province/field resident totals do not increase, including after conquest. Remaining custody and captured_current decrease together; source released increases. Named character/family persists, command is cleared, and a new paid recruitment is required for service.
- Noncombat terms restrict both anonymous source availability and named recruitment/appointment through the shared eligibility gate. Zero deadline means indefinite; positive deadlines are inclusive. Legacy reorganization apportions promise slices only among released people and does not bind a newly extracted serving officer to unrelated civilian pledges.
- Partial release keeps original initial_count while reducing live identity/wound counts. released_count preserves active-battle conservation, and completed battle snapshots remain immutable. Capture-time ransom protection survives battlefield removal and later received policy changes.
- Player propose/respond/settle commands derive actor authority from the player realm and atomically charge their clerk fee once. Reserved battlefield rewards and prospective ransom income cannot fund an unaffordable command.

## Scope decisions

The existing character wealth metric is bounded and is not a gold purse. P5.2 therefore adds the saved whole-gold unit_prize_gold account for actual capturing units, alongside existing crown/foreign WorldLedger treasuries. No family currency conversion or general banking system was added.

Family gratitude toward the player's releasing realm uses the existing ruler opinion. Gratitude toward a foreign releasing realm uses the relationship edge to its actual ruler, with the same authored reason. This avoids crediting the player for an enemy's release.
