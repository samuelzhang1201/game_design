# P4.3 — authored battle events and informed command views

Scope: P4.3 on `codex/battle-system`, based on `82591b34`. No P5 implementation, placeholder art or live API event prose. PR: https://github.com/samuelzhang1201/game_design/pull/76.

## Delivered behavior

Six authored EV-31–EV-36 scenes and choices live in `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`, with indexed `battle_events.csv` / `battle_event_choices.csv`. Unique table stems avoid collisions with the monthly story tables; the existing `StorySource` parser already reads their scenes. Five field events have real triggers and effects. Tunnel shoring/materials, rerouting/progress, declared collapse risk and stop choices are authored; P6.2 owns their engineering trigger and execution.

Offers use the saved engine RNG, journal one result per battle/day and survive retry/save/load. Authority freezes at offer: the king decides locally through the saved day hold; otherwise the actual affected commander chooses among lawful affordable options using known conditions, personality and skills. Revalidation preserves the same event when money/law changes; a vanished/captured target exposes only a zero-cost report cancellation. Selecting never restarts the clock.

Reinforcements are actual joining cohorts; held reserves still defend, eat and take losses, but do not attack. Withdrawal creates a cache from its existing food debit; lawful collection transfers only real available food within carry capacity, and destruction respects the authored cap. Wounded delays respect packing deadlines. Routed sides cannot resume attack through an event.

Remote reports are immutable per-battle payloads delivered by actual couriers. Only arrival publishes their facts and event receipts; local observation files immediately. Delivered `king_report` letters share the battle retention cap, preserving spy/general letters. `BattlePresentation` copies own permitted facts, received estimates/reports, effects, costs, delivery/cooldown and law refusals; hidden enemy mutations cannot alter the projection.

The editor-authored leather command/event panel uses the six-image ArtTable and all authored choice slots. Consequences and disabled-choice reasons remain readable in body type. Map clips and physical flags/heralds use a separate informed snapshot, omit hidden/ghost units, retain real marching during withdrawal, plant each offset signal on its own terrain and tolerate an unbound/missing world.
