# P4.2 — paid orders and daily battles

Scope: the existing battle-system plan's P4.2, on `codex/battle-system` from `f0ea2e0b`. P4.3 events/presentation and P5's prisoner/loot lifecycle remain separate units. No look resources, placeholder models or unrelated art-pipeline changes belong to this delivery.

## Behavior

`BATTLE_ORDER` uses stable warrants saved beyond the battlefield. Attack/hold/retreat/surrender are mutually exclusive; the last lawful actual arrival wins. Inspiration supports the existing action, reserves a per-person award, pays survivors once, and observes cooldown, royal presence and temporary resistance. Ordinary voluntary spending sees available gold; involuntary losses can consume a promise, so execution revalidates funding. Local surrender has no invented filing cost; accepting it transfers every fit/wounded survivor and saves the real captor, original personnel/injuries, commander and equipment for P5. Captured units cannot receive ordinary commands or march.

`BattleStrength` constructs identity/equipment once and truncates each fixed category: training, morale, discipline, supply, formation/terrain, action. Same-category deviations merge before the CSV clamp. Duplicate sources fail, and every source has a reason. The old full ArmySystem quality chain is not multiplied again.

Contact and acknowledgement do not order attack. Holding consumes ordinary food and receives couriers/reinforcements without combat rounds or draws. Actual advances and visible-opposition doctrine can initiate a legal attack. Each engaged day retains one saved engine RNG draw plus the pending outcome; retry/error/load repeats neither the draw nor applied costs/personnel. Attack consumes extra carried supplies and exposes defence; insufficient means hold with a reason. The daily side's current action selects attack/defence, irrespective of which side first made contact.

There is no five-round termination or withdrawal immunity. Morale collapse starts a real withdrawal; routed armies keep the same battle until actual separation, preventing repeated first encounters at unchanged positions. A retreat uses reachable safe ground and the existing camp deadline. No route leaves it exposed; clearance blockage retries a route without repeating withdrawal costs. New lawful hold/attack stops an ordinary withdrawal route. Pursuit obeys frozen terms; protected withdrawal suppresses its loss. Actual departures retain their cause for reports and exact-once raid spoils.

## Independent review and rulings

A fresh Sol reviewer found four substantive bugs: premature rout endings, surrendered units still commandable, sticky retreat routes and holding bypassing morale collapse. All four were reproduced by failing tests in `review-red2.log` and fixed (`review-green3.log`:25/25). Additional failed-then-passed tests cover all-wounded surrender and blocked-retreat re-aiming. No findings were deferred.

Rulings carried from architecture: factors combine by additive deviation inside a category (the P4.2 contract), superseding separate P4.1 per-source multiplication. Bounty scales to issued living beneficiaries and surviving delivery counts; later arrivals do not enlarge the promise. Rout remains in the same battle through physical departure. Terminal departure events feed both reports and spoils. Legacy tests asserting hashes, forced draws or immunity were replaced with saved-stream/real-distance checks; construction and food-horizon fixtures isolate their own mechanics from battle casualties. Risk if these rulings are wrong is balance/wording revision, not a changed save format contract.
