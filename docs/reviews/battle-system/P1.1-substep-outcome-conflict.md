# P1.1 battle timing decision — 2026-10-17

## Decision

The user chose immediate combat within each fixed tactical movement substep. A substep marches, reconciles ground/law, advances host arrivals, discovers contacts and pauses for each new encounter before losses. Once acknowledged, each not-yet-fought battle resolves one round in stable ID order before the saved substep cursor advances. Later substeps can add reinforcements to a continuing battle; they do not receive another round until the next day. Ended battles are removed before later contact discovery.

## Acceptance mapping

The old strong-defense campaign's exact no-capture/withdrawal and casualty ceiling were tied to the delayed contact boundary, so the revised campaign check compares outcomes under the approved scheduler instead of preserving an exact trace. The current strong walled garrison holds through the assault window and the besieger later retreats under supply rules, while weak defense loses its town. Capture-time HOLD and post-capture combat remain checked in the weak-garrison capture case; the existing siege tests cover later retreat behavior. Other acceptance includes casualty conservation and roster bounds, at most one round per battle/day, same-day later-substep reinforcement joining after an earlier round and appearing in the next day's participant list, and deterministic JSON continuation. Dates and counts observed in seeded runs are diagnostics, not balancing targets.
