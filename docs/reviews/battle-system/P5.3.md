# P5.3 — Delivered battle records and prisoner cards

P5.3 adds the host's prisoner and battle-report windows, an original-character read-only dossier, and production prisoner negotiation/fulfillment delivery. Wounded people and captives remain personnel, food and carrying accounts; they have no new models, extra squads or escort units.

## Behavior

- The terminal battle record freezes participants, unique reinforcements, own personnel identities, commanders, formation/orders/events, captured equipment, supply readings, custody and outcome. Departure freezes supply readings; rejoining replaces the exit reading. Later releases and spoils are separate receipts and never rewrite historical totals.
- Local observation and remote physical report arrival use the same permitted payload. Own figures are exact when known; enemy losses are estimates. Army cards, host bars and the command panel use received readings or explicitly unknown values rather than remote live casualties or invented zeroes.
- Custody distinguishes historical captures from reported current holding, names the actual observed holder and original characters, and labels stale observations. Defeating a holder in a later battle reports transferred/rescued batches from older battles while retaining their original capture reference.
- Prisoner proposals freeze selections, names, wounds, price, parties, promises and reason. Foreign capitals decide only on offer delivery. The approved policy accepts lawful ransoms within the frozen reference and designated payer's available funds, and lawful equal-count reciprocal exchanges. A missing recipient or undelivered offer is a communication failure, never a fabricated foreign rejection.
- The reply follows the moving king; only its arrival records visible foreign consent. Fulfillment instructions reach every frozen holder before one atomic transaction pays and releases both sides. A later return receipt exposes remote results. Local instructions retain immediate execution. Each new rider uses existing ordinary postage; command fees and exact retries are charged once. Issuance costs remain spent if later conditions invalidate execution.
- Expired replies show an unconfirmed outcome and a new-agreement recovery message without inventing consent or exposing the undisclosed result. Custody reconciliation, proposal display and historical-report settlements all use received projections.
- Released people are civilians. Named people preserve original Character identity/family and lose their military assignment. Noncombat promises remain inclusive through their deadline; another paid recruitment is required for service.

## Independent review

The read-only review found and reproduced host-bar disclosure, unknown figures shown as zero, missing later settlement association, old-capture custody changes omitted by later defeats, and departed units' unrelated later supplies. Regression fixes cover each. The architecture review additionally found fabricated rejection on undelivered offers, indefinite misleading waiting after expired replies, and checking a non-paying holder's treasury. Its final bounded recheck found those three addressed.
