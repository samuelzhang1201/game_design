## Sole Review acceptance

Review: `01a1597e-721f-7190-943b-21072477bc4e`, the sole independent critic, 21 October 2026. I read taste and inspected all seven raw shots, both contact sheets and all three enlarged crops before reading the implementation observations. The reference is the existing daylight war table with dark leather audience windows; no new visual design is being selected.

- **Hierarchy:** the encounter title leads, then troop facts; Continue is larger than Review deployment. EV-31's bright illustration leads before its story and choices. In the poor condition Contract the line is the only bright choice, correctly directing the next action.
- **Value:** cream body text separates clearly from dark leather in both windows. The bounty/reserve refusal reasons retain the size and brightness of their effect text, satisfying the warning principle; disabled command labels are dimmer but their reasons stay readable.
- **Colour:** gold, brown, cream and muted field colours share the existing family. Neither refusal adds a dashboard warning colour.
- **Medium:** leather, paper and the sober battlefield illustration fit the table's materials. The illustration is visibly a framed picture inside a window; this pass does not accept the surrounding map's rendering or art.
- **Edges:** neither panel loses its outer controls at either size. Scrolled troop headings are cut by their viewport as expected, while all seven clauses become visible. The event's nested border and corner ornament make its edge heavier than the encounter's, a remaining “material over ornament” concern.
- **Scale:** 1920×1200 ranks first for comfortable reading; 1280×720 ranks second because its letters and commands are much smaller, though all captured clauses, effects and refusal reasons remain legible without truncation. These two sizes do not establish accessibility or every window size.
- **Sharpness:** native raw images and enlarged crops show defined letter edges; the compact view has fewer pixels per letter. Contact-sheet reduction and nearest-neighbour enlargement are not evidence of a new font/art blur.
- **Noise:** encounter dividers group facts and event buttons group commands. The event's nested decorative frame adds no decision information. The repeated scroll instruction remains after reaching the last clause; it is harmless but no longer informative there.

Condition ranking: poor EV-31 is the stronger constraint case because both refusal reasons and the surviving action fit together; rich EV-31 independently confirms the enabled bounty appearance. Neither condition is a preferred cosmetic variant. For terms, the top view establishes troop context and the scrolled view establishes clause completeness; both are needed. The same complete scrolled terms and poor-event facts survive the compact size. The compact rich condition was not captured.
