## Delivered contracts

- `PrisonerSystem.capture` validates all sources/receivers/named assignments, calls the roster transition once, and publishes source/holder batches only after success. Stable sorted largest-remainder allocation retains actual named captors; counts and named/wounded subsets do not duplicate people. Saved exact replay returns the original result; conflicting reuse fails.
- Surrender commits its personnel and previous custody on a candidate state. All fit and wounded surrender, the receiving opposing battlefield unit owns them, and king departure remains independent of military capture and the camp gate. No surrender filing fee.
- Defeated holders are accounted before removal: rescued people become civilians; remaining enemies transfer to an explicit field receiver. With no surviving controller, all held people are explicitly released. Day-entry reconciliation also handles desertion removing the last fit guard outside battle. Command assignment is cleared by the existing personnel writer; promises remain on the original records.
- Food uses current own living personnel plus held commoner/noble weights with one final rounding. Wounded captives remain a subset. Guards reduce combat availability without moving ledger bodies. A shared day-end food phase prevents same-day double feeding; AI still anticipates its meal for retreat checks.
- Source cohorts enroll once per battlefield; departure freezes the visit, rejoining excludes losses elsewhere while allowing live recovery. Identity subtotals conserve 600/700 people. Battle ending/call-off stores a private immutable snapshot; existing information-delivery gates remain in place.
- Exact legacy surrender receipts rebuild missing custody without recapture. Ambiguous partial surviving custody explicitly errors. Pending old organization waits while source/holder custody is live so splitting cannot orphan source IDs.
