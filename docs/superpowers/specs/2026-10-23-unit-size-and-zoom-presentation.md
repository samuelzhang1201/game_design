## Owner requirements

- A physical/display group represents at most **100 fit people**, with a positive remainder forming another group. Wounded people and captives generate no soldier models.
- Every actual military unit has at most **1000 living people = fit + wounded**. Captives are additional and excluded. Recovery changes fit/model counts, not living strength.
- Oversized units split recursively into halves, larger half first: 1001 → 501 + 500; 2500 → 1250 + 1250 → 625 + 625 + 625 + 625. Preserve smaller existing units; no consolidation.
- Near view has `ceil(fit / 100)` soldier representatives, at most ten per unit, in and out of battle. Mid/far view has **one simple military symbol per actual unit**, not per group.
- Symbol mode simultaneously displays an upper-right legend. Near mode hides it.
- Symbol inspiration is the owner's [NATO joint military symbology reference](https://zh.wikipedia.org/zh-cn/北約聯合軍事符號), read on 2026-10-23. Borrow the separation of arm and relationship meaning; do not claim standards compliance, copy downloaded artwork, assume its licensing, or import modern markings wholesale.

The new 100 rule replaces the older 50 rule for this feature. Mid/far symbols replace models-at-every-zoom. Neither change retroactively passes a failed image or authorizes changes to another active PR.

## Current implementation and reach

`ArmySplit.shares` makes ceil-based balanced equipment shares; `element_shares` converts the cap using whole crews. `data/army/balance.csv:army_split_cap_men` is 600. `_make` pools guns into one royal train and leaves fleets intact. `GameState.new_campaign` obtains that roster, then `MilitaryBootstrap.initialize` imports personnel and unique commanders. Do not turn equipment elements into people or re-import historical civilians to meet the new rule.

`MilitaryRoster.living/fit` count only active cohorts. Assembling cohorts already consume civilian population but are not active; the organization split budget and recruitment headroom include their assigned fit+wounded people until activation. Thus v2 partition targets count active plus assembling living, while battle strength and soldier models count active fit only; the owner examples have no pending assembly. `RecruitmentSystem.quote/recruit/step_day/_complete` owns new, refill and reinforcement projects, equipment, appointments and fees. `MilitaryRoster.mobilize/activate/transition/transfer_units` are ledger boundaries. Recovery, hazards, foraging, desertion and battle casualties use `transfer_units`; recovery itself cannot increase fit+wounded. Current captive dispositions remain captured_current → released civilians; they do not return people to military service. Re-enlistment is a new paid mobilization subject to the same cap reservation check; do not add captive-to-fit/wounded transitions.

`MilitaryBootstrap.migrate` imports older saves. `MilitaryReorganization.reconcile` copies a candidate and publishes a fixed field list, guarded by `organization_pending`; it currently skips active battles and custody, uses equipment capacity, adds `:organization:v1:` children, and clones some live dictionaries. `Game.load_campaign` calls bootstrap then reconcile before publishing the loaded state; `TickEngine.resolve_day` reconciles before `DayResolution.begin`. `SaveLoad` writes one whole JSON file. These are the integration boundaries; a new save version alone does not migrate an already-complete organization.

`BattleGroups` owns saved physical groups and contact; `battle_group_size` is 50. N0/PR163 owns position/slot continuity, translation and round compatibility. Merged G1's `Map3DBattleGroups.sample/place` consumes admitted saved battle rows; nonbattle figures still use `Map3DFormation.tier_of` (nine/six/three), and ghosts use last-known presentation. `Map3DLayerUnits` supplies per-unit hit boxes/anchors; `Map3DHudArmies` currently draws counters only for units without pieces. `Map3DContext.regiments/ghosts` is the sight boundary. `Map3DPicker` and `Map3DSelection` must keep unit IDs. WAR ROOM keeps one piece per actual known unit.

## Design calls: deterministic organization

The following are engineering decisions, not additional owner rulings.

1. **One pure partition rule.** Add `ArmySplit.halves(living: int, cap: int) -> Array[int]`. Depth-first, larger half first, stop at cap; zero gives `[]`; invalid cap is refused by callers before mutation. Do not change the meaning of existing `shares`, whose legacy tests/import reconstruction remain useful. New organization uses living targets, not equipment capacities. Keep the configured living cap in data at 1000 from P1; P2 applies it to opening and paid entries. Opening bootstrap partitions its source-derived personnel plan before calling capped mobilization, so no published or ordinary ledger entry needs an oversized-import exemption.
2. **All arms obey it.** Artillery and naval crews have no living-cap exemption. Whole equipment elements are separately apportioned across children; equipment capacity can exceed or fall below living strength. An undersupplied crew detachment retains an explicit validated `organization_arm`, naval flag and original provenance even with zero equipment. It receives no invented vessel/gun, no equipment-derived combat power and no fabricated commander. A ship/gun is never duplicated to make exact personnel halves. New recruitment still requires whole equipment crews and may refuse a contract whose minimum crew exceeds 1000; importing existing larger crews must preserve people through detachments. Naval children start at the saved legal water point, not on a land camp; no remote berth teleport.
3. **Stable IDs.** The first leaf retains the original unit code, town seat, royal flag, commander and singular external targets. Other leaves use `<source>:living:v2:<binary-path>` (left `0`, right `1`; e.g. 2500 gives retained source, `:01`, `:10`, `:11`). Reserve/check every generated ID against both roster tables before any write; collision refuses the entire candidate, never chooses a random suffix. Save ordered `living_unit_mapping`, `living_cohort_mapping`, `living_cap_version=2` in `military_migration`; preserve older organization mappings/receipts. No aliases point back from retained source to itself; mappings describe descendants, not recursive target redirection.
4. **Exact living targets.** Reserve the indivisible incumbent commander in leaf zero. Allocate remaining named living people in sorted cohort-ID order into the first leaf with room, then anonymous fit and wounded against remaining integer living targets in that order. Carry stage, source province/population field, family, identity, origin reason and promises. Named identities stay unique; no automatic promotion or extra noble/commander recruitment. Vacant children stay explicitly vacant under existing departure/command eligibility rules.
5. **History and custody stay anchored.** For anonymous cohorts, keep each original cohort ID, captured_current/dead/released history and civilian promises on the original-ID leaf; fragment only living counts into deterministic new cohort IDs for other leaves. A named cohort stays indivisible with its original cohort ID and person_id on its allocated living leaf; nonliving named cohorts stay on the original-ID leaf. Never retain a second empty named cohort or duplicate person_id. Recompute each fragment's initial as its five status totals; the sum of fragments' initial and each status equals the original. Captured named cohorts remain whole. This retains `prisoner_batches.source_cohort`, holder/captor codes, proposal selections, original event receipts and historical battle reports without relabelling history or duplicating custody. A holder remains leaf zero. Do not reuse the current `_count_targets` history apportionment for this migration.
6. **Quantities and intent.** Apportion whole equipment/provenance parts, supplies, spoils, inventories, prize gold and arithmetic remainder accounts once using ordered living-leaf weights; largest remainder ties go to earlier leaf. Partition source_parts by actual equipment, preserving origin. Child zero retains standing army/battle tasks, delivery receipts, scout/sighting identity, king following target and individual command targets. Other leaves start with no standing task, courier queue or receipt; they retain side, received stance/formation and hold their allocated position. Hosts/war-plan membership lists expand once; town seats and singular targets do not fan out. Received realm/unit law scopes inherit without paying/re-delivering. Historical reports, requests, quotes and result receipts stay immutable. Paid assembling cohort/equipment allocation records remap once; completion reuses the original request ID and total charge.
7. **No information gain.** Existing sightings/ghosts remain historical snapshots of the original unit. Do not reveal children by expanding old observations. New enemy children appear only through ordinary subsequent sight admission. Own-unit selections keep child zero; no automatic selection of newly created children.

## Save and transaction contract

Use a candidate `GameState.from_dict(state.to_dict(), false)`; validate all IDs, origins, cohorts, allocations, legal placements and references before publishing. Add every changed field to the existing candidate publication path; avoid a second partial-save format. A failed plan leaves `SaveLoad.state_hash`, treasury, population, RNG, journals and all receipts unchanged. A second run or load of an already applied v2 migration is byte-equivalent under canonical JSON.

At load, import personnel first, then mark v2 work from the live ledger even when organization v1 was complete. Do not rebuild the saved roster from today's pack. A nonempty `day_resolution` or any nonempty `pending_round` defers organization **without editing that transaction**. Resume its original frozen inputs and receipts exactly once. Reconcile at the first boundary after the transaction clears and before the next day/round begins; if a paused event keeps it open, pending migration is explicit, recruitment is blocked, and existing temporarily oversized legacy units remain available to finish that transaction. The UI does not claim the cap is already enforced there. No permanent battle/custody exemption remains in the completed feature.

Idle migration and battle migration are verification cases in one complete saved-migration PR. Idle units can migrate within the private candidate while unrelated frozen battle units remain pending; migration version 2 becomes complete only when all eligible units are checked and no oversized unit is deferred. Active-battle migration runs between transactions, not only after combat ends. Retain battle IDs and aliases; replace the side's parent membership by ordered leaves once. Child zero retains the received task and charge state; new children start held, not charging. Existing opponent singular targets retain child zero; no additional attacks or costs are replayed.

Saved groups are partitioned in saved slot order by new child fit targets, retaining survivor metre positions and facings; a source group crossing a child boundary may supply two descendant rows at that same saved point with distinct child-local identities. Do not move either to a new deployment line. Subsequent legitimate movement separates them. A zero-fit wounded-only child has no group/model but remains a selectable unit symbol in symbol mode. Per-unit contact/strength is recomputed from allocated counts, not duplicated parent aggregates. Retain historical round/report snapshots and invalidate only derived future caches. Save immediately before/after this boundary, resume, and compare against uninterrupted execution.

Group-size migration is a distinct, explicit v2 regrouping event after N0: consume fit counts in saved slot order into groups up to 100; retain the first surviving slot/point in each consumed run, retire other slots without reuse, append only when fit growth requires it. This intentional reduction from 50-person groups is not an ordinary refresh, camera move, repeated load or anchor-follow. Preserve formation, facing, exact fit total and causal migration reason. Pending old rounds finish with their frozen 50-person inputs before regrouping. Postmigration refresh/translation resumes N0 continuity.

## Design calls: presentation

- **Near land units:** use admitted engine rows in battle; use `BattleGroups.counts(fit, configured_size)` plus existing `BattleFormation.slots` for ordinary units. Figures are representations of fit people, not equipment counts; no model per wound, prisoner, gun or ship. Fleets have no existing soldier model: near keeps one existing fleet counter per actual naval unit, with no fabricated sailor asset; the zero-to-ten soldier bound still holds (zero naval soldier models). This explicit naval presentation exception does not exempt naval personnel from 1000. Ghosts retain last-known generic presentation, at most ten, independent of hidden current fit; show no exact ghost strength.
- **Symbols:** one admitted unit = one symbol, including wounded-only units and naval detachments. Use infantry crossed strokes, archer bow, cavalry diagonal stroke, artillery solid dot, fleet anchor, royal household a small crown accent over its arm. Own frame rectangle, observed hostile frame diamond, ghost dashed frame. Use muted existing ink/realm colors, with shape as well as color conveying meaning. No strength labels, group counters or extra NATO echelon codes. Generic unknown-arm mark is `?`; legend labels it “Unknown arm”. These are original editor-authored marks, not image-generation work.
- **One mode owner:** `Map3DView` stores a view-only `unit_symbol_mode: bool`, evaluated once per camera update by pure `Map3DUnits.symbol_mode(previous: bool, distance_m: float, enter_m: float, leave_m: float) -> bool`. Enter at `distance >= enter`, leave at `distance <= leave`, retain previous between, require `0 < leave < enter`. Store calibrated `map3d_unit_symbol_enter_m`/`leave_m` in `data/screens/map3d/balance.csv`. No numerical threshold is owner-approved; capture bracketing establishes final data before merge. Opening initializes deterministically from distance with previous false.
- Feed that same committed mode to models, symbol markers, hit rectangles and legend before redraw. Military hidden/unbound means no symbols or legend. No frame with both models/symbols or symbols without legend. View mode never writes simulation or triggers contacts/group refresh.
- Add an editor-authored `MilitaryLegend` child in `world_map_3d.tscn` and a shared typed symbol Resource for glyphs/frames/labels. Static layout/styles/shapes belong in the editor. Place within the map's upper-right safe area below existing council controls, wrapping/scrolling at narrow windows without widening facts or displacing the audience. Legend contains the same arm/frame meanings used by markers; no hand-maintained second label mapping.
- Reuse HUD marker/declutter and picker/box-selection paths, one unit code and one union rect per actual unit. Symbols can be displaced in screen space for legibility using existing declutter; their world anchor and engine coordinates never move. Keep existing selection/order signals, no new group-order surface. WAR ROOM remains one known-unit piece.
