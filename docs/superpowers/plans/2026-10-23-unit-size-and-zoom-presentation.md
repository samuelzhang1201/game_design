> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans for one registered step in its own worktree. The project's one implementation + unique Review pair overrides generic subagent-driven execution; no nested agents. Steps use checkbox syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Enforce 1000 fit+wounded per actual unit, 100 fit per physical/display group, near at most ten soldiers and mid/far one unit symbol with a simultaneous upper-right legend.

**Architecture:** Extend the existing candidate-based military organization migration, preserving cohort/custody identities and frozen transaction inputs. **Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-unit-size-and-zoom-presentation.md` (read it with this plan).

## Global Constraints

- **100 fit** per physical/display group; positive remainder adds a group. **1000 living = fit + wounded** per actual unit; captives excluded. No consolidation.
- Stable recursive halves: 1001 → [501,500], 2500 → [625,625,625,625], 2001 → [501,500,1000]. Near `ceil(fit / 100) <= 10` soldier representatives, in and out of battle.
- Mid/far one simple military symbol per actual unit; legend visible simultaneously in the upper-right; near hides it. Naval near counter exception and no hidden live-strength sampling are explicit in the spec.
- Engine-pure RefCounted logic, seeded RNG unchanged by migration/presentation, integer save values, reasoned changes, all design numbers in data. Tests use SuiteData, no DataRepository.load_all.
- No equipment, population, money, named identity, custody, history, task or fee duplication; no partial candidate publication or mid-transaction splitting.
- Static look through authorized editor only. gdai unavailable is an execution dependency, not permission to text-author scenes/resources or alter MCP/trust. No new troop art batch.
- One small step/worktree/codex branch/PR and exactly one implementation + unique Review. Only root dispatches successors. Each owning Review also provides independent visual critique, no third participant.

## Review Focus

- A completed v1 organization with saved casualties must be checked against living v2, without rebuilding personnel from today's pack (P1 tests).
- Mixed fit/wounded/named/captive status and zero-equipment naval detachments preserve identities, arm, sources and future custody release (P1 tests).
- Frozen day/round or paid assembly retry never duplicates a split, activation, courier delivery, task, debit or movement (P1/P2 tests).
- A same-day fit change, hidden enemy mutation or stale ghost must not leak live strength or leave the old formation count cached (P3 tests).
- Zoom jitter and military-layer toggles preserve unit picking and always change symbols/models/legend together without changing engine state (P4 tests).
