# P4.4c2 — the saved gait and the running march

Scope: item 2 of `2026-10-19-p44c1-gait-arithmetic.md`. A land unit's commander
runs only for a §5.1 reason the engine can already see; the gait is saved on the
unit's entry; a running substep of `ArmySystem.march_day_saved` covers
`UnitGait.run_step_m` of the walk step and pays `UnitGait.run_substep` first.
No new CSV key (c1's two keys and `battle_contact_km`), no UI, no pursuit
(c3), no target tracking (c4).

Approved design: spec `2026-10-17-unit-behavior-and-battlefield-design.md` §5.1
(reasons and stop condition recorded, no hidden enemy intelligence; a camped unit
still packs a full day) and §5.2 (base ration first, decided at the start of each
fixed step, a step it cannot afford walks).

## Contract

- **Saved gait**: `entry.gait = {mode: "run", reason, until[, battle_id]}` on the
  unit's own `regiments` / `foes` entry; no key means walk (an old save walks).
  Reasons and their stop conditions:
  - `withdrawal` / until `march_end`: the unit marches with `disengaging == 1`
    (a received retreat, a rout, an evading or failing stance — every withdrawal
    march `BattleSystem._start_retreat` / `march_out` starts). Stops when the
    march ends or `disengaging` clears.
  - `join_battle` / until `battle_over`: the unit marches, is in no battle, and
    its destination is within `battle_contact_km` of the place of a live battle
    in which a unit of its own table (the crown's `regiments`, or `foes`) fights —
    a battle its side knows. Stops when that battle is gone, the unit is in a
    battle, the march ends, or the destination leaves that battle's reach.
  `UnitGait.refresh` clears a gait whose stop condition holds, then records a
  run when a reason holds (withdrawal first). A fleet never runs.
- **The running substep** (`march_day_saved`, after the step budget and the
  last-substep catch-up are fixed): only for an entry that will really march this
  substep (march order in both the live and saved entry, past camp packing).
  `base_bite = day_bite(supply_use, day)` (still eaten by `eat_day`);
  `run_bite = base_bite × fit supply‰ ÷ (living + prisoner supply‰)` — the
  wounded and prisoners keep only their base. When `run_substep` pays, the step
  becomes `run_step_m(step)`; otherwise the substep walks and nothing changes.
- **Hand checks** (flat-ground fixture, 2 300 m march, budget 767, ten
  substeps 77×7 + 76×3): running all day 92×7 + 91×3 = 917 m; walking 767 m; a
  unit holding exactly today's base walks 767 and pays nothing; JSON mid-day
  equals straight-through; packing day moves 0 and pays 0.
