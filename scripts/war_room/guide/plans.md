# Plans: supposed pieces and the staff's marks

Spec `2026-10-07-war-room-annotations-and-planning-pieces-design.md` (owner-approved, style A wax crayon). The player's own paper: no engine rule reads it.

- **State** `GameState.war_plans` (`WarPlans`, pure, `scripts/engine/`): `pieces` (one land arm, heads stepped by `war_plan_heads_*`, a foreign kingdom or `unknown`, whole metres, facing) and `strokes` (`data/war_plan_kinds.csv`: kind, colour blue / red — ink only in old saves, dashed, flip (a front's facing), name, whole-metre control points, a ring's radius, `of` the supposed piece or hint a red arrow is drawn for — removing the piece takes it), `rev` the caches' key. Saved plain (`GameState._whole`), written only by `Game.place_plan_piece` … `restore_plans`, each saying `plans_changed` (never `state_changed`). Caps `war_plan_pieces_max` / `war_plan_strokes_max`.
- **Figures** `WarGround.plan_reading` / `plan_lines`: men on this ground = heads × `ArmySystem.power_of_arms` here over on open plains, at the assumed `war_plan_training` / `war_plan_morale` and no hidden float; works only in a town its side holds; with a crown regiment chosen, both sides on this ground with the height between — never an odds. The slip shows it over a supposed piece (`plan_hovered`, the piece hover's dwell), pinned while one is chosen, and after an order arrow's tip's own lines within `war_plan_contact_km` of it.
- **Pieces** `WarPlanPieces` (`%PlanPieces`, extends `WarPieces`): the arm's carved piece in boxwood (`wood_piece.gdshader` instance `blank`, palette `plan_boxwood`) on the side's lacquer (`plan_unknown` for Unknown), a pencilled tag; a left drag carries it (`follow`); a click selects it (red).
- **Strokes** are painted into `plan_tex` (`WarRoomInk.plan_for`, `WarPlanInk` → `WarPlanDraw` through the ink viewport after the intel layer, keyed by sheet and `rev`): crayon polygons filled with the wax tooth (`GameArt.WAR_PLAN_ART` `wax_tooth`, `war_plan_tooth_scale`) and a darker pressed rim, pen lines, names; widths are table mm (`war_plan_*_mm`). A stroke shows on every sheet its box touches. The curve is `WarPlanGeometry.curve` on `MarchRoute.point` (one centripetal Catmull-Rom with the march routes). The near-zoom patch does not repaint them.
- **The roll** `%ToolRoll` (`WarToolRoll` / `WarToolSlot`, `scenes/war_room/war_tool_roll.tscn`; hidden in board mode; slot glyphs: `GameArt.WAR_TOOL_ART` by `art_key`, inked in the slot's colour (`WarToolSlot.inked_glyph`; the scroll button: `scroll`): the HAND, then two rows whose `side` meta is what they draw for — RED (theirs: its crayon, arrow, front, phase, objective | supposed piece, scraper) above BLUE (ours: the same less the piece). `WarPlanTool` holds a SIDE ("" none: nothing draws) and a kind; the side's row is lit, the other at `dim_alpha`; a slot that cannot be used now is faint with its reason (`why`: an ARROW wants a selection of its side). The pen is retired: phase lines and objectives are pen lines in the side's colour. The scraper takes its side's marks and ink (red: supposed pieces too).
- **Input** `WarPlanInput` (the view routes the left button to it first, never while a march waits on its confirm): a kind in hand draws freehand (smoothed to `war_plan_points_max`, Shift straight, a ring by compass; never pans; a press whose pointer never goes `war_rake_min_px` from it is a CLICK, not a stroke — it picks as with the hand: a supposed piece or hint, else `WarRoomPointer.click` (our piece, the selection let go), the stick kept) — a blue arrow is the selection's MARCH (`order_drawn`, above; the blue arrow is laid down once its march is armed), a red one is drawn for EACH selected supposed piece or hint (`of`, starting at that host's own spot); Shift on a blue arrow sends only its first and last samples (a straight march); a front faces the nearest of the other side's marks and pieces (`WarPlans.facing_flip` / `opposing_points`; none: the drawer's left); the piece slot opens the Place form; with the hand it selects, carries a supposed piece or the CHOSEN stroke (a press on an unchosen stroke is the view's: a drag pans, a Shift-drag boxes, a click within `war_rake_min_px` chooses it and `forget_press` keeps the pointer from also reading an open-table click), drags a chosen stroke's chalk handles and a chosen front's FACING handle (a chalk arrow at its middle, `war_plan_facing_px`: a click flips the teeth, a drag across turns them there). `WarPlanHud` draws the live layer: a stroke let go (drawn, shifted, reshaped) stays there as a ghost until the plan layer has been painted for the plans as they are (`WarRoomView.plan_key_shown()` reaches its `WarPlanInk.cache_key`, or the current one when the plans moved on, e.g. an undo, when that paint ends, a texture or not), and a stroke being carried or reshaped is left out of the plan layer meanwhile (`hide_plan_stroke`), so it never blinks out nor shows twice. The name box commits on a press on the table (`table_pressed`) or when its focus goes, so it never traps the keys. A right click (never on our pieces) opens a supposed piece's or stroke's menu (a front's: Flip the teeth, Face the enemy), else the ground menu.
- **Intel hints** (`WarRoom.intel_hints`, pure): every spy report come in whose `troops_foreign` reaches `war_intel_hint_min_heads` (the newest about a place, id `h<x>_<y>` from the place's rounded pack px), then `believed_hosts` — real enemy hosts arrive with intel P2. `WarHud` draws each as a red ring of MARCHING ANTS (`war_intel_hint_*`, its phase turned every frame, never baked); a click selects it for the red arrow.
- **Screen** `WarPlanDesk`: `%GroundMenu` (Place a supposed enemy…, Clear on the sheet — asked once), `%PlanForm` (`WarPlanForm`), `%PlanName` (a phase line's or objective's name, prefilled), keys Esc (the stick, then the chosen, then the selection, then the docked book) / 1–4 (the kinds on the side lit; none: the news says choose one) / Tab (the other side, the same kind; nothing in hand: that side's last kind, a front when its arrow has no selection) / Delete / Cmd-Z (the session's undo of marks AND drawn marches, `war_plan_undo_max`, emptied on a load or new campaign; a march's step keeps its drafts before and after and is dropped, said in the news, once they have since changed — sealed, struck, discarded or set aside by a map order — so an undo never revives a superseded draft).

## The blue front line (spec `2026-10-11-war-room-front-line-design.md`)

- A BLUE `front` drawn with our land regiments selected is ALSO an order: `WarPlanInput` names them in the stroke (`codes`; fleets alone are refused "Fleets are ordered on the map", the mark still drawn), `WarPlanDesk._plant_front` → `Game.plant_front` posts each along the line (`WarFront.assign`: the stroke's own curve cut into one length per regiment, posts taken by each regiment's place projected along it, ties by code) and pencils a MARCH draft to its post carrying `hold` {stance `hold`, facing a whole degree, 0 east / 90 south / 180 west / 270 north} and `front` (the stroke's id) — or a GARRISON draft with the hold when it stands within 1 pack px. A refused regiment is said in the news and leaves its post empty. No confirm: the resolutions scroll is the review (" · then hold, facing S (180°)", `WarFront.compass_words`). Undo is two steps: the drafts, then the stroke. Edit route… keeps the hold; a new arrow drawn for the selection drops it; flipping, reshaping or scraping the stroke leaves the drafts alone.
- Sealed, the draft's `ARMY_STANCE {stance, facing, on_arrival}` rides the march's courier; the engine parks it (`hold_after`) and takes it the day the column arrives (`ArmySystem.march_day`; `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`). The room's piece and the 3D piece turn to `ArmySystem.piece_heading` (the road, else the held facing).
