# The WAR ROOM (`scripts/war_room/`)

A SCREEN of its own (the bottom bar's WAR ROOM tab): a physical operations room in 3D after a standing officer's view, medieval throughout — ink, vellum, parchment, brass, wax, pencil; Gotham lends density and organisation, never its look. Not to be confused with THE WAR TABLE, the oak-table dressing of the court (`scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`). Specs: `2026-10-01-war-room-design.md`, `2026-10-02-war-room-v2-design.md` (plans A camera/layout, B graticule, C drafts; D the room's art is next). Every number is a `balance.csv` `war_*` key or the scene's inspector.

## Boundaries

- Nothing here reads the `Game` autoload; `WarRoomScreen` (`scripts/ui/`) is the only piece that touches `Game`. Plain Godot meshes in the authored scene `scenes/war_room/war_room.tscn`, rendering into a SubViewport at window px × the tier's `render_scale` with the tier's MSAA (the folio figure too; `WarRoomScreen.set_tier`).
- **The clock is HELD while the room is open**: opening stops it and `Game.hold_clock(true, "war_room")` holds it; every way out lets go and starts nothing. The Missive board opened OVER the room is not a way out.
- **Presentation never reaches the engine.** The pure pieces live in `scripts/engine/`: `WarSheets`, `MapCoords`, `WarDrafts`, `WarFolio`, `WarRoom`.

## The room and camera

- **It turns like the 3D map**: `WarCamera` (pure) wraps yaw through 360°, pitch on three points (near / the officer's mid / far, flatter so the room comes into view), kept inside the walls. `WarRoomGestures`: right DRAG turns, right CLICK (`war_click_slop_px`) is the plans' menus (none on our pieces), a left drag pans in screen directions — from a piece too (`WarRoomPointer.empty_press`); Shift-drag boxes a selection instead; WASD pans (never while a text box has focus), the key's rose eases back to the sheet's foot.
- **It is laid over the left column and the map at once** (`GameRoot.war_room_rect`) and **comes down like a hanging scroll** (`WarScrollReveal`: the screen beneath drawn below a descending wooden roller, rolled back up on close; none when the screen beneath is being replaced, nor from the board's Show on map).
- A picked piece shows NO card (its hover slip has said it): its folio opens docked in the room's upper-left (see The folio); the column's card is never borrowed.
- **The Missive board hangs on an easel** (`%Easel` / `%BoardPanel`) beyond the table's upper-right (north-east) corner, turned to face the table and leaning back: a board-only `MissiveScreen` in a SubViewport rendered only while any of the panel is seen (`WarRoomView.board_visible`); a click flies the camera square to it (`WarBoard`, pure), where clicks are pushed into the board as real input, a note opens the full board over the room (`GameRoot.open_missive_over_war_room`), Escape flies back. Turning to the board withdraws a march pending on its confirm and puts the HUD's marks (`%Hud`) away until the camera is back over the table.
- The room's art (`assets/3D/war_room/<name>/`): stone chamber, oak table, candlesticks — ONE key candle rakes the relief (`WarRoomLights`, `war_candle_*`; the tier's `war_candle_shadows` say how many cast), the others unshadowed accents; the rake and the order pennant.

## The sheets

- **The series** is `WarSheets` (pure): the pack's own graticule (`MapCoords` from `map_info.csv`'s `lat_n` / `lat_s` / `lon_w` / `lon_e`; a pack without them spans the globe and warns) cut into `war_sheet_deg` cells (10°: ~71 × 52 km on beria, spec `2026-10-07-war-room-scale-hover.md`), a sheet for every cell with land, named like `D4` — columns past Z run `AA`, `AB`… (`WarSheets.column_letters`) — and after the kingdom with the most land on it; `cell` is the neat line, `rect` the cell plus a `war_sheet_collar_km` collar inked faint. Places are written `D4 · c7 · 24°N 51°E`. The room opens on the sheet holding `MapManors.home_province`.
- **The sheet lies FLAT, a 2D tactical map on PARCHMENT** (`war_relief_exaggeration` 0): ground told only by contours, sea washed, a graticule of meridians and parallels with figures inside each line, the neat band chequered (`war_table.gdshader`, `WarSheetInk`). Heights are the build's committed half-float `bake/height_war.r16` via `WarBake` (else the local `height.r32`; `world_relief.png` without a build) — no 3D map needed. The room, the table and the pieces stay 3D.
- **The table** is `WarRoomTable` (pure maths; not `WarTable`, which is the court's oak-table dressing) + `war_table.gdshader`.
- **Ink** (`WarRoomInk` / `WarInkPainter` / `WarSheetInk`): roads, rivers (the 3D map's own courses, `WarBake.river_courses` from the build's `rivers.json`, else the pack's polyline smoothed; a curve with a point every `war_ink_river_step_px`, inked as ONE feathered ribbon widening by discharge, the name written along the course — `WarSheetInk.river_line`, `WarInkPainter._draw_river`), borders with the realm's watercolour band, seams, towns (walled squared with towers, ports with an anchor), bridges and fords, hollows, woods as little trees, manor edges and names (a name left out where its box would touch a town's), drawn once into one texture per sheet and cached by the ownership signature — only the laid sheet and one other kept; the ink viewport is 2x2 at rest.
- **Fine print for the leaning officer** (`WarFinePrint`, pure; a second sheet texture `fine_tex` cached with the ink, `WarRoomInk.fine_for`): each town's second line (size · works · souls · height), the routes' names, the minor peaks and hollows, figures on the `war_fine_contour_level_m` contours — public static facts only, a box left out where it would touch a town's or manor's name. `WarNear` (pure) fades it in between `war_fine_far_m` and `war_fine_near_m` and steps the shader's contour interval down from `war_contour_m` to `war_contour_near_m` as the camera leans in; the view writes both on every camera move.
- **Marks** (`WarMarks`, pure, `scripts/engine/`; `data/war_marks.csv`, spec `2026-10-07-war-room-p2-tactical-layer-design.md` §1): the map's points of interest a type row admits, public geography with no intel gate — the `sheet` layer inked with the ink (`places`), the `fine` layer and every mark's name in the fine print; `WarMarkGlyph` draws each glyph (the painter, the key slip). A hazard type (`data/war_mark_hazards.csv`: brigands, pirates) is in rubric with its reach as a dotted ring (`war_mark_reach_*`), and takes its share of a supply line passing within reach (`SupplySystem.mark_loss`, named in the line's `hazards` and the folio's supply row).
- **The war economy** (`WarEconomy`, pure, `scripts/engine/`; `data/war_works.csv`, spec `2026-10-07-war-room-p3-economy-layer-design.md` §1): public, static facts of the pack inked on every sheet — a town making `war_works_min_gold` of strategic goods a month carries its family's glyph above its ring (arms, horses, ore, naval), a market's centre town its scales below, each on a parchment knock-out and clear of a walled town's square; the pack's deals summed per road segment (`trade_flows`, built once per pack) weigh a road by tier (`war_trade_tier<N>_gold`, `war_ink_trade_px`). The fine print says what a works makes; the fine print and the slip list a works' three largest goods by name (never the pack's units), the slip with its gold a month (at home with this month's live gold), the trade on a road and a market with its food price.
- **The crown's supply lines** (`WarSupplyFlow`, owner 2026-10-07): only a FLOWING line of ours, a SOLID GOLD ribbon on the sheet (`%SupplyFlow`, its look `scenes/war_room/materials/war_supply_flow.tres` + `war_supply_flow.gdshader`: the 3D map's gold, a dark hairline edge, brighter streaks flowing town → army by `TIME`), from its town along the road to where its piece stands NOW — the roads found on a state change, the ends laid again every frame a piece is pushed (`WarRoomView.redraw_marks`); never baked into the ink. The key slip draws it from the same look.
- **A second texture of what we KNOW** (`WarIntelInk`, redrawn when its key turns): the crown's couriers and their road ahead, this spell's foul weather, every spy report that has COME IN as a dashed ring — never a foreign line or a spy still riding. Every KNOWN hazard in play is hatched in it by its kind's `ink` (unrest crossed, water level, rock one dashed diagonal, plague staggered dots; `war_hazard_hatch_km`, `war_hazard_dot_ratio`, `war_intel_hazard_px` / `_alpha`), outlined dashed, and named with its months left (`war_intel_hazard_label_px`, a paper halo) on the centre of its cell nearest its middle, kept on the sheet; the key slip draws each hatch at the same strength.
- **The index chart** lies flat on the table's north band (never on the far wall, which the officer's pitch never shows): parchment with the whole continent drawn, land cells framed, columns lettered and rows numbered (`WarIndexChart`, `WarRoomWall`); a click on a land cell TOSSES the war marker onto it (`WarToss`, pure) before the sheet is laid; an edge arrow lays at once; a second pick mid-toss is ignored.
- **The margin slip** lists regiments on other sheets, each laying its sheet; **the key slip** (`WarMarginKey`): a rose turned with the yaw, the shared `ScaleBar` restated on every zoom, a legend of every ink. The HUD is redrawn whenever the view resizes.

## The hover slip

- The pointer resting on open table (`WarHoverDwell`, pure: `war_hover_delay_s` within `war_hover_still_px`) makes the view emit `ground_hovered`; a move emits `hover_cleared`. While a blue order arrow is drawn, `order_aimed` reads its tip at once for the selection's first regiment (the days there too). Resting on a PIECE emits `piece_hovered` instead: `WarPieceSlip` (pure, `scripts/engine/`) reads the one unit it stands for — arm and heads, defend / attack on the ground where it stands (`ArmySystem.power`), readiness, morale, and supplies with their days (`Supplies 1,240 (≈ 18 days)`, `ArmySystem.days_in_hand`) — in the same slip. None over a pennant, while the camera or a sheet moves, in board mode, or while a text box has focus (`WarRoomView.hover_allowed`).
- **`WarGround`** (`scripts/engine/`, pure, read-only) reads the point: the place, ground, height (the fight's `Elevation` figure), road, town and works, today's weather; with a regiment chosen its defend / attack against open plains (`ArmySystem.power_on`; works only in a town its own kingdom holds), its height against where it stands, pace and days there. **Never a foreign force or garrison** — enemy forces are intel P2's (a SUPPOSED piece is the player's own paper and has its own slip, below).
- It names a known hazard under the pointer with its months left and what it does (`WarGround.hazard_words`), after the weather.
- It names the marks within `war_mark_hover_km` (at most `war_mark_hover_max`, nearest first; a hazard's toll on supply; the nearest's legend cut to `war_mark_legend_chars`).
- `WarRoomScreen` asks `WarGround` and fills `%HoverSlip` (`scenes/war_room/war_hover_slip.tscn`, `WarHoverSlip`: the key slip's paper, the `WarHover*` label kinds, below-right of the pointer and flipped at the view's edges, never taking the mouse).

## Pieces, orders and drafts

- **Pieces** (`WarPieces`, spec `2026-10-06-war-room-wooden-pieces.md`): a land regiment is ONE solid carved-oak piece of its art key (`MapObjects.army_piece`: the royal regiment the royal piece, artillery the trebuchet; `GameArt.WAR_PIECES` → `scenes/models/war_room/piece_<model>.tscn`) on a round plinth `war_piece_plinth_mm` across whose lacquer is the realm's colour (`wood_piece.gdshader`'s instance uniform `lacquer`, read through the piece's `_lacquer.png` mask) — one piece at any strength, no pose, the whole piece turned to its heading — and a paper tag; **plinths and bases exist only here**. A fleet is a tin token on a square base in the realm's colour with a brass plate. Each is seated at the max relief under its footprint's corners (`half` meta). A click picks the nearest piece (`war_pick_px`), rings it in chalk and opens its folio docked; tags show only within `war_tag_view_m`.
- **The pieces stand where the 3D map DRAWS them**: `WarRoomScreen` reads `Map3DView.unit_pack_positions()` and hands it to the view as `drawn` (`WarRoomView.bind`'s `drawn_fn`); `WarRoom.believed_position` / `pieces` / `partition` / `on_sheet` / `off_sheet` take it (a spot it names wins). `WarRoom.believed_position` is the ONE place the table reads a regiment's place (intel P2 will make it the last report; `WarRoom.believed_hosts` is where enemy ghosts will come).
- **One piece a unit, never merged** (`WarRoom.pieces`): each unit stands where it is (the seat's unit on its town's point), its tag its own heads and stores; a click selects that unit (no order menu: orders are drawn, below). **No two footprints overlap**: `WarPieces._spread` steps neighbours apart with `Map3DDeclutter` (the household first, then by men, then code; `war_spread_step_m` / `war_spread_max_m`), display only, the engine's places untouched; a pending march's crayon leaves the piece where it stands (spec `2026-10-07-army-split-and-ground-design.md` §5.4).
- **Selection** (spec `2026-10-08-war-room-draw-orders-design.md`, rulings at its top): `WarPlanInput.sel_side` / `sel` — ours (blue: unit codes, the 3D map's Shift-click / Shift-drag words) or theirs (red: supposed pieces' and intel hints' ids), never both. A click on our piece selects it and opens its folio docked; a click on a supposed piece or a hint selects it; Shift-click toggles; a Shift-drag on open table boxes what lies in it — ours first, at the spot they are drawn, else the supposed pieces and hints — replacing the selection, in a chalk marquee, opening no book (an empty box changes nothing); a click on open table or Esc lets it go; laying another sheet (`keep_on_sheet`) drops every unit, supposed piece or hint not on it, closing the book of a unit that goes. Rings in the side's colour (`war_select_ring_*`), drawn by `WarHud`.
- **Orders are DRAWN; the room has no order menu on a piece and no rake.** **A fleet is never marched from the room**: a selection of fleets alone greys the blue arrow ("Fleets are ordered on the map"), and a drawn order carries only the land regiments. The blue row's arrow is live only with a blue selection: its stroke (any start) is fitted (`WarOrderSketch.fit`, pure: RDP at `war_order_fit_px` into whole-metre points, at most `army_march_waypoints_max` before the tip) and each regiment joins it at its nearest point (`route_for`, `war_order_join_km`); `WarRoomScreen` hands the routes to `ArmyOrderMenu.plan_group`, which drops a waypoint the regiment cannot reach, leaves out a refused regiment with its reason and opens the march's confirm (`scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`: one row a regiment, Supply from, Add waypoint hidden). While it stands the march is drawn in blue crayon (the wax tooth tiled) along the curves the columns will walk (`aim_group`, `ArmySystem.curve_px` → `WarPlanInk`, each starting at its plinth's edge; feeders `war_order_feeder_mm` joining the path AHEAD, never back), over the other marches' arrows and the supply road (all in `WarPlanHud`), its waypoints rings: a press within `war_route_grab_px` of the lead's line or a ring bends it (`route_bent`; the others re-join). The supply pick is the room's own: `supply_pick` lit towns (`war_supply_lit_px`; a click on one chooses it, never a bend) and `supply_preview` the BOLD DASHED GOLD road to the end (`war_supply_route_*`, palette `supply_route`), apart from the flowing ribbon. The road is CLIPPED to the laid sheet's rect in pack px (`WarPlanGeometry.clip_to_rect`, runs cut exactly at the edge) before it is projected, so no gold lies on the oak; a road whose town is off the sheet gets an exit mark where it enters (`WarPlanHud` arrowhead and "from TOWN · SHEET" in the plan lettering, `war_supply_exit_font_px`); the start cut by the town's ring applies only to a town on the sheet. A sheet rolling gives the pending march up (`sheet_rolling`). **The plotter's rake** pushes a piece to where we know it stands (`WarPieces`).
- **A march STAYS its blue crayon arrow** (spec ruling 19): pencilled, sealed, riding, marching, countermanded — never a dashed or graphite line. `WarRoomPointer.orders` builds every arrow from the STATE each redraw (a draft not being re-drawn on the confirm; a march whose pennant is on the sheet), from where the piece is drawn along `ArmySystem.curve_px` to its end, so a seal, a load or Edit route… redraws them and a marching arrow shortens as its piece advances; `WarPlanHud.show_orders` paints them under the pending march, never into the sheet's ink. One drawn path is ONE arrow (`WarOrderArrows.group`: same kind and end, the one's bends the other's tail; the most bends lead with the head), its crayon one outline (`WarOrderArrows.union`). A draft's crayon is `plan_blue_draft` with a graphite (`pencil`) rim, a sealed one `plan_blue`.
- **The room's orders are DRAFTS** (`ArmyOrderMenu.drafting`): Confirm pencils a march for each regiment, Cancel march pencils a halt — `WarDrafts` (pure), `GameState.war_drafts` (saved, one per regiment, whole metres), written only by `Game.plant_draft` / `strike_draft` / `discard_drafts`; no courier and no fee. A draft (its `waypoints_m`, `carry_days`, `supply_town` / `supply_rate` too; sealed: supply point, load, march, each its own courier) is a pale pennant at its arrow's tip; a real order from the 3D map sets the draft aside (a `war_draft` effect).
- **Pennants** mark committed marches at the arrow's tip, the slip dated: "courier riding", "order received", "countermand riding", "halt pencilled". A pennant click opens the route menu (Edit route… reopens the confirm with the pending blue arrow; Strike the draft; Cancel march pencils a halt).
- **The resolutions scroll** (`WarDeployScroll`, `scenes/war_room/war_deploy_scroll.tscn`): "Resolutions of the Council", a line per draft with order, place, days and gold — each line MEASURED by running its order on a copy of the state (`Game.review_drafts`), a refused line showing its reason. Seal and send presses the crown's arms into the wax, flies the scroll off and runs every draft ONCE as its real order (`Game.deploy_drafts`; a refused one stays a draft).
- **Leaving with drafts asks** (`WarLeavePrompt`: Deploy… / Discard / Keep / Stay) at every door through `GameRoot.leave_war_room(then)`; an event's forced switch keeps the drafts silently but for a news line.

## The folio

- `WarFolioPanel` (`scenes/war_room/war_folio.tscn`) opens DOCKED: `%Docket`, a small leather book of ONE vellum page where the scene stands it in the room's upper-left under the sheet title (≤ ~30% of the width, ~45% of the height) — the ink figure beside the regiment's name and `WarFolio.brief`'s rows (pure: heads and arms, morale, supplies "N (≈ D days)", line, ground, order, pace; Intel-gated like `sections`), no dim, the room around it still in play (camera, pieces, drawing); the margin slip steps right of it (`book_rect`), or is put away where it would meet the key slip. Its + (`%DockEnlarge`) or a click on it holds the whole two-page book (`%Folder`) up full size centred over a dim; the book's − or a click on the dim lays it back (the kit taken apart goes back together). The ink figure is ONE node moved between the two slots (`%DockFigSlot`, `%FigSlot`). × closes it, and so does Escape once the desk's chain has nothing left to lay down (`escape_closes` is false: the room closes the book itself); another piece swaps it, letting OUR piece go closes it, while a side change to a supposed piece (which clears ours first) keeps the red selection; it re-lays itself while open.
- **Right page** `WarFolio.sections` (pure, six builders: composition, arms, morale, supply, ground, march; rows through `Intel`) in balanced columns so ALL SIX show without scrolling — density outranks a single column. Morale scores TODAY's stores and says so.
- **Left page** `WarInkFigure`: the chief arm's unit `.glb` in sepia outline and hatching, framed by the body (`Map3DUnitRig.head_m`), looping the current order's clip on a DUPLICATED `Animation` (`_own_clips`; never the imported one the map shares); drill tabs from `data/war_drills.csv` (a clip the model lacks shows no tab); the arms row redraws each arm's existing icon in ink (`WarInkIcon`); a 9 / 6 / 3 dot formation. An unmodelled arm (`WarFolio.modelled` false) shows its inked icon large instead, with no drill tabs and no Take apart.
- **Take apart** (`WarExplode`) flies the parts of a STATIC parts model `assets/3D/units/<arm>/<arm>_parts.glb` (one object per `war_equipment.csv` part; a skinned mesh cannot be taken apart at runtime) out with names, notes and leader lines; the button is HIDDEN until that file exists and a missing file is no error. Archers have none until they have their own model.

## Plans: supposed pieces and the staff's marks

Spec `2026-10-07-war-room-annotations-and-planning-pieces-design.md` (owner-approved, style A wax crayon). The player's own paper: no engine rule reads it.

- **State** `GameState.war_plans` (`WarPlans`, pure, `scripts/engine/`): `pieces` (one land arm, heads stepped by `war_plan_heads_*`, a foreign kingdom or `unknown`, whole metres, facing) and `strokes` (`data/war_plan_kinds.csv`: kind, colour blue / red — ink only in old saves, dashed, flip (a front's facing), name, whole-metre control points, a ring's radius, `of` the supposed piece or hint a red arrow is drawn for — removing the piece takes it), `rev` the caches' key. Saved plain (`GameState._whole`), written only by `Game.place_plan_piece` … `restore_plans`, each saying `plans_changed` (never `state_changed`). Caps `war_plan_pieces_max` / `war_plan_strokes_max`.
- **Figures** `WarGround.plan_reading` / `plan_lines`: men on this ground = heads × `ArmySystem.power_of_arms` here over on open plains, at the assumed `war_plan_training` / `war_plan_morale` and no hidden float; works only in a town its side holds; with a crown regiment chosen, both sides on this ground with the height between — never an odds. The slip shows it over a supposed piece (`plan_hovered`, the piece hover's dwell), pinned while one is chosen, and after an order arrow's tip's own lines within `war_plan_contact_km` of it.
- **Pieces** `WarPlanPieces` (`%PlanPieces`, extends `WarPieces`): the arm's carved piece in boxwood (`wood_piece.gdshader` instance `blank`, palette `plan_boxwood`) on the side's lacquer (`plan_unknown` for Unknown), a pencilled tag; a left drag carries it (`follow`); a click selects it (red).
- **Strokes** are painted into `plan_tex` (`WarRoomInk.plan_for`, `WarPlanInk` → `WarPlanDraw` through the ink viewport after the intel layer, keyed by sheet and `rev`): crayon polygons filled with the wax tooth (`GameArt.WAR_PLAN_ART` `wax_tooth`, `war_plan_tooth_scale`) and a darker pressed rim, pen lines, names; widths are table mm (`war_plan_*_mm`). A stroke shows on every sheet its box touches. The curve is `WarPlanGeometry.curve` on `MarchRoute.point` (one centripetal Catmull-Rom with the march routes). The near-zoom patch does not repaint them.
- **The roll** `%ToolRoll` (`WarToolRoll` / `WarToolSlot` glyphs, `scenes/war_room/war_tool_roll.tscn`; hidden in board mode): the HAND, then two rows whose `side` meta is what they draw for — RED (theirs: its crayon, arrow, front, phase, objective | supposed piece, scraper) above BLUE (ours: the same less the piece). `WarPlanTool` holds a SIDE ("" none: nothing draws) and a kind; the side's row is lit, the other at `dim_alpha`; a slot that cannot be used now is faint with its reason (`why`: an ARROW wants a selection of its side). The pen is retired: phase lines and objectives are pen lines in the side's colour. The scraper takes its side's marks and ink (red: supposed pieces too).
- **Input** `WarPlanInput` (the view routes the left button to it first, never while a march waits on its confirm): a kind in hand draws freehand (smoothed to `war_plan_points_max`, Shift straight, a ring by compass; never pans; a press whose pointer never goes `war_rake_min_px` from it is a CLICK, not a stroke — it picks as with the hand: a supposed piece or hint, else `WarRoomPointer.click` (our piece, the selection let go), the stick kept) — a blue arrow is the selection's MARCH (`order_drawn`, above; the blue arrow is laid down once its march is armed), a red one is drawn for EACH selected supposed piece or hint (`of`, starting at that host's own spot); Shift on a blue arrow sends only its first and last samples (a straight march); a front faces the nearest of the other side's marks and pieces (`WarPlans.facing_flip` / `opposing_points`; none: the drawer's left); the piece slot opens the Place form; with the hand it selects, carries a supposed piece or the CHOSEN stroke (a press on an unchosen stroke is the view's: a drag pans, a Shift-drag boxes, a click within `war_rake_min_px` chooses it and `forget_press` keeps the pointer from also reading an open-table click), drags a chosen stroke's chalk handles and a chosen front's FACING handle (a chalk arrow at its middle, `war_plan_facing_px`: a click flips the teeth, a drag across turns them there). `WarPlanHud` draws the live layer: a stroke let go (drawn, shifted, reshaped) stays there as a ghost until the plan layer has been painted for the plans as they are (`WarRoomView.plan_key_shown()` reaches its `WarPlanInk.cache_key`, or the current one when the plans moved on, e.g. an undo, when that paint ends, a texture or not), and a stroke being carried or reshaped is left out of the plan layer meanwhile (`hide_plan_stroke`), so it never blinks out nor shows twice. The name box commits on a press on the table (`table_pressed`) or when its focus goes, so it never traps the keys. A right click (never on our pieces) opens a supposed piece's or stroke's menu (a front's: Flip the teeth, Face the enemy), else the ground menu.
- **Intel hints** (`WarRoom.intel_hints`, pure): every spy report come in whose `troops_foreign` reaches `war_intel_hint_min_heads` (the newest about a place, id `h<x>_<y>` from the place's rounded pack px), then `believed_hosts` — real enemy hosts arrive with intel P2. `WarHud` draws each as a red ring of MARCHING ANTS (`war_intel_hint_*`, its phase turned every frame, never baked); a click selects it for the red arrow.
