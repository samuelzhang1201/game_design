# The LEDGER galaxy view (`scripts/ledger3d/`)

The realm's ontology drawn as a 3D galaxy of object, link and action types. The DATA model — belief not truth, the open schema, folding, governed accounts — is in `scripts/engine/CLAUDE.md`; the screen (`LedgerScreen`, clock hold, cards, orders) in `scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`. Specs: `2026-09-30-ledger-ontology-design.md`, `2026-09-30-ledger-galaxy-design.md`. Every number is `balance.csv` `ledger_*`, every colour `data/ledger_palette.csv`. Like `scripts/map3d/`, nothing here reads `Game`.

- **One picture**: `Ledger.galaxy` gives the realm with the economy folded by province; an OPENED province (a double click, forgotten when the LEDGER closes) is unfolded. `LedgerGalaxyLayout` places the stars within `ledger_galaxy_ms_per_frame` a frame: a redraw of the same stars runs no pass, other stars only `ledger_galaxy_resettle_passes` (survivors keep their places, the camera is kept); past `ledger_galaxy_grid_min_stars` repulsion runs through a grid within a cutoff. Clusters sit near and spread wide (`ledger_clusters.csv` anchors, a weak cross-cluster repulsion, a hub's springs longer per root of its links, a gap push).
- **Drawn** by `scenes/ledger/ledger_view.tscn`: stars with a real shine, links (`LedgerLinkMesh`, `link.gdshader`: width in screen px, shading between clusters, dashes for a `planned` link), a haze per cluster and a warm night sky in palette D (brass economy, gules war, purpure court, steel-azure intelligence). Every fade is PERCEIVED opacity (`ink × pow(fade, 2.2)`). Ink is credibility, yellowing is age; no numeric credibility is ever drawn.
- **The stars and links wear GENERATED art** (owner 2026-10-10, chose the Qwen star-chart glass orb `L-01` and the braided gold cord `L-05`, the cord thinner): a star samples a MATCAP by its view-space normal, a link a STRAND texture across and along it (`strand_px` screen px a repeat, drawn `strand_widen` wider); both are VALUE, tinted by the cluster colour, highlights toward white. The textures are the `.tres` materials' only saved values (`test_ledger_look_assets.gd`); every number is `balance.csv` `ledger_star_matcap_*` / `ledger_link_strand_*` (`_mix` 0 is the plain shine / line). New art: `assets/imagegen/ledger_art_prompts.csv` (`STYLE_CORE_ledger.txt`, canvases `C-ORB` / `C-STRAND`), cut by `tools/ledger/ledger_art_prep.py` (orb → disc-cropped 256 px matcap; strand → band-cropped, end-crossfaded 512×64) into `assets/2D/ledger/`, mipmapped.
- **Tabs (lenses) are clusters the camera flies to.** A double click on a province opens or closes it; any other star frames its web. There is no 2D view.
- **A selected star lights its LINEAGE** (`LedgerLineage.of`, pure): its own links, its whole FLOW chain followed from it alone in one direction to the end (downstream from → to, upstream to → from, never turning back) — never a SIBLING (an upstream star's other outflows, a downstream star's other inflows) nor a neighbour's own chain, its `tie` / `kin` links walked `ledger_lineage_tie_hops` (farther hops at `ledger_lineage_far_fade`); structure is never followed past the first hop; the rest stands at `ledger_focus_fade`; `frame_web` frames it. The lineage reads whole in any tab.
- **Court ties wear the clusters' colours** like every other link; their feeling is their width and their card.
- **Search** (`LedgerSearch`, pure): finds a star by label case-insensitively — also an instance folded into a province, whose province it opens first; Up/Down, Enter or a click pick one as a double click would (`LedgerScreen.pick_match` / `go_to`); Escape clears the box (never the pause menu); Space in it is typed, not the clock.
- **The stars move**: each wobbles in a Lissajous loop on the shaders' `TIME` (`star_motion.gdshaderinc`, shared by every star shader and `link.gdshader`, whose ends move with their stars via per-vertex CUSTOM2/3), and carries an additive electric current (`%Sparks`, `spark.gdshader`) that never closes into a ring nor glows wider than its share; a dimmed star the camera comes right up to fades out. `ledger_star_wobble_share` 0 / `ledger_spark_strength` 0 turn them off.
- **Property gauges** (an implementer's ruling, not yet confirmed by the owner): a star's `props` (`LedgerResolvers.props_of`) draw a ring of gauges over its name — a regiment's supplies and morale (Intel `full` only), a goods pool against 12 months of intake (`LedgerHud`). Strands of differing accounts are `LedgerView3D._split` (`ledger_strand_gap_px` apart).
- Shots: `tools/snapshot_screen.gd --ledger CLUSTER [--ledger-open CODE] [--ledger-select ID] [--ledger-search TEXT [--ledger-search-pick]]` by real clicks and keys.
