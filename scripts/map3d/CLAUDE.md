# The 3D map (`scripts/map3d/`)

The pack as a Terrain3D landscape at 50 m per pack px — the game's ONLY map. Pipeline: `tools/map3d/README.md`. Specs: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-map3d-*`, `2026-09-19-map3d-*`, `2026-09-20-map3d-*`, `2026-09-24-map3d-humankind-look-gap.md` and later. The camera values are `balance.csv` `map3d_*`.

## Shape and ownership

- **`Map3DContext` is what every piece shares** (repository, the build's `map3d_world` meta, camera and pose, terrain, live state, every scale). NO script here may touch the `Game` autoload — `map3d_view.gd` is the one exception (a test scans `scripts/map3d/*.gd` for `Game.`) — so a layer draws in the editor from a pack alone; orders live in `scripts/ui/`, the view only signals.
- **The view is an AUTHORED scene**, `scenes/map3d/world_map_3d.tscn` (`Map3DView.create()`): the Control tree — SubViewport, Display, Notice, the HUD pieces. **The 3D nodes are their own scene**, `scenes/map3d/map3d_world.tscn` (`Map3DWorld`), instanced as the SubViewport's child `World` (`Map3DView.world()`): WorldSlot, the camera rig, every layer, sky, sun, fill light. Opened alone in the editor it IS the map (`editor_preview` loads the built terrain and switches every layer's `preview` on; `preview_pack` / `preview_terrain`). Everything a layer builds is added with NO owner so a save never writes it; `NOTIFICATION_EDITOR_PRE_SAVE` runs `Map3DPreview.reset` (previews off, SubViewport `UPDATE_DISABLED`, the world handed back with `Map3DWorld.give_back`, the Display's `ViewportTexture` restored).
- **The look is `.tres` and inspector exports**: `scenes/map3d/materials/*.tres` (shader, textures, priority and the look's own values), `ground_look.tres` (`Map3DGroundLook`), `weather_look.tres` (`Map3DWeatherLook`), `world_environment.tres` (`Map3DSky`), the HUD pieces' and layers' exports in the scenes. Layers set only what comes from the pack, the camera and the window's scale (`tests/map3d/test_map3d_editor_owned_look.gd`). 512 px stand-ins in `assets/3D/terrain/textures_preview/` draw a checkout whose packs are missing.
- **A build holds DATA only** (`assets/3D/terrain/<map_id>/`, COMMITTED but the bake's three raw intermediates): the Terrain3D, the root's `map3d_world` meta, `rivers.json`, the bake's fields, the baked layers and `manifest.json`. Sun, sky and sea belong to the authored scene; `Map3DWorldLoader` strips them from an older build. `Map3DWorldLoader.status` reads the manifest first so the notice says honestly `missing` / `stale` / `bad` (and `legacy`, which still loads) and names `build_pack.sh`; a missing optional input warns once (`warn_once`).
- **`Map3DWorldCache` keeps the one built world across a screen switch** (loading costs seconds). `Map3DWorldLoader.request` starts reading a world on worker threads (`is_ready`; `status` / `instance` take that load through `packed`, kept until `Map3DWorldCache.clear`), and `Map3DView.load_step` loads a view one heavy step a call (world, houses, woods, lines, the rest) for the title; `activate()` still loads in one go. **Terrain3D's COLLISION IS OFF** (a body outliving its view's physics space crashed the swap): picking is ray-vs-heightmap.
- **Baked layers**: rivers, roads, trails and bridges are baked by `bake_layers.gd` (one mesh set per distinct step set of `graphics_quality.csv`) and used only when every input matches. The key cannot see the geometry CODE: changing any script in `Map3DLayerBake.GEOMETRY_SOURCES` fails `test_map3d_layer_bake.gd` until you bump `FORMAT`, update `GEOMETRY_MD5` and re-run `bake_layers.gd`. A stale `layers/` warns once and is rebuilt at load.
- **Graphics tiers** are `data/screens/ui/graphics_quality.csv` + `Map3DQuality` (render scale, MSAA, aniso, shadows, clouds, Terrain3D mesh, line/road/river step multipliers, HUD Hz, tree density, house budget, weather particles, the bench gate), detected from the GPU on a first run and cycled by the pause menu into `user://settings.cfg`. The view renders into its SubViewport at window px × the tier's `render_scale`, held under its `max_px_scale` SubViewport px per design px (`Map3DQuality.px_scale`; empty, ultra: none — a Retina window is pixel-bound), so **every projection, ray and screen-px uniform goes through `ctx.viewport_px_scale()`** — never the window scale alone.
- The view's SubViewport is shown by a TextureRect, never a SubViewportContainer (under `canvas_items` stretch it renders at design size and blurs); it loads on first use and renders only while shown. `GameRoot._sync_map_view` is the one rule: the 3D view on every screen, handed every theme; `screens/ui/balance.csv` `map3d_default` 0 holds it unloaded, which the suite does through `tests/pre_run.gd` (headless cannot render Terrain3D) and `test_map3d_tab.gd` lifts (`force_map3d_scene`).

## Camera and measure

- Zoom runs `map3d_zoom_min_m` to `map3d_zoom_max_m` (camera distance in world metres); the wheel and a trackpad pinch zoom about the POINTER; a two-finger drag pans. It opens on the middle of the province the player controls (`MapManors.home_province`) with the scale bar spanning `map3d_zoom_start_scale` of the pack's measure (`Map3DGeometry.distance_for_bar`) (the owner names zooms by it). Maths: `Map3DGeometry` (pure, tested).
- **The camera never sinks into the ground**: `Map3DGeometry.clear_pitch` steepens the pitch until the whole sight line stands `map3d_camera_clear_m` over the ground.
- **Every distance shown reads the pack's measure**: world metres back to pack px (`Map3DView.map_units_per_metre`) times `map_info.csv` `distance_scale` in `distance_unit`; the `map3d_*_m` camera values stay in built metres. The scale bar restates itself in the 1-2-5 ladder (`MapGeometry.nice_step`).
- The compass (`CompassLayer`, `assets/2D/ui/compass/compass.png`) turns with the view, turns clockwise with the clock while time runs, redraws only itself, and eases back to north on a click; it sits inside the map's top-left on both looks (`map_compass_size_px`, on the war table `map_compass_size_px_table`).

## Picking, hover, selection

- **A click**: a settlement within `map3d_pick_px` (or on its ring) sends `object_selected`, anywhere else `province_selected` (the province bitmap under `Map3DGeometry.ground_hit`); `MapDetailPanel.follow` takes either. Under a theme a click on land picks the manor. `map3d_picker.gd`'s `pick_at` orders: a regiment piece first (`Map3DLayerUnits.screen_rects`: the box holding the click, else within `map_army_pick_px` of one, so a far piece of a few px is still clicked) or a fleet's counter — a nearer town centre or a badge under the click wins (a badge is drawn over the pieces) — then a settlement within reach beats a landmark mark, a mark's own box beats its grown rect; picking never depends on a dot being drawn.
- **Only a clicked object is ringed**: hover names it (and swaps the left column via `country_hovered` → `CountryPanel.follow`) and draws the hovered kingdom's live outline wider in the border's ink (`lines/hover.tres`), on its land runs only, like the border; a clicked province is its own seam emphasised (`lines/selection_province.tres`), a clicked manor gold (`lines/selection_manor.tres`). A pointer move is picked once a frame (`hover_pick`); `Map3DView.kingdom_of_pick` names the holder NOW.

## Regiment pieces, couriers, HUD

- **A regiment of the player's realm is a 3D wargame piece** (`Map3DLayerUnits`, the scene's `Units` node, + the pure `Map3DUnits`): instanced SCENES from `assets/3D/units/army_models.tres` (an `ArtTable`) — `scenes/models/units/<model>.tscn`, each inheriting its `.glb` with an editable copy of its material and its shadow set there (the WAR ROOM's tin reads that material, `WarPieces`; the clips' loops stay the `.glb`'s import settings; `test_map3d_unit_scenes.gd`) — not a MultiMesh: each figure has its own `AnimationPlayer`. **Near mode draws nothing over a piece** — no counter, chip, number, bar or leader line. A near fleet (`Map3DLayerUnits.has_piece` is false) has a flat counter (`ArmyCounters`: a glyph square and a headcount box in the realm's hue), spread by `Map3DDeclutter` clear of the settlement marks and the pieces (`map_army_stack_gap_px` apart rim to rim). Every unit is its own piece; the crown is the roster's `is_royal` (DEV MODE shows every realm's units; out of it `Map3DContext.regiments()` (`Map3DSight`) adds only the foe units the crown SEES, `Sighting.seen_foes` — `fogged`: no road ahead, no order menu, a pieceless one's counter shows its men as `≈` an estimate — and `ghosts()` the rest of what it knows, each drawn as its unit's piece standing see-through by the Units node's `ghost_alpha` as a courier fades, picked as `ghost:<key>`; `scripts/engine/guide/sight_and_ghosts.md`). A SELECTED piece is shown by a faint soft glow lying on the ground under it, never a box: `Map3DUnitGlow`, a flat quad child of the piece's node on its seat (so tilted with it), depth-tested under the figures, an ELLIPSE of `map3d_unit_glow_ratio` × the formation's half extent per axis (inside the footprint the seat cleared, so it never cuts a slope), `map3d_unit_glow_lift` of its height up; a soft warm band, light lying on the ground, never a chalk line (`map3d_unit_glow_inner`..`_peak`, per-piece instance uniforms), premultiplied blend, drawn over the roads (`Map3DPriority.UNIT_GLOW`); the look `scenes/map3d/materials/unit_select_glow.tres` + `unit_select_glow.gdshader`; `Map3DView.refresh_hud` hands the selection to `Map3DLayerUnits.set_selected`. Shift + left click toggles a unit and Shift + left drag boxes several by their pieces (`Map3DInput`, `Map3DSelection.armies`).
- **A marching crown unit sweeps a SCOUTING FAN** (`Map3DUnitScout`, synced from `Map3DLayerUnits.step` by `Sighting.scout_shape`: only a crown land unit with men on MARCH, never a foe, ghost or fleet, never the war room): a polar-grid mesh (`scout_rings` x `scout_spokes`, UV u = share of the arc -1..1, v = share of the radius) at radius r_m x `m_per_px` / `ArmySystem.metres_per_pack_px`, draped on the ground (`scout_lift_m`), a WORLD-space sibling of the pieces under the Units node at the piece's drawn XZ and yaw, re-draped past `scout_redrape_m` / `scout_redrape_deg`; no owner, freed when it stops marching, is dropped or the view hides. Look: `unit_scout_fan.tres` + `unit_scout_fan.gdshader` (a faint fill feathered at apex and rim, a soft outer arc and broad wedge sweeping the sector; instance uniforms `half_arc`, `phase` hashed from the code), drawn under every line (`Map3DPriority.SCOUT_FAN`).
- **One unit, one strategic symbol**: `Map3DView.unit_symbol_mode` alone commits `Map3DUnits.symbol_mode` hysteresis using `map3d_unit_symbol_enter_m` / `leave_m`. Models, symbol hit rectangles and `MilitaryLegend` switch in the same update, bypassing HUD throttling; military off/unbound suppresses symbols and legend. `Map3DUnitSymbols` (`assets/2D/ui/military/unit_symbols.tres`) owns the shared editor-authored glyphs, frames and labels. Rectangle = own, diamond = observed hostile, dashed = stale ghost; ghosts use unknown arm without reading hidden live arm/royal/strength. No strength text or per-group marks. Declutter moves only screen rectangles; crown bounds belong to the same original unit ID for picking and Shift selection. If fallback ink overlaps, hits resolve the last-painted mark first. The scrollable legend stays at the map’s upper-right below council controls. Public admission/last-known observations are cached independently of camera projection and invalidated by their state inputs, including same-day personnel, ownership and host changes. Camera motion reuses retained per-unit ink commands, moving their nodes; appearance/selection changes redraw them. The static legend redraws on mode transitions, explicit refresh or visibility correction. Near offscreen clips stop without suppressing scouting fans or nearby sound. Roster invalidation hashes its inputs instead of querying public admission once per unit. Strategic route painting reuses the admitted list already obtained for symbol layout.
- **Its size is the camera's, not the model's** (`Map3DUnits.height_m`): one world height at every zoom (`map3d_unit_ref_px` of screen at the opening view's distance), so it grows and shrinks with the ground, while near models are shown (`map3d_unit_mark_px` / `Map3DUnits.as_counters` control couriers independently).
- Combat asks for `ATTACK`; `Map3DUnits.clip_named` prefers it and falls back to the archer’s `DRAW` clip.
- **Formation by fit people**: `Map3DFormation.fit_tier` uses `BattleGroups.counts` and configured `battle_group_size`, including the positive remainder and no figures for wounded or captive people; laid out by `Map3DFormation` in units of a figure's footprint × the Units node's `formation_gap` × the arm's `army_art.csv` `spacing` (a rest pose that reaches wide for its height — a guard stance, a shield held out — closes up by it), ranks `rank_depth` apart; resting figures out of phase, and a column marches a little OUT of step (`Map3DMarchLag`, hashed from the regiment id + figure index, no timers: each MARCH clip starts at its own phase, plays `map3d_march_desync_pace` faster or slower while marching — clamped after the jitter — and the figure trails its slot by a positional lag spring in the piece's frame, `map3d_march_lag_*`); rebuilt when the active fit count crosses a configured group boundary.
- **Seated under its whole footprint** (`Map3DUnits.footprint` / `seat`): tilted with the slope up to `map3d_unit_tilt_max_deg`, steeper lifted, raised until no sample stands above it plus `map3d_unit_seat_clear`. Each FIGURE then stands on its own ground (`Map3DUnitRig.plant`, after `seat` and after the lag spring): its local y is solved so its foot is the heightmap under it plus that clearance, so on a slope no figure hovers; the lag trail stays in the piece's plane. It faces the way it last ADVANCED (`Map3DUnits.track`: the drawn position's own step, taken only past `TRACK_MIN_M`; halted, it keeps that heading), turning at the Units node's `turn_deg_per_s`; never moved, its order's heading, else `Map3DUnits.REST` (+Z, where the opening camera stands) turned `army_art.csv` `stand_deg`. No camera term: a pan never turns a piece. It casts its shadow and has no base.
- **The ORDER picks the clip**: MARCH / GARRISON / STANDBY (`Map3DLayerUnits._sync_action` → `Map3DUnitRig.action_for`, `clip_named`; a model with no STANDBY stands by in GARRISON, one with no GARRISON garrisons in STANDBY), and a held STANCE its standing clip (`data/battle/battle_stances.csv` `stand_clip`: hold plays DEFEND, falling back to GARRISON). An action an arm CHAINS (`army_art.csv` `chains`, e.g. the infantry's `ATTACK:ATTACK;SLASH;ATTACK_VAR1`; `Map3DClipChain`) plays its clips once each by turns with a guard (GARRISON) pause between, the clip and the pause (`map3d_unit_chain_rest_min_s` / `_max_s`) hashed from regiment, figure and strike count — no figure strikes in step, a replay looks the same; chained clips are LOOP_NONE in the `.import` — a regiment holding a front (spec `2026-10-11-war-room-front-line-design.md`) also stands in the stance's `formation` (`line`: `army_formations.csv` `line_slots`, one rank abreast; `Map3DFormation.layout_of`, rebuilt when the layout changes), turns to `ArmySystem.facing_of` (re-read every frame, over its last heading), and its physical camp is drawn from the UI snapshot by `Map3DUnitCamps` (see the king/camps topic guide). The MARCH clip keeps pace with the ground (`Map3DUnitRig.march_rate`, timed from the day's PLAN, never a frame's own step; its distance the route's by its corners; held within `map3d_unit_anim_min` / `_max`; `army_art.csv` `stride_m`) — an arm with `exact_pace` 1 is never held and never jittered, so its feet match the ground at any clock; it marches out of step instead by STATURE: each figure stands at `Map3DMarchLag.stature` (1 ± `map3d_march_stature_spread`) and plays the piece's rate over it, a longer stride at a slower cadence over the same ground. `Map3DMarchLag._unit` mixes `String.hash` (fmix32): the raw hash of keys differing in their last character is consecutive, which gave every figure one phase.
- **A MARCHING piece is heard** (`Map3DUnitSound`, synced from `Map3DLayerUnits.step`): while its action is MARCH it plays its arm's loop from `GameArt.UNIT_SOUNDS` (`assets/audio/units/unit_sounds.tres`, an `ArtTable` by `army_art.csv` `piece`; no entry, silent) on an `AudioStreamPlayer3D` child of its node, so it pans with the camera (the SubViewport's 3D listener is switched on there). Loudness is the CAMERA's distance, engine attenuation off: `map3d_unit_sound_db` inside `_near_m`, linear in dB to `_floor_db` at `_far_m`, silent past it; only the `_voices` nearest sound, easing at `_fade_db_s`. Each loop starts at a point hashed from the code (no RNG). Hidden pieces are silenced; a put-away view's disabled subtree stops them and they restart on return. The loops are cut seamless from the CC0 originals (`assets/audio/units/SOURCES.md`; the originals sit git-ignored in `source/`) and loop through their `.import` (`edit/loop_mode=2`); the horsed arms (cavalry, royal) march to `march_gallop.wav`. **A halt into GARRISON lands on a footfall** (`Map3DUnitSettle`): an arm with `army_art.csv` `steps` (the MARCH clip's measured footfall shares) that goes MARCH → GARRISON keeps walking at its pace (`settling`, which `keep_pace` leaves alone) until its lead figure's next footfall at least `map3d_unit_settle_hit_s` away, then blends into its guard; its `GameArt.UNIT_SETTLE_SOUNDS` one-shot (`settle_sounds.tres`, the infantry's Equip) starts `_hit_s` early so the clank lands on that step, at `gain_db` + `map3d_unit_settle_db`. No `steps`: the switch is immediate and silent.
- **The piece walks the engine's NEXT day as the day runs** (`Map3DLayerUnits.step`, `CourierSystem.day_ahead_of` on a copy, `Map3DUnits.along_day` by `Game.day_progress`; more than `map3d_unit_snap_days` away it jumps). The road ahead (`route_px`) is a dashed line DRAPED on the ground from the piece as drawn. `Map3DView.unit_pack_positions()` gives where it draws each piece now (the WAR ROOM reads it).
- **Orders from the map**: a click on a piece raises `army_menu_requested` (`ArmyOrderMenu`; a right click on open country is not consumed). MARCH, on a PAUSED clock only, arms the AIM (`Map3DView.aiming`): a ruler draped from the piece through `aim_waypoints` to the pointer along the curve it will walk (`ArmySystem.curve_px`), realm-coloured, with distance and ONE arrival day; a left click holds the end (`aim_dest`) and the confirm panel asks (`scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`). Meanwhile a left press near the line or a waypoint's ring bends it (`Map3DRouteEdit`, `route_bent`). A committed march's end ring is clickable (`Map3DHudArmies.route_ends`): Edit route… or Cancel march.
- **Couriers** (`Map3DLayerCouriers`) ride the engine's next day as it runs (`CourierSystem.ride_ahead`), reach the column as the day ends and FADE OUT there as a ghost the moment they deliver (`Map3DCourierRide.ghost` / `fade_ghosts`, only when his last ride delivered); at the town they ride from (`CourierSystem.origin`) they wait at and ride out of its PALACE's door, and ride home into it (`Map3DCourierPalace`, drawing only); their roads and pending routes are `Map3DHudCouriers`; a click opens `CourierCard`. No weather icons.
- **Supply lines** (`Map3DSupplyLines`): a FLOWING line solid gold (a cut one is not drawn), streaks flowing town → army (`supply_flow.gdshader`), on the DRAWN road (`Map3DSupplyRoad`) to the drawn piece: a BODY re-laid every `map3d_supply_follow_s`, a TAIL every frame; a supply pick rings each realm town at its click reach (gold lit, grey not) and dashes the chosen road (`lines/supply_*.tres`). A FRONT line (its town ahead of the column: `SupplyLines.drawn_broken`) is `Map3DSupplyBroken`'s DARK DASHED road from its town toward the drawn piece, stopping where it last comes within `map3d_supply_broken_gap_m` of it with a solid tick across (`map3d_supply_broken_tick_m` each way; `MapGeometry.stop_outside` / `tick_across`), each dash over a faint pale halo dashed in step with it (the gaps show the ground; the tick has none) so it reads on dark ground (`lines/supply_broken.tres`, `supply_break.tres`, `supply_broken_halo.tres`; the dashes at `Map3DPriority.SUPPLY_BROKEN`).
- **HUD** (`Map3DHud`, one `map3d_hud_*.gd` per job, shared pieces from `scripts/ui/`: `ScaleBar`, `MapLettering`, `ManorBadges`, `ArmyCounters`, `MapRuler`): kingdom names upright, PINNED to each kingdom's live `MapOwnership.label_anchor` (an anchor off screen, no name; nothing slides it back in), sized as a word on the ground seen from its distance (`Map3DHudLabels.label_px`, never clamped) and shown only at the far, strategic zoom: whole to `map_label_fade_from_px`, faded out at `map_label_hide_px` (`label_fade`); the PLAYER's capital (while held) named in the header small capitals at ONE size, `map_capital_label_px`, above its mark (below it when a kingdom name stands there) and only far out by metres per px (`capital_entry`, `map_capital_label_*`), the fleet counters keeping off every name (`rects()`); settlement rings per `data/screens/map3d/map3d_settlements.csv`. On the war table a palette row `<key>_table` stands in for `<key>` (`MapPalette.table_key`).

## Performance gates

- 60 fps PER TIER, measured by `tools/map3d/bench.gd` (`res://scenes/tools/map3d/bench.tscn`): three zooms, median/mean/p95 against the tier's `bench_budget_ms` at its `bench_size` (a warning at any other size), exit 3 over it; `--quality`, `--render-scale`, `--out` (only under `tools/map3d/baselines/` or a scratch path, or Godot imports the CSV), `--layer`, `--weather`, `--frame-cap`, `--tier column=value`, `--bal key=value`, `--decree LAW` (a fresh decree of LAW before each zoom, so its couriers ride while it is priced). **A row is STILL-camera unless `--pan`**: a still camera pays every per-pose cut on its first frame only, so `--pan` is the honest number for a drag.
- Under Forward+ macOS caps a windowed Metal app at the display refresh (no GPU timestamps), so no row reads under it; budgets and readings: the README's Forward+ table.

- `Map3DFormation` reads a battle's saved current tactical deployment through `BattleFormation`; stance layouts remain the fallback outside battle. Unit overrides use the recipient's terrain for frontage, while legacy defaults retain side context. Narrow lines fold into ranks using the same slots as the WAR ROOM, preserving the figure-count tier and physical spacing. Deployment changes rebuild only that piece.

- **Saved physical battle groups** (`Map3DBattleGroups`, `Map3DLayerUnits`): after ordinary sight admission, each valid saved group draws one existing figure at its exact integer-metre point converted through pack pixels to built-world XZ. Only copied count/role/position/optional slot and the unit's saved group facing enter the view. Present malformed or personnel-mismatched groups are invalid; absent legacy groups, nonbattle units and ghosts retain their ordinary presentation. The physical aggregate remains one unit for picks, signals, camps, sound and WAR ROOM anchor exports; its child boxes union into that unit's hit box. Group roots stand upright, independent of march interpolation, lag, slope tilt and camera facing. Figure scale is capped by the existing group spacing/depth in built-world units; camp scale retains the ordinary reference calculation. Optional slots retain models/clip phase identities; same-day row/facing changes refresh without a camera event. `group_geometry()` measures the instantiated admitted nodes for developer captures only, never simulation state or a new player overlay.

- The near reader includes group version/size and admitted active fit in its cache
  key. Frozen legacy rows use their saved size without a new-size truncation;
  migrated rows retain their exact saved points and slots. Ghosts retain generic
  last-known presentation and never query hidden live fit. Fleets keep their
  existing single counter and never instantiate soldier groups. Empty physical
  roots contribute no hit rectangle.

- Ordinary near formations and generic ghosts retain the original reference
  scale from `Map3DUnits.scale_for`; physical battle-group spacing must not
  shrink them. Camps share that ordinary reference scale.

- `Map3DLayerUnits.figure_footprint_gap` is the editor-owned scale ceiling for
  saved physical battle-group figures only, separate from `formation_gap`,
  which spaces ordinary formation centres. It never changes saved source
  slots, XZ/facing or the camp reference scale.
