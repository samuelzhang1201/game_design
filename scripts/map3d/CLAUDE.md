# The 3D map (`scripts/map3d/`)

The pack as a Terrain3D landscape at 50 m per pack px — the game's ONLY map. Pipeline: `tools/map3d/README.md`. Specs: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-map3d-*`, `2026-09-19-map3d-*`, `2026-09-20-map3d-*`, `2026-09-24-map3d-humankind-look-gap.md` and later. Every visual change goes through the `visual-review` skill. The camera values are `balance.csv` `map3d_*`; the LOOK values are the scenes' and `.tres` files' inspector exports.

## Shape and ownership

- **`Map3DContext` is what every piece shares** (repository, the build's `map3d_world` meta, camera and pose, terrain, live state, every scale). NO script here may touch the `Game` autoload — `map3d_view.gd` is the one exception (a test scans `scripts/map3d/*.gd` for `Game.`) — so the same layer draws in the editor from a pack alone, and the orders live in `scripts/ui/` while the view only signals.
- **The view is an AUTHORED scene**, `scenes/map3d/world_map_3d.tscn` (`Map3DView.create()`): the Control tree — SubViewport, Display, Notice, the HUD pieces. **The 3D nodes are their own scene**, `scenes/map3d/map3d_world.tscn` (`Map3DWorld`), instanced as the SubViewport's child `World` (`Map3DView.world()`): WorldSlot, the camera rig, every layer, sky, sun, fill light. Opened alone in the editor it IS the map (`editor_preview` loads the built terrain and switches every layer's `preview` on; `preview_pack` / `preview_terrain`). Everything a layer builds is added with NO owner so a save never writes it; `NOTIFICATION_EDITOR_PRE_SAVE` runs `Map3DPreview.reset` (previews off, SubViewport `UPDATE_DISABLED`, the world handed back with `Map3DWorld.give_back`, the Display's `ViewportTexture` restored).
- **The look is `.tres` and inspector exports**: `scenes/map3d/materials/*.tres` (shader, textures, priority and the look's own values), `ground_look.tres` (`Map3DGroundLook`), `weather_look.tres` (`Map3DWeatherLook`), `world_environment.tres` (`Map3DSky`), the HUD pieces' and layers' exports in the scenes. Layers set only what comes from the pack, the camera and the window's scale (`tests/map3d/test_map3d_editor_owned_look.gd`). 512 px stand-ins in `assets/3D/terrain/textures_preview/` draw a checkout whose packs are missing.
- **A build holds DATA only** (`assets/3D/terrain/<map_id>/`, COMMITTED but the bake's three raw intermediates): the Terrain3D, the root's `map3d_world` meta, `rivers.json`, the bake's fields, the baked layers and `manifest.json`. Sun, sky and sea belong to the authored scene; `Map3DWorldLoader` strips them from an older build. `Map3DWorldLoader.status` reads the manifest first so the notice says honestly `missing` / `stale` / `bad` (and `legacy`, which still loads) and names `build_pack.sh`; a missing optional input warns once (`warn_once`).
- **`Map3DWorldCache` keeps the one built world across a screen switch** (loading costs seconds). **Terrain3D's COLLISION IS OFF**: a collision body outliving its old view's physics space crashed the engine on the swap, so picking is ray-vs-heightmap. Turning collision back on must be deliberate and solve that lifetime.
- **Baked layers**: rivers, roads, trails and bridges are baked by `bake_layers.gd` (one mesh set per distinct step set of `graphics_quality.csv`) and used only when every input matches. The key cannot see the geometry CODE: changing any script in `Map3DLayerBake.GEOMETRY_SOURCES` fails `test_map3d_layer_bake.gd` until you bump `FORMAT`, update `GEOMETRY_MD5` and re-run `bake_layers.gd`. A stale `layers/` warns once and is rebuilt at load.
- **Graphics tiers** are `data/graphics_quality.csv` + `Map3DQuality` (render scale, MSAA, aniso, shadows, clouds, Terrain3D mesh, line/road/river step multipliers, HUD Hz, tree density, house budget, weather particles, the bench gate), detected from the GPU on a first run and cycled by the pause menu into `user://settings.cfg`. The view renders into its SubViewport at window px × the tier's `render_scale`, so **every projection, ray and screen-px uniform goes through `ctx.viewport_px_scale()`** — never the window scale alone.
- The view renders into a SubViewport at WINDOW resolution shown by a TextureRect — never a SubViewportContainer (under `canvas_items` stretch it renders at design size and blurs). It loads on first use and renders only while shown. `GameRoot._sync_map_view` is the one rule: the 3D view on every screen, handed every theme; `balance.csv` `map3d_default` 0 holds it unloaded, which the suite does through `tests/pre_run.gd` (headless cannot render Terrain3D) and `test_map3d_tab.gd` lifts (`force_map3d_scene`).

## Camera and measure

- Zoom runs `map3d_zoom_min_m` to `map3d_zoom_max_m` (camera distance in world metres); the wheel and a trackpad pinch zoom about the POINTER; a two-finger drag pans. It opens on the middle of the province the player controls (`MapManors.home_province`) with the scale bar spanning `map3d_zoom_start_scale` of the pack's measure (`Map3DGeometry.distance_for_bar`) — the owner names zooms by the scale bar. Maths: `Map3DGeometry` (pure, tested).
- **The camera never sinks into the ground**: `Map3DGeometry.clear_pitch` steepens the pitch until the whole sight line stands `map3d_camera_clear_m` over the ground.
- **Every distance shown reads the pack's measure**: world metres back to pack px (`Map3DView.map_units_per_metre`) times `map_info.csv` `distance_scale` in `distance_unit`; the `map3d_*_m` camera values stay in built metres. The scale bar restates itself in the 1-2-5 ladder (`MapGeometry.nice_step`: "200 km", never "187 km").
- The compass (`CompassLayer`, a child of the view drawing `assets/2D/ui/compass/compass.png`, the vector rose without the art) turns with the view, turns clockwise with the clock while time runs, redraws only itself, and eases back to north on a click; on the war table the table's compass does it and the map draws none.

## Picking, hover, selection

- **A click is the old 2D map's click**: a settlement within `map3d_pick_px` (or on its ring) sends `object_selected`, anywhere else `province_selected` (the province index bitmap under the ray's ground hit, `Map3DGeometry.ground_hit`); `MapDetailPanel.follow` takes either. Under a theme a click on land picks the manor. `map3d_picker.gd`'s `pick_at` orders: a regiment piece first (`Map3DLayerUnits.screen_rects`), a settlement within reach beats a landmark mark, a mark's own box beats its grown rect; picking never depends on a dot being drawn.
- **Only a clicked object is ringed**: hover names it (and swaps the left column via `country_hovered` → `CountryPanel.follow`) and draws the hovered kingdom's live outline wider in the border's ink (`lines/hover.tres`), on its land runs only, like the border; a clicked province is its own seam emphasised (`lines/selection_province.tres`), a clicked manor gold (`lines/selection_manor.tres`). A pointer move is picked once a frame (`hover_pick`); `Map3DView.kingdom_of_pick` names the holder NOW.

## Lines and water

- **Every ribbon's look is its own `.tres` in `scenes/map3d/materials/lines/`** (`border_line.gdshader`: colours, widths, dash, gap in design px, lift, soft edge); `Map3DLayerLines.set_lines(name, lines, look, priority, colours)` scales px to the SubViewport and orders it (`Map3DPriority`). Ribbons are resampled every `map3d_line_step_m` onto the relief and widened by camera distance for a constant SCREEN width.
- **Borders** (`Map3DBorders`) are the live `MapOwnership` outlines and seams, rebuilt when the ownership signature turns. A kingdom border is three ribbons (an implementer's ruling, not yet confirmed by the owner): a REALM BAND inside each outline in the kingdom's colour (one one-sided mesh, colour per vertex, rings turned by `Map3DBorders.inward_rings`; never code the side in NORMAL's length — Godot's octahedral normals discard it), a pale halo and a warm-ink line. Outlines are centripetally smoothed with junctions pinned; seams are chained into continuous dashes. Ribbons stack band < seams < halo < kingdom ink < hover < selection (`Map3DPriority`). Kingdom ink, halo and band keep to LAND borders (`Map3DBorders.land_runs`, `map3d_border_skip_coast`).
- **Roads are worn tracks, never ruled lines** (`road_line.gdshader`, `road_line.tres` / `trail_line.tres`): width in metres drawn wider than life (`map3d_road_width_m`) with a screen-px floor, a stone texture laid along the road, fading to the look's colour far out, sides frayed by the texture's height.
- **Bridges** (`Map3DBridges`, pure, tested): where a road crosses a drawn river, a span of the river's width plus abutments, the road straightened and carried on the deck's heights, ONE mesh of every bridge (arches, piers, abutments, parapets) under the world's `Bridges` node — not a visibility range, which is measured to the world-spanning mesh's centre. Geometry: `map3d_bridge_*`.
- **Falls**: where a river's course drops steeper than `fall_grade_from` (rise over run, `water_river.gdshader`), up close it turns to white water and narrows (`fall_*`), so a cliff drop is a cascade, not a blue sheet.
- **Rivers** are carved into the heightmap by the bake and written source → mouth to `rivers.json`, clipped to the land at both ends, drawn as one ribbon (`Map3DRivers`, `water_river.gdshader`); a true mouth runs on past its last sample into the sea (`map3d_river_mouth_extend_w` local widths), so it never stops as a stub on the sand. **A river's colour belongs to the sea**: `water_river.tres` reads what it shares (mouth colour, sky, shallows, ripple LOD) from `water_sea.tres` and turns natural with the sea's share (`Map3DContext.sea_natural`); over its last stretch (`mouth_sea_from` of the mouth ramp) it BECOMES the sea — colour, light, ripple, opacity computed by the sea's own code (`water_sea.gdshaderinc`, the sea's uniforms copied by `Map3DLayerRivers.SEA_WATER`) — and hands over at the sea's own shore fade, so a river and its mouth read as one water. The ribbon is wound CLOCKWISE seen from above (Godot's front face; a test holds it): wound the other way the double-sided water was lit from below.
- **The sea** (`Map3DLayerSea`, `water_sea.gdshader`) is natural water near and **four flat bands by distance from the shore** far (scheme C, `world_sea_distance.png`), rippling, clear at the shore; it sizes its plane from the world meta.

## Ground

- **The pack's cells ARE the ground** — there is no map render PNG. The ground follows `data/map3d_bake.csv`: coast coverage, warped biome edges, a dithered blend byte, escarpments on the high ground (`terrace_*`), a moisture hue (`moist_*` / `dry_*`), scree on the uplands (`rock_*`), a snow line broken by noise (`snow_*`). Aerial terrain layers (grass, forest, rocky, cliff, snow, crop) are packed by `tools/map3d/pack_aerial.py`, which also writes their VRAM `.import` (a layer in another format greys the whole ground). The colour map can only DARKEN, which is why crops wear their own pale `aerial_crop` layer. Terrain3D 1.0.2 always height-blends (each texture's alpha is its height); the control map's per-vertex UV angle / scale bits are unused (`bake_world.py` writes base, overlay and blend only).
- **The grass is DUAL-SCALED far out** (`aerial_meadow` eases to a larger tile with camera distance; the cliff keeps its approved size by its TILE). `build_terrain.gd`'s `apply_material` must turn `dual_scaling` on BEFORE writing the shader parameters, or `dual_scale_*` / `tri_scale_reduction` save as null and the shader runs its defaults (`tests/map3d/test_map3d_build_material.gd`); a uniform's `hint_range` binds only the inspector. `build_world.gd --material-only` re-applies the material and tiles to a committed build without the raw inputs.
- **Open fields** round each settlement (`bake_world.sow_patchwork`, `data/map3d_farm_zones.csv`, `data/map3d_fields.csv`): crofts, two or three great fields in the rotation split into furlongs (`bake_world.jitter_cells`, each decided at its seed), meadow by the water, pasture to the edge of reach; balks between furlongs. `bake/fields.png` carries each field's colour and strip direction; the patched terrain shader draws the strips and furrows near and fades the crops far (the ground look's values).
- **Terrain3D shader patches** (`Map3DGroundTint.patch` rewrites the ONE `ALBEDO` line of the shader Terrain3D generates, read at load through `shader_override_enabled`, never committed; a test holds the line to exist): the HEIGHTS heat ramp, the MANORS tint, the woods' canopy, the weather and the wind stages. Terrain3D drops unknown uniforms after a load, so a layer puts them back when they read unset. Terrain3D's `mipmap_bias` is a DERIVATIVE MULTIPLIER (1.0 neutral), not a mip offset.
- **HEIGHTS**: the ground mixes toward the heat ramp by world height (stops passed as raw sRGB, blended in sRGB, turned linear in the shader); landmarks are drawn ▲ / ▽ with "N m" (the cell's metres, the engine's measure), thinned by camera distance and kept off dots, counters, legend and each other; the legend names the supply tiers.
- **MANORS** colours each manor by its house (`ManorPalette`, pure; a 128-wide LUT keyed by the cell id from `world_cell_index.png` via `texelFetch`, sRGB bytes turned linear before the mix; muted to a tincture by `map_manor_saturation` / `_value`; its EDGE smooth — the four texels round the point weighed bilinearly per id and the line where the two heaviest meet anti-aliased over a screen px by `fwidth`, so the cell staircase reads as the curve it stands for), cell edges a `ManorEdges` ribbon (`lines/manor_edge.tres`), and carries the output badges (`ManorBadges`: chief good's icon ringed in its category colour, `MapObjects.burg_visible` LOD, a smaller producer's badge dropped on overlap). MANORS also stands each manor's PEOPLE and WEALTH as two bars (`Map3DHudManorBars`, the Hud's ManorBars node, its look the node's exports; figures from `ManorBars`, `scripts/engine/CLAUDE.md`): each against its kind's most among the rows, ink-edged; a scouted foreign manor's hatched inside a dashed intel-violet edge with a whisker over the report's range; a pair steps right of a badge and out from under the counters; a key bottom-left; hover adds the figures (`ManorBars.words_of`). The estate drawings are never drawn on the 3D map. HEIGHTS and MANORS are mutually exclusive themes, and **no trees are drawn under either** (`Map3DView.set_manor_theme` hides the Trees layer: the theme's message is the ground's colour).
- **Woods** (the old "no trees" ruling lifted for the 3D map; the look (an implementer's ruling, not yet confirmed by the owner)): clumps planted by `tools/map3d/bake_trees.py` (`data/map3d_trees.csv`), drawn by `Map3DLayerTrees` as MultiMeshes per 2 km square × kind × variant × LOD, built once and culled by visibility ranges; every crown a camera-facing PHOTO CARD cut from `assets/3D/vegetation/crowns/crowns_atlas.png` (`tree_card.gdshader`, `tree_card.tres`; `map3d_tree_cards` 0 draws the 3D crowns); past them the ground's canopy tint carries the wood.
- **Sky occlusion** (`Map3DGroundRelief`, spliced after the wind stage): `bake/relief_ao.png` (`tools/map3d/bake_relief_ao.py`) darkens valleys and terrace feet by `ground_look.tres` `relief_ao` / `relief_ao_gamma` — the light a high sun cannot give the relief.
- **Wind** (`Map3DLayerWind`, its `deg` the one wind the clouds drift and the rain slants down; it advances only while the clock runs): thin crests across the wind over sown fields and open grass (`Map3DGroundWind`), turning the grass's pale side up — lighter and warm, never grey; crowns lean down the wind (`tree_sway.gdshader`).
- **March trails** (`Map3DLayerTrails`, presentation only, never saved): each walking piece leaves a ribbon stamped with the game day and faded over game time — trodden grass LIGHTER than the turf, beaten corn DARKER (read off the field map).

## Sky, sun, clouds, weather

- **The sun turns once round each game day** (`Map3DSun`, swept by `Game.day_progress`; presentation only): from the west, high in the south, east, low through the north, never jumping at the day's turn; it rides HIGH and its shadows are a light wash. The north half is DUSK (`Map3DSun.dusk_of`): a dimmer, warmer sun, faded shadows and glint, and the shadowless `FillLight` opposite the sun raised — the fill, not the ambient, keeps backlit scarps readable. `day_sweep` off holds it; `preview_day_share` sets an hour in the editor.
- **Environment**: `world_environment.tres` (`Map3DSky`: authored SSAO / SSIL / glow a tier can only take away, depth haze and SSAO radius following the camera); `Map3DEnvironment` keeps only what the tier and camera do. Emissive and unshaded colours are fitted to the tonemap (`map3d_*_emission_gamma`); a `Color` set from GDScript passes straight through — never `srgb_to_linear()` it.
- **Clouds** belong to the MAP, not the view (`Map3DLayerClouds._sky_rect` is the whole pack): `Map3DCloudField` (pure) spreads them by Mitchell's best candidate, wraps one blown off the downwind edge back upwind, nudged by overcast (`weight_fn`); `cloud_deck.gdshader` drifts with the clock. Their soft shadows are a SEPARATE `cloud_shadow.gdshader` — a shader that mentions `ALPHA_SCISSOR_THRESHOLD` anywhere pushes the material into an alpha-scissor pipeline and silently blanks its blended rendering, so the two never share a file.
- **Weather is presentation only** (`Weather` is read, never written; spec `2026-09-29-weather-visuals-design.md`): `data/weather.csv` gives each kind seven 0..1 look columns; `Map3DWeatherField` (pure) lays each land texel in its province's look as a CELL round the province's centre (patches with clear country between), softened over land only; `Map3DLayerWeather` hands this spell's field and the next's to the terrain shader and blends them over the spell's last days. Overcast dims last, over the snow too; a blizzard is veiled in haze; a wood keeps its dark under snow (`weather_look.tres` `snow_wood`); houses take snow on top and rain on walls (`house_surface.gdshader`); clouds turn storm-grey from heavy overcast; rain and snow are world-space particles (`Map3DLayerPrecip`, `precip_*.gdshader`) born round the camera's target, kept where the field's fall texture says, falling only while the clock runs and nearer than `fall_far_view_m`. `map3d_weather_look` 0 turns it off.

## Settlements and houses

- **Settlements are real house clusters on the ground**, placed by the bake (`tools/map3d/bake_houses.py`, from `sow_patchwork` where the land mask, population and footprint live, so "never in the sea" is structural and a test asserts it): lanes grown from the centre and houses packed along them — compact and messy, medieval, with negative space, never an even scatter; the landmark building at the centre on a levelled terrace with an open square ringed by houses facing it; every group's count within 5:1. `bake/houses.csv` holds world metres with no `y`.
- `Map3DHouses` / `Map3DLayerHouses` draw one MultiMesh per house TYPE, cut once per camera pose, nothing past `map3d_house_view_m`, capped by the tier's `house_budget`.
- **A house stands UPRIGHT on the HIGHEST ground under its footprint and nothing of it is ever below the ground**: `Map3DHouses.ground_under` samples a grid over the whole mesh AABB, `seat` is its highest plus a hair; every mesh carries its own stone footing traced from its walls (never a box pedestal); the bake keeps houses off ground that would need a tall one (`house_max_drop_m`, `house_landmark_max_drop_m`).
- **Houses are drawn at TWICE life size** so a settlement reads as houses, not gravel; `width_m` / `depth_m` / `height_m` in `data/map3d_houses.csv` are MEASURED from the built meshes (`scale_houses.py`), and the packer reserves each building's real rectangle. A settlement's tier reads from what stands at its centre and townhouses against cottages, never a tenfold range in roof count.
- **Walls, piers and ships**: a settlement whose `features` name `walls` is ringed by a CLOSED wall — its line kept back from the sea, a river's near bank and a cliff's edge (`wall_shore_m`, `wall_river_pull_m`, `wall_max_slope`), never a module left out — with gates where roads cross, a water gate where it crosses a river and a quay gate at its pier, and towers only at sharp turns and every few hundred metres, spaced (`wall_tower_*`). How each type stands is its table's `seat` (`Map3DHouses.seat_of`): a house its highest ground; a wall module `slope` — on its centre's ground, SHEARED along its length onto the ground's slope between its ends so the wall runs on continuously over the hill (owner 2026-10-04: 应当就是连续的根据地形铺在地面上), its own masonry running 8 m down, never a house footing; a tower or gate `ground`; a pier `sea` (deck `map3d_pier_deck_m` over it); a ship `water` (its waterline on the sea, rocking on the swell in `house_surface.gdshader` `bob_*`, set in the ship's scene). A port's pier stands on the shore NEAREST ITS HOUSES — the timber `pier` off a low shore, the `pier_cliff` (its own rock, stair and castle, at its own proportions) off a capital's cliff, the small landing `pier_stair` (a jetty and a stair up its rock to a gate) off the cliff of a town on a cliff top (`pier_stair_town_rise_m`), each walked out to sea until no taller cliff buries its head (`pier_cliff_*`, `pier_stair_*`, `bake_houses._HEADS`) — and 1-2 ships by its size lie alongside it (`ship_*`).
- **Each house type is a SCENE**: `scenes/models/buildings/<type>.tscn` inherits its `.glb` and gives every surface its `house_surface.gdshader` material in the editor — texture, roughness, vertex colour, normal map and `role` (1 wall, 2 tile roof, 0 untinted) — so opened there it is the house as the map draws it. `house_models.tres` names the scenes, `Map3DAssetCache.house_model` reads their mesh and materials, and `Map3DHouseMaterials.tinted` only COPIES them, adding the Houses node's tints (a material with no tints, `wall_count` 0, draws as built) and the weather — still one MultiMesh per type, never a node per house. The textures are the files the glTF importer extracts beside each `.glb` (`gltf/embedded_image_handling=1`), imported as Basis Universal (`compress/mode=4`) and COMMITTED; `test_map3d_house_scenes.gd` holds all of it.
- Each house wears one of the Houses node's `wall_tints` / `roof_tints` (`house_surface.gdshader`, instance custom data, `Map3DHouses.variant_of`). Roofs are tile or slate in varied colours — never thatch, never a dark brown tile. **No base plates** on any house mesh. Building style is SIMPLE REALISM, never toy. How the meshes are made: `tools/CLAUDE.md`.

## Regiment pieces, couriers, HUD

- **A regiment of the player's realm is a 3D wargame piece** (`Map3DLayerUnits`, the scene's `Units` node, + the pure `Map3DUnits`): instanced SCENES from `assets/3D/units/army_models.tres` (an `ArtTable`) — `scenes/models/units/<model>.tscn`, each inheriting its `.glb` with an editable copy of its material and its shadow set there (owner 2026-10-04: 把兵人模型也改成场景; the WAR ROOM's tin reads that material, `WarPieces`; the clips' loops stay the `.glb`'s import settings; `test_map3d_unit_scenes.gd`) — the one layer that is not a MultiMesh, since each figure is posed by its own `AnimationPlayer`. A fleet keeps the flat counter (`ArmyCounters`: a dark square with a glyph — swords, a crown for `MapObjects.royal_regiment`, waves for a fleet — joined to a box in the realm's hue stating the headcount). DEV MODE shows every realm's regiments.
- **Its size is the camera's, not the model's** (`Map3DUnits.height_m`): one world height at every zoom (`map3d_unit_ref_px` of screen at the opening view's distance), so it grows and shrinks with the ground; once it would be drawn under `map3d_unit_mark_px` no piece is drawn and every regiment is its flat counter, still clickable (`Map3DUnits.as_counters`). The headcount chip hangs over the HEADS (`Map3DUnitRig.head_m`), not the model box's top.
- **Formation by strength**: nine / six / three figures by `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (`data/army_formations.csv`), laid out by `Map3DFormation` in units of a figure's footprint × the Units node's `formation_gap`, ranks `army_art.csv` `rank_depth` apart; resting figures out of phase, a column marches in step; rebuilt when the strength crosses a tier.
- **Seated under its whole footprint** (`Map3DUnits.footprint` / `seat`): tilted with the slope up to `map3d_unit_tilt_max_deg`, steeper lifted, raised until no sample stands above it plus `map3d_unit_seat_clear`. It faces its march, turns square to the camera standing, casts its shadow, and has no base.
- **The ORDER picks the clip**: MARCH / GARRISON / STANDBY (`Map3DLayerUnits._sync_action`, `Map3DUnits.clip_named`; a model with no STANDBY stands by in GARRISON). The MARCH clip keeps pace with the ground (`Map3DUnitRig.march_rate`, timed from the day's PLAN, never a frame's own step; held within `map3d_unit_anim_min` / `_max`; `army_art.csv` `stride_m`).
- **The piece walks the engine's NEXT day as the day runs** (`Map3DLayerUnits.step`, `CourierSystem.day_ahead_of` on a copy, `Map3DUnits.along_day` by `Game.day_progress`; more than `map3d_unit_snap_days` away it jumps). The road ahead is a dashed line DRAPED on the ground from where the piece is drawn. `Map3DView.unit_pack_positions()` gives where it draws each piece now (the WAR ROOM reads it).
- **Orders from the map**: a left or right click on a piece raises `army_menu_requested` (`ArmyOrderMenu`, rows `data/army_orders.csv`; a right click on open country is not consumed). MARCH only on a PAUSED clock (greyed with its reason while time runs; an aim withdrawn the moment the clock starts) arms the AIM (`Map3DView.aiming`): a measuring ruler draped on the ground from the piece to the pointer in the realm's colour, with the distance and ONE arrival day; the next left click holds the destination (`aim_dest`) and asks COMMIT / CANCEL (`data/army_route_orders.csv`), only COMMIT runs `ARMY_MARCH`; Escape or a refusal withdraws. A committed march's end ring is clickable (`Map3DHudArmies.route_ends`): Edit route… or Cancel march.
- **Two bars over every piece or counter**: red readiness, yellow stores (`ArmySystem.bars_of`, drawn by `ArmyCounters` in the regiment's realm colour). `ArmyCounters.layout`'s `with_piece` drops the counter's icon for a regiment that has a piece, so it is never on the map twice.
- **Couriers** (`Map3DLayerCouriers`) ride the engine's next day as it runs (`CourierSystem.ride_ahead`), reach the column as the day ends and FADE OUT there as a ghost the moment they deliver (`Map3DCourierRide.ghost` / `fade_ghosts`, only when his last ride delivered); their roads and pending routes are `Map3DHudCouriers`; a click opens `CourierCard`. The map draws no weather icons — the weather is on the ground itself.
- **HUD** (`Map3DHud`, one `map3d_hud_*.gd` per job, shared pieces from `scripts/ui/`: `ScaleBar`, `MapLettering`, `ManorBadges`, `ArmyCounters`, `MapRuler`): kingdom names upright over each kingdom's live `MapOwnership.label_anchor`, sized as a word on the ground seen from its distance (`Map3DGeometry.label_px`), lettered whole (`Map3DHudLabels.inward`: a word the view's edge would cut slides inside it); settlement rings per `data/map3d_settlements.csv`. ## Performance gates

- 60 fps PER TIER, measured by `tools/map3d/bench.gd` (`res://scenes/tools/map3d/bench.tscn`): three zooms, median/mean/p95 against the tier's `bench_budget_ms` at its `bench_size` (a warning at any other size), exit 3 over it; `--quality`, `--render-scale`, `--out` (only under `tools/map3d/baselines/` or a scratch path, or Godot imports the CSV), `--layer`, `--weather`, `--frame-cap`, `--tier column=value`, `--bal key=value`. **A row is STILL-camera unless `--pan`**: a still camera pays every per-pose cut on its first frame only, so `--pan` is the honest number for a drag.
- Under Forward+ macOS holds a windowed Metal app at the display refresh and Metal gives no GPU timestamps, so no row reads under the refresh interval; the README's Forward+ table has the current budgets and readings.
