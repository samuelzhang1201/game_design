# Lines and water

- **Every ribbon's look is its own `.tres` in `scenes/map3d/materials/lines/`** (`border_line.gdshader`: colours, widths, dash, gap in design px, lift, soft edge); `Map3DLayerLines.set_lines(name, lines, look, priority, colours)` scales px to the SubViewport and orders it (`Map3DPriority`). Ribbons are resampled every `map3d_line_step_m` onto the relief and widened by camera distance for a constant SCREEN width.
- **Borders** (`Map3DBorders`) are the live `MapOwnership` outlines and seams, rebuilt when the ownership signature turns. A kingdom border is three ribbons (an implementer's ruling, not yet confirmed by the owner): a REALM BAND inside each outline in the kingdom's colour (one one-sided mesh, colour per vertex, rings turned by `Map3DBorders.inward_rings`; never code the side in NORMAL's length — Godot's octahedral normals discard it), a pale halo and a warm-ink line. Outlines are centripetally smoothed with junctions pinned; seams are chained into continuous dashes. Ribbons stack band < seams < halo < kingdom ink < hover < selection (`Map3DPriority`). Kingdom ink, halo and band keep to LAND borders (`Map3DBorders.land_runs`, `map3d_border_skip_coast`).
- **Roads are worn tracks, never ruled lines** (`road_line.gdshader`, `road_line.tres` / `trail_line.tres`): width in metres drawn wider than life (`map3d_road_width_m`) with a screen-px floor, a stone texture laid along the road, fading to the look's colour far out, sides frayed by the texture's height, and a worn verge past them (`shoulder` × the width, trodden earth from `shoulder_alpha` at the road's edge to nothing) so the ground grades into the road.
- **Bridges** (`Map3DBridges`, pure, tested): where a road crosses a drawn river, a span of the river's width plus abutments, the road straightened and carried on the deck's heights, ONE mesh of every bridge (arches, piers, abutments, parapets) under the world's `Bridges` node — not a visibility range, which is measured to the world-spanning mesh's centre. Geometry: `map3d_bridge_*`.
- **Falls**: where a river's course drops steeper than `fall_grade_from` (rise over run, `water_river.gdshader`), up close it narrows and runs down in streaks of broken water (`fall_*`), so a cliff drop is a cascade, not a blue sheet. No bridge joins a cliff top to its foot (`map3d_bridge_max_drop_m`); a true mouth grades down to the sea through a cliff (`river_mouth_grade`); a road is cut over the sea (`Map3DLayerRoads.dry_runs`).
- **Rivers** are carved into the heightmap by the bake and written source → mouth to `rivers.json`, clipped to the land at both ends, drawn as one ribbon wound CLOCKWISE from above (`Map3DRivers`, `water_river.gdshader`; the other way lights it from below); a true mouth runs on past its last sample into the sea (`map3d_river_mouth_extend_w`) and, away from a port, opens into a wandering funnel (`map3d_bake.csv` `river_estuary_*`). Far out its width floor grows with it (`floor_*`), a sub-px thread fading to `thin_alpha_min`, never gone; it takes the depth haze. **A river's colour belongs to the sea**: `water_river.tres` reads what it shares from `water_sea.tres` and turns natural with the sea's share (`Map3DContext.sea_natural`); over its last stretch (`mouth_sea_from`) it BECOMES the sea's own water, computed by the sea's code (`water_sea.gdshaderinc`, uniforms copied by `Map3DLayerRivers.SEA_WATER`), with no handover at the waterline. A look the sea gains belongs in the include, or the river's copy drifts from it.
- **The sea** (`Map3DLayerSea`, `water_sea.gdshader`) is natural water near and **four flat bands by distance from the shore** far (scheme C, `world_sea_distance.png`), rippling, clear at the shore; it sizes its plane from the world meta. Near the coast and up close (`shore_reach_px`, `shore_depth_far_m`) it is also drawn wherever the ground (the depth buffer) lies under its surface, so the waterline follows the terrain, not the 50 m raster (square notches beside a river's mouth). Its colours read the shore distance smoothed over a ring of taps (`smooth_px`, no contour stairs); up close its light follows the ripples (`nat_ripple_gain`) and wind lanes drift over it (`nat_sheen`, near only). Up close a greener shelf lies over the shallows (`nat_shelf_*`), gone far out (a pale ring round every coast).
