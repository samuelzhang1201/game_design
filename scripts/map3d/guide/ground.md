# Ground

- **The pack's cells ARE the ground** — there is no map render PNG. The ground follows `data/screens/map3d/map3d_bake.csv`: coast coverage, warped biome edges, a dithered blend byte, escarpments on the high ground (`terrace_*`), a moisture hue (`moist_*` / `dry_*`), scree on the uplands (`rock_*`), a snow line broken by noise (`snow_*`). Aerial terrain layers (grass, forest, rocky, cliff, snow, crop) are packed by `tools/map3d/pack_aerial.py`, which also writes their VRAM `.import` (a layer in another format greys the whole ground). The colour map can only DARKEN, which is why crops wear their own pale `aerial_crop` layer. Terrain3D 1.0.2 always height-blends (each texture's alpha is its height); the control map's per-vertex UV angle / scale bits are unused (`bake_world.py` writes base, overlay and blend only).
- **Landform** (spec `2026-10-13-landform-rework-design.md`): the ridged noise is `map3d_bake.csv` `relief_ridge_*`. A river's ridge and terrace fade narrows to `river_head_share` over its first `river_head_m`, so a valley starts in a notch. `bake_world.erode` cuts stream-power gullies and spurs on an `erosion_cell_m` grid (`erosion_*`, a soft cap at `erosion_max_cut_m`) after the terraces and before the rivers carve. The bank's `river_bank_profile` / `_slope_jitter` exist but stay 1 / 0: jittered banks read as walls.
- **Variant sets** (spec `2026-10-12-ground-detail-textures-design.md`): the grass and the crops wear several low-feature pictures (`g2_*`), `bake_world.split_variants` moving each class's weight between them on slow noise (`map3d_bake.csv` `variant_*`, `crop_variant_wave_m`) and the grass on high ground to the upland turfs — never between classes, so the colour map and the far view stay. Only `g2_meadow_a` is dual-scaled.
- **The grass is DUAL-SCALED far out** (`aerial_meadow` eases to a larger tile with camera distance; the cliff keeps its approved size by its TILE). `build_terrain.gd`'s `apply_material` must turn `dual_scaling` on BEFORE writing the shader parameters, or `dual_scale_*` / `tri_scale_reduction` save as null and the shader runs its defaults (`tests/map3d/test_map3d_build_material.gd`); a uniform's `hint_range` binds only the inspector. `build_world.gd --material-only` re-applies the material and tiles to a committed build without the raw inputs. Each layer's detiling is `TEXTURES`' rotation and shift (the ground layers shift 0.5; rotation stays small — doubled, neighbouring cells' crop rows disagree in a CG checker, spec `2026-10-12-ground-detail-textures-design.md`).
- **Open fields** round each settlement (`bake_world.sow_patchwork`, `data/screens/map3d/map3d_farm_zones.csv`, `data/screens/map3d/map3d_fields.csv`): crofts, two or three great fields in the rotation split into furlongs (`bake_world.jitter_cells`, each decided at its seed), meadow by the water, pasture to the edge of reach. `bake/fields.png` carries each field's colour and strip direction (the arable and the crofts' corn plots, each plot's strips along its close); the patched terrain shader draws the strips and fine furrow lines near (`Map3DGroundStrips`: the pattern drawn for each of the four 40 m texels round a pixel and blended — blending the DIRECTION drew arcs, the nearest texel 40 m steps) and fades the crops far (the ground look's values). Furlong blocks each keep their own direction: one grain per region, or strips down the fall line, run neighbours into sheets that the opening zoom cannot read (spec `2026-10-13-open-field-layout-design.md`). A grass field's colour answers its ground (`bake_world.grass_interior`, `map3d_bake.csv` `grass_*`): hollows and the wet band darker and greener, rises warmer, the pasture and crofts by the village browner, a soft field-scale cloud — each term only darkens or turns a channel down, never lighter. The fields stand in CLEARINGS: `bake/settled.png` (crofts, fields, meadow 1, pasture `pasture_open`) thins the woods before their edge squeeze (`map3d_trees.csv` `settled_clear`), and within reach the forest floor turns to grass (`settled_grass`). **Hedges stand where history put them** (spec `2026-10-13-open-field-layout-design.md`, owner 2026-10-13): round each township's in-field (open field, meadows, crofts as one closed whole) and round its crofts, along the lanes outside the in-field; never between furlongs, the shared meadows open. `bake_world.hedge_map` writes `bake/hedge_lines.png` (R the in-field, G the crofts: SIGNED metres per level, blurred; a hedge is the least |distance| AFTER each channel's sample, so no false line midway between two), and `bake_trees.hedge_line_points` plants a CONTINUOUS row along each zero contour every `hedge_line_spacing_m` (`hedgerow` habit: `hedge_bush`, its crowns laid in a row along the line, `map3d_tree_species.csv` `row`), gaps from `hedge_gap_cut`, a hedge tree every `hedge_tree_every_m`, the row's cards ending at their species' `far_m` (faded over half of it; Godot fades AFTER the end, to a chunk's middle), hedges kept out of `woods.png`'s far canopy (`hedgerow_canopy`: smeared over 40 m texels they drew ink lines round the fields at 3–5 km), tested at its own spot (`hedge_max_slope`, `hedge_house_clear_m`, `hedge_road_clear_*`). Crofts are decided per CLOSE (`field_cells` centres), so no hedge is a circle. Bankside rows run along every river (`riparian_*`).
- **Ground edges per pixel** (`Map3DGroundEdge`): Terrain3D blends between 20 m vertices; the patch bends the fragment's vertex coordinates by a fine noise before the lookups (the ground look's `edge_warp_*`), never the geometry; Terrain3D's `blend_sharpness` is low (`build_terrain.gd`) so the change between two vertices is a soft cross-fade. The fields' grass balks are LINES (`Map3DGroundBalk`) along `bake/field_edges.png` (a field's signed metres to its edge, per vertex), the ground look's `balk_*`; a hedge line on the ground is the look's `hedge_*` over `hedge_lines.png` (0 off: the bushes and their shadows carry the hedge — a band under a gap read as a painted path; the map is not loaded while off); where a cell straddles a field's edge Terrain3D's `accumulate_material` reruns with the corners weighted by the pixel's coverage (`field_rim`): no 20 m steps.
- **Terrain3D shader patches** (`Map3DGroundTint.patch` rewrites the ONE `ALBEDO` line of the shader Terrain3D generates, read at load through `shader_override_enabled`, never committed; a test holds the line to exist): the HEIGHTS heat ramp, the MANORS tint, the woods' canopy, the weather and the wind stages. Terrain3D drops unknown uniforms after a load, so a layer puts them back when they read unset. Terrain3D's `mipmap_bias` is a DERIVATIVE MULTIPLIER (1.0 neutral), not a mip offset.
- **HEIGHTS**: the ground mixes toward the heat ramp by world height (stops passed as raw sRGB, blended in sRGB, turned linear in the shader); landmarks are drawn ▲ / ▽ with "N m" (the cell's metres, the engine's measure), thinned by camera distance and kept off dots, counters, legend and each other; the legend names the supply tiers.
- **MANORS** colours each manor by its house (`ManorPalette`, pure; a 128-wide LUT keyed by the cell id from `world_cell_index.png` via `texelFetch`, sRGB bytes turned linear before the mix; muted to a tincture by `map_manor_saturation` / `_value`; its EDGE smooth — the four texels round the point weighed bilinearly per id and the line where the two heaviest meet anti-aliased over a screen px by `fwidth`, so the cell staircase reads as the curve it stands for), cell edges a `ManorEdges` ribbon (`lines/manor_edge.tres`), and carries the output badges (`ManorBadges`: chief good's icon ringed in its category colour, `MapObjects.burg_visible` LOD, a smaller producer's badge dropped on overlap). MANORS also stands each manor's PEOPLE and WEALTH as two bars (`Map3DHudManorBars`, the Hud's ManorBars node, its look the node's exports; figures from `ManorBars`, `scripts/engine/CLAUDE.md`): each against its kind's most among the rows, ink-edged; a scouted foreign manor's hatched inside a dashed intel-violet edge with a whisker over the report's range; a pair steps right of a badge and out from under the counters; a key bottom-left; hover adds the figures (`ManorBars.words_of`). The estate drawings are never drawn on the 3D map. HEIGHTS and MANORS are mutually exclusive themes, and **no trees are drawn under either** (`Map3DView.set_manor_theme` hides the Trees layer: the theme's message is the ground's colour).
- **Woods** (an implementer's look, not yet the owner's): clumps planted by `tools/map3d/bake_trees.py` (`data/screens/map3d/map3d_trees.csv`) on a seeded blue-noise (Poisson-disk) set no two nearer than `poisson_r_m`, kept by the woodedness chance (edge squeeze, capped by `wood_cap`, opened by `glade_*`) — woods with clearings, never a carpet. Each clump is a SPECIES, a row of `data/screens/map3d/map3d_tree_species.csv` (the `kind` column of `trees.bin`), picked by `bake_trees.pick_species` from the ground under it: its habit (wood, lone, hedge, bank), the biome shares, its height against the tree line (`h_gain`) and a slow noise per species so a mixed wood holds patches of each; a row with `enabled` 0 waits for its atlas art and is never planted. `Map3DLayerTrees` draws MultiMeshes per 2 km square × species × variant (`map3d_tree_variants` layouts each, crowns / spread / size / tint range from the species row); every crown a camera-facing PHOTO CARD cut from `assets/3D/vegetation/crowns/crowns_atlas.png` (`tree_card.gdshader`, `tree_card.tres`; `map3d_tree_cards` 0 draws the 3D crowns). A lower `tree_density` thins EVENLY by place (`Map3DGeometry.thin_rank`), never in random holes.
- **Height gates (LOD)**: the cards are drawn whole out to `map3d_tree_card_far_m`, then the same clumps thinned evenly to `map3d_tree_card_thin` out to `map3d_tree_card_thin_far_m` (each switch faded over `map3d_tree_card_fade_m`), and past `map3d_lod_trees_m` of camera distance the whole wood is hidden (its `Woods` holder, so the theme's own hiding of the layer stays separate); the ground's canopy tint (`wood_far_*`; `woods.png`'s share raised by `canopy_gain`) carries the wood beyond. At the zooms the player plays the woods thin, never vanish wholesale (taste 2026-10-02). Houses hide past `map3d_lod_houses_m`, the wind stage past `map3d_lod_wind_m` (beyond the opening view: the owner wants its gusts there); each shows again `map3d_lod_margin_m` under its line (`Map3DGeometry.lod_shown`). The meadow mottle is skipped past `meadow_mottle_far_m`.
- **Sky occlusion** (`Map3DGroundRelief`, spliced after the wind stage): `bake/relief_ao.png` (`tools/map3d/bake_relief_ao.py`) darkens valleys and terrace feet by `ground_look.tres` `relief_ao` / `relief_ao_gamma` — the light a high sun cannot give the relief.
- **Wind** (`Map3DLayerWind`, its `deg` the one wind the clouds drift and the rain slants down; it advances only while the clock runs): thin crests across the wind over sown fields and open grass (`Map3DGroundWind`), turning the grass's pale side up — lighter and warm, never grey; the stalks a crest lays over lean the shading normal down-wind as far as the crest lays them (export `tilt` → uniform `wind_tilt`, clamped, R8), so the light on a crest travels with it — the wave's slope instead was a line a few pixels wide that no shot could see; a later export value reaches the material too (the layer re-pushes on any change); crowns lean down the wind (`tree_sway.gdshader`).
- **March dust** (`Map3DLayerDust`, World/Dust, presentation only): a walking piece raises a short string of soft puffs (`march_dust.tres`) settling over `life_days` of game time, only while the clock runs; sizes are shares of a piece's height.
