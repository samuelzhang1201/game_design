# Ground

- **The pack's cells ARE the ground** — there is no map render PNG. The ground follows `data/map3d_bake.csv`: coast coverage, warped biome edges, a dithered blend byte, escarpments on the high ground (`terrace_*`), a moisture hue (`moist_*` / `dry_*`), scree on the uplands (`rock_*`), a snow line broken by noise (`snow_*`). Aerial terrain layers (grass, forest, rocky, cliff, snow, crop) are packed by `tools/map3d/pack_aerial.py`, which also writes their VRAM `.import` (a layer in another format greys the whole ground). The colour map can only DARKEN, which is why crops wear their own pale `aerial_crop` layer. Terrain3D 1.0.2 always height-blends (each texture's alpha is its height); the control map's per-vertex UV angle / scale bits are unused (`bake_world.py` writes base, overlay and blend only).
- **The grass is DUAL-SCALED far out** (`aerial_meadow` eases to a larger tile with camera distance; the cliff keeps its approved size by its TILE). `build_terrain.gd`'s `apply_material` must turn `dual_scaling` on BEFORE writing the shader parameters, or `dual_scale_*` / `tri_scale_reduction` save as null and the shader runs its defaults (`tests/map3d/test_map3d_build_material.gd`); a uniform's `hint_range` binds only the inspector. `build_world.gd --material-only` re-applies the material and tiles to a committed build without the raw inputs.
- **Open fields** round each settlement (`bake_world.sow_patchwork`, `data/map3d_farm_zones.csv`, `data/map3d_fields.csv`): crofts, two or three great fields in the rotation split into furlongs (`bake_world.jitter_cells`, each decided at its seed), meadow by the water, pasture to the edge of reach. `bake/fields.png` carries each field's colour and strip direction; the patched terrain shader draws the strips and fine furrow lines near and fades the crops far (the ground look's values). The fields stand in CLEARINGS: `bake/settled.png` (crofts, fields, meadow 1, pasture `pasture_open`) thins the woods before their edge squeeze (`map3d_trees.csv` `settled_clear`), and within reach the forest floor turns to grass (`settled_grass`). Hedgerow clumps run along the field edges (`map3d_trees.csv` `hedge_*`), bankside rows along every river (`riparian_*`).
- **Ground edges per pixel** (`Map3DGroundEdge`): Terrain3D blends between 20 m vertices; the patch bends the fragment's vertex coordinates by a fine noise before the lookups (the ground look's `edge_warp_*`), never the geometry; Terrain3D's `blend_sharpness` is low (`build_terrain.gd`) so the change between two vertices is a soft cross-fade. The fields' balks and croft hedges are LINES (`Map3DGroundBalk`) along `bake/field_edges.png` (a field's signed metres to its edge, per vertex), the ground look's `balk_*`; where a cell straddles a field's edge Terrain3D's `accumulate_material` reruns with the corners weighted by the pixel's coverage (`field_rim`): no 20 m steps.
- **Terrain3D shader patches** (`Map3DGroundTint.patch` rewrites the ONE `ALBEDO` line of the shader Terrain3D generates, read at load through `shader_override_enabled`, never committed; a test holds the line to exist): the HEIGHTS heat ramp, the MANORS tint, the woods' canopy, the weather and the wind stages. Terrain3D drops unknown uniforms after a load, so a layer puts them back when they read unset. Terrain3D's `mipmap_bias` is a DERIVATIVE MULTIPLIER (1.0 neutral), not a mip offset.
- **HEIGHTS**: the ground mixes toward the heat ramp by world height (stops passed as raw sRGB, blended in sRGB, turned linear in the shader); landmarks are drawn ▲ / ▽ with "N m" (the cell's metres, the engine's measure), thinned by camera distance and kept off dots, counters, legend and each other; the legend names the supply tiers.
- **MANORS** colours each manor by its house (`ManorPalette`, pure; a 128-wide LUT keyed by the cell id from `world_cell_index.png` via `texelFetch`, sRGB bytes turned linear before the mix; muted to a tincture by `map_manor_saturation` / `_value`; its EDGE smooth — the four texels round the point weighed bilinearly per id and the line where the two heaviest meet anti-aliased over a screen px by `fwidth`, so the cell staircase reads as the curve it stands for), cell edges a `ManorEdges` ribbon (`lines/manor_edge.tres`), and carries the output badges (`ManorBadges`: chief good's icon ringed in its category colour, `MapObjects.burg_visible` LOD, a smaller producer's badge dropped on overlap). MANORS also stands each manor's PEOPLE and WEALTH as two bars (`Map3DHudManorBars`, the Hud's ManorBars node, its look the node's exports; figures from `ManorBars`, `scripts/engine/CLAUDE.md`): each against its kind's most among the rows, ink-edged; a scouted foreign manor's hatched inside a dashed intel-violet edge with a whisker over the report's range; a pair steps right of a badge and out from under the counters; a key bottom-left; hover adds the figures (`ManorBars.words_of`). The estate drawings are never drawn on the 3D map. HEIGHTS and MANORS are mutually exclusive themes, and **no trees are drawn under either** (`Map3DView.set_manor_theme` hides the Trees layer: the theme's message is the ground's colour).
- **Woods** (an implementer's look, not yet the owner's): clumps planted by `tools/map3d/bake_trees.py` (`data/map3d_trees.csv`), drawn by `Map3DLayerTrees` as MultiMeshes per 2 km square × kind × variant × LOD, built once and culled by visibility ranges; every crown a camera-facing PHOTO CARD cut from `assets/3D/vegetation/crowns/crowns_atlas.png` (`tree_card.gdshader`, `tree_card.tres`; `map3d_tree_cards` 0 draws the 3D crowns); past them the ground's canopy tint carries the wood.
- **Sky occlusion** (`Map3DGroundRelief`, spliced after the wind stage): `bake/relief_ao.png` (`tools/map3d/bake_relief_ao.py`) darkens valleys and terrace feet by `ground_look.tres` `relief_ao` / `relief_ao_gamma` — the light a high sun cannot give the relief.
- **Wind** (`Map3DLayerWind`, its `deg` the one wind the clouds drift and the rain slants down; it advances only while the clock runs): thin crests across the wind over sown fields and open grass (`Map3DGroundWind`), turning the grass's pale side up — lighter and warm, never grey; crowns lean down the wind (`tree_sway.gdshader`).
- **March trails** (`Map3DLayerTrails`, presentation only, never saved): each walking piece leaves a ribbon stamped with the game day and faded over game time — trodden grass LIGHTER than the turf, beaten corn DARKER (read off the field map).
