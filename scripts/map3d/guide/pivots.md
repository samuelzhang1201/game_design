# Pivots for any turn (`scripts/map3d/`)

- **Pivots for any turn** (`Map3DPivotProvider`, the shell an arm's `solve(player, skeleton, clip, turn_udeg, phase)` plugs into; certifier and consumer share it). The infantry's `Map3DInfantryLocomotion` re-solves the coupled pivot plan (`assets/3D/units/infantry/infantry_pivot_plan.json`, generated by `tools/units3d/soldier_arp/pivot_mapping.py plan`) for the committed turn, its upper body scaled by |turn| / 90°. Its certificate (`docs/reviews/cluster-formations/evidence/locomotion/infantry/`) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 25/28 only: a consumer finishes the stride to that phase, holds that MARCH pose and starts from it. The archers' `Map3DArcherLocomotion` plays the 180° pivot at the phase and turns each foot's world track by (|turn| / 180° − 1) × its native heading (two-bone leg IK, body eased by |turn| / 180°); its certificate (`…/locomotion/archers/`) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 0 only. Editing a certified input voids the certificate (re-run `certify_locomotion.py`).
- **The royal pivot** (`Map3DRoyalPivot`, no ordinary caller): the royal `solve` for `Map3DPivotProvider`. It leaves, rejoins and holds MARCH at `royal_pivot_plan.json` `march_phase` (never plays the PIVOT clips), solves each hoof by `Map3DCavalryPivot.sample` and never moves the root. Frames: `tools/units3d/royal_install/pivot_frames.gd`. Its certificate (`docs/reviews/cluster-formations/evidence/locomotion/royal/`, on the re-keyed MARCH of LOCO-ROY-MARCH, `tools/units3d/royal_install/march_reauthor.gd`) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 7/16 only; the re-keyed MARCH keeps the delivered pose there, so re-keying it again must too or the pivots' review and R-B skin check no longer hold.
- **The cavalry pivot** (`Map3DCavalryLocomotion`, no ordinary caller): the royal solver bound to the cavalry (`cavalry_pivot_plan.json`, hold at MARCH 7/16; landmarks from the installed skin). Its installed PIVOT clips are never played. Its certificate (`docs/reviews/cluster-formations/evidence/locomotion/cavalry/certificate.json`, on the re-authored MARCH, stride 2.4) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 7/16 only.
