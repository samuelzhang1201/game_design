# Pivots for any turn (`scripts/map3d/`)

- **Pivots for any turn** (`Map3DPivotProvider`, the shell an arm's `solve(player, skeleton, clip, turn_udeg, phase)` plugs into; certifier and consumer share it). The infantry's `Map3DInfantryLocomotion` re-solves the coupled pivot plan (`assets/3D/units/infantry/infantry_pivot_plan.json`, generated by `tools/units3d/soldier_arp/pivot_mapping.py plan` from the installed GLB's own capture) for the committed turn, its upper body scaled by |turn| / 90°; the bones that carry the hips (`root.x`) keep the clip's own bob at every turn, since the held feet were placed for it (eased toward the entry pose, the hips rise out of reach on small turns). The plan's NEUTRAL / FOOT phases and micrometre bounds belong to the installed MARCH: a new MARCH re-derives them (`tools/units3d/infantry_arp/README.md`). The heavy infantry's `Map3DHeavyInfantryLocomotion` is the same solver (`solve_plan`) on its own body's plan (`heavy_infantry_pivot_plan.json`, `pivot_mapping.py --arm heavy_infantry`). Its certificate (`docs/reviews/cluster-formations/evidence/locomotion/infantry/`) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 13/14 only: a consumer finishes the stride to that phase, holds that MARCH pose and starts from it. The axemen's `Map3DAxemenLocomotion` is that solver on the hand-axe soldier's plan (`axemen_pivot_plan.json`, `--arm axemen`; it walks the infantry's MARCH, so its certificate `…/locomotion/axemen/` admits the infantry's phase). The militia's `Map3DMilitiaLocomotion` is that solver on the militiaman's own skeleton and plan (`militia_pivot_plan.json`, `--arm militia`; certificate `…/locomotion/militia/`, the infantry's phase): the plan's `held` names the skirt's 30 bones, which keep the PIVOT clip's keys at every turn (those keys hold MARCH's passing frame with the legs and swing the front chains out over a stepping knee; eased toward the entry pose with the arms, a leg cuts the cloth). The archers' `Map3DArcherLocomotion` is that solver on the longbowman's plan (`archers_pivot_plan.json`, `--arm archers`: the infantry's rig contract with his own retargeted MARCH, so his own phases); its certificate (`…/locomotion/archers/`) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 400/420 only (the thighs most nearly parallel: a pivot's step lifts with the turn but travels whatever the turn, so the entry is the held frame and the held feet stand where its soles do, `PLACE_NATIVE`). Editing a certified input voids the certificate (re-run `certify_locomotion.py`).
- **The royal pivot** (`Map3DRoyalPivot`, no ordinary caller): the royal `solve` for `Map3DPivotProvider`. It leaves, rejoins and holds MARCH at `royal_pivot_plan.json` `march_phase` (never plays the PIVOT clips), prepares each turn once per figure (`prepared`: the MARCH pose, seeked from the rest pose after the turn's PIVOT clip because the import drops MARCH's constant Saddle, Sword and Chest tracks; `contacts_for`; `Map3DCavalryPivot.prepare`) and solves each phase by `sample_prepared`, the pose `sample`'s bit for bit (GUT), and never moves the root. Cost: `tools/units3d/bench_pivot_solve.gd --arm royal`. Frames: `tools/units3d/royal_install/pivot_frames.gd`. Its certificate (`docs/reviews/cluster-formations/evidence/locomotion/royal/`, on the re-keyed MARCH of LOCO-ROY-MARCH, `tools/units3d/royal_install/march_reauthor.gd`) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 7/16 only; the re-keyed MARCH keeps the delivered pose there, so re-keying it again must too or the pivots' review and R-B skin check no longer hold.
- **The cavalry pivot** (`Map3DCavalryLocomotion`, no ordinary caller): the royal solver bound to the cavalry (`cavalry_pivot_plan.json`, hold at MARCH 63/104 of the heavy knight's run; landmarks from the installed skin). Its installed PIVOT clips are never played. Its stand is the held MARCH pose with every hoof seated (`stand_clip` MARCH: from the run's body the solver cannot set down STANDBY's forward left fore); `solve` prepares each turn once per figure (`prepared`: the MARCH pose, `contacts_for`, and `Map3DCavalryPivot.prepare`'s validation of rest, entry, exit and contacts), so a frame only blends and calls `sample_prepared`; the pose is `Map3DRoyalPivot.sample`'s bit for bit (GUT). Cost: `tools/units3d/bench_pivot_solve.gd`. Its certificate (`docs/reviews/cluster-formations/evidence/locomotion/cavalry/certificate.json`, stride 2.4224) admits stop, start and hold at MARCH 63/104 only.
