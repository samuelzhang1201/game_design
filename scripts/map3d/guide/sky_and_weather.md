# Sky, sun, clouds, weather

- **The sun turns once round each game day** (`Map3DSun`, swept by `Game.day_progress`; presentation only): from the west, high in the south, east, low through the north, never jumping at the day's turn; it rides HIGH and its shadows are a light wash. The north half is DUSK (`Map3DSun.dusk_of`): a dimmer, warmer sun, faded shadows and glint, and the shadowless `FillLight` opposite the sun raised — the fill, not the ambient, keeps backlit scarps readable. `day_sweep` off holds it; `preview_day_share` sets an hour in the editor.
- **Environment**: `world_environment.tres` (`Map3DSky`: authored SSAO / SSIL / glow a tier can only take away, depth haze and SSAO radius following the camera, the haze thinner up close by `fog_near_*`, the far land softened by the node's camera attributes' far blur at `dof_far_*` camera distances); `Map3DEnvironment` keeps only what the tier and camera do. Emissive and unshaded colours are fitted to the tonemap (`map3d_*_emission_gamma`); a `Color` set from GDScript passes straight through — never `srgb_to_linear()` it.
- **Clouds** belong to the MAP, not the view (`Map3DLayerClouds._sky_rect` is the whole pack): `Map3DCloudField` (pure) spreads them by Mitchell's best candidate, wraps one blown off the downwind edge back upwind, nudged by overcast (`weight_fn`); `cloud_deck.gdshader` drifts with the clock. Their soft shadows are a SEPARATE `cloud_shadow.gdshader` — a shader that mentions `ALPHA_SCISSOR_THRESHOLD` anywhere pushes the material into an alpha-scissor pipeline and silently blanks its blended rendering, so the two never share a file.
- **Weather is presentation only** (`Weather` is read, never written; spec `2026-09-29-weather-visuals-design.md`): `data/weather.csv` gives each kind seven 0..1 look columns; `Map3DWeatherField` (pure) lays each land texel in its province's look as a CELL round the province's centre (patches with clear country between), softened over land only; `Map3DLayerWeather` hands this spell's field and the next's to the terrain shader and blends them over the spell's last days. Overcast dims last, over the snow too; a blizzard is veiled in haze; a wood keeps its dark under snow (`weather_look.tres` `snow_wood`); houses take snow on top and rain on walls (`house_surface.gdshader`); clouds turn storm-grey from heavy overcast; rain and snow are world-space particles (`Map3DLayerPrecip`, `precip_*.gdshader`) born round the camera's target, kept where the field's fall texture says, falling only while the clock runs and nearer than `fall_far_view_m`. `map3d_weather_look` 0 turns it off.
