# Picking, hover, selection

Loaded on demand from `scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md` (Topic guides).

- **A click**: a settlement within `map3d_pick_px` (or on its ring) sends `object_selected`, anywhere else `province_selected` (the province bitmap under `Map3DGeometry.ground_hit`); `MapDetailPanel.follow` takes either. Under a theme a click on land picks the manor. `map3d_picker.gd`'s `pick_at` orders: first, off a badge (drawn over the pieces) and before the ground test, the piece whose FIGURES the click's ray meets nearest the camera (`Map3DPicker.ray_depth`, each shown mesh's world box) — it beats the town under it, and the sky; pieces round a town overlap on screen, so a box's centre never decides between them; then a piece by its box (`screen_rects`: the box holding the click, else within `map_army_pick_px` of one, so a far piece of a few px is still clicked) or a fleet's counter — a nearer town centre or a badge under the click wins — then a settlement within reach beats a landmark mark, a mark's own box beats its grown rect; picking never depends on a dot being drawn.
- **Only a clicked object is ringed**: hover names it (and swaps the left column via `country_hovered` → `CountryPanel.follow`) and draws the hovered kingdom's live outline wider in the border's ink (`lines/hover.tres`), on its land runs only, like the border; a clicked province is its own seam emphasised (`lines/selection_province.tres`), a clicked manor gold (`lines/selection_manor.tres`). A pointer move is picked once a frame (`hover_pick`); `Map3DView.kingdom_of_pick` names the holder NOW.
- **Armies** (spec `2026-11-27-army-groups-design.md`): a plain click on a member of an `ArmyGroups` army selects the whole army, the clicked unit first (`Map3DSelection.group_with`); Shift-click and Shift-drag are unchanged. `Map3DView.select_armies(codes)` is the regiment card's SELECT. A selected unit's `object_selected` entry carries `codes`.
