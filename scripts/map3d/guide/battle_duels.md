# Front-rank duels

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-infantry-duel-animation-design.md` (owner: spectacle, not a readout of the battle). View-only: no engine state, no save field, no RNG draw.

- **Who duels.** An arm whose `army_art.csv` `duel` names STRIKE, BLOCK and HIT clips (the chains' grammar, `Map3DClipChain.of_column`), every one installed on the figure and given a `contact` share (`Map3DDuel.duels`). DEATH is the fall's. The infantry row names the NEW infantry variant's clips (`BLOCK..BLOCK_4`, `HIT..HIT_3`, `DEATH`, `DEATH_2`); the installed knight has none, so nothing duels until that GLB is installed and its `contact` shares measured (the blade's meeting share of each strike, the shield's full raise of each BLOCK, the jolt of each HIT).
- **Pairs.** `Map3DDuelDriver.gather` takes each battle's drawn `physical_groups` pieces (no ghost), their `front` figures that duel, by side (`a` = the battle record's `att`, never a foe's order), from the battle's first round (`rounds > 0`). `Map3DDuel.pairs` sorts both sides along the front, lays the shorter against the longer at the closest offset (ties: the smallest) and drops a pair farther apart than `map3d_duel_reach_h` figure heights. A pair whose two figures still stand within reach keeps its exchange; only the figures left over are paired again (a lost figure never restarts the rest). The front axis is the attacking side's first unit's facing, whatever the pieces' order. Extra figures keep the piece's own action (the attack chain or the guard); reserves stand guard.
- **Turns.** A new pair stands its guard a hashed `0..map3d_duel_open_max_s` before its first turn, so a front never opens in unison. One schedule drives both figures of a pair (`Map3DDuel.turn`, hashed from the pair key and turn count): turn 0 is `a`'s, then they alternate, `map3d_duel_double` gives the same striker twice, `map3d_duel_steal` lets `a` take a `b` turn, `map3d_duel_hit` replies with HIT instead of BLOCK. `Map3DDuel.timing` starts the earlier clip at 0 so both contacts meet; a late frame seeks into the clip. Both stand guard for `map3d_duel_rest_min_s`..`_max_s`, then the next turn.
- **Clock.** The duel's time runs in real seconds while the game clock runs (any speed); paused, its figures' `speed_scale` is 0 and its time stands.
- **Stepping in.** Each paired figure steps toward the other until `map3d_duel_close_h` heights apart, at most `map3d_duel_step_max_h`, and faces it: `duel_offset` / `duel_yaw` on its group child, which `Map3DBattleGroups._target` adds over the saved point, so the battle walk eases it in and back out, facing its foe both ways (`step_facing` → `Map3DBattleGroups.eased(…, keep_facing)`), and the turn clock waits while either figure is still `walking`. The saved point never moves.
- **Hand-over.** A claimed figure carries the `duel` meta: `Map3DClipChain.stop`, and `Map3DUnitRig.play` skips it. Released (pair gone, or its partner's node freed), it drops the offsets and takes its piece's action again (its chain if it chains).
- **Falls** (owner: 前排倒，后排补位). The engine trims losses from a unit's TAIL rows (a flank figure). When a new sample only lost people (`Map3DBattleGroups.is_loss`: same group size and version, fewer men) and the state was not swapped (the layer passes `_duels` only while `_same_state`), `Map3DBattleGroups.sync` asks `Map3DDuelDriver.plan_falls` (`Map3DFall.plan`: one distinct duellist per removed slot, a removed duellist or none left falls as itself) and `fall_swap` takes each faller's child out as the BODY while the removed slot's child stands for its row and walks there. A body plays one of its arm's `DEATH` clips, lies whole `map3d_fall_hold_s`, fades over `map3d_fall_fade_s` (`Map3DFall.alpha`, `Map3DCourierRide.apply`) and is freed; paused, it stands still; no DEATH clip, it is freed at once. The walker (`step_up`) walks up turning into its way, is claimed by the faller's pair again and the turn waits until it arrives. A body carries the `fallen` meta: no release gives it back its piece's action. A whole piece dropped (battle over, a load) frees as before.
- **Cost.** Whether a figure can duel is worked out once per AnimationPlayer (`Map3DDuelDriver.can_duel`, kept on its group child); battles before their first round are skipped before their groups are read.
- **Hooks.** `Map3DLayerUnits.step` calls `_duels.step(ctx, _pieces, delta, _place)` after the actions are synced; the layer is at the shape cap (`test_map3d_shape.gd`), so the duel's code stays in its own files.
