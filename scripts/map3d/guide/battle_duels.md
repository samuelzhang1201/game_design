# Front-rank duels

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-16-infantry-duel-animation-design.md` (owner: spectacle, not a readout of the battle). View-only: no engine state, no save field, no RNG draw.

- **Who duels.** An arm whose `army_art.csv` `duel` names STRIKE, BLOCK and HIT clips (the chains' grammar, `Map3DClipChain.of_column`), every one installed on the figure and given a `contact` share (`Map3DDuel.duels`). DEATH is the fall's. The infantry row names the NEW infantry variant's clips (`BLOCK..BLOCK_4`, `HIT..HIT_3`, `DEATH`, `DEATH_2`); the installed knight has none, so nothing duels until that GLB is installed and its `contact` shares measured (the blade's meeting share of each strike, the shield's full raise of each BLOCK, the jolt of each HIT).
- **Pairs.** `Map3DDuelDriver.gather` takes each battle's drawn `physical_groups` pieces (no ghost), their `front` figures that duel, by side (`a` = the battle record's `att`, never a foe's order), from the battle's first round (`rounds > 0`). `Map3DDuel.pairs` sorts both sides along the front, lays the shorter against the longer at the closest offset (ties: the smallest) and drops a pair farther apart than `map3d_duel_reach_h` figure heights. A pair whose two figures still stand within reach keeps its exchange; only the figures left over are paired again (a lost figure never restarts the rest). The front axis is the attacking side's first unit's facing, whatever the pieces' order. No one fights air (spec `2026-11-21-infantry-melee-scrum-design.md`): a front figure left unpaired in a fought battle whose other side has a duelling front figure is claimed too (`waiting`: the `duel` meta, GARRISON, `duel_yaw` toward the nearest foe's stand, no step) and pairs once a foe is free (a partner's fall, a new foe); a duellist whose partner goes waits so, stepping back; the battle gone, it is released like a duellist. Reserves stand guard.
- **The mixed fight** (`Map3DScrum`, pure; `Map3DDuelScrum`, run by the driver; spec `2026-11-21-infantry-melee-scrum-design.md`; owner: 双方兵人应该在战场上混在一起). Under `map3d_melee_scrum` 1, `gather` takes BOTH ranks of every duelling piece but its standard bearer (`groups[0]`, `Map3DUnitStandard.first_figure`'s; it keeps its slot and its piece's action), each row carrying `rank` and `bearer`, and the battle `scrum`; 0 gives the front-line duels below (a hand-built battle with no `scrum` too). Per battle ONE area over all its opposed infantry (`att` against `def`; other arms never gathered, so archers keep their blocks): `Map3DScrum.area` spans both sides' stands along the front and `map3d_melee_depth_h` heights across, centred between the sides' means. Each figure takes a point hashed inside it (`target`), in its own side's half and past the centre line by at most `map3d_melee_cross` of the other (points anywhere put foes under the other side's standard and crossed every walk-in), KEPT while it fights; a pair that still has both figures keeps on, the rest pair nearest across the area (`pairs_nearest`, no reach); each frame a pair's points are laid `map3d_duel_close_h` apart about their midpoint, then one `separate` pass pushes every two points closer than `map3d_melee_space_h` apart (each at most `map3d_melee_push_h`) and `inside` holds them in the area. A figure's `duel_offset` is its point less its stand (no `step_max_h` clamp), taken only once its point moved past `map3d_melee_settle_h`, and while it walks only past `map3d_duel_step_max_h` (it takes the rest on arrival): a restarted walk re-begins its turn into its way, so constant re-aiming moved figures sideways at a slow cadence, a glide (and the turn waits while a figure walks). A move past `map3d_duel_step_max_h` WALKS turned into its way (`step_up`, paced), a shorter one steps facing its foe; a figure leaving the scrum (`scrum_far`) walks back to its slot turned into its way. A figure left over waits AT its point facing the nearest foe's point; a partner gone, it stays there. A side with no figure drawn (unseen, a ghost) forms no area: nobody is claimed. A fall lies where it fought (the body is never placed again); the tail man walks from where he stands to the faller's point. Picks: `Map3DBattleGroups.place` records each drawn soldier's screen box (`figure_rects`) and `Map3DPicker.figure_at` picks the unit of the soldier under the click (several: the lowest bottom edge, nearest the camera) before the unit boxes; each soldier's selection glow is a child of its own node, so it lies under him in the crowd. Bench: `tools/map3d/bench.gd --battle-arm foot` (the field battle staged by `tools/battle_stage.gd`, shared with `snapshot_screen`).
- **Turns.** A new pair stands its guard a hashed `0..map3d_duel_open_max_s` before its first turn, so a front never opens in unison. One schedule drives both figures of a pair (`Map3DDuel.turn`, hashed from the pair key and turn count): turn 0 is `a`'s, then they alternate, `map3d_duel_double` gives the same striker twice, `map3d_duel_steal` lets `a` take a `b` turn, `map3d_duel_hit` replies with HIT instead of BLOCK. `Map3DDuel.timing` starts the earlier clip at 0 so both contacts meet; a late frame seeks into the clip. Both stand guard for `map3d_duel_rest_min_s`..`_max_s`, then the next turn.
- **Impact** (`Map3DDuelMelee`, run by the driver; spec `2026-11-21-infantry-melee-scrum-design.md`; owner: 交战的时候能够有碰撞感). `Map3DDuel.timing`'s `contact_at` is the blow's instant; most replies are HITs (`map3d_duel_hit`). At contact both figures hold a hit-stop (`speed_scale` 0 for `map3d_melee_hitstop_s`, the turn clock standing with them, the overshoot of a late frame spent on it; a pause keeps its remainder), and the contact is told once through `Map3DDuelDriver.take_impacts()` (`{kind HIT|BLOCK, at, striker, struck}`, drained on take, cleared each `run`; nothing plays it yet — the melee sounds, plan PR 4, will). Within `map3d_impact_near_m` of the camera the struck figure is knocked back away from its striker (`knock_offset` on its group child, `Map3DImpact.knock`: `map3d_melee_knock_h` heights, out over `_knock_out_s`, back over `_knock_back_s`, a block × `map3d_melee_block_knock`), drawn by `Map3DBattleGroups.place` OVER the battle walk (never walked: a walk would set `walking` and stop the turn), and a pooled one-shot burst shows at the pair's midpoint `map3d_impact_at_h` figure heights up (where blade meets shield; also the told `at`) (`Map3DImpactBursts`, `GameArt.IMPACT_BURSTS`: sparks on a BLOCK, dust on a HIT; at most `map3d_impact_burst_max` burn). The burst scenes (`scenes/map3d/effects/impact_*.tscn`, their process material and quad as `.tres` beside them so a shot can bracket them with `--set`) are authored in figure heights: a burst scales its node (spread, speed) and its own copy of the quad (a node's scale never reaches a particle's size). A one-shot with full explosiveness drops `emitting` after its first frame, so a burst counts as burning until its `finished`. A release clears every hold and knock.
- **Clock.** The duel's time runs in real seconds while the game clock runs (any speed); paused, its figures' `speed_scale` is 0 and its time stands.
- **Stepping in.** Each paired figure steps toward the other until `map3d_duel_close_h` heights apart, at most `map3d_duel_step_max_h`, and faces it: `duel_offset` / `duel_yaw` on its group child, which `Map3DBattleGroups._target` adds over the saved point, so the battle walk eases it in and back out, facing its foe both ways (`step_facing` → `Map3DBattleGroups.eased(…, keep_facing)`), and the turn clock waits while either figure is still `walking`. The saved point never moves. Every step WALKS: while a figure's child is `walking`, `Map3DDuelDriver._pace` plays MARCH (the combat walk) at `speed_scale` = `step_rate` (its ground moved this frame along its facing ÷ dt ÷ miniature scale × the clip's length ÷ its `stride_m`, both from `army_art.csv`), so the feet stay planted and a figure backing away walks the loop backwards; under 0.05 it stands in GARRISON, and on arrival it takes the guard again. A step from where it stands (`step_facing` / `step_up`) lasts `Map3DBattleGroups.paced_s` (its length ÷ the stride's pace at that scale) instead of `map3d_battle_walk_s`, so it walks at the clip's own cadence; a pair claimed while its figures are still in the battle walk turns into its way (no `step_facing`, or it would crab) and paces that walk's speed. A released figure keeps the duel meta while it walks back (`_stepping`), then `_hand_back` gives it its piece's action. Paused, it stands at speed 0 and its walk's clock is held.
- **Hand-over.** A claimed figure carries the `duel` meta: `Map3DClipChain.stop`, and `Map3DUnitRig.play` skips it. Released (pair gone, or its partner's node freed), it drops the offsets and takes its piece's action again (its chain if it chains).
- **Falls** (owner: 前排倒，后排补位). The engine trims losses from a unit's TAIL rows (a flank figure). When a new sample only lost people (`Map3DBattleGroups.is_loss`: same group size and version, fewer men) and the state was not swapped (the layer passes `_duels` only while `_same_state`), `Map3DBattleGroups.sync` asks `Map3DDuelDriver.plan_falls` (`Map3DFall.plan`: one distinct duellist per removed slot, a removed duellist or none left falls as itself) and `fall_swap` takes each faller's child out as the BODY while the removed slot's child stands for its row and walks there. A body plays one of its arm's `DEATH` clips, then lies whole `map3d_fall_hold_s` from the clip's END (DEATH_2's head is down only at 3.4 s), fades over `map3d_fall_fade_s` (`Map3DFall.alpha`, `Map3DCourierRide.apply`) and is freed; paused, it stands still; no DEATH clip, it is freed at once. The walker (`step_up`) is claimed by the faller's pair again (the body keeps the pair key it fell from) and holds back, its walk kept at its start, while that body falls (`_wait_for_bodies`: walking up at once, he stood on the body's legs); once it lies he walks up turning into his way, and the turn waits until he arrives. He sets off at hold + fade − his paced walk after the clip ends, so he arrives as the body is gone (a longer lie left the victor idle over it; a slow fade read as a grey ghost). A body carries the `fallen` meta: no release gives it back its piece's action. A whole piece dropped (battle over, a load) frees as before.
- **Cost.** Whether a figure can duel is worked out once per AnimationPlayer (`Map3DDuelDriver.can_duel`, kept on its group child); battles before their first round are skipped before their groups are read.
- **Hooks.** `Map3DLayerUnits.step` calls `_duels.step(ctx, _pieces, delta, _place)` after the actions are synced; the layer is at the shape cap (`test_map3d_shape.gd`), so the duel's code stays in its own files.
