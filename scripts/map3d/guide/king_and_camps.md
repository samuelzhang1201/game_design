# King and field camps

- `GameRoot` sends copied presentation snapshots through `Map3DView.apply_snapshot` to `Map3DKing` and `Map3DLayerUnits`/`Map3DUnitCamps`. The UI bridge owns engine prediction; these renderers do not advance or mutate simulation state. Snapshots contain only visible camps, never hidden foe positions or ghosts.
- The king is an independently selected model in the authored world's `King` node, separate from royal regiments and army multi-selection. Its scene and stride are inspector values. It walks today's predicted route corners; replacing a route mid-day starts at the position already drawn. Its banner is part of the king asset, never a camp royal marker. Ordinary actions use `KingOrderMenu`; active paid inspiration belongs to the battle command implementation.
- `KingMapBridge` respects completed royal movement in a suspended day transaction: a committed day is not predicted again. A newly loaded view uses the current saved physical position.
- A committed day's camp staging uses its completed day fraction, including when a suspended save loads with a fresh UI clock. Clearing a king selection closes its detail card just as clearing an army selection does.
- `GameArt.CAMP_MODELS` binds `building`, `camped`, `packing` to the authored field-camp scene. Saved phase/build progress and packing dates stage the six named facilities. Only tents lower; carts, stores and watch equipment retain their proportions. Attacks never restore dismantled facilities.
- Camps remain attached to saved metre `site`, with a fixed orientation and the layer's `camp_back_m` offset. Regiment turns, formation changes and screen recreation cannot reposition them. `camp_size_ratio` is authored on the Units inspector.
- Each facility stands plumb on its own footprint's highest terrain sample. A bounded neighbouring search rejects sea, rivers, roads, houses, canopy and ground falling farther than `map3d_bake.csv`'s `house_max_drop_m`; if none is legal, that facility is omitted. No pedestal, cleared terrain or simulated relocation is created.
- Courier palace dressing uses each ride's physical departure or arrival point. A field dispatch gets no palace override; today's king position never rewrites an old courier's visual origin.
- Art review fixture: `scenes/tools/map3d/king_camp_review.tscn` extends the normal `snapshot_screen.gd` path and seeds only scenario state. Use `--screen root --quiet --hold-line 1 --resolution 2560x1600 --zoom3d <world metres>` with `--p33-case flat|slope|water|field`, optional `--p33-phase building|camped|packing` and `--p33-share`. `--p33-select` clicks the real king menu; `--p33-animate` runs presentation fractions over one day for `--also` frame shots. Always use a real window.
