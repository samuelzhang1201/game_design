# The simulation (`scripts/engine/`)

The root `CLAUDE.md`'s invariants rule here first: deterministic, `RefCounted`, no `Game`, no wall-clock, every number from `data/`, a reason on every state move. Tuning values are `balance.csv` keys named below; read the CSV for the value. Design: `docs/design/MANOR_SYSTEM.md`, `COUNCIL_SYSTEM.md`, `CHAPTER_0.md`; each feature's spec is under `docs/superpowers/specs/`. The WAR ROOM's pure pieces here (`WarSheets`, `WarDrafts`, `WarFolio`, `WarRoom`) are described in `scripts/war_room/CLAUDE.md`.

## Manors and houses

- **Every land cell of the pack is a manor** with a proper name (`political/manors.csv`, forged by `NameForge` / `ManorNames` from the export's name banks; `manor_name_overrides.csv` is hand-maintained) held by a **house** (a family name; the crown is the player character's live family). An unmoved manor belongs to its province's house, so `GameState.manor_owner` holds only what moved, and `MapManors.set_owner` needs a reason (`manor_log`).
- **People are never stored and are WHOLE**: a province's ledger population is apportioned to its manors by area × land value (largest remainder, `MapManors.people_table`), so manors always sum EXACTLY to their province.
- **The economy is `ManorEconomy` and `RealmEconomy`, not `MapManors`.** `ManorEconomy.line_of` is one manor's MONTH — whole people, whole-gold tax (its town's tariff included), the goods it ships and their worth in the strategic and civil pools — THE ONLY ROUNDING IN THE ECONOMY. `RealmEconomy` sums those lines into provinces and the realm (columnar, a province key as the dirty flag, the month's work spread over balanced day buckets; `line_of` / `lines_of_province` / `lines_table` / `tax_table` check the key once per bulk read, never per row), settled at the month's end into `metrics.treasury`, `metrics.strategic` and `metrics.grain` — the CIVIL pool, whose key stays `grain` for its readers. Spec `2026-09-14-economy-and-staging-design.md`.
- Buildings (`data/manor_buildings.csv`, priced in civil goods by `civil_cost`; `cost_gold` is unused), defence, value (`MapManors.value_of_line`) and the holding house's asking price (marked up by its councillor's opinion) are `MapManors`, every number `balance.csv` `manor_*`.
- **Orders** `MANOR_BUY` / `MANOR_SELL` / `MANOR_BUILD` / `MANOR_DEMOLISH` / `MANOR_FOCUS` / `MANOR_SEIZE` (a skeleton gated by `manor_seize_enabled`) resolve a manor by code, label or id (`MapManors.find`) and run from the manor card through `Game.try_execute`; no model call. `world_cell_index.png` is how a click finds its manor — only in the MANORS layer; elsewhere land means province.
- **Estates**: each manor without a town is an estate by its INDUSTRY and its people — `data/manor_estates.csv` gives each industry's `min_people` rows, `MapObjects.estate_tier(repo, focus, people)` picks the row (an industry with no rows wears the first industry's); the card names industry and size and paints the row's `painting`. Never place separate procedural farmsteads and fields by hash.
- **The player is the king of the realm and the lord of ONE province**: a province is controlled by the house holding the most of its manors (`MapManors.controller_of`, ties to the province's own house); the crown starts controlling the capital's province alone (`house_bindings.csv`). Everything that reads as the crown's economy — the lord's purse, the dashboard's people and provinces, `WorldLedger.belongs_to_player`, the order prompt's "Your provinces" — is `MapManors.controlled_provinces`; the host stays the realm's. BUILD and FOCUS need a controlled province, SEIZE any province of the realm.
- **Two purses** (MANOR_SYSTEM.md §5c): the CROWN's `treasury`/`strategic`/`grain` take `crown_share_permille` of the realm's other provinces and pay the court and the orders' fees; the LORD's `lord_gold`/`lord_strategic`/`lord_civil` take the controlled provinces in full, the harvest and the buildings, and pay every manor order inside the realm. `RealmEconomy.purses` is the one split.
- **Buying land** (`MapManors.purchase_kind`): your own province or a realm province bordering one, from the lord's purse; across the border only a manor whose cell touches the realm, from the treasury, annexed (`MapManors.annex`, `GameState.manor_province`, saved) into the bordering realm province with the most tax and held by its house; anything else refused. A manor's province is therefore STATE: `province_of` / `manors_of_province` take the state, and `MapOwnership.province_loops` merges annexed cells into the outlines by edge parity, so borders, seams, wash and `RealmEconomy`'s index follow.
- **Every manor works one chief good** (`data/manor_focus.csv`, allowed by biome and coast). The default FOLLOWS THE TERRAIN: the first row whose `default_on` claims the cell (`coast`, `high`/`peak` by `manor_focus_*_height`, or biome ids) and the land allows, else the first allowed. `GameState.manor_focus` holds only chosen ones with the tick, ramping in over `transition_ticks`. Yield is people × land value × `yield_per_1000` × the good's growth, whole units a month (`ManorEconomy.focus_units`), counted in the value; `MapEconomy.output_of_provinces(repo, provinces, state)` sums the same lines the tick settles.
- **Buildings take time and room**: `build_days` per building, works in `GameState.manor_works`, `manor_slots_base` (+`manor_slots_burg_bonus`) slots per manor; `MANOR_DEMOLISH` refunds `manor_demolish_refund` of `civil_cost` to the civil stores. Finished buildings add `*_per_tick` effects as ledger lines after the month's settlement and `produce_good/units` to the manor's output.
- **Building rules**: `manor_buildings.csv` has three `tier`s; `land` is tokens joined by `|` of which ONE must hold — `coast`, `high` / `peak`, `town`, `focus:<code>`, a biome id, or `any` (`MapManors.land_allows` / `describe_land`, the language of `manor_focus.csv` `default_on`) — and `prereq` names buildings that must ALL stand first (`building_prereqs`). `MapManors.building_blocked` gives the reason and `ActionSystem` refuses a MANOR_BUILD with it. Every building yields something (a test holds it).
- **`ManorBars`** (pure) gives the MANORS bars' figures: every manor of a province the crown controls exactly — wealth is its month's worth, tax and goods in gold (`worth_of`) — and any other manor only once a CREDIBLE spy's report on that very manor has come in, as the report's estimate (a manor report carries `worth`, `intel_facts.csv`).
- `MapEconomy` sums `economy/production.csv` (per burg) into provinces and provinces into realms **through live ownership**; `MapEconomy.burg_cells` are the working manors.

## Time

- **Time runs in days**: `GameState.day` (1..30) under the tick is the day NOW RUNNING — the date the top bar shows. `TickEngine.advance_day` ENDS it (works, marches, couriers, supply, the host eats) then turns the calendar; the thirtieth day ends inside the tick (`advance`), before the month settles. The reign's opening tick ends no day, so day 1 is played. The clock (`||` `>` `2x` `4x`, `time_day_seconds`, `time_speed_*`) is driven only by `GameRoot._process` (court AND council stage) through `Game.advance_day`. No End Turn.
- `Game.hold_clock(on, who)` is a SET of named holders (`holds_clock`, `release_clock_holds` on a new reign or restart): while any holds it, Space and the play buttons start nothing. The LEDGER and the WAR ROOM hold it while open; closing, like an order, starts nothing.

## Events and the prologue

- **The prologue is a CHAIN on the existing event system**, not a second system: its beats are in `data/chapter_events.csv` / `chapter_event_choices.csv` / `chapter_branches.csv`, prose and titles in `game_story/chapter_0/chapter_0_events.md` (`StorySource.DOCUMENTS` lists it and `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`; `### C0-NN` headings, the same `**Scene:**` contract), outcome lines in `templates.csv` as `event_c0_*`, illustrations library `V-35`..`V-55` installed to `assets/2D/events/illustrations/<event_id>.png`. Chapter beats are NOT in `data/events.csv` (the random roll samples it; a scripted beat must never be) but ARE in `events_by_id`; `EventSystem` reads `DataRepository.event_choices_all()`. `GameState.new_campaign` schedules whichever chapter row carries a `start_tick`. The chapter calls no model at all.
- Engine additions it relies on: `TriggerDsl`'s `never`, `!` negation and `trait_of=<seat or id>:<trait>`; `EventEffects`' `branch:<branch_id>` op (schedules the first `chapter_branches.csv` rule whose `requires` holds, at that rule's delay); the event title comes from the story document's heading (`event_titles`), never a `templates.csv` key (the content test forbids it).
- **An event waits as a councillor's matter; the clock runs on.** While one waits the engine rolls no other and holds any chapter beat behind it (`EventSystem.roll`). The presenter is `data/event_presenters.csv`'s seat for the event's id, else for its category, else the first `council_seats.csv` seat with `presents_events` 1 (`ConversationBeats.event_presenter`). How it is shown: `scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`.
- Events are fixed authored prose (root `CLAUDE.md`, `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`).

## House arms

`HouseArms` (pure, cached, forgotten on `load_map`) resolves family → seat → texture. A house wears its SEAT's arms: its first province by `province_id` among those `MapObjects.house_name_of` gives it, that province's capital town's `coa` (the province's when the town has none). Keys: `kingdom_<code>`, `seat_<capital burg>`, `province_<code>`. The crown resolves through `player_setup.house_was`, so renaming the royal family keeps its arms. A new map needs no new arms: a key wears `data/maps/<id>/arms_bindings.csv`'s pin, else the pack's installed art, else arms BORROWED from other packs in the pack's order, never twice while the pool lasts (`HouseArms.resolved_path` / `report`; `tools/arms_report.gd --map <id>`). Generating arms: `tools/CLAUDE.md`. The realm and the ruler are not customised: a reign begins with the founding names.

## Height

- One measure: `Elevation.metres_of_cell` — (cell height − `elevation_sea_level`) × `elevation_m_per_unit`, the same two keys `bake_world.py` builds the relief from.
- **Landmarks** are `Elevation.landmarks` (pure, cached): each cell a local extreme against its `landmark_ring` rings of neighbours by at least `landmark_min_prominence_m`, `lod` by `landmark_lod_far_m` / `_mid_m`, named after the nearest town from `data/landmark_names.csv` (a pack pins names in `landmark_name_overrides.csv`, reserved first). `MapObjects.landmark_lines` gives the tooltip and card.
- **Mountains are HEIGHT, not a biome**: `MapIndex.raise_high_ground` rewrites a land pixel's biome to 13 hill / 14 mountain / 15 peak by height (`map_biome_hill_height` / `_mountain_height` / `_peak_height`). `MapIndex.bake_relief` lays each cell's height plus seeded, domain-warped ridged detail (`map_relief_detail_*`, held down near rivers and the coast); the high-ground bands follow that detailed field.
- In war: `ArmySystem.height_factor` scales each side in `battle_odds` by the height DIFFERENCE (`army_height_bonus_permille_per_100m`, capped by `army_height_bonus_cap_permille`); supply use is × the tier's `supply_permille` in `data/army_height_supply.csv`, and the terrain's hill and mountain lines ARE those tiers (`Elevation.tier_of`).

## Regiments

- Regiments carry state — morale, wounded, supplies, hidden training — in `GameState.regiments`, settled monthly by `ArmySystem` (`balance.csv` `army_*`, `data/army_*.csv`; spec `2026-09-17-regiment-state-design.md`).
- **The march is engine state**: a regiment carries `x_m`/`y_m`/`dest_x_m`/`dest_y_m`/`order` in whole METRES of the pack's measure (pack px would round a day to nothing; floats break the JSON save). Every map, card and terrain lookup reads `ArmySystem.position_of`. `ArmySystem.march_day` runs under `TickEngine.advance_day` every one of the 30 days (the tick's included), in code order, EVENLY as a courier rides: each day what is ahead over the whole days left at the day's pace where the column stands (`day_march_at`: `pace_tenths × 100` × the weather × the ground), rounded up, so an N-day march ends exactly as the Nth day ends. Every arrival figure (days out, the aim ruler, `CourierSystem.arrival_days`) is `march_days_between`, the march walked day by day. It lands exactly on the destination and GARRISONS; arrivals go on `TickEngine.day_arrivals` for the news, not the return value. Straight-line; no pathfinding yet.
- **Pace** `ArmySystem.speed_km_per_day`: the slowest arm's pace (`data/army_speed.csv`) × readiness, the weighted mean (`army_speed_w_*`) of arm, supplies (`ArmySystem.supply_score`: full at `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, linear to 0 at none; only a hungry column drags), morale and training — whole tenths of a km — × the carried load (`ArmySystem.load_permille`, `data/army_carry_loads.csv`) × the weather (`ArmySystem.day_march_m`) × the ground (`ground_permille`, `data/army_ground_speed.csv`: within `army_road_reach_m` of a road or trail its row × the height tier's, else the terrain's).
- **Orders** `ARMY_MARCH` / `ARMY_GARRISON` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows; for a land regiment they DISPATCH a courier (below). `ArmySystem.ORDER_STANDBY` at the campaign's start, `ORDER_GARRISON` on every arrival and by a Cancel march; ARMY_GARRISON is otherwise only the route menu's Cancel march.
- **Towns** (spec `2026-10-06-army-location-and-town-defence-design.md`): a land regiment is IN a town within its group's `data/army_town_reach.csv` km (`ArmySystem.town_of`, the nearest); `held_town_of` only one its kingdom holds; `works_of` (town / walls / citadel) multiplies `data/army_town_defence.csv` onto the terrain factor for the defender. A GARRISON is only in a held town (`SupplySystem.garrisoned_in`); halted in the open it is a field camp. The pack quarters every land regiment in a town of its province.
- **Strength**: `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (fit men per mille of the headcount; a foreign regiment read as it would start) picks the row of `data/army_formations.csv` (nine / six / three figures).
- **Bars**: `ArmySystem.bars_of` — readiness (`readiness_permille`) and stores (`stores_permille`: days in hand over the carried load, falling daily). Supplies are whole units; DAYS in hand (`ArmySystem.days_in_hand`, never saved) is units × 30 / `supply_use`, capped by `army_supply_max_days`; `carry_days` is the standing load order. They are EATEN a day at a time (`ArmySystem.eat_day`, the month's use spread over thirty days in whole supplies by `day_bite`).

## Couriers

- An order to a land regiment DISPATCHES a courier (`CourierSystem`, `GameState.couriers`, saved) from the capital (else the most populous town of the first province the realm holds) along the road network (`RoadGraph`: roads and trails, vertices snapped by `courier_graph_snap_px`, Dijkstra). The order takes effect only when he reaches the column, re-aiming his last leg as it moves. Spec `2026-09-27-courier-core-design.md`.
- **He arrives as the day ENDS**: an order on a paused clock moves nothing; once play runs he sets out (`CourierSystem.setting_out`) and rides EVENLY (`day_pace`: what is ahead over the whole days left, `days_for`, rounded up, never past `day_budget`), so an N-day trip delivers as the Nth day ends. `TickEngine` marches the columns BEFORE the couriers ride (in `advance_day` and the tick's day), so the order is marched on from the NEXT day.
- **Every day count shown counts day-ends, the running day first** (`eta_days` / `days_left` / `spy_eta_days` read the weather of `absolute_day()`). The arrival day shown for an order is ONE number, the ride then the march (`CourierSystem.arrival_days`; `days_to_arrival` counts a march order still riding). A higher courier id is a newer order; an older one arriving late is set aside.
- Pace: `courier_base_km_per_day` × `courier_kinds.csv` × the stretch's kind (`road_speed.csv`) × the height tier × the weather. His fee is a ledger line; fleets are exempt for now. `ride_ahead` / `day_ahead_of` preview the next day on a copy for the map.

## Weather

`Weather` is stateless: hashed from the seed per province per `weather_spell_days` spell, odds by season and tier in `weather_odds.csv`. It scales couriers and marches. The map only reads it (visuals: `scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md`).

## Intel, missives and spies

- **A FOG OF KNOWLEDGE, not of the map**: `Intel` is the one gate every card and panel asks — `full` (provinces the crown controls, its regiments) exact figures, `general` (the rest of the realm) words, `basic` (abroad) only holder, stance (`diplomacy.csv`) and wealth. The newest report on a place overrides the words with its estimate, tagged by source and date. DEV MODE (`Intel.dev_all`) lifts it. Spec `2026-09-28-missive-board-and-spies-design.md`.
- SPY couriers (`SPY_SEND`) ride the roads to a POINT and file a report (`SpySystem`): one quality roll from `GameState.rng` shifted by the place (`spy_difficulty.csv`). A report on a town's cell carries `town_people` (the town card's) beside the manor's `people`; a manor's `output` fact is its CHIEF good's units a month, named (`SpySystem.chief_output`).
- **Wealth tiers are STATE**: `GameState.wealth_tiers` (the provinces' terciles), settled by `TickEngine` after the month's economy and by `new_campaign`, computed once for an old save (`Intel.ensure_wealth_tiers`).
- Every letter is a MISSIVE (`MissiveLog`, `GameState.missives`, saved, capped per column): receipts, the steward's monthly report, spy reports, audit letters. `Intel._latest` skips any missive whose subject kind is `"ledger"`.

## The LEDGER's model (`ledger.gd`, `ledger_resolvers.gd`, `ledger_cards.gd`)

- **It draws BELIEF, not truth**: `Ledger.galaxy` reads each live link through a named reader in `LedgerResolvers` and passes every figure through `Intel`; no numeric credibility is ever drawn. `Ledger.snapshot` (one lens) stays for the tests. Spec `2026-09-30-ledger-ontology-design.md`, `2026-09-30-ledger-galaxy-design.md`.
- **All schema is DATA**: `data/ledger_lenses.csv`, `ledger_object_types.csv` (`cluster` / `fold_to` / `star`), `ledger_link_types.csv` (`look`), `ledger_actions.csv`, `ledger_loops.csv`, `ledger_palette.csv`, `ledger_static_nodes.csv` / `ledger_static_links.csv`, `balance.csv` `ledger_*`. A link not yet built is a `planned` row naming its milestone; a milestone turns it `live` and registers its reader.
- **Folding**: the economy folds by province — a manor, building or town outside a controlled or opened province becomes part of its province's star (`folded`), its links re-pointed and merged (`count`, flows summed only within one Intel level). FOLDING is open only as far as the code can place a star: `fold_to` may name only `province`, only a type in `Ledger.PLACED` may fold, a governed flow's type never folds. `Ledger.validate` names a bad row (also a cluster `colour` or a `lens` that does not exist).
- **Governed accounts**: a governed thread's accounts are its `claims` (the governor's word, then on an instance thread the receiver's count) plus the audit's. An audit counts only while `state.tick` is its letter's tick AND the governor's live claim equals the claim it recorded (`Ledger._audit_figure`); equal figures merge into one account naming every source, differing ones draw as separate strands. `SupplySystem.plan` / `WorkshopSystem.plan` are memoised for one snapshot (`LedgerResolvers.begin_memo` / `end_memo`). `Ledger.actions_for`'s `character=@council` rows expand to a "Give it to <name>" row per councillor but the flow's governor.
- **Governance links**: `governs` / `skims` are live link types (`LedgerResolvers._governs` / `_skims`). An empty seat still draws the type-level `governs` tie, `vacant`, grey, "no one governs it"; `governs` ties carry the audit and give orders (`ledger_actions.csv`); their card has the factors line ("Runs it at N% of what it could — skill S", the opinion only when it costs something — `held_back`; never the skim); a character's card folds his ties into one line per flow and appends his `records`. A `skims` link is drawn only once someone is caught: one per caught man and flow, on his own ego only (`GameState.caught`). An audit letter reads "N (he reported M)" (`MissiveLog.exact`).
- A garrison stands in a `town`, and `garrisons` runs regiment → town for an explicit GARRISON order only. The Court lens is the court's web (`RelationshipGraph`, unchanged).
- The view: `scripts/ledger3d/CLAUDE.md`.

## Hazards

`Hazards` (spec `2026-10-07-war-room-p2-tactical-layer-design.md` §2) are STATE: `GameState.hazards` (id → kind, label, cells, start, end, source), seeded once from the pack's zones (`seed_pack`, `hazards_seeded`), saved whole; `hazards_rev` (not saved) bumps on every change for the caches. What a kind does is `data/hazard_kinds.csv` (months, public, wounds, morale, supply loss, pace, ink). In the crown's realm every hazard is known; abroad only a `public` kind (`known`). The month's end runs `settle_regiments` after `ArmySystem.settle_month` and `settle_month` (endings, news only for a known one) after the day turns; a march's pace multiplies by `pace_permille`, a supply line's road loses its share, sampled every `war_hazard_sample_km` (`SupplySystem.hazard_loss`; an unknown one is "trouble on the road").

## Supply, morale and the war workshop

Spec `2026-10-07-army-supply-lines-design.md` (and its rulings).

- **Two reads per system** (`WorkshopSystem`, `SupplySystem`): `plan()` is the read-only month for card, LEDGER and audit; the truth is `SupplyLines.day_step` and `settle_month`.
- **The war workshop** (`war_workshop` building) turns up to `workshop_capacity_gold` of the LORD's civil goods a month into `workshop_ratio_permille` as much strategic goods at the `WORKSHOP_RATE` (`data/workshop_rates.csv`, `GameState.workshop_rate`, forgotten with the workshop or its manor; `rate_of` is full with none standing). Only on a manor the crown's own house holds (`WorkshopSystem.cells`; an implementer's ruling, unconfirmed).
- **Town stores** (`TownStores`, `GameState.town_stock`: burg → `{crown, lord, house}`, whole SUPPLY UNITS; `army_supply_gold_permille` is a unit's gold). `metrics.strategic` / `lord_strategic` are the `crown` / `lord` sums in gold, rounded down once (`sync_metrics`). `reconcile` absorbs any other writer's move on a metric: a rise lands in the DEPOT (the capital while held), a fall comes off the towns in proportion to their stock; a lost town takes its stores. `TickEngine` runs it after the month's writers and first in each `day_step`.
- **Catchment** (`TownStores.catchment`, cached): a manor feeds the NEAREST town of its province (ties to the lowest burg code; none there, the nearest realm town). **The month's deposit** (`deposit_month`, beside the purse lines that raise the metrics by the same gold): a manor in a crown-controlled province gives all its strategic goods to `lord`; any other realm manor gives `crown_share_permille` to `crown` (apportioned to equal `RealmEconomy.purses`) and the rest to `house`, which keeps at most `town_house_stock_months` of its inflow.
- **The day's order** in `TickEngine._end_day`: works, marches, couriers, then `SupplyLines.day_step` (reconcile, refills, each line's state, interception and delivery), `eat_day`, `hunger_day` (not for one that eats nothing), `SupplyLines.warn_day` (low / empty / town-low, each once till it recovers), ONE dispatch letter (`MissiveWriter.supply_dispatch`).
- **Refill in town**: a land regiment in a realm town (`SupplyLines.home_town`, any order) is topped up to its `carry_days` free of cartage and road: from its town (crown, lord, house bought; nothing past its own town is bought), then the depot (crown, lord), then the CROWN's stock of the other realm towns, nearest first. Past its town it is said once as `town_dry` (`supply_dry` / `supply_fed`).
- **Lines** (`SupplyLines`, `GameState.supply_lines`: one per regiment at most, whole numbers): ORDERED while the courier rides, RIDING while the first wagons roll (`ready_day`, `supply_wagon_km_per_day`), then FLOWING, or CUT on a day a hostile regiment (`data/army_hostile_stances.csv`) stands within `supply_cut_reach_m` of the road (`cut_point`; no rerouting; cut, it draws and pays nothing). The road (`road_px`) is re-aimed daily; a town is ELIGIBLE (`eligibility`) within `supply_point_max_days` wagon days. A line whose town leaves the realm is `cancelled` that day; an ORDERED line whose courier is lost is dropped (`order_lost`). A SET for the town a FLOWING or CUT line draws on changes only its rate.
- **Delivery and draw**: a flowing line sends `day_bite` of the rate's share of the month's use, never past `army_supply_max_days` after the meal. `_draw` takes `crown`, then `lord`, then BUYS the house's stock at its value plus `supply_buy_markup_permille` while the treasury pays. What arrives is thinned by the road's loss (`SupplySystem.total_loss`: `loss_of` its length plus the marks' and hazards' `road_loss`, once a line a day) and the marshal's efficiency, less his skim. CARTAGE is charged DAILY: `supply_cart_milligold_per_unit_km` per unit per km, whole gold as it crosses (shown in tenths). Every fraction is a MILLI remainder carried on the line (`*_milli`), so the month's draws equal `plan()`.
- **The marshal's month**: his daily counters (`m_*`) sit on the line (a retired line's, and the in-town refills', on the regiment's entry). `settle_month` writes one row per land regiment for his report (`MissiveWriter.marshal_report`: days, load, point, rate, km, cartage, road loss, units bought — his CLAIM), banks his skims (`Governance.add_skims`; a sub-gold fraction carries) and resets the counters. Supply letters keep their own count (`missive_keep_supply`, `MissiveLog.SUPPLY_KINDS`), never evicting a spy's report or receipt.
- **The three orders** `SUPPLY_POINT_SET` / `SUPPLY_POINT_CLEAR` / `SUPPLY_CARRY_SET` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows, offered only while paused, riding by courier: the first two to the TOWN (`to_town`), the carry order to the column; `CourierSystem.pending_for` (the march / halt reader) ignores them.
- **Morale**: the monthly drift (`army_morale_drift_per_month`) heads for a mark set by the days in hand as the month turns (`ArmySystem.morale_mark`: `army_morale_starving` … `army_morale_fed`, + `army_morale_garrison_bonus` under an explicit GARRISON order, `SupplySystem.garrisoned_in`). Under `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, `ArmySystem.hunger_day` takes `army_morale_hungry_day` a day (`army_morale_starving_day` at none). `TriggerDsl`'s `host_hungry` drives the LEDGER's `hungry_host` loop.

## Governors, skimming and the audit

- Each governed flow (`data/ledger_governance.csv`: the workshops under the steward, the supply lines under the marshal, `works` under the master of works, `trade_route` under the steward) runs as its governor runs it: `Governance.factors` (pure) — skill against the row's `skill_mid` sets the ceiling, opinion under `gov_opinion_cooperate` cuts it further, both clamped to `min_permille`/`max_permille`. Spec `2026-09-30-ledger-governors-design.md`.
- `Governance.skim_of`: a venal governor (trait flag `skims_treasury`) or one of `integrity` below `gov_skim_integrity_below` SKIMS a FIXED share, never at random; an honest one (`truthful_advice`) never does. He REPORTS the flow whole either way: each plan row gives `made` (the truth), `skimmed` and `claimed` (`== made`).
- `Governance.governor_of` falls back to the flow's SEAT holder the moment a given governor no longer sits on the council, and returns `""` for a seat naming no living character. `Governance.add_skims(state, rows, flow)` banks a skim into `GameState.skims[character][flow]` (whole gold, per flow; skips an ungoverned flow and a zero skim).
- `GOVERNOR_SET` gives a flow to another councillor (`gov_passed_over_opinion` / `gov_new_governor_opinion` charged in `execute()`; an unknown councillor refused). `LEDGER_AUDIT` (refused when no one governs the flow) files a credible `audit` missive of `AuditWriter.facts`' true figures beside each claim, and CATCHES every man with an uncaught skim on THAT flow: a `GameState.caught` entry, a permanent `GameState.records` line, `gov_caught_opinion`; only the governor NOW takes `gov_audited_opinion`. Opinions move before the letter is written. `governors` / `skims` / `caught` / `records` are saved.

## Industry and trade

- A building that transforms goods EATS its inputs (`data/building_inputs.csv`): `ManorEconomy.line_of` nets them against its manor's units first; `IndustrySystem` buys the rest from the lord's pool at the month's end, or the building stands IDLE next month (`GameState.idle_buildings`, part of `RealmEconomy.province_key`). A province's townsfolk speed its works (`MapManors.work_speed_permille`, `artisan_*`, scaled by the master of works); works accumulate `done` permille-days. Spec `2026-09-30-ledger-industry-trade-design.md`.
- The pack's markets and `deals.csv` are the realm's trade through LIVE ownership (`MarketSystem`, `DataRepository.deals`): a route per foreign buyer kingdom, `trade_toll_permille` of its value into the treasury, scaled and skimmed by the steward, closed or reopened by `TRADE_ROUTE_SET`. `GOODS_BUY` / `GOODS_SELL` trade at a market's basket price, BUY floored at `goods_trade_spread` under the catalogue (`MarketSystem.buy_permille`), SELL capped at the cheapest BUY anywhere and the market's basket less the spread (`sell_permille`) — no round trip is ever a sure profit.
- `WarEconomy` reads the same pack tables for the WAR ROOM only (works, markets, trade on the roads); it never feeds the economy — `ManorEconomy` and `MarketSystem` stay the source of every gold figure.
