## Manors and houses

- **Every land cell of the pack is a manor** with a proper name (`political/manors.csv`, forged by `NameForge` / `ManorNames` from the export's name banks; `manor_name_overrides.csv` is hand-maintained) held by a **house** (a family name; the crown is the player character's live family). An unmoved manor belongs to its province's house, so `GameState.manor_owner` holds only what moved, and `MapManors.set_owner` needs a reason (`manor_log`).
- **People are never stored and are WHOLE**: a province's ledger population is apportioned to its manors by area × land value (largest remainder, `MapManors.people_table`), so manors always sum EXACTLY to their province.
- **The economy is `ManorEconomy` and `RealmEconomy`, not `MapManors`.** `ManorEconomy.line_of` is one manor's MONTH — whole people, whole-gold tax (its town's tariff included), the goods it ships and their worth in the strategic and civil pools — THE ONLY ROUNDING IN THE ECONOMY. Its tax and tariff are the laws IN FORCE in the manor's province (`ManorEconomy.rates_of`, resolved once per province and passed to `line_of`; part of the province key), never `metrics.tax_rate` / `tariff_rate`, which only mirror the decree (a test scans for it); the unrest pressure reads `LawSystem.weighted_number`, the court's ration the grain law of `TickEngine.court_province`. `RealmEconomy` sums those lines into provinces and the realm (columnar, a province key as the dirty flag, the month's work spread over balanced day buckets; `line_of` / `lines_of_province` / `lines_table` / `tax_table` check the key once per bulk read, never per row), settled at the month's end into `metrics.treasury`, `metrics.strategic` and `metrics.grain` — the CIVIL pool, whose key stays `grain` for its readers. Spec `2026-09-14-economy-and-staging-design.md`.
- Buildings (`data/manor_buildings.csv`, priced in civil goods by `civil_cost`; `cost_gold` is unused), defence, value (`MapManors.value_of_line`) and the holding house's asking price (marked up by its councillor's opinion) are `MapManors`, every number `balance.csv` `manor_*`.
- **Orders** `MANOR_BUY` / `MANOR_SELL` / `MANOR_BUILD` / `MANOR_DEMOLISH` / `MANOR_FOCUS` / `MANOR_SEIZE` (a skeleton gated by `manor_seize_enabled`) resolve a manor by code, label or id (`MapManors.find`) and run from the manor card through `Game.try_execute`; no model call. `world_cell_index.png` is how a click finds its manor — only in the MANORS layer; elsewhere land means province.
- **Estates**: each manor without a town is an estate by its INDUSTRY and its people — `data/manor_estates.csv` gives each industry's `min_people` rows, `MapObjects.estate_tier(repo, focus, people)` picks the row (an industry with no rows wears the first industry's); the card names industry and size and paints the row's `painting`. Never place separate procedural farmsteads and fields by hash.
- **The player is the king of the realm and the lord of ONE province**: a province is controlled by the house holding the most of its manors (`MapManors.controller_of`, ties to the province's own house); the crown starts controlling the capital's province alone (`house_bindings.csv`). Everything that reads as the crown's economy — the lord's purse, the dashboard's people and provinces, `WorldLedger.belongs_to_player`, the order prompt's "Your provinces" — is `MapManors.controlled_provinces`; the host stays the realm's. BUILD and FOCUS need a controlled province, SEIZE any province of the realm.
- **Two purses** (MANOR_SYSTEM.md §5c): the CROWN's `treasury`/`strategic`/`grain` take `crown_share_permille` of the realm's other provinces and pay the court and the orders' fees; the LORD's `lord_gold`/`lord_strategic`/`lord_civil` take the controlled provinces in full, the harvest and the buildings, and pay every manor order inside the realm. `RealmEconomy.purses` is the one split.
- **Buying land** (`MapManors.purchase_kind`): your own province or a realm province bordering one, from the lord's purse; across the border only a manor whose cell touches the realm, from the treasury, annexed (`MapManors.annex`, `GameState.manor_province`, saved) into the bordering realm province with the most tax and held by its house; anything else refused. A manor's province is therefore STATE: `province_of` / `manors_of_province` take the state, and `MapOwnership.province_loops` merges annexed cells into the outlines by edge parity, so borders, seams, wash and `RealmEconomy`'s index follow.
- **Every manor works one chief good** (`data/manor_focus.csv`, allowed by biome and coast). The default FOLLOWS THE TERRAIN: the first row whose `default_on` claims the cell (`coast`, `high`/`peak` by `manor_focus_*_height`, or biome ids) and the land allows, else the first allowed. `GameState.manor_focus` holds only chosen ones with the tick, ramping in over `transition_ticks`. Yield is people × land value × `yield_per_1000` × the good's growth, whole units a month (`ManorEconomy.focus_units`), counted in the value; `MapEconomy.output_of_provinces(repo, provinces, state)` sums the same lines the tick settles.
- **Buildings take time and room**: `build_days` per building, works in `GameState.manor_works`, `manor_slots_base` (+`manor_slots_burg_bonus`) slots per manor; `MANOR_DEMOLISH` refunds `manor_demolish_refund` of `civil_cost` to the civil stores. Finished buildings add `*_per_tick` effects as ledger lines after the month's settlement and `produce_good/units` to the manor's output.
- **Building rules**: `manor_buildings.csv` has three `tier`s; `land` is tokens joined by `|` of which ONE must hold — `coast`, `high` / `peak`, `town`, `focus:<code>`, a biome id, or `any` (`MapManors.land_allows` / `describe_land`, the language of `manor_focus.csv` `default_on`) — and `prereq` names buildings that must ALL stand first (`building_prereqs`). `MapManors.building_blocked` gives the reason and `ActionSystem` refuses a MANOR_BUILD with it. Every building yields something (a test holds it).
- **`ManorBars`** (pure) gives the MANORS bars' figures: every manor of a province the crown controls exactly — wealth is its month's worth, tax and goods in gold (`worth_of`) — and any other manor only once a CREDIBLE spy's report on that very manor has come in, as the report's estimate (a manor report carries `worth`, `intel_facts.csv`).
- `MapEconomy` sums `economy/production.csv` (per burg) into provinces and provinces into realms **through live ownership**; `MapEconomy.burg_cells` are the working manors.

## Time

- **Time runs in days**: `GameState.day` (1..30) under the tick is the day NOW RUNNING — the date the top bar shows. `TickEngine.advance_day` ENDS it (works, marches, couriers, supply, the host eats) then turns the calendar; the thirtieth day ends inside the tick (`advance`), before the month settles. The reign's opening tick ends no day, so day 1 is played. The clock (`||` `>` `2x` `4x`, `time_day_seconds`, `time_speed_*`) is driven only by `GameRoot._process` (court AND council stage) through `Game.advance_day`. No End Turn.
- `Game.hold_clock(on, who)` is a SET of named holders (`holds_clock`, `release_clock_holds` on a new reign or restart): while any holds it, Space and the play buttons start nothing. The LEDGER and the WAR ROOM hold it while open; closing, like an order, starts nothing.

## Events and the prologue

- **The prologue is a CHAIN on the existing event system**, not a second system: its beats are in `data/chapter_events.csv` / `chapter_event_choices.csv` / `chapter_branches.csv`, prose and titles in `game_story/chapter_0/chapter_0_events.md` (`StorySource.DOCUMENTS` lists it and `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`; `### C0-NN` headings, the same `**Scene:**` contract), outcome lines in `templates.csv` as `event_c0_*`, illustrations library `V-35`..`V-55` installed to `assets/2D/events/illustrations/<event_id>.png`. Chapter beats are NOT in `data/events.csv` (the random roll samples it; a scripted beat must never be) but ARE in `events_by_id`; `EventSystem` reads `DataRepository.event_choices_all()`. `GameState.new_campaign` schedules whichever chapter row carries a `start_tick`. The chapter calls no model at all.
- Engine additions it relies on: `TriggerDsl`'s `never`, `!` negation and `trait_of=<seat or id>:<trait>`; `EventEffects`' `branch:<branch_id>` op (schedules the first `chapter_branches.csv` rule whose `requires` holds, at that rule's delay); the event title comes from the story document's heading (`event_titles`), never a `templates.csv` key (the content test forbids it).
- **An event waits as a councillor's matter; the clock runs on.** While one waits the engine rolls no other and holds any chapter beat behind it (`EventSystem.roll`). The presenter is `data/event_presenters.csv`'s seat for the event's id, else for its category, else the first `council_seats.csv` seat with `presents_events` 1 (`ConversationBeats.event_presenter`). How it is shown: `scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`.
- Events are fixed authored prose (root `CLAUDE.md`, `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`).

## Height

- One measure: `Elevation.metres_of_cell` — (cell height − `elevation_sea_level`) × `elevation_m_per_unit`, the same two keys `bake_world.py` builds the relief from.
- **Landmarks** are `Elevation.landmarks` (pure, cached): each cell a local extreme against its `landmark_ring` rings of neighbours by at least `landmark_min_prominence_m`, `lod` by `landmark_lod_far_m` / `_mid_m`, named after the nearest town from `data/landmark_names.csv` (a pack pins names in `landmark_name_overrides.csv`, reserved first). `MapObjects.landmark_lines` gives the tooltip and card.
- **Mountains are HEIGHT, not a biome**: `MapIndex.raise_high_ground` rewrites a land pixel's biome to 13 hill / 14 mountain / 15 peak by height (`map_biome_hill_height` / `_mountain_height` / `_peak_height`). `MapIndex.bake_relief` lays each cell's height plus seeded, domain-warped ridged detail (`map_relief_detail_*`, held down near rivers and the coast); the high-ground bands follow that detailed field.
- In war: `ArmySystem.height_factor` scales each side in `battle_odds` by the height DIFFERENCE (`army_height_bonus_permille_per_100m`, capped by `army_height_bonus_cap_permille`); supply use is × the tier's `supply_permille` in `data/army_height_supply.csv`, and the terrain's hill and mountain lines ARE those tiers (`Elevation.tier_of`).

## Couriers

- An order to a land regiment DISPATCHES a courier (`CourierSystem`, `GameState.couriers`, saved) from the capital (else the most populous town of the first province the realm holds) along the road network (`RoadGraph`: roads and trails, vertices snapped by `courier_graph_snap_px`, Dijkstra). The order takes effect only when he reaches the column, re-aiming his last leg as it moves. Spec `2026-09-27-courier-core-design.md`.
- **He arrives as the day ENDS**: an order on a paused clock moves nothing; once play runs he sets out (`CourierSystem.setting_out`) and rides EVENLY (`day_pace`: what is ahead over the whole days left, `days_for`, rounded up, never past `day_budget`), so an N-day trip delivers as the Nth day ends. `TickEngine` marches the columns BEFORE the couriers ride (in `advance_day` and the tick's day), so the order is marched on from the NEXT day.
- **Every day count shown counts day-ends, the running day first** (`eta_days` / `days_left` / `spy_eta_days` read the weather of `absolute_day()`). The arrival day shown for an order is ONE number, the ride then the march (`CourierSystem.arrival_days`; `days_to_arrival` counts a march order still riding). A higher courier id is a newer order; an older one arriving late is set aside.
- Pace: `courier_base_km_per_day` × `courier_kinds.csv` × the stretch's kind (`road_speed.csv`) × the height tier × the weather. His fee is a ledger line; fleets are exempt for now. `ride_ahead` / `day_ahead_of` preview the next day on a copy for the map.

## Weather

`Weather` is stateless: hashed from the seed per province per `weather_spell_days` spell, odds by season and tier in `weather_odds.csv`. It scales couriers and marches. The map only reads it (visuals: `scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md`).

## Intel, missives and spies

- **A FOG OF KNOWLEDGE, not of the map**: `Intel` is the one gate every card and panel asks — `full` (provinces the crown controls, its regiments) exact figures, `general` (the rest of the realm) words, `basic` (abroad) only holder, stance (`diplomacy.csv`) and wealth. The newest report on a place overrides the words with its estimate, tagged by source and date. DEV MODE (`Intel.dev_all`) lifts it. Spec `2026-09-28-missive-board-and-spies-design.md`.
- SPY couriers (`SPY_SEND`) ride the roads to a POINT and file a report (`SpySystem`): one quality roll from `GameState.rng` shifted by the place (`spy_difficulty.csv`). A report on a town's cell carries `town_people` (the town card's) beside the manor's `people`; a manor's `output` fact is its CHIEF good's units a month, named (`SpySystem.chief_output`).
- **Wealth tiers are STATE**: `GameState.wealth_tiers` (the provinces' terciles), settled by `TickEngine` after the month's economy and by `new_campaign`, computed once for an old save (`Intel.ensure_wealth_tiers`).
- Every letter is a MISSIVE (`MissiveLog`, `GameState.missives`, saved, capped per column): receipts, the steward's monthly report, spy reports, audit letters. `Intel._latest` skips any missive whose subject kind is `"ledger"`.

## Hazards

`Hazards` (spec `2026-10-07-war-room-p2-tactical-layer-design.md` §2) are STATE: `GameState.hazards` (id → kind, label, cells, start, end, source), seeded once from the pack's zones (`seed_pack`, `hazards_seeded`), saved whole; `hazards_rev` (not saved) bumps on every change for the caches. What a kind does is `data/hazard_kinds.csv` (months, public, wounds, morale, supply loss, pace, ink). In the crown's realm every hazard is known; abroad only a `public` kind (`known`). The month's end runs `settle_regiments` after `ArmySystem.settle_month` and `settle_month` (endings, news only for a known one) after the day turns; a march's pace multiplies by `pace_permille`, a supply line's road loses its share, sampled every `war_hazard_sample_km` (`SupplySystem.hazard_loss`; an unknown one is "trouble on the road").
