## Manors and houses

- **Every land cell of the pack is a manor** with a proper name (`political/manors.csv`, forged by `NameForge` / `ManorNames` from the export's name banks; `manor_name_overrides.csv` is hand-maintained) held by a **house** (a family name; the crown is the player character's live family). An unmoved manor belongs to its province's house, so `GameState.manor_owner` holds only what moved, and `MapManors.set_owner` needs a reason (`manor_log`).
- **People are never stored and are WHOLE**: a province's ledger population is apportioned to its manors by area × land value (largest remainder, `MapManors.people_table`), so manors always sum EXACTLY to their province.
- **The economy is `ManorEconomy` and `RealmEconomy`, not `MapManors`.** `ManorEconomy.line_of` is one manor's MONTH — whole people, whole-gold tax (its town's tariff included), the goods it ships and their worth in the strategic and civil pools — THE ONLY ROUNDING IN THE ECONOMY. Its tax and tariff are the laws IN FORCE in the manor's province (`ManorEconomy.rates_of`, resolved once per province and passed to `line_of`; part of the province key), never `metrics.tax_rate` / `tariff_rate`, which only mirror the decree (a test scans for it); the unrest pressure reads `LawSystem.weighted_number`, the court's ration the grain law of `TickEngine.court_province`. `RealmEconomy` sums those lines into provinces and the realm (columnar, a province key as the dirty flag, the month's work spread over balanced day buckets; `line_of` / `lines_of_province` / `lines_table` / `tax_table` check the key once per bulk read, never per row), settled at the month's end into `metrics.treasury`, `metrics.strategic` and `metrics.grain` — the CIVIL pool, whose key stays `grain` for its readers. Spec `2026-09-14-economy-and-staging-design.md`.
- Buildings (`data/economy/manor_buildings.csv`, priced in civil goods by `civil_cost`; `cost_gold` is unused), defence, value (`MapManors.value_of_line`) and the holding house's asking price (marked up by its councillor's opinion) are `MapManors`, every number `balance.csv` `manor_*`.
- **Orders** `MANOR_BUY` / `MANOR_SELL` / `MANOR_BUILD` / `MANOR_DEMOLISH` / `MANOR_FOCUS` / `MANOR_SEIZE` (a skeleton gated by `manor_seize_enabled`) resolve a manor by code, label or id (`MapManors.find`) and run from the manor card through `Game.try_execute`; no model call. `world_cell_index.png` is how a click finds its manor — only in the MANORS layer; elsewhere land means province.
- **Estates**: each manor without a town is an estate by its INDUSTRY and its people — `data/economy/manor_estates.csv` gives each industry's `min_people` rows, `MapObjects.estate_tier(repo, focus, people)` picks the row (an industry with no rows wears the first industry's); the card names industry and size and paints the row's `painting`. Never place separate procedural farmsteads and fields by hash.
- **The player is the king of the realm and the lord of ONE province**: a province is controlled by the house holding the most of its manors (`MapManors.controller_of`, ties to the province's own house); the crown starts controlling the capital's province alone (`house_bindings.csv`). Everything that reads as the crown's economy — the lord's purse, the dashboard's people and provinces, `WorldLedger.belongs_to_player`, the order prompt's "Your provinces" — is `MapManors.controlled_provinces`; the host stays the realm's. BUILD and FOCUS need a controlled province, SEIZE any province of the realm.
- **Two purses** (MANOR_SYSTEM.md §5c): the CROWN's `treasury`/`strategic`/`grain` take `crown_share_permille` of the realm's other provinces and pay the court and the orders' fees; the LORD's `lord_gold`/`lord_strategic`/`lord_civil` take the controlled provinces in full, the harvest and the buildings, and pay every manor order inside the realm. `RealmEconomy.purses` is the one split.
- **Buying land** (`MapManors.purchase_kind`): your own province or a realm province bordering one, from the lord's purse; across the border only a manor whose cell touches the realm, from the treasury, annexed (`MapManors.annex`, `GameState.manor_province`, saved) into the bordering realm province with the most tax and held by its house; anything else refused. A manor's province is therefore STATE: `province_of` / `manors_of_province` take the state, and `MapOwnership.province_loops` merges annexed cells into the outlines by edge parity, so borders, seams, wash and `RealmEconomy`'s index follow.
- **Every manor works one chief good** (`data/economy/manor_focus.csv`, allowed by biome and coast). The default FOLLOWS THE TERRAIN: the first row whose `default_on` claims the cell (`coast`, `high`/`peak` by `manor_focus_*_height`, or biome ids) and the land allows, else the first allowed. `GameState.manor_focus` holds only chosen ones with the tick, ramping in over `transition_ticks`. Yield is people × land value × `yield_per_1000` × the good's growth, whole units a month (`ManorEconomy.focus_units`), counted in the value; `MapEconomy.output_of_provinces(repo, provinces, state)` sums the same lines the tick settles.
- **Buildings take time and room**: `build_days` per building, works in `GameState.manor_works`, `manor_slots_base` (+`manor_slots_burg_bonus`) slots per manor; `MANOR_DEMOLISH` refunds `manor_demolish_refund` of `civil_cost` to the civil stores. Finished buildings add `*_per_tick` effects as ledger lines after the month's settlement and `produce_good/units` to the manor's output.
- **Building rules**: `manor_buildings.csv` has three `tier`s; `land` is tokens joined by `|` of which ONE must hold — `coast`, `high` / `peak`, `town`, `focus:<code>`, a biome id, or `any` (`MapManors.land_allows` / `describe_land`, the language of `manor_focus.csv` `default_on`) — and `prereq` names buildings that must ALL stand first (`building_prereqs`). `MapManors.building_blocked` gives the reason and `ActionSystem` refuses a MANOR_BUILD with it. Every building yields something (a test holds it).
- **`ManorBars`** (pure) gives the MANORS bars' figures: every manor of a province the crown controls exactly — wealth is its month's worth, tax and goods in gold (`worth_of`) — and any other manor only once a CREDIBLE spy's report on that very manor has come in, as the report's estimate (a manor report carries `worth`, `intel_facts.csv`).
- `MapEconomy` sums `economy/production.csv` (per burg) into provinces and provinces into realms **through live ownership**; `MapEconomy.burg_cells` are the working manors.

## Laws

Spec `2026-10-08-laws-tab-design.md`. The laws in `data/realm/laws.csv`, on three pages; a law's value is always a String (a range's digits, a toggle's or choice's name).

- **One way in**: `LawSystem.value(repo, state, law, scope_code)` (a province for a `province` law, a unit for a `camp` law; `number` for a range's int). No code reads `law_in_force` or the rate metrics otherwise (`tests/engine/test_law_guard.gd`, a source scan; the realm economy's `realm_signature` alone hashes them for its cache).
- **Decreed vs in force**: `GameState.law_decree` (law → {value, decree_id, day, sent}) is what the crown decreed; `law_in_force` (scope key → law → {value, decree_id}) what each place follows; `law_cooldown` (law → the tick it runs to). Scope keys: `province:<code>` (economy and political laws), `town:<code>` (a camp law in the camp round a held town), `unit:<code>` (a camp law in a land unit in the field or on the march). A place with no entry follows the law's default. A courier writes his place's entry only when his `decree_id` is HIGHER than the entry's (or the same decree with another value); else he is set aside as stale. A unit camped by a held town reads the higher of the town's entry and its own; settling into a camp (`ArmyGround.settle`, `take_seat` → `LawSystem.on_settle`) makes that its own, and leaving (`ArmyGround.leave` → `on_leave`, read while `camp_town` still names the camp) keeps it. Only a courier writes a town's entry. A camp no courier reached counts as told when every unit camped there reads the decree (`delivery`).
- **`LAW_SET {law, value}`** (`ActionSystem._decree` → `LawSystem.decree`): one ordinary courier to each province the realm holds (ending at its capital town), or for a camp law one to each held town with a camp of the realm's land units and one to each land unit camped by none (aimed at it, re-aimed as it moves); never a fleet. A place courier's target is the scope key (`CourierSystem.place_bound`: never lost for want of a regiment, never re-aimed). It pays the `order_costs` row, the law's `gold_cost` and `cost_ops` (the `EventEffects` op grammar) and a fee per courier, and starts the law's own cooldown (`cooldown_ticks`). **A re-send** — the decreed value again while `delivery(...).missing` is not empty — sends couriers only to the missing places with the SAME `decree_id`, pays only the order row and the fees, drifts no opinion and skips the cooldown. Refusals are `LawSystem.refusal_of` (keys worded by `law_words.csv` `refusal_*`); `preview` is the screen's cost; `delivery` / `untold` its counts and list.
- **The mirror**: `metrics.tax_rate`, `tariff_rate` and `grain_ration` hold the DECREED value for display (`LawSystem._mirror`, at decree time); no formula reads them as the rate in force. A state with no law state at all (a hand-built test one) reads them instead (R8). `LawSystem.ensure` seeds an old save from them at load.
- **Effects** are `data/realm/law_effects.csv` (`LawEffects`; columns `law_code,value,channel,target,amount,timing,share,reason`, a reason on every row). `amount` is a number or a `balance.csv` key (`-key` negates it). A `once` row (`share=people`) is paid as a province's courier CHANGES its value to the row's value (to pay it again the value must change first; a range law's once-row has a blank value and is paid per point of change), as that province's part of the old realm-wide total: the total is apportioned over the realm's provinces by people (`LawEffects.apportion`, largest remainder — whole units for treasury and grain, tenths otherwise — invariant 11), so delivered everywhere the parts sum to the total exactly; the shares are taken on the people at each delivery's moment, so a realm that changes mid-decree drifts from it. The lines gather under the month's one `LAW_EFFECTS` outcome. `monthly` rows: `share=unit` (`morale_mark`, `pace`) per land unit on its own value (`unit_mark` rounded once; `unit_pace_permille` multiplies `day_step_m` and `speed_km_per_day`); `share=army` (`month_lines`, after the regiments' month) scaled by the share of the realm's land men under the value, one rounding a line — tenths, or whole gold for the treasury, whose amount is per 1,000 men; `flag` rows change nothing (their system reads `LawSystem.value`). Every toggle defaults to its first value (`off`); only `quarter` give and `ransom` ransom pay rows undecreed. `LawEffects.summary` spells a value's rows for the screen. A monthly `share=flat` row on a metric channel is paid whole by `month_lines` while its value is decreed; a `dev_*` channel is never a metric (`DevelopmentSystem.settle_month`, from `TickEngine.advance`, pays it).
- **An event's op `law:<code>:<value>`** (`LawSystem.impose`; a range takes a signed step, `law:justice_severity:+1`, clamped) is a new decree in force at once everywhere it would be sent: no courier, no cooldown, no one-shot paid.
- **`LawSystem.reconcile`** (each day's end, after `ArmyGround.reconcile`) forgets a province the realm lost, a town lost or left without a camp (the realm's units still camped there keep what they read; another kingdom's never take a law), and the `unit:` entry of a unit gone or no longer the realm's land unit. A retaken or annexed province reads the default until a courier reaches it (R13).
- **Letters and news**: a decree files a `law` letter at once (`MissiveWriter.law_decree`: its couriers, the longest ride); when its last courier is home, set aside, lost or out of time, `LawSystem.close_rides` (after `CourierSystem.ride_day`) files the reach letter (`law_reach`: reached of its recipients now, the days since the decree's day, the places left out) once and marks that day's last event of it with `reach`. The `law` letters keep their own count (`missive_keep_law`, `MissiveLog.LAW_KINDS`). The news bar speaks through `CourierSystem.news`: a decree's couriers are ONE line as they set out and ONE when it closes ("in force everywhere", else how many it reached); their deliveries and weather say nothing; those set aside, lost or out of time in one batch are one counted line (a lone one its `headline`). `headline` still words a single law courier (the card).

## Time

- **Time runs in days**: `GameState.day` (1..30) under the tick is the day NOW RUNNING — the date the top bar shows. `TickEngine.resolve_day` ENDS it (works, marches, couriers, battles, supply and economy) then turns the calendar; the thirtieth day ends before the month settles. A saved `DayResolution` may return blocked at an encounter; acknowledgement and manual resume continue it exactly once (`guide/battle_and_ai.md`). The reign's opening tick ends no day, so day 1 is played. The clock (`||` `>` `2x` `4x`, `time_day_seconds`, `time_speed_*`) is driven only by `GameRoot._process` (court AND council stage) through `Game.advance_day`. No End Turn.
- `Game.hold_clock(on, who)` is a SET of named holders (`holds_clock`, `release_clock_holds` on a new reign or restart): while any holds it, Space and the play buttons start nothing. The LEDGER and the WAR ROOM hold it while open; closing, like an order, starts nothing.

## Events and the prologue

- **The prologue is a CHAIN on the existing event system**, not a second system: its beats are in `data/story/chapter_events.csv` / `chapter_event_choices.csv` / `chapter_branches.csv`, prose and titles in `game_story/chapter_0/chapter_0_events.md` (`StorySource.DOCUMENTS` lists it and `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`; `### C0-NN` headings, the same `**Scene:**` contract), outcome lines in `templates.csv` as `event_c0_*`, illustrations library `V-35`..`V-55` installed to `assets/2D/events/illustrations/<event_id>.png` (`V-56`, `war_01`'s, is planned in `event_art.csv`, not yet rendered). The war's beats (`war_*`, `game_story/chapter_0/war_events.md`, `### W-NN`) share the chapter tables but are scheduled by the engine (`DefeatSystem`), not the chain, and yield the month's roll to any due chain beat. Chapter beats are NOT in `data/story/events.csv` (the random roll samples it; a scripted beat must never be) but ARE in `events_by_id`; `EventSystem` reads `DataRepository.event_choices_all()`. `GameState.new_campaign` schedules whichever chapter row carries a `start_tick`. The chapter calls no model at all.
- Engine additions it relies on: `TriggerDsl`'s `never`, `!` negation and `trait_of=<seat or id>:<trait>`; `EventEffects`' `branch:<branch_id>` op (schedules the first `chapter_branches.csv` rule whose `requires` holds, at that rule's delay); the event title comes from the story document's heading (`event_titles`), never a `templates.csv` key (the content test forbids it).
- **An event waits as a councillor's matter; the clock runs on.** While one waits the engine rolls no other and holds any chapter beat behind it (`EventSystem.roll`). The presenter is `data/story/event_presenters.csv`'s seat for the event's id, else for its category, else the first `council_seats.csv` seat with `presents_events` 1 (`ConversationBeats.event_presenter`). How it is shown: `scripts/ui/CLAUDE.md`.
- Events are fixed authored prose (root `CLAUDE.md`, `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`). `trait_at_court` reads current `council` appointees plus `you`, unique and sorted; identity, nationality and stale seats do not imply presence. Missing people are skipped. `trait_of` remains an explicit person/seat query.

## Height

- One measure: `Elevation.metres_of_cell` — (cell height − `elevation_sea_level`) × `elevation_m_per_unit`, the same two keys `bake_world.py` builds the relief from.
- **Landmarks** are `Elevation.landmarks` (pure, cached): each cell a local extreme against its `landmark_ring` rings of neighbours by at least `landmark_min_prominence_m`, `lod` by `landmark_lod_far_m` / `_mid_m`, named after the nearest town from `data/world/landmark_names.csv` (a pack pins names in `landmark_name_overrides.csv`, reserved first). `MapObjects.landmark_lines` gives the tooltip and card.
- **Mountains are HEIGHT, not a biome**: `MapIndex.raise_high_ground` rewrites a land pixel's biome to 13 hill / 14 mountain / 15 peak by height (`map_biome_hill_height` / `_mountain_height` / `_peak_height`). `MapIndex.bake_relief` lays each cell's height plus seeded, domain-warped ridged detail (`map_relief_detail_*`, held down near rivers and the coast); the high-ground bands follow that detailed field.
- In war: `ArmySystem.height_factor` scales each side in `battle_odds` by the height DIFFERENCE (`army_height_bonus_permille_per_100m`, capped by `army_height_bonus_cap_permille`); supply use is × the tier's `supply_permille` in `data/army/army_height_supply.csv`, and the terrain's hill and mountain lines ARE those tiers (`Elevation.tier_of`).

## Couriers

- `GameState.king_state` is the king's saved whole-metre position, route, follow target, travel state and last advanced day; only `KingSystem.step_day` advances him, once per absolute day, from the army balance pace. `KING_MOVE` and `KING_FOLLOW` are typed actions, not entries in the manual regiment map menu. `KingSystem.present` checks actual distance to the active battle; passive morale support is derived while in range and never accumulates. A safe departure changes only the king's route. Spec `2026-10-16-battle-system-design` §11.
- A battle outside the king's physical command radius files its ordinary battle letter immediately and sends a paid `king_report` courier from the battle event's saved metre coordinates. The rider follows the king's current position, replanning each day from its own current position; delivery files a separate credible report. Both the rider and king route round-trip through `GameState` save data.
- An order to a land regiment DISPATCHES a courier (`CourierSystem`, `GameState.couriers`, saved) from the king's saved physical position (`KingSystem.position`, whole metres); a new reign starts at the bound capital and an old save without this state is initialized there. The courier takes the network's fastest route (`RoadGraph.courier_of_pack`: roads, trails and RIVERS at `road_speed.csv`'s `river` pace, vertices snapped by `courier_graph_snap_px`, Dijkstra). An edge over cells steeper than `army_max_slope` goes round as `offroad` / `sea` stretches (its cost their sum); legs off the network use `MarchPath.of_pack(repo, true)`, the grid where water is OPEN (armies keep the land grid), water at `sea` pace; a re-aim plans the off-road tail again (`RoadGraph.across`). The drawn network (`RoadGraph.of_pack`) stays for trade. Dispatch saves its origin and path, so later king movement cannot reset a courier already on the road. The order takes effect only when he reaches the column, re-aiming his last leg as it moves. The king does not move the capital's stores. Spec `2026-09-27-courier-core-design.md` and `2026-10-16-battle-system-design.md` §11.
- **He arrives as the day ENDS**: an order on a paused clock moves nothing; once play runs he sets out (`CourierSystem.setting_out`) and rides EVENLY (`day_pace`: what is ahead over the whole days left, `days_for`, rounded up, never past `day_budget`), so an N-day trip delivers as the Nth day ends. `TickEngine` marches the columns BEFORE the couriers ride (in `advance_day` and the tick's day), so the order is marched on from the NEXT day.
- **Every day count shown counts day-ends, the running day first** (`eta_days` / `days_left` / `spy_eta_days` read the weather of `absolute_day()`). The arrival day shown for an order is ONE number, the ride then the march (`CourierSystem.arrival_days`; `days_to_arrival` counts a march order still riding). A higher courier id is a newer order; an older one arriving late is set aside.
- **One courier per target batch** (owner 2026-10-10): a courier holds `orders` (run in sequence on delivery; an old save's `order` loads as `orders` of one; read through `orders_of` / `first_order` / `order_with` / `march_order`, never a key). An order for a regiment whose newest courier waits to set out (same kind, issued today, no day ridden, bound for the column; never a spy, a decree or a town-bound supply order) joins him (`joinable`): no second fee (`ActionSystem._send_courier`), ONE receipt listing every order. The march's confirm and sealed drafts send supply point (`with_column`), load and march so.
- Pace: `courier_base_km_per_day` × `courier_kinds.csv` × the stretch's kind (`road_speed.csv`) × the height tier × the weather. His fee is a ledger line; fleets are exempt for now. `ride_ahead` / `day_ahead_of` preview the next day on a copy for the map.

## Weather

`Weather` is stateless: hashed from the seed per province per `weather_spell_days` spell, odds by season and tier in `weather_odds.csv`. It scales couriers and marches. The map only reads it (visuals: `scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md`).

## Intel, missives and spies

- **A FOG OF KNOWLEDGE, not of the map**: `Intel` is the one gate every card and panel asks — `full` (provinces the crown controls, its regiments) exact figures, `general` (the rest of the realm) words, `basic` (abroad) only holder, stance (`diplomacy.csv`) and wealth. The newest report on a place overrides the words with its estimate, tagged by source and date. DEV MODE (`Intel.dev_all`) lifts it. Spec `2026-09-28-missive-board-and-spies-design.md`.
- A spy is refused (`SpySystem.is_controlled`, ONE predicate for `ActionSystem.validate` and `quality()`; key `spy_refuse_controlled`) at a point in a province the crown CONTROLS (`MapManors.controlled_provinces`): its facts are exact. The LEDGER offers no spy row on such a province or town. Foreign, lost and wild ground are allowed.
- SPY couriers (`SPY_SEND`) ride the roads to a POINT and file a report (`SpySystem`): one quality roll from `GameState.rng` shifted by the place (`spy_difficulty.csv`). A report on a town's cell carries `town_people` (the town card's) beside the manor's `people`; a manor's `output` fact is its CHIEF good's units a month, named (`SpySystem.chief_output`).
- **Wealth tiers are STATE**: `GameState.wealth_tiers` (the provinces' terciles), settled by `TickEngine` after the month's economy and by `new_campaign`, computed once for an old save (`Intel.ensure_wealth_tiers`).
- Every letter is a MISSIVE (`MissiveLog`, `GameState.missives`, saved, capped per column): receipts, the steward's monthly report, spy reports, audit letters, a decree's letters. **The crown's own acts are `truth`** (owner 2026-10-10): receipts, supply dispatches and the marshal's month, decree letters, audits, beside the monthly report; battle reports and spy reports never; the one truth letter that is not the crown's own act is a CONTACT letter (a foe within `battle_contact_km` of a unit of ours: seen with our own eyes, `guide/sight_and_ghosts.md`). A kind in `MissiveLog.SUPPLY_KINDS`, `BATTLE_KINDS`, `LAW_KINDS`, `RECEIPT_KINDS` or `SCOUT_KINDS` (scout, contact) counts against its own keep key within the column (`keep_group`: `missive_keep_supply` / `_battle` / `_law` / `_receipt` / `_scout`), never the column's. `Intel._latest` skips any missive whose subject kind is `"ledger"`. The MISSIVE tab counts letters not yet SEEN (`MissiveLog.unseen`: ids above the saved `missives_seen_id`, or one marked `seen` alone), marked by `Game.mark_missives_seen` when a board is shown or a spy's report opens; `read` is only the note's gold edge.

## Hazards

`Hazards` (spec `2026-10-07-war-room-p2-tactical-layer-design.md` §2) are STATE: `GameState.hazards` (id → kind, label, cells, start, end, source), seeded once from the pack's zones (`seed_pack`, `hazards_seeded`), saved whole; `hazards_rev` (not saved) bumps on every change for the caches. What a kind does is `data/army/hazard_kinds.csv` (months, public, wounds, morale, supply loss, pace, ink). In the crown's realm every hazard is known; abroad only a `public` kind (`known`). The month's end runs `settle_regiments` after `ArmySystem.settle_month` and `settle_month` (endings, news only for a known one) after the day turns; a march's pace multiplies by `pace_permille`, a supply line's road loses its share, sampled every `war_hazard_sample_km` (`SupplySystem.hazard_loss`; an unknown one is "trouble on the road").
