# Battles and the enemy

## Saved day resolution

`TickEngine.resolve_day` owns the JSON-saved day transaction. Frozen daily march budgets distribute over ten substeps: move columns, reconcile ground/law, synchronize the king, advance host arrivals, discover contacts. Acknowledge each encounter before THAT battle's round; earlier substeps may already have fought. Battles run once daily in ID order; later reinforcements join continuing battles for tomorrow. Ended battles disappear before later contact. `BattleEvents.offer` precedes each first daily round; blocked/error work retains its cursor. `finish_day` applies formations/stances once after all substeps, before siege/supply. Validate protocol/order before mutation and preserve saved month-end intent. Enemy arrival substeps never feed or think.


Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §4. Every number is a `battle/balance.csv` `battle_*` key or a row of `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv` / `battle_stances.csv`; every figure whole. Battle strength, orders, saved random draws and daily resolution follow `battle_deployment.md` below.

Field camps are saved in each unit's `camp_state`. `BattleSystem.side_power` applies one camp defence factor only to a defender still at its saved site; stance defence remains a separate factor. Building progress survives combat, and packing retains reduced defence. A packing unit remains contactable even when its host is homeward or it is marked disengaging. Retreat, AI re-aim, and courier orders preserve the saved departure deadline and pass through `CampSystem.can_march` before movement.

- **Who fights**: crown land regiments against foreign land regiments with fit personnel. Withdrawing, routed or homeward columns remain contactable until actual distance separates them. Neither `disengaging` nor legacy `disengaged_until` grants immunity.
- **A round**: `BattleStrength.unit` builds equipment and identity once, then `combine` applies fixed categories with integer truncation, unique explanatory sources and CSV bounds. `BattleSystem.odds_permille`, `eff_permille`, `tier_of`, `split_losses` and `apportion` retain whole-number arithmetic. A pending round stores one `GameState.rng` draw and its planned result before atomic personnel writes; error retries and JSON loads reuse it. Costs and round progress commit only after personnel succeeds.
- **A battle** is a saved dictionary in `GameState.battles`: participants, contact place/day, engagement rounds, frozen agreements, formations, delivered actions, inspiration, pending round and last resolved day. `prepare_day` detects the first substep’s contacts; later substeps discover new contacts. `step_battle` runs each ID at most once daily; `finish_day` advances deployments and delivered stances once after all substeps. Acknowledging an encounter resumes time without issuing attack. Both holding consume ordinary food and receive couriers/reinforcements without casualties, rounds or random draws.
- **Merging** (`BattleMerge.day`, from `prepare_day` after the deploy and group refresh, before withdrawals and detection): battles A < B merge when a crown group and a foe group, one from each, stand within `battle_group_contact_m` (inclusive); anchors, boxes, circles and same-allegiance proximity never merge, nor two town battles, a battle mid-round (`pending_round`) or one with an empty side. The lower id survives unless only the other holds a `town`; it repeats until no pair is left. Units are unioned by allegiance (`_side_for`), every per-unit or accumulated record (`personnel_entries`, `unit_entries`, `unit_formations`, `reserves`, `abandoned_supplies`, `event_checks`, evidence, costs, modifiers, reinforcement and journal lists) is folded in (the survivor's entry wins, an entry's `side` is re-read), `rounds`, `last_resolved_day` and `deployed_day` take the larger and `start_day` the smaller, so nothing reruns and no group fights twice a day. The absorbed side-level state (`parley`, `commanders`, `actions`, `formations`, `routed_side`, inspiration, place, start day) stays verbatim under `sources[<id>]`; each agreement keeps its own `parties` (`legal_actions` scopes terms to them, see battle_deployment). The absorbed id lives on in `GameState.battle_aliases` (saved, default `{}`): `BattleSystem.canonical` and `battle_by_id` follow it, so tasks, warrants, reports and offers holding the old id reach the survivor. One `{kind: "merge", battle_id, absorbed, reason}` event is journaled. A siege and its assault are untouched. The merge also picks ONE commander per side (`BattleCommand.unify`): the two sources' commanders still eligible, a valid explicit nominee first (both: newer `commander_nominee_seq`, then smaller id), else `select`; journaled in `command_unions`, the other commands his own unit, a pending warrant from him is refused at delivery, and only incapacity re-elects afterwards. A pending side warrant of the absorbed battle gets `request.side` rewritten to its units' survivor side with a `merge_remap` note (the original request still re-issues as the same warrant), and `BattlePresentation` compares stored battle ids through `canonical`.
- **Contact**: in the open, a foe and a player regiment within `battle_contact_km` (inclusive); at a town, a foe within the town's `army_town_reach.csv` reach of a held town a player unit GARRISONS (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`: its seat, its camp, or a unit under a garrison order inside its reach — `siege.md`) — a column merely standing by there, off its seat and camp, is no garrison. At a town the foe attacks and the battle is fought at the town's point, ONE battle a town: a town contact joins the battle already fought there (each side's cap holding; one left out waits) and never starts a second; in the open the side that marched into a standing enemy attacks, else the foe, fought where the defender stands. A regiment not yet in a battle that touches one already fighting JOINS it (`BattleSystem.join`, its own allegiance's side, at most `battle_max_units` a side); a full side leaves it out. A foe whose host (`foes[code].host`) is in mode `DEPLOYING` or `SIEGE` makes no contact with the besieged town's GARRISON (`BattleSystem.siege_town_of`: the host's `target_id` while its `assault` flag is down) — the garrison waits behind its walls for the assault, which is this same town battle on the day `ai_hosts[host].assault == 1`. A raised assault flag does not turn a contact with an outer camp into a town battle: only the town's actual reach or seat-clearance contact establishes that battle; an outer-camp victory cannot breach the walls. Any other regiment of the player's (a relief column in the open, a column merely standing by) meets the besiegers as ever: a field battle, which besiegers within reach join; the garrison never sorties into it. Contact and joining HALT the regiment: its order becomes garrison where it stands (`_halt` — a STANDBY column too, so in a held town it is then a garrison), and a column in a battle does not march.
- **Leaving**: legal attack consumes extra carried supplies for each actual attack day; shortages fall back to hold with a reason. Morale collapse, no fighting personnel, surrender and actual separation end combat; there is no round cap. A retreat uses a reachable held town beyond contact range and existing march/camp gates, remaining in the same battle until all opposing units are out of contact. An unreachable destination leaves the unit exposed. Host withdrawal requests this same route rather than removing its units. Pursuit must be lawful and requires attacking; hold gives up pursuit, and protected withdrawal suppresses its casualties. Equipment type selects melee/ranged/shock context; retreat selects withdrawal/pursuit context.
- **Stances** (`data/battle/battle_stances.csv`): a regiment's state carries `stance` (`ArmySystem.STANCE_DEFAULT` steady; an old save reads and is seeded steady). `ARMY_STANCE {regiment, stance}` is an order like ARMY_GARRISON — validated as the player's own land regiment (never a foe), a stance of the table, a courier the chest can pay; it rides by courier. He lands it as the day ends into `stance_next` (newest wins by `stance_order_id`, apart from the march's `order_id`; `CourierSystem.pending_for` ignores it), and `settle_stances` makes it the stance at the end of that day's battles: the day he lands still fights the old stance, the next day the new. The map menu has no stance buttons; stance changes come through royal orders and front drafts. A FRONT's draft (spec `2026-10-11-war-room-front-line-design.md`) sends `ARMY_STANCE {stance, facing, on_arrival}` on the march's courier: `facing` is a whole degree 0..359 (`WarFront`'s frame, validated), `on_arrival` parks it on the column as `hold_after` {stance, facing, id} (stale with its march) and `ArmySystem.march_day` takes it the day the column arrives — the facing always (`facing_deg`), the stance only when no newer `stance_order_id` has come (`apply_hold`); `set_march_m`, `aim_entry`, `set_garrison` and a battle's `_halt` drop a parked hold (`drop_hold`: caught on the road, no front), a new march also clears `facing_deg`. `ArmySystem.facing_of` (ZERO while marching) / `piece_heading` are what the pieces turn to.
- **The day hook and report**: `TickEngine.resolve_day` offers saved decisions before each battle's round (`battle_events.md`). Public reports use actual engine events and reasons, fogging enemy losses. Local observation files immediately; immutable remote facts and event receipts publish only when their `king_report` courier arrives. Siege/raid notices retain their separate route. Battle letters retain `missive_keep_battle`.

- **The battle stance** of each of the CROWN's regiments is a `Battle stance` field on its card (`MapObjects.army_card`) and a row of the folio's march page (`WarFolio`), by its `battle_stances.csv` label with a stance landed for tomorrow beside it (`BattleSystem.stance_words`); never a foe's. ("Stance" alone is the realms' diplomatic word.)
- **Under arms** on the regiment card and folio is active living personnel (fit plus wounded), excluding captives, released civilians and dead people. Supply uses the same living people with identity weights and current prisoner burden, never equipment capacity as a live population.

- **The hosts take the field** (`EnemyAi`, pure, no random number): `GameState.new_campaign` calls `EnemyAi.spawn(repo, state, row)` for every `repo.raid_hosts` row AFTER the army split (`start_x` / `start_y` are PACK pixels, stored as whole metres via `ArmySystem.pack_to_metres`). A row's `regiments` are pack regiment codes of the raider realm; each raises the UNITS split from it (`EnemyAi.host_units`: a code the roster has is that unit, else every land unit whose `parent_code` it is — its guns stay in the kingdom's royal train, which no host takes — in code order, none twice). Each unit becomes a `GameState.foes` entry keyed by its unit code — `ArmySystem.starting_state` plus `host`, `camp_town` "" — standing at the assembly point with `order` standby; a seat it held is emptied and refilled from its old camp (`ArmyGround.refill`), and `ArmyGround.seated_in` / `camped_by` / `garrison_town` read nothing for a foe, and the host a `GameState.ai_hosts[code]` mind `{mode: ASSEMBLE, target_kind, target_id, think_day, plunder, siege_progress}`. A save from before the hosts has no foes and none are made for it: such a campaign never gets the invasion (a release note, not a migration). `EnemyAi.step_day` turns a host `ASSEMBLE` → `ADVANCE` on the absolute day `raid_hosts.appear_day` (an event `host_advance`, once); `host_codes` lists the minds in code order and `regiments_of(state, host)` a host's foes in code order.
- **A foe is placed once**: its roster row (`MapObjects.regiments_of(raider, state)`, the map in DEV MODE, the supply lines' hostile list, the camps' taken ground, a spy's count) is only where the split put it; every reader asks `ArmySystem.position_of`, which prefers `GameState.foes`, so the unit is at its foe place and nowhere else. Never put a foe in `regiments`, on a town's seat or in a camp.
- **A foe marches** after crown columns in each `ArmySystem.march_day_saved` substep, in code order, using its frozen daily budget: any foe under `order == march` outside battle or on its validated withdrawal route — a column that fell back (`disengaging`) is walked home by it too. On arrival the order becomes garrison; a foe's arrival is not in the returned list (`TickEngine.day_arrivals` is the player's news). Setting a foe's destination (and a retreat's) is `EnemyAi`'s, never a courier's. Foes eat nothing in `eat_day` / `hunger_day` (they iterate `state.regiments`); a foe's supply is `EnemyAi`'s own (below).
- **A foe's card is fogged**: `MapObjects.army_card` reads a regiment's state only for a viewer who knows it `full` (`Intel.level`: the player's own, or dev mode); a foe's card shows the headcount as a word and none of the inner figures (morale, stores, training, the men under arms).
- **The crown sees a foe** only within `player_sight_km` of its land units and held towns, and keeps what it last knew as GHOSTS (`Sighting`, `GameState.sightings`; the rumour's host ghost, a spy's columns, the pre-movement scout phase and post-battle `Sighting.update` in `TickEngine.resolve_day`): `scripts/engine/guide/sight_and_ghosts.md`.

## The hosts' minds (`EnemyAi`, spec §3)

- **Modes**: `ASSEMBLE` → `ADVANCE` (on `appear_day`) → `DEPLOYING` at a defended town, then `SIEGE` once an arrived column takes up position; undefended targets use `RAID`; `HOLD` after a capture (SiegeSystem: it occupies the town's manors and stays in the town, eats nothing of its own — it lives off the town — never thinks again and is never re-targeted; a TARGETING mode, so beaten or broken in morale it still turns RETREAT, but never for hunger); `RETREAT` (beaten, morale, hunger) and `WITHDRAW` (nothing worth taking, or the target lost) march the host to its start point (`home_m`, the row's PACK point in metres) and choose nothing more. `TARGETING` = ADVANCE, DEPLOYING, SIEGE, RAID, HOLD; `HOMEWARD` = RETREAT, WITHDRAW.
- **`EnemyAi.step_day`**, per host in code order: an assembling host waits (fed at home); field hosts reserve the day-end meal and anticipate it for retreat checks; `EnemyAi.eat_day` deducts it once after battles and supply; a TARGETING host whose `should_retreat` gives a reason turns RETREAT; an ADVANCING host with a target checks `arrival_mode`; then `_march_host` sends every idle column on — an advancing, besieging or raiding host's for its target (the columns behind the first come up), a homeward host's home (a column falling back from a battle too: the host owns its retreat) — never one in a battle (an order would take it out of the fight), and an advancing host never redirects a `disengaging` one. Last, a reserve besieging or holding beside a main host that turned home today follows it at once (`SiegeSystem.follow_main`), so it starts its physical withdrawal before that day's battles. Events: `host_advance`, and `host_mode {host, mode, why, day}` for every change of mode (`think`'s WITHDRAW included: `think` / `think_day` return their events). It runs exactly once in the daily AI phase; arrival substeps update physical arrivals and deployment without repeating meals or thinking.
- **The score** (`EnemyAi.score`): `value × greed_permille / 1000 − distance_days × dist_cost − defence × caution_permille / 1000 − overlap`, whole gold, each per-mille term rounded down. The doctrine is the host's `role` row of `data/battle/ai_doctrines.csv` (`doctrine_of`). `value` = `MapManors.value_of_line` rounded, plus the doctrine's `town_bonus` for a town (`target_value`); `overlap` = `ai_target_overlap_permille` of the score before it, never below 0 (`overlap_of`), paid only on a target another non-reserve host in a TARGETING mode holds (`taken_by_others`). At 1000 (the data's value) it is a BAN: a taken target scores 0 and `rank` never takes a target at 0, however rich, so two hosts never share one. `rank` takes the highest, ties to the earlier id (string order, then kind); a best not above 0 is no target.
- **Candidates** (`EnemyAi.candidates`): every manor of a province the player's realm holds (`MapOwnership.provinces_of`, `MapManors.manors_of_province` with the state) whose point lies within `ai_target_reach_km` of the host's ANCHOR — its slowest live column (`anchor_of`). A manor carrying a burg is the TOWN (kind `town`, id the burg code, point the burg); any other is kind `manor`, id its cell id, point the cell centre (`target_point_m`; `target_cell` maps a town back to its manor cell, cached per repository AND `map_id`). `distance_days` is `ArmySystem.march_days_between` for the anchor, walked at most `supply_reach_days` + 1 days (its `max_days`: further is no candidate however far, so the walk never runs to `army_march_days_cap`); a target more days away than the host's `supply_reach_days` (its hungriest live column's `days_in_hand` less `ai_retreat_supply_days` less `ai_supply_margin_days`, the meals' whole-unit rounding) is no candidate — it would turn home hungry on the road — and `_choose` skips it the same way; so is a manor a raid has stripped bare (`RaidSystem.stripped`). `defence` is the summed `ArmySystem.power(…, "defend")` — the town's works (`army_town_defence.csv`) are inside it — of the player's regiments the host SEES that stand at the target (`defenders_of`: within `battle_contact_km` of the point, or in a town's reach).
- **Sight** (`seen_by`): the player's land regiments with fit men within `ai_sight_km` of any live column of THAT host. A garrison out of sight is no defence; towns and manors are known by reach alone.
- **Thinking** (`EnemyAi.think`, `think_day` runs the hosts with targets of their own in code order, then the reserves): only an ADVANCING host thinks. The `reserve` follows on EVERY call (no cadence): it takes the first main host's target (WITHDRAWs when the main host goes home, waits while it has none) and counts as no overlap. Any other host chooses only with no target or once `ai_think_days` have passed since `think_day` — between, it keeps its target. Any other host takes `rank`'s best; none above 0 → WITHDRAW (target cleared). A choice orders every column at once (no courier). `_choose` is `rank(candidates(…))` with the march days walked only where they can matter (candidates in order of their score at no distance, stopping at the first whose bound is below the best found) — keep the two picking the same (a test holds it). No random number anywhere; `GameState.rng` is never drawn.
- **Taking contact**: `BattleSystem._start` calls `EnemyAi.engages` against opposition the host can see. Its doctrine threshold selects attack or a standoff; a received advance plan may initiate a field fight. Legal actions are checked again before combat. A homeward host orders retreat.
- **Retreat** (`EnemyAi.should_retreat`, a reason or ""): a column of the host `disengaging` (fell back beaten from a battle); the live columns' MEAN morale below the doctrine's `retreat_morale`; or the hungriest live column's `ArmySystem.days_in_hand` below `ai_retreat_supply_days` (not for a HOLD host). Leaving DEPLOYING/SIEGE for a mode outside those two clears the siege's figures and assault flag at once (`EnemyAi.set_mode` → `SiegeSystem.clear`). Every mode change — EnemyAi's and SiegeSystem's — goes through `set_mode` and is a `host_mode` event with its why; the siege's land on `TickEngine.day_ai` too.
- **Arrival and deployment** (`EnemyAi.arrival_mode`, `ensure_deployments`): the first live column contacting a defended town starts `DEPLOYING`. Its `deployment {town, started_day, complete, completed_day}` records the establishment of a physical siege, not a roll call. `_march_host` and each tactical arrival substep station arrived columns and clear their obsolete routes. Stationing retires that column’s saved daily march immediately; distant columns retain their frozen routes and budgets. Existing sieges station later reinforcements without changing the completed deployment record. A live, non-disengaging column actually under a garrison order at the target completes deployment; this may occur in the same day, with no extra delay. Later arrivals do not restart the siege; only deployed columns contribute power. Required-ID lists in old records do not gate progress. Deployment suppresses automatic isolated assaults on the target's garrison, while relief troops and sorties into the field still fight normally. An incomplete deployment grants no progress, siege days, blanket supply cut or capture. A non-reserve host finding another non-reserve deployment/siege there chooses another target; a reserve may join. An open target follows the existing RAID/LOST arrival rules.
- **Legacy deployment**: `ensure_deployments(repo, state)` reconstructs a missing deployment from actual positions without advancing time or changing `day_resolution`. A legacy siege with no physically deployed column becomes DEPLOYING while retaining progress and days; its armed assault becomes owed, with execution no earlier than the day after completion. Completed deployment records survive JSON saves.
- **Supply**: a host starts with its foes' `army_start_supply_days` and eats from the day after it sets out; nothing refills it but raiding (`RaidSystem`, below), so a long siege starves the besieger into RETREAT.

## The invasion on the day loop (spec §2, §7)

- **The day order** in `TickEngine.resolve_day`: works → freeze march routes/budgets → couriers → daily host steps/thinking/warnings → scouts → ten tactical substeps → once-daily battle finish → sieges → raids → camps/sight → supply, meals, hunger, conduct/recruitment → economy → reports → month settlement when due → calendar. Each substep moves columns, reconciles ground/law, advances the king before contact, and handles host arrivals, contacts and battles. Each encounter blocks immediately before that battle’s first daily round; earlier battles may already have resolved. Only unprocessed battles run in stable ID order, and later reinforcements wait for tomorrow’s round. Host events collect in `day_ai`, sieges in `day_siege`, raids in `day_raid`; reports use their saved events.
- **The whole of it** is held by `tests/engine/test_invasion_end_to_end.gd` (a weakly held town falls and costs the crown; a strongly held walled one outlasts the host; both deterministic across a mid-run save) and printed day by day by `tools/sim_invasion.gd` (`tools/CLAUDE.md`). A host that sees a strong garrison at its town re-targets at its next think (`ai_think_days`) and raids elsewhere; the strong-town siege is played with the plan held.
- **The rumour** (`EnemyAi.warnings`, the letters to file today): on the absolute day `raid_rumour_lead_days` before the earliest `appear_day` among the hosts the campaign HAS (`state.ai_hosts`; a save without hosts hears nothing), one letter `MissiveWriter.raid_rumour` — kind and column `rumour`, subject the point where the first host to cross gathers (its `home_m`), the place named by `Intel.place_label`, no figure. Equality with today makes it once.
- **The scout**: `SpySystem.report` adds `foe_columns` and `foe_troops` (`intel_facts.csv`) when any foe stands within `spy_radius_km` of his point (`SpySystem.foes_near`: the foes' live places, men under arms = pack headcount less `dead`, a column with none left uncounted) — estimates by the spy's own quality and bias like every other fact, so a credible spy brackets the truth and a poor one is a rumour. With no foe in reach the report has no foe fact. `troops_own` / `troops_foreign` (`SpySystem.troops_near`) count men under arms the same way (headcount less `dead`, each regiment at its live place), so beside a host the foreign troops include exactly the foe troops.
- **The host news** (`MissiveWriter.host_news`): a day with a `host_advance` or a `host_mode` of DEPLOYING, SIEGE, RAID, WITHDRAW or RETREAT files ONE letter, kind `host_news`, a line per event (`templates.csv` `host_news_*`): where — a siege's or a raid's target, else the host's first column — and never a figure or the engine's `why` (the enemy's morale and stores are not the crown's to read). Column `credible` when a siege or a raid is in it (the crown's own people see it), else `rumour`. A `host_mode` ADVANCE (setting out again from a stripped manor) is no news. Fights are the battle report's.
- **The words** are fixed prose in `templates.csv` (`raid_rumour*`, `host_news_*`, filled through `MissiveWriter.battle_words` with the realm slots); no model sees a warning or a number.

## Resumable day and encounter decisions

`TickEngine.resolve_day` is the production boundary. `GameState.day_resolution` saves protocol/order, day/month-end intent, phase and substep cursors, processed battle IDs, pending decisions, consumed tokens and the daily journal. A blocked decision stops subsequent work; retries and JSON reloads resume its exact cursor. Each new encounter must be acknowledged before THAT battle’s first daily round. King movement follows the same ten substeps, reads actual unit positions after ground reconciliation, and precedes every contact scan. Saved progress prevents replaying king travel, rounds or calendar advancement.

`DayResolution.acknowledge` validates the exact token/choice; stale, invalid and duplicate submissions produce no effects. `Game.resolve_battle_decision` releases only the named battle hold after acceptance and keeps time manually paused. GameRoot discards accumulated catch-up time on blocking, resumes an existing transaction without charging another day, and publishes its saved outputs exactly once on completion. An old confirmation cannot hide a newer pending notice.

**Old-save compatibility:** protocol v3 retains its saved substep order and encounter batch, with one king advance in `CAMPS_AND_SIGHTING`. Recognized unversioned `prepare` / `encounters` / `battles` / `finish` transactions retain their whole-day interpreter and journaled king advance. Neither interpreter replays completed work or changes month-end intent. Unknown protocols fail before mutation.

Compatibility `advance_day` leaves an unstarted month end to `advance`; `advance` retains one end-of-day plus monthly settlement and the opening-tick exception. Neither bypasses an existing transaction or changes its saved month-end flag. `BattleSystem.settle_day` is a noninteractive arithmetic adapter and refuses to run during a day transaction. Test helpers and simulation tools must explicitly opt into acknowledging encounters; production never auto-confirms them.

- **Personnel commit:** a round plans both sides, then submits one atomic source-preserving personnel batch with a stable battle/round ID. Only success applies morale and advances the round and day cursor. Exact saved retries return the original result; conflicting reuse is refused. A failed round reports an error and leaves its cursor in place so the UI can pause without charging the day again.

## Custody and personnel history

`PrisonerSystem.capture` is the sole capture transaction: stable receiver allocation, one `MilitaryRoster.transition`, then uniquely held batches. `BattleSystem` resolves a holder with no fit guards before battlefield removal; actual opposing receivers rescue their own people to civilians or explicitly take other captives. Without surviving controllers, all held people are explicitly released. Defeat and surrender receipts are replay-safe. At day entry, `PrisonerSystem.settle_unguarded` explicitly releases custody whose last fit guard was lost outside an active battle, before pending organization runs; active battle holders retain field-controller settlement.

`BattleReport` enrolls each source cohort at first contact/join, excluding pre-entry dead/captured/released personnel. Leaving freezes its battle counts; rejoining retains unique enrollment, excludes outside losses, and permits recovery to change live status. End/call-off saves one private immutable `battle_records` snapshot with identity subtotals and custody evidence. These engine records do not grant presentation access before courier delivery.

Enemy hosts reserve their meal in `EnemyAi.step_day` and anticipate it for retreat decisions; actual deductions run once in `EnemyAi.eat_day`, beside the crown’s meal after battles and supply deliveries. Captures and custody transfers therefore change the one actual day-end feeding owner. Assembling and town-holding hosts retain local feeding.

Prisoner disposition and delivered battle records: `guide/prisoners_and_reports.md`.
