# Battles and the enemy

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §4. Every number is a `balance.csv` `battle_*` key or a row of `data/battle_outcomes.csv` / `battle_stances.csv`; every figure whole; the dice are a hash, never `GameState.rng`.

- **Who fights**: the player's regiments (`GameState.regiments`) against the foes (`GameState.foes`), never each other. Only a land regiment with fit men (`ArmySystem.fit_heads` > 0) fights; one marked `disengaging` (falling back or ordered away from a battle) is passed over until its march ends, and one whose `disengaged_until` (an absolute day) is still to come is passed over until that day.
- **A round** (`BattleSystem`): odds = `1000 × A / (A + D)` over `side_power` (each regiment's `ArmySystem.power` × the height factor against the ENEMY side's mean height — `side_power` must be given the other side as `enemy_codes`; the defender's stance `defence_permille` scales the defending side only), `roll` = `hash([campaign seed (rng.seed_value, never a draw), sorted codes a, sorted codes b, absolute day, round]) % 1000`, `eff_permille` = odds + (roll − 500) × `battle_variance_permille` / 1000 clamped, `tier_of` the `battle_outcomes.csv` row. `apply_round` spreads a tier's losses over a side by fit heads (largest remainder, `BattleSystem.apportion`, ties to the earlier code), the lost part's wounded share over what each lost, the rest `dead`; morale moves by the tier's whole points (a FALL × the regiment's stance `morale_decay_permille`, toward zero), held to 0..100; each result carries its reason string.
- **A battle** is a Dictionary in `GameState.battles` (saved): `{id, att, def, start_day, rounds, place_x_m, place_y_m, town}`. Its id is the next `foe_next_id` (the counter foes and battles share). `BattleSystem.settle_day(repo, state)` runs once a day after the march and the couriers: `detect_contacts`, then one round per battle in id order (a battle started today fights its first round today), then who leaves and which end, then `settle_stances`. It returns the day's events for the report: `start`, `join`, `round` (powers, odds, roll, eff, tier, `apply_round`'s results, `left`) and `end` (`why`, `winner`, the survivors).
- **Contact**: in the open, a foe and a player regiment within `battle_contact_km` (inclusive); at a town, a foe within the town's `army_town_reach.csv` reach of a held town a player regiment GARRISONS (`SupplySystem.garrisoned_in`) — a column merely standing there is no garrison. At a town the foe attacks and the battle is fought at the town's point; in the open the side that marched into a standing enemy attacks, else the foe, fought where the defender stands. A regiment not yet in a battle that touches one already fighting JOINS it (`BattleSystem.join`, its own allegiance's side, at most `battle_max_units` a side); a full side leaves it out. A foe whose host (`foes[code].host`) is in mode `SIEGE` makes no contact with the besieged town's GARRISON (`BattleSystem.siege_town_of`: the host's `target_id` while its `assault` flag is down) — the garrison waits behind its walls for the assault, which is this same town battle on the day `ai_hosts[host].assault == 1`. Any other regiment of the player's (a relief column in the open, a column merely standing by) meets the besiegers as ever: a field battle, which besiegers within reach join; the garrison never sorties into it. Contact and joining HALT the regiment: its order becomes garrison where it stands (`_halt` — a STANDBY column too, so in a held town it is then a garrison), and a column in a battle does not march.
- **Leaving, after the day's round**, in this order: a regiment under a march order (a courier's ARMY_MARCH landed) leaves on its own march; one with no fit man left is out; a side the tier routs (`rout`) breaks whole; a stance with `retreats_on_contact` (evade) leaves after its first round; one whose side's odds (the attacker's `odds`, the defender's `1000 − odds`) are below its stance's `retreat_odds_permille` retreats (hold's is 0: never). One that falls back marches for the nearest town of its own kingdom beyond `battle_contact_km` of the battle; with none it halts where it is and pauses as after a draw. Leaving costs no pursuit loss (none is modelled). The battle ENDS on a rout, a side emptied (no empty battle is ever kept; a regiment whose state vanished is dropped first) or `battle_max_rounds`. A battle run to `battle_max_rounds` is a DRAW: every survivor gets `disengaged_until` = today + `battle_disengage_days` (orders still work); if the two are still in contact after it, a NEW battle (new id) starts. The survivors stay halted — the player's await orders, a foe's host decides (EnemyAi).
- **Stances** (`data/battle_stances.csv`): a regiment's state carries `stance` (`ArmySystem.STANCE_DEFAULT` steady; an old save reads and is seeded steady). `ARMY_STANCE {regiment, stance}` is an order like ARMY_GARRISON — validated as the player's own land regiment (never a foe), a stance of the table, a courier the chest can pay; it rides by courier. He lands it as the day ends into `stance_next` (newest wins by `stance_order_id`, apart from the march's `order_id`; `CourierSystem.pending_for` ignores it), and `settle_stances` makes it the stance at the end of that day's battles: the day he lands still fights the old stance, the next day the new. The map menu offers one row per stance (`data/army_orders.csv` `params` `stance=…`); the war room drafts no stance.
- **The day hook and the report** (spec §4.5): `TickEngine._end_day` runs `BattleSystem.settle_day` AFTER `CourierSystem.ride_day` and the hosts' minds (a delivered stance or march is in the state before the round; the day order is below) and before the supply lines and the meal; the events are `TickEngine.day_battles` (reset every day). A day with any event files ONE letter, `MissiveWriter.battle_report` (kind `battle`, column `credible`, subject the first of the player's regiments in it at the battle's place): a fact for each `start`, `join`, `round` (strength, odds, side totals), each regiment's `dead`, `wounded` and morale change WITH the engine's reason, each regiment that leaves and why, and the `end` told from the player's side (`battle_end_won` / `_lost` / `_draw` for a battle run to the round cap / `_none`). Numbers are the events' own, never recomputed; the words are `templates.csv` `battle_*` rows (`MissiveWriter.battle_words`, the `{player_realm}` / `{raider_realm}` slots filled at load, never a spelled kingdom). A calm day files nothing. Battle letters keep their own count (`missive_keep_battle`, `MissiveLog.BATTLE_KINDS`), like the supply letters', so a siege never pushes a receipt or a spy's report off the board. A morale change exists only in the round's results, so the letter is its record: a new morale move in a battle must carry its reason through `apply_round` or it is not in the report.
- **Under arms** on the regiment card and the folio is headcount less the dead; the fit and wounded rows are unchanged. Supply use (`supply_use`) still counts the full pack headcount, dead and wounded alike (a fed-by-the-pack roll, not touched).

- **The hosts take the field** (`EnemyAi`, pure, no random number): `GameState.new_campaign` calls `EnemyAi.spawn(repo, state, row)` for every `repo.raid_hosts` row (pack regiment codes of the raider realm; `start_x` / `start_y` are PACK pixels, stored as whole metres via `ArmySystem.pack_to_metres`). Each regiment becomes a `GameState.foes` entry — `ArmySystem.starting_state` plus `host` — standing at the assembly point with `order` standby, and the host a `GameState.ai_hosts[code]` mind `{mode: ASSEMBLE, target_kind, target_id, think_day, plunder, siege_progress}`. A save from before the hosts has no foes and none are made for it. `EnemyAi.step_day` turns a host `ASSEMBLE` → `ADVANCE` on the absolute day `raid_hosts.appear_day` (an event `host_advance`, once); `host_codes` lists the minds in code order and `regiments_of(state, host)` a host's foes in code order.
- **A foe is seated once**: its pack row (`MapObjects.regiments_of(raider)`, the map in DEV MODE, the supply lines' hostile list, the garrisons, a spy's count) is only where the pack put it; every reader asks `ArmySystem.position_of`, which prefers `GameState.foes`, so the regiment is at its foe place and nowhere else. Never seat a foe in `regiments`.
- **A foe marches** in `ArmySystem.march_day`'s SECOND pass, in code order, through the same `_march_one` (the even step: `march_days_between` is its arrival day): any foe under `order == march` that `BattleSystem.battle_of` finds in no battle — a column that fell back (`disengaging`) is walked home by it too. On arrival the order becomes garrison; a foe's arrival is not in the returned list (`TickEngine.day_arrivals` is the player's news). Setting a foe's destination (and a retreat's) is `EnemyAi`'s, never a courier's. Foes eat nothing in `eat_day` / `hunger_day` (they iterate `state.regiments`); a foe's supply is `EnemyAi`'s own (below).
- **A foe's card is fogged**: `MapObjects.army_card` reads a regiment's state only for a viewer who knows it `full` (`Intel.level`: the player's own, or dev mode); a foe's card shows the headcount as a word and none of the inner figures (morale, stores, training, the men under arms).

## The hosts' minds (`EnemyAi`, spec §3)

- **Modes**: `ASSEMBLE` → `ADVANCE` (on `appear_day`) → `SIEGE` / `RAID` on arrival; `HOLD` after a capture (SiegeSystem: it occupies the town's manors and stays in the town, eats nothing of its own — it lives off the town — never thinks again and is never re-targeted; a TARGETING mode, so beaten or broken in morale it still turns RETREAT, but never for hunger); `RETREAT` (beaten, morale, hunger) and `WITHDRAW` (nothing worth taking, or the target lost) march the host to its start point (`home_m`, the row's PACK point in metres) and choose nothing more. `TARGETING` = ADVANCE, SIEGE, RAID, HOLD; `HOMEWARD` = RETREAT, WITHDRAW.
- **`EnemyAi.step_day`**, per host in code order: an assembling host waits (fed at home); every other EATS a day first (`ArmySystem.eat_one` on each foe, the same `day_bite` of `supply_use` where it stands as the player's columns); a TARGETING host whose `should_retreat` gives a reason turns RETREAT; an ADVANCING host with a target checks `arrival_mode`; then `_march_host` sends every idle column on — an advancing, besieging or raiding host's for its target (the columns behind the first come up), a homeward host's home (a column falling back from a battle too: the host owns its retreat) — never one in a battle (an order would take it out of the fight), and an advancing host never redirects a `disengaging` one. Events: `host_advance`, and `host_mode {host, mode, why, day}` for every change of mode (`think`'s WITHDRAW included: `think` / `think_day` return their events). It runs exactly once a day (it feeds the hosts), from `TickEngine._end_day` (below).
- **The score** (`EnemyAi.score`): `value × greed_permille / 1000 − distance_days × dist_cost − defence × caution_permille / 1000 − overlap`, whole gold, each per-mille term rounded down. The doctrine is the host's `role` row of `data/ai_doctrines.csv` (`doctrine_of`). `value` = `MapManors.value_of_line` rounded, plus the doctrine's `town_bonus` for a town (`target_value`); `overlap` = `ai_target_overlap_permille` of the score before it, never below 0 (`overlap_of`), paid only on a target another non-reserve host in a TARGETING mode holds (`taken_by_others`). At 1000 (the data's value) it is a BAN: a taken target scores 0 and `rank` never takes a target at 0, however rich, so two hosts never share one. `rank` takes the highest, ties to the earlier id (string order, then kind); a best not above 0 is no target.
- **Candidates** (`EnemyAi.candidates`): every manor of a province the player's realm holds (`MapOwnership.provinces_of`, `MapManors.manors_of_province` with the state) whose point lies within `ai_target_reach_km` of the host's ANCHOR — its slowest live column (`anchor_of`). A manor carrying a burg is the TOWN (kind `town`, id the burg code, point the burg); any other is kind `manor`, id its cell id, point the cell centre (`target_point_m`; `target_cell` maps a town back to its manor cell, cached per repository AND `map_id`). `distance_days` is `ArmySystem.march_days_between` for the anchor; a target more days away than the host's `supply_reach_days` (its hungriest live column's `days_in_hand` less `ai_retreat_supply_days` less `ai_supply_margin_days`, the meals' whole-unit rounding) is no candidate — it would turn home hungry on the road — and `_choose` skips it the same way; so is a manor a raid has stripped bare (`RaidSystem.stripped`). `defence` is the summed `ArmySystem.power(…, "defend")` — the town's works (`army_town_defence.csv`) are inside it — of the player's regiments the host SEES that stand at the target (`defenders_of`: within `battle_contact_km` of the point, or in a town's reach).
- **Sight** (`seen_by`): the player's land regiments with fit men within `ai_sight_km` of any live column of THAT host. A garrison out of sight is no defence; towns and manors are known by reach alone.
- **Thinking** (`EnemyAi.think`, `think_day` runs the hosts with targets of their own in code order, then the reserves): only an ADVANCING host thinks. The `reserve` follows on EVERY call (no cadence): it takes the first main host's target (WITHDRAWs when the main host goes home, waits while it has none) and counts as no overlap. Any other host chooses only with no target or once `ai_think_days` have passed since `think_day` — between, it keeps its target. Any other host takes `rank`'s best; none above 0 → WITHDRAW (target cleared). A choice orders every column at once (no courier). `_choose` is `rank(candidates(…))` with the march days walked only where they can matter (candidates in order of their score at no distance, stopping at the first whose bound is below the best found) — keep the two picking the same (a test holds it). No random number anywhere; `GameState.rng` is never drawn.
- **Engaging** (`EnemyAi.engages(repo, state, host, seen)`): `BattleSystem.odds_permille` of the host's `side_power` attacking against the seen regiments' defending, each against the other's height, at least the doctrine's `engage_odds_permille`. Nothing seen is a fight taken.
- **Retreat** (`EnemyAi.should_retreat`, a reason or ""): a column of the host `disengaging` (fell back beaten from a battle); the live columns' MEAN morale below the doctrine's `retreat_morale`; or the hungriest live column's `ArmySystem.days_in_hand` below `ai_retreat_supply_days` (not for a HOLD host). Leaving SIEGE for any mode clears the siege's figures and assault flag at once (`EnemyAi.set_mode` → `SiegeSystem.clear`). Every mode change — EnemyAi's and SiegeSystem's — goes through `set_mode` and is a `host_mode` event with its why; the siege's land on `TickEngine.day_ai` too.
- **Arrival** (`EnemyAi.arrival_mode`) is the FIRST live column's, never all of them (the columns march on their own; waiting for the last let a lone column start a town battle and bypass the siege). Nothing while any live column is in a battle. At a town a player regiment GARRISONS (`garrison_of`, `SupplySystem.garrisoned_in`): the first column that touches it as a battle would (within `battle_contact_km` of a garrisoning regiment or of the town's point, or within its `army_town_reach.csv` reach) → SIEGE (any role; the reserve joins the main host's siege) — unless the host is not a reserve and another NON-reserve host already besieges that town: then it drops its target, `think_day` 0 makes it choose again, and it stands aside that day (`ARRIVAL_RETHINK`, `_stand_aside`: its free columns halt where they stand and get `disengaged_until` = tomorrow, so none fights the town alone; no second siege). The reserve is never turned away: it sits down to its main host's siege. Otherwise, the first column within `battle_contact_km` of the target's point: the player no longer holds the target (`target_held`) → WITHDRAW; a seen player regiment at the target → nothing (contact fights it); else → RAID (an open manor, or a town with no garrison).
- **Supply**: a host starts with its foes' `army_start_supply_days` and eats from the day after it sets out; nothing refills it but raiding (`RaidSystem`, below), so a long siege starves the besieger into RETREAT.

## The invasion on the day loop (spec §2, §7)

- **The day order** in `TickEngine._end_day` (the tick's thirtieth day runs the same `_end_day`, once): works → `ArmySystem.march_day` → `CourierSystem.ride_day` → `EnemyAi.step_day` → `EnemyAi.think_day` (the SAME day, so a host that set out or stood aside chooses before its columns march tomorrow) → `EnemyAi.warnings` and the host news → `BattleSystem.settle_day` → `SiegeSystem.settle_day` (right after, given the day's battle events: an assault is that day's town battle) → `RaidSystem.settle_day` (the same events: spoils, then the raids' take) → supply lines, meal, hunger, economy. The minds read the places and the garrisons the battles will read (after the couriers), and a column that marched into a garrisoned town's reach has put its host in SIEGE before the contacts are taken. Never call `step_day` twice a day. The hosts' events (step_day's, then think_day's, then the sieges' and raids' `host_mode`s) are `TickEngine.day_ai`, the sieges' `TickEngine.day_siege`, the raids' `TickEngine.day_raid`, reset every day; `SiegeSystem.news` and `RaidSystem.news` ride in the day's battle letter.
- **The rumour** (`EnemyAi.warnings`, the letters to file today): on the absolute day `raid_rumour_lead_days` before the earliest `appear_day` among the hosts the campaign HAS (`state.ai_hosts`; a save without hosts hears nothing), one letter `MissiveWriter.raid_rumour` — kind and column `rumour`, subject the point where the first host to cross gathers (its `home_m`), the place named by `Intel.place_label`, no figure. Equality with today makes it once.
- **The scout**: `SpySystem.report` adds `foe_columns` and `foe_troops` (`intel_facts.csv`) when any foe stands within `spy_radius_km` of his point (`SpySystem.foes_near`: the foes' live places, men under arms = pack headcount less `dead`, a column with none left uncounted) — estimates by the spy's own quality and bias like every other fact, so a credible spy brackets the truth and a poor one is a rumour. With no foe in reach the report has no foe fact. `troops_foreign` still counts every foreign regiment by pack headcount, foes included.
- **The host news** (`MissiveWriter.host_news`): a day with a `host_advance` or a `host_mode` of SIEGE, RAID, WITHDRAW or RETREAT files ONE letter, kind `host_news`, a line per event (`templates.csv` `host_news_*`): where — a siege's or a raid's target, else the host's first column — and never a figure or the engine's `why` (the enemy's morale and stores are not the crown's to read). Column `credible` when a siege or a raid is in it (the crown's own people see it), else `rumour`. A `host_mode` ADVANCE (setting out again from a stripped manor) is no news. Fights are the battle report's.
- **The words** are fixed prose in `templates.csv` (`raid_rumour*`, `host_news_*`, filled through `MissiveWriter.battle_words` with the realm slots); no model sees a warning or a number.

## The siege (`SiegeSystem`, spec §5.1–5.2)

- **One siege a town**, kept on its LEADING host's mind (`lead_of`: the first non-reserve host in SIEGE before it, else the first; it takes over the figures a besieger kept before it — a reserve that sat down first — so no progress is orphaned): `siege_progress` (of 1000), `siege_days`, `siege_town`, `assault`, `assault_due`, `starved` — whole, saved in `ai_hosts`; an old mind reads them as 0 / "". A reserve beside its main host adds its columns and keeps no figures; it follows its main host out of a SIEGE (WITHDRAW, RETREAT home; HOLD with it) and out of a HOLD when it goes home.
- **The day** (`settle_day(repo, state, battle_events)`, host code order): the besieged town no longer the crown's (`EnemyAi.target_held`) → every besieger WITHDRAWs, no progress, no capture; today's assault rounds (below); the town falls at 1000 or with its garrison (`EnemyAi.garrison_of`) slain to the last fit man; a town with NO garrison left (marched off) is no siege — the hosts turn RAID on it (as an open town on arrival), nothing taken, no progress; else `gain = SiegeSystem.day_gain(A, D, siege_rate, starving, siege_starve_bonus_permille)` = `step` (`siege_rate × A / (A + D)`, 0 at A + D = 0) plus `step × bonus / 1000` while starving. A = `attack_power`: the besieging hosts' live columns AT the town (`at_town`: within `battle_contact_km` of its point or its reach), `ArmySystem.power` attack; D = `defend_power`: the garrison's `ArmySystem.power` defend (the works inside it). Progress is held to 0..1000.
- **Hunger**: the town STARVES (`starving`) only when its own `TownStores` (crown + lord + house) are spent AND its garrison's hungriest regiment has no day in hand — the men eat what they carry first. A besieged town (`besieged`) feeds its garrison from its own stores alone — `SupplyLines` sends no depot or realm stock in — so a long siege empties both and `hunger_day` takes the garrison's morale. The besieger's hunger is `should_retreat`'s.
- **The assault** (Ruling 3): every `siege_assault_days` days of siege an assault is owed (`assault_due`) and the flag `assault` is raised for the NEXT day's battles (SiegeSystem runs after them); the flag stands while a battle at the town holds a besieging column, and drops at the settlement after; an owed assault that found no battle (a draw's pause) is tried the next day until fought — the flag never outlives the siege. Each ROUND of it the besiegers fought (a `round` event at the town) moves the siege by its tier's `siege` column in `data/battle_outcomes.csv`: `repulsed` (attacker_checked) less `siege_assault_fail_permille` a round, never below 0; `breach` (defender_routed) 1000; blank nothing (a routed besieger falls back and its host RETREATs on `should_retreat`). An assault that ENDS with the besiegers keeping the field (the garrison broke or left) is a breach too.
- **The fall is MANOR-level** (`capture(repo, state, host, events)`, refused — `{}` — unless the host is in SIEGE before a town the crown still holds, so no town is taken twice): the town's own manor and its province's manors within `siege_capture_radius_km` of the town (`taken_cells`) go to the host's kingdom's ruling house (`MapManors.set_owner`, the house of its capital's province) with the reason `taken by <realm>'s <role> host: siege of <town>`; each one's house before is kept on the host's mind (`occupied`: cell string → house, `occupied_house`). No province, border, capital or other town moves (`MapOwnership` is not touched). An assault still being fought there is called off (`BattleSystem.call_off`); the garrison still standing marches out for the nearest town of its own (`BattleSystem.march_out`, disengaging); every host before the town turns HOLD. While occupied, the town and those manors are no target of anyone's (`target_held` asks `SiegeSystem.occupier_of`). It calls no defeat consequence yet.
- **Liberation**: a HOLD host whose town's manor another house holds again (taken back), or with no live column left, RETREATs; a host that took a town and holds it no more (RETREAT, WITHDRAW, beaten) gives each manor its house still holds back to the house it had (`MapManors.set_owner`, reason `liberated: <host> left <town>`), told once as `siege_liberated`.
- **Lifted**: a host that leaves its siege any other way (beaten — a column fell back, e.g. before a relief force — hungry, low morale, its main host gone) has it told once as `siege_lifted`.
- **The news** rides in the day's battle letter (`MissiveWriter.battle_report`, after the fighting): `SiegeSystem.news` is every `siege_*` event but the quiet `siege_day` — `siege_begin` (the strengths and the day's gain), `siege_starving`, `siege_repulsed`, `siege_fallen` (`siege_fallen_slain` words when no one of the garrison marched out), `siege_undefended`, `siege_lifted`, `siege_liberated`, a line each from `templates.csv` (the realm slots, the event's own figures). Never a random number.

## Raids and spoils (`RaidSystem`, spec §5.3)

- **The take** (`day_take`, `_take`): a host in RAID with a live column at its target (within `battle_contact_km` of its point, or a town's reach), none of its columns in a battle or `disengaging`, the target still the crown's (`target_held`), takes from the target's manor (`target_cell`) `take_on(month, raid_plunder_permille, n, taken)` = min(`cap_of` = month × permille / 1000, month × permille × (n + 1) / 30000) − taken: whole gold, the remainders carried on the running sum, never more than the share of a month from one manor summed over every host (`taken_of`). `month` = `ManorBars.worth_of(ManorEconomy.line_of(…, state.tick))` — read, never rounded again (inv. 11). The record is the host's mind: `raided` (cell string → `{days, taken}`), saved; `plunder` whole gold.
- **Food**: `raid_supply_permille` of the take is food, `food_units` = take × it / `army_supply_gold_permille`, loaded into the host's live columns by their free room (`BattleSystem.apportion` over `max_units − supplies`, never past `army_supply_max_days`); the plunder gains the take less the loaded food's gold rounded up (`food_gold`). The rest of what the columns cannot carry stays plunder.
- **The crown's books** (Ruling 4): a ledger line WITH its reason, `WorldLedger.apply` (source `raid`, `world_log`), `raided by <host>: <manor>` — the lord's purse (`metrics.lord_gold`, a `world_schema.csv` row with `llm_cap` 0) the whole take when the crown controls the manor's province, else the treasury its `crown_share_permille` of it on the running sum (what it would have had). `line_of` and the month's settlement are untouched.
- **The hazard**: a host's first take marks the manor `Raided` (`data/hazard_kinds.csv`, public; id `raided_<cell>`, `Hazards.add` once ever, the kind's months from this tick), so its morale, pace and supply-line loss reach the crown's columns through the hazards' own paths.
- **Stripped**: a RAID host whose manor is `stripped` (raided and its share taken) or no longer the crown's turns ADVANCE with no target and `think_day` 0 (`EnemyAi.set_mode`, a `host_mode` event), so it chooses again the next day — never that manor (`candidates` skips it); with nothing within supply reach, it WITHDRAWs.
- **Spoils** (`spoils(repo, state, host, winner, place)`): a host the crown beat today — a column of it left a battle `rout`, `retreat` or `broken` (the round events' `left`, `_beaten`) — pays `battle_spoils_permille` of its plunder into `metrics.strategic` (`WorldLedger.apply`, source `spoils`, reason `spoils: <host> routed at <place>`, the battle's `place_label`); its plunder is then 0, so a defeat pays once. Nothing for a winner other than the player's realm or a host with no plunder.
- **The day** (`settle_day(repo, state, battle_events)`, host code order): spoils, then the take, then the stripped check.
