# The siege (`SiegeSystem`)

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §5.1–5.2; the hosts, battles and day order it plugs into: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`; what a fall costs: `scripts/engine/guide/raid_and_defeat.md`. Every number is a `battle/balance.csv` `siege_*` key or a `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv` `siege` cell; whole numbers; no random number.

- **The garrison** (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`, read by `EnemyAi.garrison_of`, the town battles' contact and `SupplyLines.siege_cut`): the player's land units standing (not on the march) on the town's SEAT (`ArmyGround.seated_in`) or in its CAMP (`camped_by`, `camp_town`: the hex rings outside its reach), whatever their standing order, and any unit under a garrison order standing inside its reach (a column halted there). The town and its camp are invested together: the besiegers make no contact with any of them until the assault, the camp's men eat from the town's stores, and its supply lines are cut. Only the seat fights from the works (`ArmySystem.works_of`); the camp fights the assault on the field's ground. A unit standing by in the reach off seat and camp, or anywhere else, is a relief force. A town an enemy host occupies (`SiegeSystem.occupier_of`) is held by no one's seat or camp (`ArmyGround.holds`): the garrison that marches out empties the seat and nothing refills it until liberation.
- **One siege a town**, kept on its LEADING host's mind (`lead_of`: the first non-reserve host in SIEGE before it, else the first; it takes over the figures a besieger kept before it — a reserve that sat down first — so no progress is orphaned): `siege_progress` (of 1000), `siege_days`, `siege_town`, `assault`, `assault_due`, `starved` — whole, saved in `ai_hosts`; an old mind reads them as 0 / "". A reserve beside its main host adds its columns and keeps no figures; it follows its main host out of a SIEGE (WITHDRAW, RETREAT home; HOLD with it) and out of a HOLD when it goes home (`follow_main`, at the end of `EnemyAi.step_day` — before the day's battles — and again in `settle_day`).
- **The day** (`settle_day(repo, state, battle_events)`, host code order): the besieged town no longer the crown's (`EnemyAi.target_held`) → every besieger WITHDRAWs, no progress, no capture; today's assault rounds (below); the town falls at 1000 or with its garrison (`EnemyAi.garrison_of`) slain to the last fit man; a town with NO garrison left (marched off) is no siege — the hosts turn RAID on it (as an open town on arrival), nothing taken, no progress; else `gain = SiegeSystem.day_gain(A, D, siege_rate, starving, siege_starve_bonus_permille)` = `step` (`siege_rate × A / (A + D)`, 0 at A + D = 0) plus `step × bonus / 1000` while starving. A = `attack_power`: the besieging hosts' live columns AT the town (`at_town`: within `battle_contact_km` of its point or its reach), `ArmySystem.power` attack; D = `defend_power`: the garrison's `ArmySystem.power` defend (the works inside it). Progress is held to 0..1000.
- **Hunger**: the town STARVES (`starving`) only when its own `TownStores` (crown + lord + house) are spent AND its garrison's hungriest regiment has no day in hand — the men eat what they carry first. A besieged town (`besieged`) feeds its garrison from its own stores alone — `SupplyLines` sends no depot or realm stock in, and a supply line whose regiment garrisons it is CUT (`SupplyLines.siege_cut`: a `cut` event by the besiegers' kingdom, `host` the leading host; it flows again once the siege is gone), so no supply point feeds a siege's hunger — and a long siege empties both and `hunger_day` takes the garrison's morale. The besieger's hunger is `should_retreat`'s.
- **The assault** is the ordinary town battle: every `siege_assault_days` days of siege an assault is owed (`assault_due`) and the flag `assault` is raised for the NEXT day's battles (SiegeSystem runs after them); the flag stands while a battle at the town holds a besieging column, and drops at the settlement after; an owed assault that found no battle (a draw's pause) is tried the next day until fought — the flag never outlives the siege. Each ROUND of it the besiegers fought (a `round` event at the town) moves the siege by its tier's `siege` column in `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv`: `repulsed` (attacker_checked) less `siege_assault_fail_permille` a round, never below 0; `breach` (defender_routed) 1000; blank nothing (a routed besieger falls back and its host RETREATs on `should_retreat`). An assault that ENDS with the besiegers keeping the field (the garrison broke or left) is a breach too.
- **The fall is MANOR-level** (`capture(repo, state, host, events)`, refused — `{}` — unless the host is in SIEGE before a town the crown still holds, so no town is taken twice): the town's own manor and its province's manors within `siege_capture_radius_km` of the town (`taken_cells`) go to the host's kingdom's ruling house (`MapManors.set_owner`, the house of its capital's province) with the reason `taken by <realm>'s <role> host: siege of <town>`; each one's house before is kept on the host's mind (`occupied`: cell string → house, `occupied_house`). No province, border, capital or other town moves (`MapOwnership` is not touched). But who CONTROLS the town's province (`MapManors.controller_of`, most manors) can change: on the default pack 5 of the realm's 65 towns take every manor, or most, of a small province with them, so its control (the lord's purse, BUILD and FOCUS there) passes to the raider's house until liberation; the capital's province is not among them. An assault still being fought there is called off (`BattleSystem.call_off`); the garrison still standing marches out for the nearest town of its own (`BattleSystem.march_out`, disengaging); every host before the town turns HOLD. While occupied, the town and those manors are no target of anyone's (`target_held` asks `SiegeSystem.occupier_of`). Then the loss costs the crown: `DefeatSystem.on_town_lost` (`guide/raid_and_defeat.md`), its `defeat_town_lost` event after `siege_fallen`.
- **Liberation**: a HOLD host whose town's manor another house holds again (taken back), or with no live column left, RETREATs; a host that took a town and holds it no more (RETREAT, WITHDRAW, beaten) gives each manor its house still holds back to the house it had (`MapManors.set_owner`, reason `liberated: <host> left <town>`), told once as `siege_liberated`.
- **Lifted**: a host that leaves its siege any other way (beaten — a column fell back, e.g. before a relief force — hungry, low morale, its main host gone) has it told once as `siege_lifted`.
- **The news** rides in the day's battle letter (`MissiveWriter.battle_report`, after the fighting): `SiegeSystem.news` is every `siege_*` event but the quiet `siege_day` — `siege_begin` (the garrison's strength and the day's gain — never the besiegers' strength, the fog's), `siege_starving`, `siege_repulsed`, `siege_fallen` (`siege_fallen_slain` words when no one of the garrison marched out), `siege_undefended`, `siege_lifted`, `siege_liberated`, a line each from `templates.csv` (the realm slots, the event's own figures). Never a random number.
