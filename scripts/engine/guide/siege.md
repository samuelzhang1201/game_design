# The siege (`SiegeSystem`)

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §5.1–5.2; the hosts, battles and day order it plugs into: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`; what a fall costs: `scripts/engine/guide/raid_and_defeat.md`. Every number is a `battle/balance.csv` `siege_*` key or a `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv` `siege` cell; whole numbers; no random number.

- **The garrison** (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`, read by `EnemyAi.garrison_of`, the town battles' contact and `SupplyLines.siege_cut`): the player's land units standing (not on the march) on the town's SEAT (`ArmyGround.seated_in`) or in its CAMP (`camped_by`, `camp_town`: the hex rings outside its reach), whatever their standing order, and any unit under a garrison order standing inside its reach (a column halted there). The town and its camp are invested together: the besiegers make no contact with any of them until the assault, the camp's men eat from the town's stores, and its supply lines are cut. Only the seat fights from the works (`ArmySystem.works_of`); the camp fights the assault on the field's ground. A unit standing by in the reach off seat and camp, or anywhere else, is a relief force. A town is held by its HOLDER (`SiegeSystem.holder_of`: its occupier while occupied, else its province's owner; `ArmyGround.holds`, `EnemyAi.target_held`): the crown's garrison that marches out of a town the enemy occupies empties the seat and nothing of the crown's refills it until liberation.
- **Activation**: `SiegeSystem.active(repo, state, host)` requires SIEGE, a completed deployment record, and at least one live, fit, non-disengaging column physically stationed at the town (`deployed_at`: garrison order and `at_town`). It computes presence when read, so a retreat or destruction cannot leave a stale blockade. `besieged`, `besiegers`, assaults and capture share this gate. DEPLOYING neither advances siege days/progress nor cuts all supply routes; physical enemy units still intercept actual roads. Legacy minds are normalized with `EnemyAi.ensure_deployments` before settlement, retaining historical progress and pending assaults.
- **One siege a town, one record** (`SiegeRecord`, `GameState.sieges[id]`, id `"s<n>"` from the saved `siege_next_id`; spec `2026-11-18-player-siege-design.md` §3): `town`, `attacker` / `defender` (kingdom codes; the defender is the town's holder when it opened), `hosts` (the hosts in it, code order), `units` (a crown besieger's units, code order; `SiegeRecord.open_units`), `task_id` ("" for the AI), `opened_day`, `days`, `integrity` (set at open: 1000 for walls or a citadel, else 0; nothing moves it yet), `progress` (of 1000; transitional), `assault`, `assault_due`, `starved`, `entries`, `stores` (the blockade, below), `alloc`, `history` (the men's plan and its cuts, below; absent in an old save, read empty) — whole numbers and strings. The day is run by the LEADING host (`lead_of`: the first non-reserve host in SIEGE before it, else the first); every besieger joins the record (`ensure_for`), so a reserve that sat down first opened it and its figures carry on. A mind keeps only `siege_id`, until the siege's end is told. A host leaving SIEGE or DEPLOYING leaves the record (`EnemyAi.set_mode` → `SiegeSystem.clear` → `SiegeRecord.leave`); the figures reset once no host is left, and the record is erased once no mind names it (`drop`). An old save's mind figures (`siege_progress`, `siege_days`, `siege_town`, `assault`, `assault_due`, `starved`) convert once at the end of `EnemyAi.ensure_deployments` (`SiegeRecord.migrate`, logged in `military_migration.siege_record`): an armed assault becomes owed, a walled town's integrity is 1000 − progress, a left siege still untold keeps a host-less record so its lift is told. A reserve beside its main host adds its columns; it follows its main host out of a SIEGE (WITHDRAW, RETREAT home; HOLD with it) and out of a HOLD when it goes home (`follow_main`, at the end of `EnemyAi.step_day` — before the day's battles — and again in `settle_day`).
- **The sides** (player siege spec §5, §7, step 3; `tests/engine/test_siege_sides.gd`): the record's `attacker` / `defender` drive it. The besiegers are the hosts' stationed columns plus the record's stationed `units` (`stationed`, `record_active`; `at_town` / `deployed_at` read a crown unit too); the defenders are `defenders_of(town)`: the crown's garrison while the crown holds it, else the holder's foes standing at the town with fit men (the occupying host's columns, woken units, a militia). `garrison_fit`, `defend_power`, `attack_power` read them; a town the crown does not hold starves by its record's stores (the blockade, below). A crown record runs the same `_siege_day` after the hosts' (`settle_day` → `_crown_sieges`, id order), with no assault timer: its `assault` rises only from `assault_due`, which only an order sets (step 12, spec §4.4). Its events are `siege_ours_*` (`begin`, `day`, `repulsed`, `taken`, `lifted`, `liberated`), which `news` leaves out: the crown's own letters ride by courier (step 12). It lifts (`siege_ours_lifted`) when the town's holder is no longer its defender or its units are all gone.
- **Militia** (spec §5, step 4a; `TownMilitia`, `data/battle/town_militia.csv`; `tests/engine/test_siege_militia.gd`): a record opened (`SiegeRecord._open`, never by `migrate`: an old save's running siege raises none) before a town with no defenders and no unit on its seat raises ONE unit `militia:<siege id>`: each row of the town's best works (`works_of_town`: citadel over walls over none) gives min(population × `men_permille` / 1000, `men_cap`) men in whole crews, the arms in file order each taking at most what is left of `MilitaryPopulation.available` (the province's urban) and `army_living_cap_men` (`size`). Its men are one commoner cohort enlisted by `MilitaryRoster.mobilize` (event `militia:<id>`, reason `militia of <town> raised`; no population debit — only a death debits it), its row like a recruit's (label `<town> militia`, `organization_arm` its first arm, whose piece it keeps for its card, footprint and bodies, and `figure` `TownMilitia.FIGURE`, the `army_art.csv` row the 3D map draws it with), for the record's defender: in `regiments` for the crown, in `foes` with `host` "" otherwise (`ArmyGround.seated_in` lets a host-less foe hold a seat), on the town's seat with `militia_town`; the record's `militia` lists it. So `defenders_of`, the garrison, the works and the no-contact rule count it. It never marches: ARMY_MARCH, ARMY_SEAT and an area task on it are refused (`task_words.csv` `militia`); a fall marches it nowhere (`_take`). Beaten, it never withdraws: when it would leave a battle or its side retreats (`BattleSystem._exits`, `_start_retreat`), it breaks up where it stands (`TownMilitia.disperse`: fit and wounded released, event `<code>:broke`, reason `militia of <town> broke`) and leaves the battle broken, so the battle ends instead of waiting for a column that cannot move. When no record lists it and it fights no battle, `TownMilitia.stand_down` (end of `settle_day`) releases its fit and wounded (`MilitaryRoster.transition`, event `<code>:stood_down`, reason `militia of <town> stood down`) and, with no captive of it left, erases its entry, row, personnel roster, cohorts and seat (a captive keeps the row until released).
- **Woken garrisons** (spec §5, step 4b; `tests/engine/test_siege_woken.gd`): before raising a militia, a record whose defender is not the crown wakes the town's standing garrison (`TownMilitia.wake`; `standing_at`: the defender's land roster units with no state, on the seat or with `camp_town` the town, code order). Each goes into `foes` with its `ArmySystem.starting_state`, `host` "" and `garrison_of` the town, an ordinary foe that eats, fights and can be captured; the seat holder keeps its seat and fights from the works. The record's `woken` lists them, and a town that has them raises no militia. `defenders_of` counts a woken unit at its post (`at_post`: not marching, at the town or within `battle_contact_km` of its row's own camp spot, as a crown camp is invested with its town). `migrate` wakes none. When no record lists it and it fights no battle, `TownMilitia.send_back` (end of `settle_day`, after `stand_down`) erases its `foes` entry when it is alive, at its post and its kingdom still holds the town (event `garrison_returned`). It is a standing roster unit again, its losses kept in its personnel roster. Otherwise it stays a foe and loses the mark.
- **The enemy's sight of a militia** (spec §5): a town candidate with no seen defender takes `TownMilitia.defend_power` as its `defence` (`EnemyAi._gather`): `power_of_arms` "defend" of the militia `size` would raise, at `army_start_training` / `army_start_morale`, on the town's ground behind its works at full strength. A host reaching a held town with no seen defender BESIEGES it (`arrival_mode` → DEPLOYING, or RETHINK when another non-reserve host besieges it; RAID is for a plain manor only), and the militia rises with the record: at once for an arrival in the morning's `step_day` (`SiegeRecord.ensure_for`), at the day's `settle_day` for one in a body-day slice (a slice's work changes no body or personnel), before that day's assault or fall.
- **The day** (`settle_day(repo, state, battle_events)`, host code order): the besieged town no longer the crown's (`EnemyAi.target_held`) → every besieger WITHDRAWs, no progress, no capture; today's assault rounds (below); a defended town falls at 1000; with no fit garrison left, the established siege keeps its occupation intent, stops engineering gains and assaults, and sends each host’s nearest available column toward the town using normal movement. Other camps hold. Capture requires an actually stationed column within the town’s true contact reach (`BattleSystem.town_contact`), so a distant or battle-blocked column cannot take it. A never-besieged open target still follows the RAID arrival rule; else `gain = SiegeSystem.day_gain(A, D, siege_rate, starving, siege_starve_bonus_permille)` = `step` (`siege_rate × A / (A + D)`, 0 at A + D = 0) plus `step × bonus / 1000` while starving. A = `attack_power`: the besieging hosts' live columns physically stationed at the town (`deployed_at`), `ArmySystem.power` attack; D = `defend_power`: the garrison's `ArmySystem.power` defend (the works inside it). Progress is held to 0..1000.
- **The blockade** (spec §4.1, step 6; `SiegeBlockade`, `tests/engine/test_siege_blockade.gd`): the record's `entries` (set at `_open`, after the levy; an old save's on its first day, `ensure`): one per pack route (`geography/routes.csv`) with the town as `from_burg` or `to_burg`, on the route `siege_entry_km` from its end nearest the town, plus `countryside` at the town's point; `inflow_share` is `data/battle/siege_entries.csv` by route group, normalised to 1000 (`TownStores.apportion`). Each siege day (`_siege_day`, after the assault and the fall checks, before the gain; `SiegeBlockade.day`) the entries close afresh (`close`): every entry but a `searoutes` one (never closed), by share descending then id, takes the stationed besiegers (`stationed`) until it holds `siege_blockade_men_per_entry` fit men — first those already standing at it (a road entry), then the rest in code order, so an arrival that sorts first moves no one; one short of them stays open, and so does every entry after it; a unit closes one entry at most. Only the plan's blockade men count (`SiegeTactics.men_for`, each unit giving at most its `men_of`, below). The closing units camp on the entry's point when its ground is free (`ArmyGround.free_slot` within `siege_entry_reach_m`, as `_spread_foe` places a host's columns), never in a battle or with `body_groups`. `closed_by` is the first of them standing at the entry (within `siege_entry_reach_m`; the countryside: stationed at the town), else "" — so a unit that falls back or dies reopens it, and a relief column near it does not. `open_share` is the open entries' shares. The record's `stores`: a town the crown does not defend opens with `need` × `data/battle/siege_tactics.csv` `stores_days` by works (`need`: the defenders' `day_bite` of their `supply_use` plus population × `siege_civil_need_permille` / 1000) and moves by `stores_day` = max(0, stores − need + need × open_share / 1000); at 0 each defending foe loses `army_morale_starving_day` (a foe is never read by `hunger_day`). A crown town keeps 0 there and eats from `TownStores`, whose inflows the blockade gates: `TownStores.deposit_month` gives a besieged town each pool × open_share / 1000, the crown's and the lord's shut-out goods landing in the nearest realm town not besieged (`_outside`; itself with none), the house's lost; and a line whose regiment garrisons it (`SupplyLines.siege_cut(repo, state, code, route)`) is CUT only where its road passes within `siege_entry_reach_m` of a closed road or trail entry (`at` that entry's point; never the countryside or the sea).
- **The men pool and the plan** (spec §3, §4, step 7; `SiegeTactics`, `tests/engine/test_siege_tactics.gd`): each siege day (`_siege_day`, just before the blockade; `SiegeTactics.day`) the POOL is `men_of` over the record's `stationed` besiegers: fit men less prisoner guards (`PrisonerBurden.guard_count`), none while the unit fights a battle not at the town (its assault keeps them). An arrival adds its men from the day it stands at the town; a column on the road adds none. The record's `alloc` (tactic → whole men over `blockade`, `mine`, `bombard`, `ladders`, `engines`; {} = no plan received) never sums above it: `allocate` stores a plan with all five keys or refuses it (`refusal`: an unknown tactic, a figure not whole and non-negative, more men than the pool) with the plan kept. An AI record plans afresh each day by its leading host's doctrine (`EnemyAi.doctrine_of`, `ai_doctrines.csv` `siege_<tactic>_permille`, each row summing to 1000; `plan`: `TownStores.apportion`, largest remainder, ties by name). A standing plan (the crown's, from `SIEGE_PLAN`, step 12) shrinks with its pool by `cut` in the order engines → ladders → mine → bombard → blockade, each cut a `history` line {day, kind `alloc_cut`, tactic, from, to, reason `siege of <town> down to <n> men: <tactic> cut from <a> to <b>`}. A crown record with no plan blockades with its whole pool; men a plan leaves out stand idle. Only the blockade spends its men yet (rows 8–10 give the others work).
- **Hunger**: the town STARVES (`starving`): a crown town when its own `TownStores` (crown + lord + house) are spent AND its garrison's hungriest regiment has no day in hand — the men eat what they carry first; any other town when its record's `stores` are 0 with defenders standing. A besieged crown town (`besieged`) feeds its garrison from its own stores alone (`SupplyLines` sends no depot or realm stock in), so a long siege empties both and `hunger_day` takes the garrison's morale. The besieger's hunger is `should_retreat`'s.
- **Yielding to hunger** (spec §4.1): a starving town whose defenders' mean morale (by fit men, `SiegeBlockade.mean_morale`) is below `siege_yield_morale` yields when its first defender's `own_surrender` is `allow` and the first stationed besieger's `quarter` is `give` (`LawSystem.value`, code order each; `yields`). The hand-over (`hand_over`): every defender's fit and wounded become that besieger's captives (`PrisonerSystem.capture`, id `siege_yield:<id>:<day>`, reason `<town> yielded to hunger`; the prisoners they held pass by `holder_defeated`), and the town falls (`_fall` with `yielded`: `_take` with no column needed at the gate; the fall's event carries `yielded` 1). Under `quarter: refuse` no yield is accepted and the town holds on, starving; a capture that cannot be made (the king inside) leaves the siege as it was.
- **The assault** is the ordinary town battle: every `siege_assault_days` days of siege an assault is owed (`assault_due`) and the record's flag `assault` is raised for the NEXT day's battles (`SiegeSystem.assault_armed`: true only for a host in the record's `hosts`) (SiegeSystem runs after them); the flag stands while a battle at the town holds a besieging column, and drops at the settlement after; an owed assault that found no battle (a draw's pause) is tried the next day until fought — the flag never outlives the siege. Each ROUND of it the besiegers fought (a `round` event at the town) moves the siege by its tier's `siege` column in `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv`: `repulsed` (attacker_checked) less `siege_assault_fail_permille` a round, never below 0; `breach` (defender_routed) 1000; blank nothing (a routed besieger falls back and its host RETREATs on `should_retreat`). An assault that ENDS with the besiegers keeping the field (the garrison broke or left) is a breach too.
- **The fall is MANOR-level** (`capture(repo, state, host, events)`, refused — `{}` — unless the host has an active deployment-complete SIEGE before a town the crown still holds, so no town is taken twice): the town's own manor and its province's manors within `siege_capture_radius_km` of the town, excluding every other town’s own manor (`taken_cells`) go to the attacker's ruling house (`_occupy`, `MapManors.set_owner`: a host's kingdom's capital-province house, the crown's `MapManors.crown`) with the reason `taken by <taker>: siege of <town>` (`<realm>'s <role> host`, or `<realm>'s royal army`); the town is OCCUPIED: `GameState.occupations[town]` {occupier, from, house, host (the capturing host, "" for the crown), by, day, cells: cell string → house before}, saved, either side's (an old save's mind `occupied` / `occupied_house` converts once in `SiegeRecord.migrate`). A capture whose attacker is the occupation's `from`, besieging its occupier, is a RETAKE: each manor the occupier's house still holds goes back to its house before, reason `liberated: <taker> retook <town> from <occupier>`, and the occupation ends; every host HOLDing the town turns RETREAT ("the town was taken back"). No province, border, capital or other town moves (`MapOwnership` is not touched). But who CONTROLS the town's province (`MapManors.controller_of`, most manors) can change: on the default pack 5 of the realm's 65 towns take every manor, or most, of a small province with them, so its control (the lord's purse, BUILD and FOCUS there) passes to the raider's house until liberation; the capital's province is not among them. An assault still being fought there is called off (`BattleSystem.call_off`); the crown's garrison still standing marches out for the nearest town of its own (`BattleSystem.march_out`, disengaging); every host before the town turns HOLD; a crown record closes. While occupied, the town and those manors are no target of anyone's (`target_held` asks `SiegeSystem.holder_of` / `occupier_of`). Then a crown town's loss costs the crown: `DefeatSystem.on_town_lost` (`guide/raid_and_defeat.md`), its `defeat_town_lost` event after `siege_fallen`.
- **Liberation**: a HOLD host whose town's manor another house holds again (taken back), or with no live column left, RETREATs; a host that took a town and holds it no more (RETREAT, WITHDRAW, beaten) gives each manor its house still holds back to the house it had (`_give_back`, reason `liberated: <host> left <town>`), told once as `siege_liberated` — only the occupation's own `host`, so a reserve HOLDing beside it frees nothing. A town the crown occupies is freed the same way when its manor is another house's again or no crown land unit with fit men stands at it (`_crown_hold_day`, reason `liberated: <realm>'s royal army left <town>`, `siege_ours_liberated`).
- **What a capture gives the crown** (spec §7, step 3b; `tests/engine/test_siege_gains.gd`): a fresh crown occupation of a foreign town (never a retake) runs `_gains` once per occupation (`GameState.town_takes` {town, day}, saved). The council: each seat +`opinion_gain` = round(`battle_victory_opinion` × (1 + w)), w the `OpinionDrift.weight` of `TOWN_TAKEN` (`action_signatures.csv`, `BATTLE_LOST`'s axes), so 0..2 × base, never a loss; reason `took <town> from <realm> (supports, <axis>)`. The sack: when the plunder law of the record's first unit (code order) reads `allow`, the town's own manor month (`RaidSystem.month_of`, read before the fall) × `siege_sack_permille` / 1000, whole gold, into `metrics.strategic` (`WorldLedger`, source `sack`, reason `sacked <town>`; terms' `no_sack` is step 11). Both ride on `siege_ours_taken` (`opinion`, `sack`). The occupied manors pay their holder as any manor does, nothing extra; and the crown's control stops at its realm (`MapManors.crown_controls` asks `MapOwnership.owner_of`), so an occupied foreign province is never a crown province.
- **Lifted**: a host that leaves its siege any other way (beaten — a column fell back, e.g. before a relief force — hungry, low morale, its main host gone) has it told once as `siege_lifted`.
- **The news** rides in the day's battle letter (`MissiveWriter.battle_report`, after the fighting): `SiegeSystem.news` is every `siege_*` event but the quiet `siege_day` — `siege_begin` (the garrison's strength and the day's gain — never the besiegers' strength, the fog's), `siege_starving`, `siege_repulsed`, `siege_fallen` (`siege_fallen_slain` words when no one of the garrison marched out), `siege_undefended`, `siege_lifted`, `siege_liberated`, a line each from `templates.csv` (the realm slots, the event's own figures). Never a random number.
