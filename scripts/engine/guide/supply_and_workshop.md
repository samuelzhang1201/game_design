# Supply, morale and the war workshop

Spec `2026-10-07-army-supply-lines-design.md` (and its rulings).

- **Two reads per system** (`WorkshopSystem`, `SupplySystem`): `plan()` is the read-only month for card, LEDGER and audit; the truth is `SupplyLines.day_step` and `settle_month`.
- **The war workshop** (`war_workshop` building) turns up to `workshop_capacity_gold` of the LORD's civil goods a month into `workshop_ratio_permille` as much strategic goods at the `WORKSHOP_RATE` (`data/economy/workshop_rates.csv`, `GameState.workshop_rate`, forgotten with the workshop or its manor; `rate_of` is full with none standing). Only on a manor the crown's own house holds (`WorkshopSystem.cells`; an implementer's ruling, unconfirmed).
- **Town stores** (`TownStores`, `GameState.town_stock`: burg → `{crown, lord, house}`, whole SUPPLY UNITS; `army_supply_gold_permille` is a unit's gold). `metrics.strategic` / `lord_strategic` are the `crown` / `lord` sums in gold, rounded down once (`sync_metrics`). `reconcile` absorbs any other writer's move on a metric: a rise lands in the DEPOT (the capital while held), a fall comes off the towns in proportion to their stock; a lost town takes its stores. `TickEngine` runs it after the month's writers and first in each `day_step`.
- **Catchment** (`TownStores.catchment`, cached): a manor feeds the NEAREST town of its province (ties to the lowest burg code; none there, the nearest realm town). **The month's deposit** (`deposit_month`, beside the purse lines that raise the metrics by the same gold): a manor in a crown-controlled province gives all its strategic goods to `lord`; any other realm manor gives `crown_share_permille` to `crown` (apportioned to equal `RealmEconomy.purses`) and the rest to `house`, which keeps at most `town_house_stock_months` of its inflow.
- **The day's order** in `TickEngine._end_day`: works, marches, couriers, then `SupplyLines.day_step` (reconcile, refills, each line's state, interception and delivery), `eat_day`, `hunger_day` (not for one that eats nothing), `SupplyLines.warn_day` (low / empty / town-low, each once till it recovers), ONE dispatch letter (`MissiveWriter.supply_dispatch`).
- **Refill in town**: a land unit camped by a realm town, the seat's included (`SupplyLines.home_town`: `ArmyGround.camped_by`, any order) is topped up to its `carry_days` free of cartage and road: from its town (crown, lord, house bought; nothing past its own town is bought), then the depot (crown, lord), then the CROWN's stock of the other realm towns, nearest first — but a town under siege (`SiegeSystem.besieged`) feeds its seat and camp from its own stores alone. Past its town it is said once as `town_dry` (`supply_dry` / `supply_fed`).
- **Lines** (`SupplyLines`, `GameState.supply_lines`: one per regiment at most, whole numbers): ORDERED while the courier rides, RIDING while the first wagons roll (`ready_day`, `supply_wagon_km_per_day`), then FLOWING, or CUT on a day a hostile regiment (`data/army/army_hostile_stances.csv`) stands within `supply_cut_reach_m` of the road (`cut_point`; no rerouting; cut, it draws and pays nothing), or FRONT on a day its town lies AHEAD of the column (`front_of`: past `supply_ahead_km`, back within it less `supply_front_resume_km`; it draws and pays nothing; CUT wins; said once as `front` / `front_cleared`, in the dispatch and the news bar via `SupplyLines.news`; ruling 20 of spec `2026-10-08-war-room-draw-orders-design.md`). `SUPPLY_POINT_SET` refuses a town ahead when run (`mark_ahead`). The road (`road_px` / `road_between_px`) is the courier's road (`CourierSystem.path_between`), which goes round ground too steep, crosses water and takes rivers (`Couriers` in `CLAUDE.md`); it is re-aimed daily; a town is ELIGIBLE (`eligibility`) within `supply_point_max_days` wagon days. A line whose town leaves the realm is `cancelled` that day; an ORDERED line whose courier is lost is dropped (`order_lost`). A SET for the town a FLOWING or CUT line draws on changes only its rate. The line of a unit garrisoning a besieged town (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`) is CUT by the besiegers (`SupplyLines.siege_cut`, `guide/siege.md`).
- **Delivery and draw**: a flowing line sends `day_bite` of the rate's share of the month's use, never past `army_supply_max_days` after the meal. `_draw` takes `crown`, then `lord`, then BUYS the house's stock at its value plus `supply_buy_markup_permille` while the treasury pays. What arrives is thinned by the road's loss (`SupplySystem.total_loss`: `loss_of` its length plus the marks' and hazards' `road_loss`, once a line a day) and the marshal's efficiency, less his skim. CARTAGE is charged DAILY: `supply_cart_milligold_per_unit_km` per unit per km, whole gold as it crosses (shown in tenths). Every fraction is a MILLI remainder carried on the line (`*_milli`), so the month's draws equal `plan()`.
- **The marshal's month**: his daily counters (`m_*`) sit on the line (a retired line's, and the in-town refills', on the regiment's entry). `settle_month` writes one row per land regiment for his report (`MissiveWriter.marshal_report`: days, load, point, rate, km, cartage, road loss, units bought — his CLAIM), banks his skims (`Governance.add_skims`; a sub-gold fraction carries) and resets the counters. Supply letters keep their own count (`missive_keep_supply`, `MissiveLog.SUPPLY_KINDS`), never evicting a spy's report or receipt.
- **The three orders** `SUPPLY_POINT_SET` / `SUPPLY_POINT_CLEAR` / `SUPPLY_CARRY_SET` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows, offered only while paused, riding by courier: the first two to the TOWN (`to_town`) on their own, the carry order to the column; a point set on the march's confirm or a sealed draft (`with_column`), or given while a courier for that regiment waits to set out, rides WITH the column's courier instead (`ActionSystem._send_supply`, owner 2026-10-10) and its wagons leave the town on his delivery. Every delivered warrant that starts the wagons is a `set_out` event (`SupplyLines.set_out_events`, said first in the day's dispatch, `set_out_news`). `CourierSystem.pending_for` (the march / halt reader) passes a courier carrying only supply orders by.
- **Morale**: the monthly drift (`army_morale_drift_per_month`) heads for a mark set by the days in hand as the month turns (`ArmySystem.morale_mark`: `army_morale_starving` … `army_morale_fed`, + `army_morale_garrison_bonus` on a seat, `ArmyGround.seated_in`). Under `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, `ArmySystem.hunger_day` takes `army_morale_hungry_day` a day (`army_morale_starving_day` at none). `TriggerDsl`'s `host_hungry` drives the LEDGER's `hungry_host` loop.

- Personnel supply reads fit plus wounded people physically with the unit from MilitaryRoster; dead, released and current captives do not consume their former unit’s supplies. Equipment counts never substitute for crew. Noble-specific multipliers belong to the recruitment stage.
