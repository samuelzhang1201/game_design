# Authored battlefield decisions (`BattleEvents`)

`battle_events.csv` and `battle_event_choices.csv` are independent of the monthly story pool. Their unique EV headings and Scene prose live in `RANDOM_EVENTS.md`; `StorySource` already reads them. A field battle never invents the tunnel engineering trigger: P6 owns its connection to real engineering work and application of the authored material/progress/risk schema: shoring specifies timber and gold, rerouting specifies lost progress, and continuing declares a bounded collapse probability and warning.

`offer` checks actual morale/flanking, fit nobles, genuinely joined reinforcements, withdrawing wounded with a real route, or physically accessible abandoned food. One engine RNG draw selects an eligible weighted event or no event; `battle.event_checks` and `GameState.battle_events` save that result per battle/day. Retried windows and JSON loads never reroll. The durable token journal retains selected options, actor, reasons, actual effects and receipt after the battle disappears. Cooldowns, costs, strengths and personality weights live in the battle tables.

Authority freezes when an event is offered. The king's local event blocks the saved day cursor; a later departure keeps that decision pending. Remote events use the affected unit's actual commander, legal affordable options, own conditions, personality and skills, with stable choice IDs breaking ties. A later royal arrival never undoes a commander decision. `choices_for` returns labels, costs, effects and current refusal reasons. `choose` revalidates target, resources, received laws and frozen terms before costs or consumption. Invalid choices preserve the same event; every event has a lawful conservative alternative. If the regiment is removed, captured or its battle has ended, only a contextual report-cancellation choice remains legal; it consumes no resources and closes the unexecuted decision. Event costs do not charge ordinary order/courier fees.

Actual joining records unit/side/day evidence. Keeping that regiment in reserve removes only its attacking contribution: it remains physically present, eats normally and defends/suffers normal exposure. Explicit lawful attack or reserve commitment clears the reserve flag. No cohort or replacement troop is created. Temporary action factors use unique event sources in `BattleStrength`'s existing single action category and expire on saved absolute days. Wounded-road choices use `event_march_after` through `CampSystem.can_march_entry`; they cannot bypass an existing packing deadline.

Withdrawal's existing store subtraction creates one cache at the unit's actual position with battle/source/side/amount/reason. Capture requires actual reach and lawful seizure; protected withdrawal bars it. Collection transfers only available cache food that fits the receiver's real carry capacity and pays a temporary action diversion. Destruction removes food without credit. The cache is never debited from the withdrawing unit twice. Leaving and reporting preserves the cache when access or law changes.

`TickEngine` groups battle reports by battle ID. Local observation files that battle's letter; remote facts and event receipts ride in an immutable `king_report` payload from that battlefield. Nothing files a remote battle letter immediately. Arrival alone appends `king_state.battle_reports` and sets remote journal `received_day`; later state changes cannot rewrite the sent payload. Siege/raid notices without battle IDs retain their own existing route. `BattlePresentation` must whitelist data and hide unresolved remote events, raw evidence and unreceived receipts.
