## Formations and per-clause agreements (P4.1)

`BattleRules.freeze` snapshots each side's received laws once at contact, using the first unit in sorted code order as the saved representative. `LawSystem.received` reads the newer town/unit version; foreign columns carry independent `received_laws`, seeded from declared defaults, never crown decrees. Legacy columns without that map read defaults. Existing battles without a snapshot acquire one at the first resumed day. Semantic proposals compare all conditions, scopes and deadlines independently, with both proposals and a reason preserved on rejection. The saved `parley` remains bound to the battle through new laws, commanders, reinforcements and JSON saves. `legal_actions` applies live own laws plus shared obligations; protected withdrawal/ceasefire overrides pursuit, and accepting surrender prohibits a red refusal flag without prohibiting one's own surrender. There is no king renegotiation action. `parley_clauses.csv` maps each clause to its law; prisoner treatment, wounded evacuation, safe withdrawal, permitted tactics and own surrender are separate military policies.

`BattleFormation` owns deployment independently of stance/action. `formations.csv` declares four shapes, frontage, terrain/arm/morale/training suitability, centre requirements, factors and reforming costs. A diamond's positive protection requires an actual nearby king or wounded personnel; absent a centre its penalties remain. `BATTLE_FORMATION` is a paid typed order through `ActionSystem` and the existing local/courier path; delivery revalidates membership and suitability, uses a separate side order id and saved within-courier sequence, and expires with the battle. Same-target requests do nothing; retargeting preserves the longest disorder deadline. Saved current/previous/target, progress and last-progress day advance once per completed day using fit-personnel discipline. During reforming only current deployment plus disorder applies; each factor has one source and reason, with integer truncation after each. WAR ROOM and map share its shape; narrow lines fold into extra ranks instead of overlapping figures. Figure-count tiers remain `army_formations.csv`'s responsibility. Normal battle commands, duration and resolution changes belong to P4.2.
