# The crown's sight and the enemy's ghosts (`Sighting`)

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-player-sight-and-ghosts-design.md`; the hosts, battles and day order it reads: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`. Keys `player_sight_km`, `player_sight_estimate_quality`, `ghost_keep_days`; words `templates.csv` `sighting_*`. Whole metres, squared whole distances, no random number.

- **Sight** (`seen_foes`): a foe unit with men under arms (headcount less `dead`, `men_of`) is SEEN within `player_sight_km` (inclusive) of a point of `viewers_m` — each land unit of the player's realm with men under arms (a fleet sees nothing), then each town the crown holds (`TownStores.realm_towns` filtered by `ArmyGround.holds`: an occupied town sees nothing). Out of DEV MODE the map draws the player's units and these (`Map3DContext.regiments`), at their true places.
- **Ghosts** are `GameState.sightings` (saved; an old save reads `{}`): key → `{kind, code, x_m, y_m, day, source, men}`. A sight and a spy name units: a unit's ghost is keyed by its code (`kind` `unit`). The rumour names a host: its ghost is `HOST_KEY` + the host code (`kind` `host`, `men` 0), at `EnemyAi.home_m`; the first ghost of one of its units (`record`) takes it away. `record` keeps the newer day, and on the same day seen > spy > rumour.
- **Who writes them**: `update` once a day at the END of `TickEngine._end_day` (after the battle letter, before the supply lines) — the rumour's host ghost on `EnemyAi.rumoured_host`'s day (the host `warnings` writes the letter about), each seen unit refreshed (`source` `seen`, `men` = `about` at `player_sight_estimate_quality`, bias 0), then `forget`; `on_spy` from `SpySystem.observe` after the report is filed — every column of `SpySystem.foe_codes_near` (the report's `foe_columns`) at its TRUE place, `men` at the spy's own quality and bias (no new draw). Nothing else writes `sightings`.
- **Forgotten** (`forget`, `_gone`): older than `ghost_keep_days` (kept at exactly that age), a unit gone from `foes` or with no man under arms (destroyed in the day's battles), a host no longer in `ai_hosts`. `ghosts` (what the map draws) also leaves out a unit SEEN now (its piece stands there) and a host one of whose units is seen.
- **Never the exact men**: `about` is the middle of `SpySystem.estimate`'s span rounded half up to `intel_facts.csv` `foe_troops`, at least one step for a unit with men. A seen foe's chip shows `≈` it, no bars; its card (`MapObjects.army_card`, intel `basic`) gains a `Sighted` field (`card_line`); a ghost's card (`ghost_card`, reached by a pick of `GHOST_CODE` + key through `army_card`) stands at the ghost's place with `line_of`'s words.
- **The map side** (`Map3DContext.fogged` / `seen_foes` / `ghosts`, `Map3DLayerUnits`' ghost pieces): `scripts/map3d/CLAUDE.md`.
