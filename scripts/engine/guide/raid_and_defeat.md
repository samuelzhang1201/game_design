# Raids and spoils (`RaidSystem`)

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §5.3; the hosts, battles and day order it plugs into: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`. Every number is a `balance.csv` key named here; whole numbers; no random number.

- **The take** (`day_take`, `_take`): a host in RAID with a live column at its target (within `battle_contact_km` of its point, or a town's reach), none of its columns in a battle or `disengaging`, the target still the crown's (`target_held`), takes from the target's manor (`target_cell`) `take_on(month, raid_plunder_permille, n, taken)` = min(`cap_of` = month × permille / 1000, month × permille × (n + 1) / 30000) − taken: whole gold, the remainders carried on the running sum, never more than the share of a month from one manor summed over every host (`taken_of`). `month` = `ManorBars.worth_of(ManorEconomy.line_of(…, state.tick))` — read, never rounded again (inv. 11). The record is the host's mind: `raided` (cell string → `{days, taken}`), saved; `plunder` whole gold.
- **Food**: `raid_supply_permille` of the take is food, `food_units` = take × it / `army_supply_gold_permille`, loaded into the host's live columns by their free room (`BattleSystem.apportion` over `max_units − supplies`, never past `army_supply_max_days`); the plunder gains the take less the loaded food's gold rounded up (`food_gold`). The rest of what the columns cannot carry stays plunder.
- **The crown's books** (Ruling 4): a ledger line WITH its reason, `WorldLedger.apply` (source `raid`, `world_log`), `raided by <host>: <manor>` — the lord's purse (`metrics.lord_gold`, a `world_schema.csv` row with `llm_cap` 0) the whole take when the crown controls the manor's province, else the treasury its `crown_share_permille` of it on the running sum (what it would have had). `line_of` and the month's settlement are untouched.
- **The hazard**: a host's first take marks the manor `Raided` (`data/hazard_kinds.csv`, public; id `raided_<cell>`, `Hazards.add` once ever, the kind's months from this tick), so its morale, pace and supply-line loss reach the crown's columns through the hazards' own paths.
- **Stripped**: a RAID host whose manor is `stripped` (raided and its share taken) or no longer the crown's turns ADVANCE with no target and `think_day` 0 (`EnemyAi.set_mode`, a `host_mode` event), so it chooses again the next day — never that manor (`candidates` skips it); with nothing within supply reach, it WITHDRAWs.
- **Spoils** (`spoils(repo, state, host, winner, place)`): a host the crown beat today — a column of it left a battle `rout`, `retreat` or `broken` (the round events' `left`, `_beaten`) — pays `battle_spoils_permille` of its plunder into `metrics.strategic` (`WorldLedger.apply`, source `spoils`, reason `spoils: <host> routed at <place>`, the battle's `place_label`); its plunder is then 0, so a defeat pays once. Nothing for a winner other than the player's realm or a host with no plunder.
- **The day** (`settle_day(repo, state, battle_events)`, host code order): spoils, then the take, then the stripped check. Events: `raid_spoils {spoils, plunder, place, place_x_m/y_m}`, `raid_begin {cell, place, …}` (the host's first day at a manor), the quiet `raid_day {take, food, plunder, debit, purse}`, `host_mode`. The battle letter gives `raid_begin` / `raid_spoils` a line each (`templates.csv`, the realm slots, the event's figures); a letter of raid news alone is about the raid's place.
- **Tuning**: `raid_supply_permille` is set so a raiding host eats more than its raid feeds it: plunder keeps it in the field past its own stores, yet it still turns home (in the no-orders run the main host raids the richest open town for the thirty days of its share, then withdraws); a value that lets it feed itself sends it roaming from town to town with no end. The key's note in `balance.csv` has the figures; `test_raid` holds the run.
- **Accepted limits**:
  - The cap is ONE month's share per manor for the manor's whole life — no monthly reset: a stripped manor stays stripped (`raided` is never cleared).
  - The Raided hazard is laid once ever per manor, on its own cell only (`raided_<cell>`); a manor raided again after it ends is not marked again.
  - Two hosts raiding one manor each keep their own running sum (`raided` on each mind), so it drains faster; the total over every host never passes the cap (`taken_of`).
  - For a manor of a province the crown does NOT control the treasury pays only `crown_share_permille` of the take — what the crown actually loses; the lord's purse pays the whole take of a controlled province.
  - The take is debited wholly as GOLD, though the month counts the goods' worth too; no goods pool is debited.
  - Any column of a host leaving a battle beaten (rout, retreat, broken) makes the WHOLE host pay its spoils, as `should_retreat` turns the whole host for one beaten column.
  - The ledger lines go only into `world_log` (the player sees the purse and the letter).

# A lost town (`DefeatSystem`)

Spec §6: a loss costs the crown, never ends the campaign. `SiegeSystem.capture` calls `DefeatSystem.on_town_lost(repo, state, town, host)` once the town's manors have moved; its `defeat_town_lost` event follows `siege_fallen` among the day's events and gets a line in the battle letter (`templates.csv` `defeat_town_lost`: the town, the event's `gold` and `morale`; a 0 figure drops its clause — `_gold`, `_people`, `_bare`).

- **Treasury**: `battle_defeat_treasury_permille` of a POSITIVE `metrics.treasury`, whole gold rounded down (`treasury_loss`), one `WorldLedger.apply` line (source `defeat`, reason `lost <town> to <host label>`). An empty or indebted treasury (the schema lets it go below 0) loses nothing more; a share of a positive one never makes it negative.
- **The people**: `battle_defeat_morale` points of `metrics.peasant_contentment` (the schema's row, 0..100), as far as its floor lets it fall; reason `−<N>: lost <town> to the raiders` with N what actually fell; nothing left to fall, no line.
- **The council**: each seat −round(`battle_defeat_opinion` × (1 + `OpinionDrift.weight`)) (`opinion_loss`): the drift's own weight (alignment × `policy_sensitivity` × (1 − `pragmatism`)) of the `BATTLE_LOST` row of `action_signatures.csv` (not an order: militarism and expansionism), so a seat that prizes arms takes it harder; never a gain. `Character.change_opinion` with OpinionDrift's reason form, `lost <town> to the raiders (opposes, <axis>)`. No other opinion code.
- **The beat**: chapter beat `war_01` (`DefeatSystem.EVENT_ID`; `data/chapter_events.csv` `trigger` `never`, its two choices with signatures in `chapter_event_choices.csv`, prose in `game_story/chapter_0/war_events.md` under `### W-01`, presented by the Marshal) is put on `event_state.scheduled` for the next month's roll, unless one already waits there. A war beat (`DefeatSystem.BEAT_PREFIX`) YIELDS: `EventSystem.roll` fires any other due beat first, so the prologue's month-exact chain never slips; the war beat waits for a roll with no chain beat due (in the chain's dense stretch, possibly its end). It is outside the chain, so `test_chapter_zero`'s reachability and path checks cover `c0_*` only; its art is planned (`V-56`, `event_art.csv`) and listed in that test's `PENDING_INSTALL` until rendered. Every `war_*` row is in `DefeatSystem.BEATS` (no dead war beat).
- **Once per loss**: `GameState.town_losses` (saved) keeps `{town, host, day}`; the same key again returns `[]`. Taking the town back (liberation) calls nothing here: no refund.
