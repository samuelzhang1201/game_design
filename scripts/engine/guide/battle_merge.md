# Battle merging (`BattleMerge`)

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-unit-behavior-and-battlefield-design.md` §11; the battle lifecycle it plugs into: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`; agreements and their parties: `scripts/engine/guide/battle_deployment.md`.

**Merging** (`BattleMerge.day`, from `prepare_day` after the deploy and group refresh, before withdrawals and detection): battles A < B merge when a crown group and a foe group, one from each, stand within `battle_group_contact_m` (inclusive), or an attacking ranged-equipped unit has positive actual firing personnel against the other field through `BattleRanged.counts`. Cross-field fire retains the shooter's received task, action, reserve status, pursuit bounds and facing, checks local sight and both sources' party-scoped protection, and includes friendly blockers from both fields. Range alone is insufficient. Before either trigger, every cross-field unit pair must retain the same hostile/friendly relationship under the allegiance union (`ArmySystem._hostile`); incompatible relations stay separate. Anchors, boxes, circles and same-allegiance proximity never merge, nor two town battles, a battle mid-round (`pending_round`) or one with an empty side. The lower id survives unless only the other holds a `town`; it repeats until no pair is left. Units are unioned by allegiance (`_side_for`), every per-unit or accumulated record (`personnel_entries`, `unit_entries`, `unit_formations`, `reserves`, `abandoned_supplies`, `event_checks`, evidence, costs, modifiers, reinforcement and journal lists) is folded in (the survivor's entry wins, an entry's `side` is re-read), `rounds`, `last_resolved_day` and `deployed_day` take the larger and `start_day` the smaller, so nothing reruns and no group fights twice a day. The absorbed side-level state (`parley`, `commanders`, `actions`, `formations`, `routed_side`, inspiration, place, start day) stays verbatim under `sources[<id>]`; each agreement keeps its own `parties` (`legal_actions` scopes terms to them, see battle_deployment). The absorbed id lives on in `GameState.battle_aliases` (saved, default `{}`): `BattleSystem.canonical` and `battle_by_id` follow it, so tasks, warrants, reports and offers holding the old id reach the survivor. One `{kind: "merge", battle_id, absorbed, reason}` event is journaled. A siege and its assault are untouched. The merge also picks ONE commander per side (`BattleCommand.unify`): the two sources' commanders still eligible, a valid explicit nominee first (both: newer `commander_nominee_seq`, then smaller id), else `select`; journaled in `command_unions`, the other commands his own unit, a pending warrant from him is refused at delivery, and only incapacity re-elects afterwards. A pending side warrant of the absorbed battle gets `request.side` rewritten to its units' survivor side with a `merge_remap` note (the original request still re-issues as the same warrant), and `BattlePresentation` compares stored battle ids through `canonical`.

## Candidate field union

`BodyBattle` accepts the closed CF05 `{kind:"merge",first_id,second_id}` request (canonical decimal ids, first < second) at the contact stage, and `contacts` discovery merges two distinct live fields the same way. Both fields must be canonical and live with both sides occupied; two towns, a pending or frozen round (`pending_round` / `body_round`) and an allegiance union that `_compatible` refuses defer unchanged. The union needs lawful physical cross-field contact in either direction through `BodyBattleContact` (both sources' party protections apply; friendly frames and anchors never count); the plan carries those pairs. With no melee contact, any attacking ranged-equipped member of either field defers the whole operation as `direct_fire_dependency` until the CF06 firing query exists; otherwise it defers with no contact. On private staging both fields' parties freeze from their current members first, then every absorbed unit's exact store, anchor and facing transfers to its survivor side by allegiance (`side_map_of`, so reversed sides swap) through `UnitBodies.transfer_battle_candidate`, and only then does `BattleMerge.absorb` — the same fold the production `_merge` uses — stash the absorbed side-level state under `sources`, fold the records, take the max resolved day, remap warrants, offers and aliases, unify command and delete the absorbed field. The candidate never calls `BattleGroups.refresh`: no row is reseeded or moved, no RNG or cost changes. One merge event is noted on the survivor and journaled; the outer transaction seals one receipt, and exact direct/JSON retries are no-ops. Ordinary `BattleMerge.day` dispatch is unchanged.
