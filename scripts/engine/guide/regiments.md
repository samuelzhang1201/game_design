# Regiments

- Regiments carry state — morale, wounded, supplies, hidden training — in `GameState.regiments`, settled monthly by `ArmySystem` (`balance.csv` `army_*`, `data/army_*.csv`; spec `2026-09-17-regiment-state-design.md`).
- **The march is engine state**: a regiment carries `x_m`/`y_m`/`dest_x_m`/`dest_y_m`/`order` in whole METRES of the pack's measure (pack px would round a day to nothing; floats break the JSON save). Every map, card and terrain lookup reads `ArmySystem.position_of`. `ArmySystem.march_day` runs under `TickEngine.advance_day` every one of the 30 days (the tick's included), in code order, EVENLY as a courier rides: each day what is ahead over the whole days left at the day's pace where the column stands (`day_march_at`: `pace_tenths × 100` × the weather × the ground), rounded up, so an N-day march ends exactly as the Nth day ends. Every arrival figure (days out, the aim ruler, `CourierSystem.arrival_days`) is `march_days_between`, the march walked day by day. It lands exactly on the destination and GARRISONS; arrivals go on `TickEngine.day_arrivals` for the news, not the return value. The foes (`GameState.foes`) are walked after the player's columns by the same step (`battle_and_ai.md`). Straight-line; no pathfinding yet.
- **Pace** `ArmySystem.speed_km_per_day`: the slowest arm's pace (`data/army_speed.csv`) × readiness, the weighted mean (`army_speed_w_*`) of arm, supplies (`ArmySystem.supply_score`: full at `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, linear to 0 at none; only a hungry column drags), morale and training — whole tenths of a km — × the carried load (`ArmySystem.load_permille`, `data/army_carry_loads.csv`) × the weather (`ArmySystem.day_march_m`) × the ground (`ground_permille`, `data/army_ground_speed.csv`: within `army_road_reach_m` of a road or trail its row × the height tier's, else the terrain's).
- **Orders** `ARMY_MARCH` / `ARMY_GARRISON` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows; for a land regiment they DISPATCH a courier (`scripts/engine/CLAUDE.md`, Couriers). `ArmySystem.ORDER_STANDBY` at the campaign's start, `ORDER_GARRISON` on every arrival and by a Cancel march; ARMY_GARRISON is otherwise only the route menu's Cancel march.
- **Towns** (spec `2026-10-06-army-location-and-town-defence-design.md`): a land regiment is IN a town within its group's `data/army_town_reach.csv` km (`ArmySystem.town_of`, the nearest); `held_town_of` only one its kingdom holds; `works_of` (town / walls / citadel) multiplies `data/army_town_defence.csv` onto the terrain factor for the defender. A GARRISON is only in a held town (`SupplySystem.garrisoned_in`); halted in the open it is a field camp. The pack quarters every land regiment in a town of its province.
- **The dead are a count beside the immutable pack headcount**: a regiment's state carries `wounded` and `dead` (whole heads; `dead` defaults 0, an old save has none). Fit = headcount − wounded − dead, never below 0 (`ArmySystem.fit_heads`, used by `power_on`, `fit_permille`, the hazards' wounds and the folio's Fit row); the arms scale through fit proportionally, so nothing is split per arm. The dead never return (`wound_recovery` gives back only the wounded).
- **Battles** (`BattleSystem`: contact, rounds, stances, leaving, the end) are their own guide, `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`. A regiment's state also carries its battle `stance` (default steady; an old save is seeded steady by `ensure`).
- **Strength**: `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (fit men per mille of the headcount; a foreign regiment read as it would start) picks the row of `data/army_formations.csv` (nine / six / three figures).
- **Bars**: `ArmySystem.bars_of` — readiness (`readiness_permille`) and stores (`stores_permille`: days in hand over the carried load, falling daily). Supplies are whole units; DAYS in hand (`ArmySystem.days_in_hand`, never saved) is units × 30 / `supply_use`, capped by `army_supply_max_days`; `carry_days` is the standing load order. They are EATEN a day at a time (`ArmySystem.eat_day`, the month's use spread over thirty days in whole supplies by `day_bite`).
- **Foes** (spec `2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §2): the raider realm's regiments in the field live in `GameState.foes` (code → the same state shape), NEVER in `regiments`, so the player-only iterations (supply lines, the LEDGER, hazards, the map) cannot see them; `ArmySystem.state_of` reads a foe's stored state first and `ArmySystem.is_foe` tells them apart. The hosts' minds are `GameState.ai_hosts`, the fights `GameState.battles`, ids `foe_next_id` (all saved, absent keys load empty). Tables: `data/battle_outcomes.csv`, `battle_stances.csv`, `ai_doctrines.csv`, the pack's `military/raid_hosts.csv` (`DataRepository.raid_hosts`), and the `battle_*` / `ai_*` / `siege_*` / `raid_*` balance keys.
