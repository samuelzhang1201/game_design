# Regiments

- Regiments carry state — morale, wounded, supplies, hidden training — in `GameState.regiments`, settled monthly by `ArmySystem` (`balance.csv` `army_*`, `data/army_*.csv`; spec `2026-09-17-regiment-state-design.md`).
- **The march is engine state**: a regiment carries `x_m`/`y_m`/`dest_x_m`/`dest_y_m`/`order` in whole METRES of the pack's measure (pack px would round a day away; floats break the save); through waypoints also `route_m` (the `MarchRoute` curve ahead, `army_route_sample_m`: the line drawn IS the line walked) and `waypoints_m` (spec `2026-10-07-march-waypoints-design.md`). Every lookup reads `ArmySystem.position_of`. `ArmySystem.march_day` runs under `TickEngine.advance_day` every one of the 30 days, EVENLY as a courier rides: each day what is ahead along the route over the whole days left at the day's pace where the column stands (`day_march_at`: `pace_tenths × 100` × the load × the weather × the ground), rounded up, so an N-day march ends as day N ends. Every arrival figure is `march_days_between` (waypoints and a load optional), the march walked day by day. It lands exactly on the destination and GARRISONS, then settles (Units and ground); arrivals go on `TickEngine.day_arrivals`. No pathfinding yet.
- **Pace** `ArmySystem.speed_km_per_day`: the slowest arm's pace (`data/army_speed.csv`) × readiness, the weighted mean (`army_speed_w_*`) of arm, supplies (`ArmySystem.supply_score`: full at `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, linear to 0 at none; only a hungry column drags), morale and training — whole tenths of a km — × the carried load (`ArmySystem.load_permille`, `data/army_carry_loads.csv`) × the weather (`ArmySystem.day_march_m`) × the ground (`ground_permille`, `data/army_ground_speed.csv`: within `army_road_reach_m` of a road or trail its row × the height tier's, else the terrain's).
- **Orders** `ARMY_MARCH` (`waypoints` ≤ `army_march_waypoints_max`, each checked as the end is) / `ARMY_GARRISON` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows; for a land regiment they DISPATCH a courier (below). `ArmySystem.ORDER_STANDBY` at the campaign's start, `ORDER_GARRISON` on every arrival and by a Cancel march; ARMY_GARRISON is otherwise only the route menu's Cancel march.
- **Units and ground** (spec `2026-10-07-army-split-and-ground-design.md`): the army is `state.army_roster`, split at campaign start by `ArmySplit`; read it through `ArmySystem.roster` / `regiment(repo, code, state=null)` and `MapObjects.regiments_of(repo, kingdom, state=null)`, never the pack table (`test_roster_guard.gd`). ONE unit per held town holds its seat (`state.town_seat`, `ArmyGround.seated_in`: the works and the garrison morale); the others camp round it (`camp_town`, `ArmyGround.camped_by`: the free supply). A unit settles on arrival (`ArmyGround.settle`; a halted column too, `set_garrison`) into hex rings (`data/army_split.csv`, `army_camp_blocked.csv`, keys `army_slot_*`), round a town starting past its reach (R16); no free slot on allowed terrain retries on any terrain, then the unit keeps its arrival point. `ARMY_SEAT` swaps by courier (a lapsed order is the courier event "lapsed"); a seat refills from the camp when its holder marches out; a lost town's seat clears (`ArmyGround.reconcile`). A town's reach is `data/army_town_reach.csv` (`ArmySystem.town_at`); `works_of` multiplies `data/army_town_defence.csv` onto the defender's terrain factor.
- **Strength**: `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (fit men per mille of the headcount; a foreign regiment read as it would start) picks the row of `data/army_formations.csv` (nine / six / three figures).
- **Bars**: `ArmySystem.bars_of` — readiness (`readiness_permille`) and stores (`stores_permille`: days in hand over the carried load, falling daily). Supplies are whole units; DAYS in hand (`ArmySystem.days_in_hand`, never saved) is units × 30 / `supply_use`, capped by `army_supply_max_days`; `carry_days` is the standing load order. They are EATEN a day at a time (`ArmySystem.eat_day`, the month's use spread over thirty days in whole supplies by `day_bite`).
