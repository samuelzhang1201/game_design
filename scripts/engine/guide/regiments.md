# Regiments

- Regiments carry state — morale, wounded, supplies, hidden training — in `GameState.regiments`, settled monthly by `ArmySystem` (`army/balance.csv` `army_*`, `data/army_*.csv`; spec `2026-09-17-regiment-state-design.md`).
- **The march is engine state**: a regiment carries `x_m`/`y_m`/`dest_x_m`/`dest_y_m`/`order` in whole METRES of the pack's measure (pack px would round a day away; floats break the save); through waypoints also `route_m` (the `MarchRoute` curve ahead, `army_route_sample_m`: the line drawn IS the line walked) and `waypoints_m` (spec `2026-10-07-march-waypoints-design.md`). Every lookup reads `ArmySystem.position_of`. `ArmySystem.march_day` runs under `TickEngine.advance_day` every one of the 30 days, EVENLY as a courier rides: each day what is ahead along the route over the whole days left at the day's pace where the column stands (`day_march_at`: `pace_tenths × 100` × the load × the weather × the ground), rounded up, so an N-day march ends as day N ends. Every arrival figure is `march_days_between` (waypoints and a load optional), the march walked day by day. It lands exactly on the destination and GARRISONS, then settles (Units and ground); arrivals go on `TickEngine.day_arrivals`. The foes (`GameState.foes`) are walked after the player's columns by the same step and never settle (`battle_and_ai.md`).
- **Slopes and water are gone round** (spec `2026-10-10-march-slope-detour`): `MarchPath` reads the pack's `geography/march_grid.png` (one cell per `army_march_grid_px`, the steepest rise per run in hundredths as the 3D map draws it, 255 water); a cell steeper than `army_max_slope` (per mille) or water is CLOSED. `ArmySystem.route_m_of` is the one road maker: no waypoints and a clear straight line ⇒ `[to]` and no `route_m` stored (the old march); a blocked line ⇒ the SHORTEST way round (A* eight ways, whole-number costs, distance only — the ground's pace stays the march's own — then pulled taut by line of sight), stored as `route_m`; with waypoints each curve leg that crosses a closed cell is replaced by the way round, the waypoint kept on it. The ends' own cells never block; from/to a closed cell the road starts at the nearest open one within `army_march_snap_cells`; no way round (another piece of open ground) walks the straight line. A fleet is never routed. `set_march_m`, `march_days_between`, `curve_px` (the plans; `naval` for a fleet) and `aim_entry` (the foes' daily re-aim and a beaten column's retreat: a route to the same end is kept) all go through it; `_path_of` ignores a route that no longer ends on the destination. `SuiteData.open_country` (in `flat_ground` too) sets `army_march_grid_px` 0 so a mechanics test walks straight. The 3D piece walks its day through the route corners it passes (`CourierSystem.day_path_of`, `MarchRoute.along`). Couriers and supply wagons use the same grid with water OPEN (`MarchPath.of_pack(repo, true)`): they go round a scarp and cross water; armies keep the land grid.
- **Pace** `ArmySystem.speed_km_per_day`: the slowest arm's pace (`data/army/army_speed.csv`) × readiness, the weighted mean (`army_speed_w_*`) of arm, supplies (`ArmySystem.supply_score`: full at `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, linear to 0 at none; only a hungry column drags), morale and training — whole tenths of a km — × the carried load (`ArmySystem.load_permille`, `data/army/army_carry_loads.csv`) × the weather (`ArmySystem.day_march_m`) × the ground (`ground_permille`, `data/army/army_ground_speed.csv`: within `army_road_reach_m` of a road or trail its row × the height tier's, else the terrain's).
- **Orders** `ARMY_MARCH` (`waypoints` ≤ `army_march_waypoints_max`, each checked as the end is) / `ARMY_GARRISON` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows; for a land regiment they DISPATCH a courier (below). `ArmySystem.ORDER_STANDBY` at the campaign's start, `ORDER_GARRISON` on every arrival and by a Cancel march; ARMY_GARRISON is otherwise only the route menu's Cancel march.
- **Personnel has one ledger** (`MilitaryRoster`, `MilitaryPopulation`, `MilitaryMigration`): `GameState.military_cohorts` holds mutually exclusive fit, wounded, captured_current, dead and released counts; initial is their sum. Unit rosters reference cohort IDs and a counted one-person noble commander. `mobilize` and `transition` validate complete batches and source population before committing; historical initialization and organization migration preserve existing personnel without replaying recruitment costs or historical deaths. Legacy regiment casualty fields are derived read-only projections. Captives occupy their original province's residents, released personnel are civilians and cannot return to service except through a new paid cohort.
- **Equipment is separate**: `MapObjects.regiment_equipment_count` counts ships/guns/elements; `regiment_personnel` and `ArmySystem.headcount` multiply by the current pack's positive integer `crew`. Ordinary units split by the `army_split_cap_men` personnel cap, fleets and the one royal train retaining their organization exceptions. `source_parts` saves pre-pooling provenance; sources use province population weighted by the arm's rural/urban rates. A legacy pooled royal train with no saved parts uses its saved garrison province as an explicitly estimated origin, marked in saved source parts and bootstrap/organization explanations; existing recorded parts remain authoritative. Unknown sources, zero weights and insufficient historic residents produce migration conflicts, never lost troops or negative populations.
- **Existing saves migrate from their saved force**: first children retain old IDs, sibling IDs and aliases are saved, stocks and losses remain whole and conserved, paid orders are remapped without replaying charges. An in-flight day keeps its participants/cursors and bootstraps unsplit personnel first; reorganization waits until `TickEngine.resolve_day` clears the completed transaction. Organization version and tokens make repeated loads stable.
- **Command is personal** (`BattleCommand`): eligible authored `commanders.csv` roles of the unit's realm can hold one unit each; rulers are excluded. Appointments carry reasons and refer to a person already in noble personnel. A wounded, dead or captured commander loses the appointment; recovery/release does not restore it. Battlefield command prefers a legal nomination, then martial skill, prestige, stable ID. Ordinary reinforcements retain a valid incumbent. Departure requires a valid unit commander.
- **Battles** (`BattleSystem`: contact, rounds, stances, leaving, the end) are their own guide, `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`. A regiment's state also carries its battle `stance` (default steady; an old save is seeded steady by `ensure`).
- **Units and ground** (spec `2026-10-07-army-split-and-ground-design.md`): the army is `state.army_roster`, split at campaign start by `ArmySplit`; read it through `ArmySystem.roster` / `regiment(repo, code, state=null)` and `MapObjects.regiments_of(repo, kingdom, state=null)`, never the pack table (`test_roster_guard.gd`). ONE unit per held town holds its seat (`state.town_seat`, `ArmyGround.seated_in`: the works and the garrison morale); the others camp round it (`camp_town`, `ArmyGround.camped_by`: the free supply). A unit settles on arrival (`ArmyGround.settle`; a halted column too, `set_garrison`) into hex rings (`data/army/army_split.csv`, `army_camp_blocked.csv`, keys `army_slot_*`), round a town starting past its reach (R16); no free slot on allowed terrain retries on any terrain, then the unit keeps its arrival point. The map has no seat or garrison button: settling and refilling are automatic. A royal `ARMY_SEAT` order swaps by courier (a lapsed order is the courier event "lapsed"); a seat refills from the camp when its holder marches out; a lost town's seat clears (`ArmyGround.reconcile`). A town's reach is `data/army/army_town_reach.csv` (`ArmySystem.town_at`); `works_of` multiplies `data/army/army_town_defence.csv` onto the defender's terrain factor. Against the enemy a town's seat AND its camp are its garrison (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`, `guide/siege.md`); a town an enemy host occupies is held by no one's seat or camp (`ArmyGround.holds`).
- **A held facing** (spec `2026-10-11-war-room-front-line-design.md`): a regiment's state may carry `facing_deg` (a whole degree, 0 east / 90 south / 180 west / 270 north in the pack's y-down frame; `WarFront.facing_vector`) and `hold_after` (a stance parked for its arrival); both save as ints and an old save has none; `hold_days` counts the days settled in hold, not marching and in no battle (`ArmySystem.hold_day` in `TickEngine` after the raids; capped at balance `camp_after_days`, reset by a march, another stance or a battle) and `ArmySystem.camped` is that cap reached in the field (no `ArmyGround.garrison_town`). `battle_and_ai.md` has the stance rule.
- **Strength**: `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (fit men per mille of the headcount; a foreign regiment read as it would start) picks the row of `data/army/army_formations.csv` (nine / six / three figures).
- **Bars**: `ArmySystem.bars_of` — readiness (`readiness_permille`) and stores (`stores_permille`: days in hand over the carried load, falling daily). Supplies are whole units; DAYS in hand (`ArmySystem.days_in_hand`, never saved) is units × 30 / `supply_use`, capped by `army_supply_max_days`; `carry_days` is the standing load order. They are EATEN a day at a time (`ArmySystem.eat_day`, the month's use spread over thirty days in whole supplies by `day_bite`; one regiment's meal is `eat_one`, which `EnemyAi` also feeds the foes through).
- **Foes** (spec `2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §2): the raider realm's units in the field — each `raid_hosts.csv` regiment raises the units split from it (`EnemyAi.host_units`), keyed by unit code and leaving their camps and seats — live in `GameState.foes` (code → the same state shape), NEVER in `regiments`, so the player-only iterations (supply lines, the LEDGER, hazards, the map) cannot see them; `ArmySystem.state_of` reads a foe's stored state first and `ArmySystem.is_foe` tells them apart. The hosts' minds are `GameState.ai_hosts`, the fights `GameState.battles`, ids `foe_next_id` (all saved, absent keys load empty). Tables: `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv`, `battle_stances.csv`, `ai_doctrines.csv`, the pack's `military/raid_hosts.csv` (`DataRepository.raid_hosts`), and the `battle_*` / `ai_*` / `siege_*` / `raid_*` balance keys.
- **Clearance** (spec `2026-10-14-unit-clearance-and-scouting-design`, owner rulings 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15): no two LAND units with men (the crown's, the foes', the roster's others at their pack places; a fleet is exempt) stand within D = r_a + r_b of each other, r the unit's ARM radius (`data/army/army_clearance.csv`, sized to the drawn piece; a royal unit reads its chief arm; `army_clearance_on` 0 turns the rule off, `SuiteData.no_clearance`). `MarchClearance` is the pure geometry (whole metres, every function takes D per blocker and works on the disc of D + 1: `first_entry`, `halt_point`, the octagon of radius `r8` = ceil(D / cos 22.5) + 1 with ceil'd diagonals, `hull`, `clump`, `detour`); `ArmySystem.clearance_blockers` is the record of who stands where (built once by `march_day`, which writes each mover's place back, crown codes then foe codes; the previews `march_ahead` / `CourierSystem.day_path_of` share one record per state, day and set of places; the forecasts `march_days_between` / `days_out` pass none, "if the road is clear"). `_march_one(…, blockers)` clips the day's walk against the others' CURRENT places inside a box round the walk. A unit the column stands inside the disc of is let go only while its next leg does not close on it; one it closes on stops an enemy-bound column on the spot. The first disc the walk enters decides: a unit AT WAR with it (`_enemies`: kingdoms at war — the crown and the raider realm, or a diplomacy `Enemy` — so the raider's STANDING roster units too, not only its hosts) anywhere on the road, or a unit within D of the destination, or a clump whose hull holds the destination, is a HALT; any other unit is gone round (an octagon round it and the STANDING units whose octagons touch its, none further than three pair radii from it, plus the units an earlier pass went round; the hull's shorter side, the right side on a tie; each leg checked on the march grid; spliced into `route_m`) or waited behind (the column stands at the hull's entry, its order stays march). An enemy is NEVER gone round. A column that waits two days running gives way (R-C): of two waiting on each other the later code stops where it stands (order garrison, `road_blocked_by`), a crown unit's stop is the news line `army_road_blocked`; the AI re-plans. A halt is an arrival: order garrison, destination here, `halted_by` (the blocker's code), `halt_day`, `halt_aim_m` (where it was bound), the parked hold dropped, `facing_deg` toward the blocker; the news bar says `army_halt_short`. A column halted before a non-enemy camps (`ArmyGround.settle` with `near_m`: the free slot nearest the halt point); before an enemy it stays where it halted. A HOST column halted by a friendly within `army_spread_reach_m` of its target takes the nearest free camp slot round the target from ring 1 (`ArmySystem._spread_foe`, `ArmyGround.free_slot`), so a host spreads round its town instead of queueing; `SiegeSystem.at_town` counts that reach as at the town; `EnemyAi._stands_halted` keeps a standoff (same aim, an enemy still obstructing the onward road, or a friendly blocker at a slot) from being re-aimed. `set_march_m` and `aim_entry` clear the halt fields (and the facing of a new destination); `facing_of` reads a foe's too. Camps: the lattice pitch `army_slot_spacing_m` holds two of the widest units (4400); `ArmyGround.is_free(…, radius)` refuses within the radii's sum (half a pitch when the rule is off), `take` records a radius, `held_taken` counts marchers, a seat is taken only while no unit stands within clearance of the town's point, a seat a march empties is refilled by `ArmyGround.reconcile` once the point is clear (at once, in `leave`, with the rule off); `EnemyAi.spawn` puts a host's first unit on its assembly point and spreads the rest on the lattice. Accepted: two columns both moving may cross on screen mid-day; clearance holds at every day's end and always against standing units.

- `ArmySystem.personnel_by_arm` is the shared live headcount for the realm card and host panel: fit plus wounded, excluding captives and terminal states, directly from cohorts where present. Mixed legacy units attribute their unspecified arm losses proportionally to equipment×crew with stable largest remainders. The realm card keeps its equipment-weighted chart measure by dividing personnel by crew; displayed counts remain people.
