# Regiments

- Regiments carry state — morale, wounded, supplies, hidden training — in `GameState.regiments`, settled monthly by `ArmySystem` (`balance.csv` `army_*`, `data/army_*.csv`; spec `2026-09-17-regiment-state-design.md`).
- **The march is engine state**: a regiment carries `x_m`/`y_m`/`dest_x_m`/`dest_y_m`/`order` in whole METRES of the pack's measure (pack px would round a day away; floats break the save); through waypoints also `route_m` (the `MarchRoute` curve ahead, `army_route_sample_m`: the line drawn IS the line walked) and `waypoints_m` (spec `2026-10-07-march-waypoints-design.md`). Every lookup reads `ArmySystem.position_of`. `ArmySystem.march_day` runs under `TickEngine.advance_day` every one of the 30 days, EVENLY as a courier rides: each day what is ahead along the route over the whole days left at the day's pace where the column stands (`day_march_at`: `pace_tenths × 100` × the load × the weather × the ground), rounded up, so an N-day march ends as day N ends. Every arrival figure is `march_days_between` (waypoints and a load optional), the march walked day by day. It lands exactly on the destination and GARRISONS, then settles (Units and ground); arrivals go on `TickEngine.day_arrivals`. The foes (`GameState.foes`) are walked after the player's columns by the same step and never settle (`battle_and_ai.md`).
- **Slopes and water are gone round** (spec `2026-10-10-march-slope-detour`): `MarchPath` reads the pack's `geography/march_grid.png` (one cell per `army_march_grid_px`, the steepest rise per run in hundredths as the 3D map draws it, 255 water); a cell steeper than `army_max_slope` (per mille) or water is CLOSED. `ArmySystem.route_m_of` is the one road maker: no waypoints and a clear straight line ⇒ `[to]` and no `route_m` stored (the old march); a blocked line ⇒ the SHORTEST way round (A* eight ways, whole-number costs, distance only — the ground's pace stays the march's own — then pulled taut by line of sight), stored as `route_m`; with waypoints each curve leg that crosses a closed cell is replaced by the way round, the waypoint kept on it. The ends' own cells never block; from/to a closed cell the road starts at the nearest open one within `army_march_snap_cells`; no way round (another piece of open ground) walks the straight line. A fleet is never routed. `set_march_m`, `march_days_between`, `curve_px` (the plans; `naval` for a fleet) and `aim_entry` (the foes' daily re-aim and a beaten column's retreat: a route to the same end is kept) all go through it; `_path_of` ignores a route that no longer ends on the destination. `SuiteData.open_country` (in `flat_ground` too) sets `army_march_grid_px` 0 so a mechanics test walks straight. The 3D piece walks its day through the route corners it passes (`CourierSystem.day_path_of`, `MarchRoute.along`).
- **Pace** `ArmySystem.speed_km_per_day`: the slowest arm's pace (`data/army_speed.csv`) × readiness, the weighted mean (`army_speed_w_*`) of arm, supplies (`ArmySystem.supply_score`: full at `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, linear to 0 at none; only a hungry column drags), morale and training — whole tenths of a km — × the carried load (`ArmySystem.load_permille`, `data/army_carry_loads.csv`) × the weather (`ArmySystem.day_march_m`) × the ground (`ground_permille`, `data/army_ground_speed.csv`: within `army_road_reach_m` of a road or trail its row × the height tier's, else the terrain's).
- **Orders** `ARMY_MARCH` (`waypoints` ≤ `army_march_waypoints_max`, each checked as the end is) / `ARMY_GARRISON` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows; for a land regiment they DISPATCH a courier (below). `ArmySystem.ORDER_STANDBY` at the campaign's start, `ORDER_GARRISON` on every arrival and by a Cancel march; ARMY_GARRISON is otherwise only the route menu's Cancel march.
- **The dead are a count beside the immutable pack headcount**: a regiment's state carries `wounded` and `dead` (whole heads; `dead` defaults 0, an old save has none). Fit = headcount − wounded − dead, never below 0 (`ArmySystem.fit_heads`, used by `power_on`, `fit_permille`, the hazards' wounds and the folio's Fit row); the arms scale through fit proportionally, so nothing is split per arm. The dead never return (`wound_recovery` gives back only the wounded).
- **Battles** (`BattleSystem`: contact, rounds, stances, leaving, the end) are their own guide, `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`. A regiment's state also carries its battle `stance` (default steady; an old save is seeded steady by `ensure`).
- **Units and ground** (spec `2026-10-07-army-split-and-ground-design.md`): the army is `state.army_roster`, split at campaign start by `ArmySplit`; read it through `ArmySystem.roster` / `regiment(repo, code, state=null)` and `MapObjects.regiments_of(repo, kingdom, state=null)`, never the pack table (`test_roster_guard.gd`). ONE unit per held town holds its seat (`state.town_seat`, `ArmyGround.seated_in`: the works and the garrison morale); the others camp round it (`camp_town`, `ArmyGround.camped_by`: the free supply). A unit settles on arrival (`ArmyGround.settle`; a halted column too, `set_garrison`) into hex rings (`data/army_split.csv`, `army_camp_blocked.csv`, keys `army_slot_*`), round a town starting past its reach (R16); no free slot on allowed terrain retries on any terrain, then the unit keeps its arrival point. `ARMY_SEAT` swaps by courier (a lapsed order is the courier event "lapsed"); a seat refills from the camp when its holder marches out; a lost town's seat clears (`ArmyGround.reconcile`). A town's reach is `data/army_town_reach.csv` (`ArmySystem.town_at`); `works_of` multiplies `data/army_town_defence.csv` onto the defender's terrain factor. Against the enemy a town's seat AND its camp are its garrison (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`, `guide/siege.md`); a town an enemy host occupies is held by no one's seat or camp (`ArmyGround.holds`).
- **A held facing** (spec `2026-10-11-war-room-front-line-design.md`): a regiment's state may carry `facing_deg` (a whole degree, 0 east / 90 south / 180 west / 270 north in the pack's y-down frame; `WarFront.facing_vector`) and `hold_after` (a stance parked for its arrival); both save as ints and an old save has none; `hold_days` counts the days settled in hold, not marching and in no battle (`ArmySystem.hold_day` in `TickEngine` after the raids; capped at balance `camp_after_days`, reset by a march, another stance or a battle) and `ArmySystem.camped` is that cap reached in the field (no `ArmyGround.garrison_town`). `battle_and_ai.md` has the stance rule.
- **Strength**: `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (fit men per mille of the headcount; a foreign regiment read as it would start) picks the row of `data/army_formations.csv` (nine / six / three figures).
- **Bars**: `ArmySystem.bars_of` — readiness (`readiness_permille`) and stores (`stores_permille`: days in hand over the carried load, falling daily). Supplies are whole units; DAYS in hand (`ArmySystem.days_in_hand`, never saved) is units × 30 / `supply_use`, capped by `army_supply_max_days`; `carry_days` is the standing load order. They are EATEN a day at a time (`ArmySystem.eat_day`, the month's use spread over thirty days in whole supplies by `day_bite`; one regiment's meal is `eat_one`, which `EnemyAi` also feeds the foes through).
- **Foes** (spec `2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §2): the raider realm's units in the field — each `raid_hosts.csv` regiment raises the units split from it (`EnemyAi.host_units`), keyed by unit code and leaving their camps and seats — live in `GameState.foes` (code → the same state shape), NEVER in `regiments`, so the player-only iterations (supply lines, the LEDGER, hazards, the map) cannot see them; `ArmySystem.state_of` reads a foe's stored state first and `ArmySystem.is_foe` tells them apart. The hosts' minds are `GameState.ai_hosts`, the fights `GameState.battles`, ids `foe_next_id` (all saved, absent keys load empty). Tables: `data/battle_outcomes.csv`, `battle_stances.csv`, `ai_doctrines.csv`, the pack's `military/raid_hosts.csv` (`DataRepository.raid_hosts`), and the `battle_*` / `ai_*` / `siege_*` / `raid_*` balance keys.
