# Regiments

- Regiments carry morale, supplies and hidden training in `GameState.regiments`; their personnel lives in `GameState.military_cohorts`, settled monthly by `ArmySystem` (`army/balance.csv` `army_*`, `data/army_*.csv`; spec `2026-09-17-regiment-state-design.md`).
- Field camps are saved per unit in `camp_state` (`none` / `building` / `camped` / `packing`), with fixed-point construction progress, a metre-coordinate site, and once-per-absolute-day advancement by `CampSystem.finish_day`. Only full idle field days count; moving, naval, town-seated, threatened-in-battle, and packing units do not build. `army_camp_idle_days` and `army_camp_build_days` are provisional balance values. Combat pauses construction without deleting it.
- Leaving a built or partial camp saves a `departure` and waits one whole packing day. Local orders received before movement use that day as D; courier, AI, and battle-retreat orders received after movement start packing on D+1 and can move no earlier than D+2. Re-aiming preserves the original deadline. Movement starts through the same `CampSystem.can_march` gate; being in battle does not erase the saved order or progress, and a packing column remains attackable. Camp defence is derived once at its saved physical site, while packing retains its reduced factor; supply benefit reduces existing incoming transport loss only and never creates stores. See `battle_and_ai.md` and `supply_and_workshop.md`.
- **The march is engine state**: Whole-metre `x_m`/`y_m`/`dest_x_m`/`dest_y_m` and `order` save the march; `route_m` (`MarchRoute`, `army_route_sample_m`) saves the drawn/walked curve and `waypoints_m` its waypoints (spec `2026-10-07-march-waypoints-design.md`). Read positions through `ArmySystem.position_of`. `TickEngine.resolve_day` freezes daily routes and movement budgets, then `ArmySystem.march_day_saved` distributes their integer remainders across ten tactical substeps: Daily budget is ceil(route left / days left) at `day_march_at` (`pace_tenths × 100` × load × weather × ground), arriving at day N’s end. Saved running gait scales the walk step through `UnitGait.run_step_m` (`battle_deployment.md`). ETAs use `march_days_between`, with optional waypoints/load, walking day by day. Arrival lands exactly, GARRISONS and settles (Units and ground), recorded in `TickEngine.day_arrivals`. Foes march after crown columns each substep and never settle (`battle_and_ai.md`).
- **Slopes and water are gone round** (spec `2026-10-10-march-slope-detour`): `MarchPath` reads the pack's `geography/march_grid.png` (one cell per `army_march_grid_px`, the steepest rise per run in hundredths as the 3D map draws it, 255 water); a cell steeper than `army_max_slope` (per mille) or water is CLOSED. `ArmySystem.route_m_of` is the one road maker: no waypoints and a clear straight line ⇒ `[to]` and no `route_m` stored (the old march); a blocked line ⇒ the SHORTEST way round (A* eight ways, whole-number costs, distance only — the ground's pace stays the march's own — then pulled taut by line of sight), stored as `route_m`; with waypoints each curve leg that crosses a closed cell is replaced by the way round, the waypoint kept on it. The ends' own cells never block; from/to a closed cell the road starts at the nearest open one within `army_march_snap_cells`; no way round (another piece of open ground) walks the straight line. A fleet is never routed. `set_march_m`, `march_days_between`, `curve_px` (the plans; `naval` for a fleet) and `aim_entry` (the foes' daily re-aim and a beaten column's retreat: a route to the same end is kept) all go through it; `_path_of` ignores a route that no longer ends on the destination. `SuiteData.open_country` (in `flat_ground` too) sets `army_march_grid_px` 0 so a mechanics test walks straight. The 3D piece walks its day through the route corners it passes (`CourierSystem.day_path_of`, `MarchRoute.along`). Couriers and supply wagons use the same grid with water OPEN (`MarchPath.of_pack(repo, true)`): they go round a scarp and cross water; armies keep the land grid.
- **Pace** `ArmySystem.speed_km_per_day`: the slowest arm's pace (`data/army/army_speed.csv`) × readiness, the weighted mean (`army_speed_w_*`) of arm, supplies (`ArmySystem.supply_score`: full at `army_supply_low_days` days in hand, linear to 0 at none; only a hungry column drags), morale and training — whole tenths of a km — × the carried load (`ArmySystem.load_permille`, `data/army/army_carry_loads.csv`) × the weather (`ArmySystem.day_march_m`) × the ground (`ground_permille`, `data/army/army_ground_speed.csv`: within `army_road_reach_m` of a road or trail its row × the height tier's, else the terrain's).
- **Orders** `ARMY_MARCH` (`waypoints` ≤ `army_march_waypoints_max`, each checked as the end is) / `ARMY_GARRISON` are ordinary actions with signatures and `order_costs` rows; for a land regiment they DISPATCH a courier (below). `ArmySystem.ORDER_STANDBY` at the campaign's start, `ORDER_GARRISON` on every arrival and by a Cancel march; ARMY_GARRISON is otherwise only the route menu's Cancel march.
- **Personnel reads**: `personnel_capacity(repo, row)` (and compatibility alias `headcount`) counts equipment times `unit_types.crew`. `fit_count`, `living_count` and `wounded_count` read the active personnel ledger: living is fit+wounded, excluding captives and civilians. A non-null state without a roster returns zero; only an explicit null-state preview uses full capacity. `power_of_arms` scales equipment counts by fit personnel / personnel capacity, so full crews do not multiply equipment strength. Supply remains monthly and uses living personnel, including wounded. It rounds once after all factors; a positive living population with positive per-head and terrain consumption consumes at least one grain unit per month, the resource's smallest unit. Empty units or an explicitly zero consumption factor consume zero, so fractional rations cannot exempt living remnants from hunger and desertion. Legacy dead/wounded/deserted fields are migration inputs only; they are not writable personnel balances or read authorities.
- **Recovery and noncombat losses**: month recovery traverses every military roster, including undeployed foreign units; morale/training still settle only the player's deployed regiments. Recovery, hazards and foraging submit `MilitaryRoster.transfer_units` with reasons and stable system/month/unit IDs; desertion uses the absolute day. `conduct_marks` records consumed months even for zero losses/recovery, and `desert_day` guards the fractional carry. Replays after JSON cannot heal, wound or add a fraction twice. Clearance and its preview cache use living ledger counts.
- **Battles** (`BattleSystem`: contact, rounds, stances, leaving, the end) are their own guide, `scripts/engine/guide/battle_and_ai.md`. A regiment's state also carries its battle `stance` (default steady; an old save is seeded steady by `ensure`).
- **Units and ground** (spec `2026-10-07-army-split-and-ground-design.md`): the army is `state.army_roster`, split at campaign start by `ArmySplit`; read it through `ArmySystem.roster` / `regiment(repo, code, state=null)` and `MapObjects.regiments_of(repo, kingdom, state=null)`, never the pack table (`test_roster_guard.gd`). ONE unit per held town holds its seat (`state.town_seat`, `ArmyGround.seated_in`: the works and the garrison morale); the others camp round it (`camp_town`, `ArmyGround.camped_by`: the free supply). A unit settles on arrival (`ArmyGround.settle`; a halted column too, `set_garrison`) into hex rings (`data/army/army_split.csv`, `army_camp_blocked.csv`, keys `army_slot_*`), round a town starting past its reach (R16); no free slot on allowed terrain retries on any terrain, then the unit keeps its arrival point. The map has no seat or garrison button: settling and refilling are automatic. A royal `ARMY_SEAT` order swaps by courier (a lapsed order is the courier event "lapsed"); a seat refills from the camp when its holder marches out; a lost town's seat clears (`ArmyGround.reconcile`). A town's reach is `data/army/army_town_reach.csv` (`ArmySystem.town_at`); `works_of` multiplies `data/army/army_town_defence.csv` onto the defender's terrain factor. Against the enemy a town's seat AND its camp are its garrison (`ArmyGround.garrison_town`, `guide/siege.md`); a town an enemy host occupies is held by no one's seat or camp (`ArmyGround.holds`).
- **A held facing** (spec `2026-10-11-war-room-front-line-design.md`): a regiment's state may carry `facing_deg` (a whole degree 0 east / 90 south / 180 west / 270 north in the pack's y-down frame; `WarFront.facing_vector`) and `hold_after` (a stance parked for its arrival); both save as ints and an old save has none. `ArmySystem.camped` now reads only a fully built, physically occupied `camp_state`; it is not derived from the stance or a second hold-day counter. `battle_and_ai.md` has the stance rule.
- **Strength**: `ArmySystem.fit_permille` (active fit personnel per mille of equipment personnel capacity, for every realm) picks the row of `data/army/army_formations.csv` (nine / six / three figures).
- **Bars**: `ArmySystem.bars_of` — readiness (`readiness_permille`) and stores (`stores_permille`: days in hand over the carried load, falling daily). Supplies are whole units; DAYS in hand (`ArmySystem.days_in_hand`, never saved) is units × 30 / `supply_use`, capped by `army_supply_max_days`; `carry_days` is the standing load order. They are EATEN a day at a time (`ArmySystem.eat_day`, the month's use spread over thirty days in whole supplies by `day_bite`; one regiment's meal is `eat_one`, which `EnemyAi` also feeds the foes through).
- **Foes** (spec `2026-10-07-enemy-ai-and-battle-design.md` §2): the raider realm's units in the field — each `raid_hosts.csv` regiment raises the units split from it (`EnemyAi.host_units`), keyed by unit code and leaving their camps and seats — live in `GameState.foes` (code → the same state shape), NEVER in `regiments`, so the player-only iterations (supply lines, the LEDGER, hazards, the map) cannot see them; `ArmySystem.state_of` reads a foe's stored state first and `ArmySystem.is_foe` tells them apart. The hosts' minds are `GameState.ai_hosts`, the fights `GameState.battles`, ids `foe_next_id` (all saved, absent keys load empty). Tables: `data/battle/battle_outcomes.csv`, `battle_stances.csv`, `ai_doctrines.csv`, the pack's `military/raid_hosts.csv` (`DataRepository.raid_hosts`), and the `battle_*` / `ai_*` / `siege_*` / `raid_*` balance keys.
- **Clearance** (spec `2026-10-14-unit-clearance-and-scouting-design`, owner rulings 2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15): no two LAND units with men (the crown's, the foes', the roster's others at their pack places; a fleet is exempt) stand within D = r_a + r_b of each other, r the unit's ARM radius (`data/army/army_clearance.csv`, sized to the drawn piece; a royal unit reads its chief arm; `army_clearance_on` 0 turns the rule off, `SuiteData.no_clearance`). A unit in a battle with saved `groups` holds its deployment: its radius is the larger of its arm radius and the farthest group's distance from its anchor (rounded up) plus half `battle_group_spacing_m`, so outside columns go round the ground it occupies. `MarchClearance` is the pure geometry (whole metres, every function takes D per blocker and works on the disc of D + 1: `first_entry`, `halt_point`, the octagon of radius `r8` = ceil(D / cos 22.5) + 1 with ceil'd diagonals, `hull`, `clump`, `detour`); `ArmySystem.clearance_blockers` is the record of who stands where (built for each tactical movement substep, which writes each mover's place back, crown codes then foe codes; the previews `march_ahead` / `CourierSystem.day_path_of` share one record per state, day and set of places; the forecasts `march_days_between` / `days_out` pass none, "if the road is clear"). `_march_one(…, blockers)` clips the day's walk against the others' CURRENT places inside a box round the walk. A unit the column stands inside the disc of is let go only while its next leg does not close on it; one it closes on stops an enemy-bound column on the spot. The first disc the walk enters decides: a unit AT WAR with it (`_enemies`: kingdoms at war — the crown and the raider realm, or a diplomacy `Enemy` — so the raider's STANDING roster units too, not only its hosts) anywhere on the road, or a unit within D of the destination, or a clump whose hull holds the destination, is a HALT; any other unit is gone round (an octagon round it and the STANDING units whose octagons touch its, none further than three pair radii from it, plus the units an earlier pass went round; the hull's shorter side, the right side on a tie; each leg checked on the march grid; spliced into `route_m`) or waited behind (the column stands at the hull's entry, its order stays march). An enemy is NEVER gone round. A column that waits two days running gives way (R-C): of two waiting on each other the later code stops where it stands (order garrison, `road_blocked_by`), a crown unit's stop is the news line `army_road_blocked`; the AI re-plans. A halt is an arrival: order garrison, destination here, `halted_by` (the blocker's code), `halt_day`, `halt_aim_m` (where it was bound), the parked hold dropped, `facing_deg` toward the blocker; the news bar says `army_halt_short`. A column halted before a non-enemy camps (`ArmyGround.settle` with `near_m`: the free slot nearest the halt point); before an enemy it stays where it halted. A HOST column halted by a friendly within `army_spread_reach_m` of its target takes the nearest free camp slot round the target from ring 1 (`ArmySystem._spread_foe`, `ArmyGround.free_slot`), so a host spreads round its town instead of queueing. A column just outside that circle also enters when its actual friendly blocker is inside it, the stored halt aim matches, and their current separation is no greater than their clearance radii plus `army_clearance_touch_m`; stale remote halt metadata is insufficient. The spread search uses `free_slot`'s optional `max_radius_m` to filter candidates to the target circle; other callers retain the unbounded default; `SiegeSystem.at_town` counts that reach as at the town; `EnemyAi._stands_halted` keeps a standoff (same aim, an enemy still obstructing the onward road, or a friendly blocker at a slot) from being re-aimed. `set_march_m` and `aim_entry` clear the halt fields (and the facing of a new destination); `facing_of` reads a foe's too. Camps: the lattice pitch `army_slot_spacing_m` holds two of the widest units (4400); `ArmyGround.is_free(…, radius)` refuses within the radii's sum (half a pitch when the rule is off), `take` records a radius, `held_taken` counts marchers, a seat is taken only while no unit stands within clearance of the town's point, a seat a march empties is refilled by `ArmyGround.reconcile` once the point is clear (at once, in `leave`, with the rule off); `EnemyAi.spawn` puts a host's first unit on its assembly point and spreads the rest on the lattice. Accepted: two columns both moving may cross on screen mid-day; clearance holds at every day's end and always against standing units.

## Military personnel foundations

`MilitaryRoster` separates people from equipment (`unit_types.crew`); `composition` includes the officer within nobles. `ArmySplit.opening_sources` pools gun equipment/provenance before personnel planning. Preview whole crews use `army_living_cap_men`; `army_split_cap_men` serves legacy reconstruction only. Invalid opening refuses the campaign.

`GameState.military_cohorts` is the single personnel record; `military_rosters` and `recruitment_projects` refer to its IDs. `fit`, `wounded`, `captured_current`, `dead` and `released` are mutually exclusive and sum to `initial`. Assembling cohorts reserve people but cannot fight. Released civilians and dead people cannot return through a status reversal; a new paid recruitment creates a new cohort.

`MilitaryRoster.mobilize` preflights origins and active+assembling fit+wounded against `army_living_cap_men`; activation rechecks current data, or the recorded mobilization cap for direct calls. Captives do not count. `transition` cannot grow living: recovery is legal, captive return is forbidden. `military_events` replays identical IDs and refuses conflicting requests. Death writes origin population through `WorldLedger`; release frees reservations. `MilitaryPopulation.occupied` counts fit+wounded+captured; civilian availability applies `recruitment_eligible_permille` minus reservations. Kingdom population is derived; civil losses cannot consume reservations. State is integer JSON with arbitrary IDs preserved.

`PrisonerSystem.capture` preflights active source counts and actual fit land-unit receivers, reserves named captives’ places, then applies one saved `MilitaryRoster.transition` before publishing unique `prisoner_batches`. Named IDs and `wounded_subset` are subsets of batch commoners+nobles. `origin_realm` and `initial_count` freeze at capture; current custody is only `disposition=held`. Defeated holders transfer custody to explicit battlefield controllers or release rescued compatriots as civilians through the personnel ledger; with no surviving controller all captives are explicitly released; appointments stay cleared and promises remain. Saved `prisoner_events` replay identical requests before changed-source validation. `migrate_legacy` reconstructs old surrender custody only from matching saved handoff/personnel receipts and refuses ambiguous or missing evidence.

Named officers have a one-person noble cohort with `person_id`. `MilitaryCommanders` reads explicit eligibility from `army/commanders.csv`, checks realm, arm, active council offices and fitness, and appoints the existing person without adding headcount. One person cannot serve in two cohorts or command two units. A casualty clears the appointment with a reason; recovery does not restore it automatically, and release requires fresh enlistment. The king never enters a capturable cohort.

Each split unit retains `source_parts` (original regiment, opening province, arm, equipment count and crew per element); the royal train retains every contributing regiment's parts. Its capital is its station, not the source of every gun crew. Opening province is pack evidence; rural/urban recruitment origins require an explicit initialization rule, not inference from present camp location. Source counts serialize as integers while CSV text remains text.

`MilitaryRoster.transfer_units` converts whole-unit transfers into source-cohort changes before the same atomic transition writer runs. It apportions combined losses first, then destinations, so wound/death rounding cannot claim a person twice. Replays compare the original unit request, not a new allocation over survivors.

`MilitaryBootstrap.initialize` plans source personnel once, privately partitions with the living allocator, then mobilizes capped leaves and appoints each source officer once; children stay vacant. People, equipment, provenance, royal flags and seats are conserved; invalid allocation/camp refuses atomically. `migrate` preserves equipment, casualties and pending days; `ArmySplit.saved_sources` validates/reconstructs provenance without replacing armies. Population conflicts preserve personnel and block recruitment. Organization status is separate from personnel import. Pending new projects own only validated assembling rosters; living allocation remaps their paid people/gear without deployment. Completion creates their allocated units once at legal assembly points.

`MilitaryReorganization.reconcile` caps assigned active+assembling fit+wounded using `army_living_cap_men`; captives do not count. Recursive larger-half-first leaves never consolidate or exempt an arm. Leaf zero retains code, seat, commander and received intent; binary `:living:v2:` descendants retain arm even with zero gear and remain unappointed. Ordered living weights allocate whole equipment/provenance, supplies, inventories, prize gold and carries. Named IDs stay indivisible, including released history after re-enlistment. Running-supply debt joins other carries. Anonymous history/custody/promises stay on the original-ID leaf; only living counts fragment. `military_migration` saves `living_unit_mapping`, `living_cohort_mapping`, `living_cap_version`; older mappings/receipts stay immutable.

One private candidate makes failure atomic and repetition JSON-stable. Open days defer migration; pending rounds defer their participants, allowing idle work. Load marks outstanding live-ledger work even after historical organization; pending blocks recruitment. Reconcile before new days and immediately after a day clears. Between transactions, battle IDs/reports and saved group points/facing persist; boundary rows split in place, wounded-only leaves have no rows. Anchor snapshots and slot counters preserve continuity. Saved unit `living_hold_battle` follows battle aliases and holds children without tasks/receipts; genuine received tasks override it, rout/withdrawal takes precedence. Expand side/host/war-plan/law membership once, retaining singular targets, tasks, couriers, sightings/ghosts and historical projects. Remap unfinished paid cohort/gear allocations without changing request, charge, result or completion IDs. Naval leaves keep their saved legal water point; idle land leaves require legal neighboring camps.

`RecruitmentSystem.quote` requires a held province, rural/urban origin and assembly town. Officers count within the noble cap. Reinforcement fills spare gear before buying whole elements; every arm obeys the assigned living cap, excluding captives and spare gear. `army/recruitment.csv` sets prices, duration and ports. Unaffordable valid quotes retain their breakdown. `recruit` atomically reserves people/officer/gear, charges `WorldLedger` once and saves request/receipt. Identical retries return the receipt before costs/opinions; conflicting IDs refuse.

Assembly advances once per effective day-end. Lost source/town, an unfit officer or an absent receiving unit pauses progress without losing the payment or reserved people. Completion activates cohorts and equipment atomically at the legal assembly position, with no free grain. The noble cap is checked against active survivors plus the new people when payment reserves the fixed composition; subsequent casualties do not change or strand that paid contract. Completion saves actual surviving composition per receiving unit, while the next independent recruitment checks its current survivors again. Fleets assemble at a real adjacent sea berth; stationary reinforcement must be within `recruitment_port_reach_m`. Navigation uses exact polygon containment for fleets, while land orders retain `MapEconomy.cell_at` shoreline tolerance. Organization inheritance and atomic publication of delivered military laws go through `LawSystem.inherit_unit_scopes` and `commit_scopes`.

`MilitaryQuality` reads `army/noble_quality.csv`: fit people determine combat and discipline; living fit and wounded people determine food and wages. Assembling, captured, released and dead people do not contribute active-unit demand or power. Apply identity quality once alongside existing equipment, training and morale factors. Live opening armies receive configured food days at their actual demand; a null-state preview remains commoner equipment capacity. Wages settle once per month through a saved military event and `WorldLedger`, in whole gold; retries cannot charge again.

Custody sources and holders retain their original IDs through living-cap migration; an existing proposal or release continues to resolve the same original cohort and holder.

`PrisonerDisposition` separates frozen proposals, explicit realm consent and fulfilled settlements. Each selection pins current custody, names and wounds; stale ownership, counts or received share requires a new proposal. Ransom moves exact whole gold from the original realm's treasury, apportions gross by selected personnel across sorted holders, then floors each holder's received `ransom_captor_share` once; the remainder goes to that holder's crown. `unit_prize_gold` is the actual unit's saved whole-gold prize account: character wealth is an attribute, not a currency purse. Payments never transfer custody, and receipts retain original captor and current holder.

Release, ransom and bilateral exchange share one `captured_current` → `released` transition. Partial release reduces current batch counts and wounds while retaining `initial_count`; `released_count` prevents live reports counting those captives again as separate releases. Historical battle snapshots never change. Civilian residence stays at the original province/field even after conquest. Named officers retain their character/family and remain unappointed; a new paid recruitment creates a new cohort. A fulfilled noncombat term appends a once-only `civilian_promises` slice to the original cohort (missing term: unrestricted; zero deadline: indefinite; positive deadline: inclusive). `MilitaryPopulation.available` excludes active promised civilians; `MilitaryCommanders.candidate_error` shares the named gate across recruitment, mobilization, assembly and appointments.

Living-cap migration retains each civilian promise with its original released-personnel cohort; restrictions and deadlines survive JSON replay without spreading historical custody.

Legacy migration preserves original-source populations, equipment, funds, receipts and in-flight day cursors. Recorded `source_parts` are authoritative; a pooled royal train without parts uses its saved garrison province as an explicitly estimated origin, with saved `origin_basis` and reason. Unknown sources, zero allocation weights or insufficient historical residents are migration conflicts. Completed living-cap migration is idempotent. `MilitaryBootstrap` validates a private candidate before publishing; reorganization preserves open day/round transactions and retries at their safe boundary.

`MapObjects.regiment_equipment_count` counts elements; `regiment_personnel` counts capacity through positive integer crew. Migration and organization validate crew rather than silently dropping equipment. `ArmySystem.personnel_by_arm` supplies live fit plus wounded counts to realm and host panels; equipment strength remains equipment-based. Hypothetical personnel strength uses the explicit `power_of_personnel` preview.

Departure requires `MilitaryCommanders.eligible_for_unit` for the current appointment when the unit has a personnel roster, both when the order is issued and when its courier delivers. This pure predicate shares appointment validation without creating or moving an officer. `BattleCommand.select` only ranks already eligible candidates; callers enforce presence, rank and existing appointments. `update_battle` filters each side’s existing unit appointments through that pure eligibility gate, retains a valid incumbent on reinforcement, and validates every explicit nomination before publishing either side.

## Area tasks (P6.1a)

- `data/army/task_kinds.csv` lists the four area-task kinds (`search`, `raid`, `destroy`, `siege`; spec 2026-10-16 battle-system §13) as `repo.task_kinds[kind]` = `{label, needs_target, default_encounter, allowed_encounters}`. Only `search` offers `keep_distance`; the others always approach.
- `TaskCoverage` is the pure integer search grid: cells of `task_cell_m` metres (floor division, negatives floored), a cell counts when its CENTRE is inside the circle, a coverage Dictionary maps `"x:y"` to the newest day searched (never lowered). Covered never means "no enemy there".

- **The preview (P6.1b)**: `ArmyTasks.preview(repo, state, realm, task)` is free, pure and never writes: it refuses (`data/army/task_words.csv` words) an empty or non-land unit list, an unknown kind, a radius above `task_max_radius_m`, an off-map centre, a policy the kind does not allow, a destroy with no object kind, a siege with no KNOWN town in the circle (several and no `known_target_id` → choose one). Otherwise it returns units (commander, `CourierSystem.arrival_days` to the centre), the known towns in the circle, the cell count, the `ARMY_TASK` cost (+ one ordinary courier fee per remote unit without a joinable waiting courier), the encounter policy and the received-law limits (a raid under a forbidden `plunder`). `cost.courier` reports whether any selected unit is beyond `king_command_radius_m`, even when a waiting courier carries the order for no new fee. A town is KNOWN when `Intel.level` of it is above `basic` or a `town_people` report covers it (`ArmyTasks.known_town`); a hidden foe or unknown town never changes the result.

- **Issuing (P6.1c)**: `ARMY_TASK` (the preview's fields + `request_id`, `task_id` for a new revision of the same units) and `ARMY_TASK_CANCEL` pay the cost row once plus a courier fee per unit beyond the king's range (`ArmyTasks.courier_fees`) and save `GameState.army_tasks[id]` (revision snapshots, `return_target`, per-unit status and `received_revision`, reasoned `history`). Each unit has its own courier order (local in range); `ArmyTasks.receive` takes only a newer revision (older = `superseded`), a recall is a revision, a lost courier leaves the unit as it was. A unit acts on `ArmyTasks.acting` only; retry by request id replays.

- **Gait choice**: Every substep rechecks the live commander. After withdrawal/pursuit/battle joining, a crown march into an `ArmyTasks.known_town` runs for `key_point` / `town_clear` plus town code while a positive sight/spy unit record within `ghost_keep_days` remains in its reach. Arrival/re-aim/lost evidence stops it. No hidden foe truth; existing camp/food gates apply.
